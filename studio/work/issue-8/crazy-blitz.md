# BLITZ — crazy-ideas filing, issue #8 (Drawer)

Filed by Blitz (crazy-ideas, dedicated-100 programme) after the cell closed
DECLARED. Additions for the finish programme and Ren's pricing. Laws held
throughout: the Drawer holds finished work forever, with NO scores, NO
judgement, NO forward summons; shelf rows carry no counts (audit ruling);
near-silence (Feel 2); no theatre in the archive (Feel 3) — where an idea
rubs a ruling, the rub is named in its entry. Nothing here was compiled.

### D-1 · INK AGEING
Stored tasks rest one ink-step quieter per untouched season (4 steps max,
floored at secondary ink — legibility never dies). REOPEN re-inks to full.
The record is forever; only the ink breathes. Ink step = f(months stored,
months since reopen) over existing type levels. ~2 hours. Feel 5's five-year
test, answered in the type itself.

### D-2 · THE SPINE
Each MONTHS shelf row gains the month's first stored task's opening words,
set quiet after the month name: `OCTOBER 2026 — "ILL Adrenal, one-shot…"`.
Retrieval by recognition, not measurement — the no-counts ruling kept in
spirit and letter. Earliest-task-per-month query + one capped line. ~half
a day.

### D-3 · THE STAIRCASE
Shelf rows recess one step per year boundary (indent + one ground-step,
capped at three). Scrolling = descending into older floors of the same
house. Spacing and ground tone only; no motion, nothing counted. ~1 hour.
Pairs with D-1: depth in two channels.

### D-4 · THE FORGE STAMPS THE RECEIPT
The audit escalated where the forge announces itself and whether a repeat
forge prints. Answer: the finish receipt gains the forged artifact's
silhouette stamped in ink at its foot — a press mark. Repeat castings stamp
the same mark + a `SECOND CASTING` line. The forge's public life lives
entirely on paper he keeps; no roster anywhere prints a fraction of six
again. Deterministic path per artifact id on the receipt composable +
casting count from the existing forged-set read. ~half a day. Neutral on
the parked living-toys decision — stamps work for words or machines alike.

### D-5 · SEALED ENVELOPES
A task may finish *sealed*: files as an envelope, title visible, detail
folded behind an 800ms hold-to-unseal, re-sealing on exit. Bad-test days
stay filed forever without ambushing a casual scroll — and survive the
purge-of-feelings moments that delete unwrapped records. One boolean +
folded pose + the hold dialect the app already speaks. ~half a day.

### D-6 · THE MARGIN
Any stored task may take ONE margin line: a later note in italic Atkinson
under the provenance block, carrying its own date (`— written 14 Mar
2027`). The original record is never edited; the margin is visibly later
ink. The archive becomes a book he can still think in, without falsifying
what the day said. One nullable field + timestamp. ~2 hours.

### D-7 · THE ANNIVERSARY OPENING
On a stored task's anniversary (same date, ≥1 year on), the landing holds
that one task up for that day under a bare dateline: `ONE YEAR AGO TODAY`.
No comment, no verdict, no progress talk. Per-task dismiss-forever, never
more than one, silent in every channel. The Drawer's only forward gesture
points backward — which is why it may live in the no-summons room. Date
query + one-row surface + opt-out flag. ~half a day. Opening it re-inks
it (D-1).

### D-8 · THE SETTLING
First Drawer open of a new month: the month just ended settles onto the
shelf in one slow seat (house curves, no sound), never to perform again.
Filed *against* Feel 3's no-theatre ruling deliberately and openly: a
cabinet visibly filing the month, exactly once, is the room doing its one
job in front of him. If the feel seats convict it, it dies clean — D-1/D-3
carry the depth without it. `shelfSettledThrough` stamp + one conditional
transition. ~2 hours.

## Ranking
**D-4** first — it closes the audit's own escalated forge questions with
paper, not promises. Then **D-1 + D-2** together (under a day combined):
the shelf becomes felt and findable. D-6 is the sleeper — one field turns
a freezer into a notebook.
