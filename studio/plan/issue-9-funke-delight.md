## §0 · THE THESIS — PARK'S DELIGHT IS RELIEF

Every other screen in this app can afford a moment of theatre.
PARK cannot, and — this is the part the delight seat exists to
say — *should not want to*. PARK is opened at 1 AM by a tired man
who wants to know one thing: is the machine telling the truth
about itself? The pleasure available here is specific and real:

**Relief.** The drawer opens, the board is calm, the words are
plain, the one piece of good news arrives at the volume of a
sigh, and he puts it away and forgets it. Feel-4 (atmosphere)
ruled "calm is not its styling, calm is the feature" — this seat
adopts that as the delight spec itself. A-13/A-14's anti-ritual
fence is not a constraint on my pass; it *is* my pass's frame.
Everything below either (a) spends relief more precisely, or
(b) defends relief against a named threat — including threats
filed in delight's own name.

Walls: Sora / Atkinson Hyperlegible / Anonymous Pro off the
existing ladder; scheme tokens only, no new hex; zero green or
near-green; zero orange on this drawer (house law, stands);
no icons, no imagery; bad news is print, never noise (A-8).

## FP-1 · THE NIGHT WATCHMAN, FUNKE FORM (Komet's seed, adopted and dressed) — PRICE M

Komet's seed: Park leaves a dated trail while closed, so opening
it can say what changed — or that nothing did. This seat adopts
it as the one new delight on this screen, and files the form:

**Placement:** the foot block, as its last line — below the
signed foot line and the LAST PROVED colophon. It is biography,
not state; the board above it owns the present, and a history
line that stood *among* board rows would argue with them.

**Dress:** Anonymous Pro 10sp, plate body dress
(`onSurfaceVariant`) — the furniture tier, in day and night
alike. It never wears full ink: even its bad news is *past*
news, and the board's full-ink MISSING already carries the
present fact at full volume. One fact, one volume, per tense.

**Exact words** (trail = counted-door verdict changes stamped
while the drawer was closed; window = since the drawer last
closed):

- Clean trail: `WHILE YOU WERE GONE — NOTHING CHANGED`
- One door died and is still down:
  `WHILE YOU WERE GONE — USAGE DIED 02:47`
  (the board's own door name; wall time; A-10 vocabulary — no
  blame words exist in this line's grammar: DIED is a machine
  word, BROKEN/FAILED/LOST are banned here as everywhere here.)
- Several changes: the house's count-and-first grammar (the
  Guard line's own precedent):
  `WHILE YOU WERE GONE — 3 CHANGES · FIRST: USAGE DIED 02:47`
- No trail exists (first-ever open, or the log starts after the
  window): **the line prints nothing.** Absent, never guessed —
  an unmeasured history is treated exactly like an unmeasured
  board: the surface declines to speak.

**Life cycle — the rule that keeps it a kindness and not a
tile:** the line is a report on arrival for *this visit*. It
prints on open from the closed-period trail and retires at the
earlier of (a) the drawer closing, (b) the next CHECK press.
It is NOT struck by the open-sweep's landing: the open-sweep
measures the present (the board's fact), the Watchman reports
the closed period (a different fact), and retiring it 300ms
after arrival would make it unreadable at the hour it exists
for. On the next open it re-derives from the new window or is
absent. It never stands across two visits.

**Machinery, priced honestly:** a change-log of counted-door
verdict transitions (item, from → to, wall stamp), stamped by
the same shield-loop re-stamps that already occur — the sibling
of Vega's Death Certificate (`MISSING SINCE`), and one log
should serve both filings; Ren sequences it. The print half is
S inside that M. Filed, not built, in this pass.

**Kill gate:** if the trail ever disagrees with the board's
current print, the line dies first and permanently. The board
is the present; the line is history; history loses every tie.

**Why this is delight and not telemetry:** `NOTHING CHANGED`
is the only sentence in the app that can make a man *glad he
opened a settings drawer* — proof, dated, that the night held
while he wasn't watching. It costs one line, at plate volume,
for one visit. That exchange rate is the whole design.

## FP-2 · FIXED WHISPERS, STILL OPEN SPEAKS (FIX return verdict dress) — PRICE S

Wave-2 landed P-5's printed return verdict — `FIXED — X` /
`STILL OPEN — X` under the key, persisting until the next press
or close (Muede's win over silent acquittal). Its *dress* was
never specified. Unspecified dress defaults to one ink for both
directions, and that wastes the one volume knob this verdict
has left, because its sound knob is already spent by law
(FIXED keeps its acquitted firm click; STILL OPEN keeps
silence).

**Spec:**

| Verdict | Print dress | Sound (stands) |
|---|---|---|
| `FIXED — X` | quiet tier — `onSurfaceVariant`, the board's own quiet-word dress (GRANTED / RUNNING wear it, A-6) | one firm click |
| `STILL OPEN — X` | full ink — `onBackground`, lack dress | silence |

Both Atkinson Hyperlegible Bold 15sp, same slot, same
persistence as wave-2 built. Nothing else moves.

**The principle, stated so it outlives this item:** on this
drawer, *good news is always one step quieter than bad news at
the same grammatical rank.* A-14.1 already rules the asymmetry
for CHECK (success prints nothing; failure gets a line). FIX's
verdict is the one place both directions must print — Muede
won that, correctly, because silence there read identically
for fixed and still-broken — so the asymmetry moves from
*presence* to *dress*, its only remaining lawful form.

**Kill gate (device, his eye is the judge):** if the quiet
FIXED ever reads as *unfinished* rather than calm — if he
re-checks the row to confirm what the verdict already said —
the swap reverts and both directions print full ink. Calm
that must be verified is not calm; the test is his behaviour,
not our taste.

## FP-3 · PROTECT THE COLUMN OF NOWs (fence, no feature) — PRICE 0

Wave-2's age column (P-4's print cure) gives a freshly swept
board a full column of `NOW` beside its words. This seat names
that sight as **Park's signature pleasure**: every row just
measured, nothing ageing, the board at its one moment of
complete freshness — the visual form of the relief this screen
sells. It is pleasing precisely because it is rare (45 seconds
later it is gone) and because nobody animated it into being.

**Fence:** ages re-strike in place at their bucket boundaries
only (`NOW` → `45S AGO` → `12M AGO`, wave-2's forms), atomically,
per row. No age ever counts upward live, no seconds tick
anywhere in this drawer, no "refreshing in…" print of any
kind. The reasoning is A-14.3's and it is a delight argument
before it is a truth one: moving numbers are *watched*
numbers, a watched drawer is a dashboard, and a dashboard
cannot produce relief — only attendance. The column of NOWs
delights because he catches it, not because it performs for
him. Any future patch that makes an age move to feel "live"
kills the thing it claims to improve.

## FP-4 · STRUCK FROM THE DELIGHT SEAT, WITH REASONS

Filed against Komet's seeds (comment 6512111638 carries the
short forms; these are the full arguments):

- **CHECK, Held — struck.** A ~4s press-and-hold to earn
  PROVED contradicts the verb's built grammar (CHECKING is
  generation-tied to the sweep's completion; the dip is held
  for the act's duration, Designer §1.5) and misdiagnoses why
  PROVED is believed. Belief here does not come from a cost the
  *man* pays; it comes from the board's demonstrated
  willingness to print NOT PROVED and name the door. Charging
  breath for a measurement inserts theatre between a tired man
  and a fact, at the exact screen whose whole claim is that it
  never does. `CHECK ABANDONED` additionally invents a failure
  word for an act that is not a failure — releasing a key —
  against A-10's vocabulary law.
- **Sweep as Sound — struck.** Already ruled against by Hof's
  cell-close addendum (CHECK-unwhole = silence + the printed
  line, Feel-2's sound law), and the delight lens strikes it
  independently: six differentiated ticks per sweep convert a
  measurement into a small concert, repeated at every opening,
  and promote a *missing* tick into an alarm heard before it
  can be read — bad news as noise, banned by A-8. Park's entire
  sound-of-good-news budget is one firm click on a whole
  landing. One click is a sigh. Six ticks is a performance,
  and performances demand audiences; this drawer must never
  demand one.
- **The Confession Line (standalone) — merged, not shipped.**
  Its three tenses re-render three facts other surfaces own:
  LAST PROVED (colophon), the death time (FP-1's trail), and
  NOT CHECKED (the header line, at full dress, already
  unblankable). As a separate line it is one fact in three
  renderings — the exact disease the Deck audit convicted six
  times on one face. Its honest core survives inside FP-1.

## FP-5 · THE SIGH, NAMED AND BUDGETED (fence for all future lanes)

For the record, Park's complete delight budget for proof is
the existing whole-landing sequence, named here as one event
so it can be protected as one event: **the sigh** — one firm
click, CHECK demoting itself to print dress, the foot line's
instant advancing, and nothing else: no line, no banner, no
second sound, in any dress, ever. Any future proposal that
adds an element to the whole-landing moment must name which
existing element it removes. A delight budget that can only
grow is how a drawer becomes a dashboard one tasteful line at
a time.
