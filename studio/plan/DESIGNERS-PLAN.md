  SLEEP · 7H 30M`. SLEEP wraps past midnight as ONE row: duration carries
  the span, `ENDS 03:30` appears only inside the state word — never as a
  printed range. Rows sort strictly by start time; a new routine inserts
  by its hour, never appends like a settings item.
- **Create:** a printed line under the last row, 52dp: `+ NEW ROUTINE`

  orange, not a card — adding a routine is editing the day. **Duplicate:**
