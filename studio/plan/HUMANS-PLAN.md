## §2 The five days, re-run against the plan — collisions and amendments

### Foundations (bind all five days)

- **HPA-01 — A routine is a WINDOW, not a firing.** A window opens a loadout and primes the Deck. Sessions inside a window are still his slides. Attendance is judged from the first arm inside the window, never from window-open. Nothing seals, cues demandingly, or starts counting at window-open. *(Selbst #1 — the plan's single most load-bearing undefined word.)*
- **HPA-02 — Silence = NOT RUN.** A phone untouched inside a window (face-down through lunch, left in another room) records nothing: no minutes, no miss, nothing follows from it. The same untouched phone must never read as consent on one surface and failure on another. *(Freund F1.)*
- **HPA-03 — Pre-flight before the gesture, always.** Accessibility/permission health is read on Deck open and rendered in the chrome, silent when true. If dead, the slide is inert and the bar itself says `BLOCKING IS OFF — TAP TO FIX`. As planned, his one committing slide at 05:00 is spent *before* the app checks the permission — a dead permission costs 7–10 Settings gestures at his weakest hour and the fasted block with it. No arm may ever cost more than the slide that fires it. *(Muede; Panik P-2. Critics' A-1 converges — see §7.)*
- **HPA-04 — A routine never seals an unattended phone.** Windows prime; only his hand seals. Any reading of "firing" that auto-seals is a ghost-session machine: it seals a sick man's phone at 05:00 and escape costs him his weekly hold. *(Wieder W-2.)*
- **HPA-05 — Misses count days, not firings; no note after 20:00.** Auto-pause arithmetic, where any survives, is per-day. Nothing about attendance is ever rendered after his sleep time. *(Freund F2. On auto-pause itself see HPA-13.)*

### The normal day (Dopa + Selbst)

- **HPA-17 — Pay the block at the block.** The intercept is the most-seen surface in the app and Rounds 0–6 budget it zero: HELD survives only as a routine-conflict label and a chrome count slated for demotion. Spec for the intercept moment, at the moment (~05:35 on day 1, ~05:35 on day 140): payment lands ≤300ms · zero decisions · HELD hero ≥64sp · one converted-minutes line (what the blocked reach would have cost him) · a distinct thud-latch haptic, not the seal's sound · session fraction visible. The plan currently budgets 0 of these 6. Wave-1's demand (H6) was dropped, not adopted — this amendment restores it.
- **HPA-18 — The block/intercept screen gets an owner and a round.** It currently has neither in Rounds 0–6. A surface he sees more than any other cannot be unowned.
- **HPA-19 — One closing per day.** The finish ceremony is per-block only as a light receipt line; DAY CLOSED is the day's single ceremony, once, at the last block. As written, one reading of the spec inflates the ceremony ×10/day and the other leaves 9 of 10 finishes unpaid. And the 19:30–20:00 gap is declared free — rendered as nothing, owed as nothing.
- **HPA-20 — Firing payment honesty.** "The firing is paid" (sound/haptic at window-open) reaches a present user roughly 1 firing in 3 — the 09:00 sound pays an empty room. Payment belongs where his hand is: the in-window slide keeps its full arm climax on routine days too; demoting the slide's payoff because a routine "already fired" withdraws the best-priced payment in the app. *(Dopa.)*

### Saturday — test day + Friday eve (Panik P-1…P-9)

- **HPA-07 — A test date is a date exception on routines.** On that date FASTED BLOCK and DAY BLOCKS are paused for the date only: no fire, no cue, no mint, no miss; they resume Sunday untouched. Shield rows print `SAT — TEST DAY · PAUSED` all week, so the pause is never a Saturday surprise. Cost on Saturday: 0s, 0 decisions — paid once, rested, when the date is set.
- **HPA-08 — Saturday morning face.** On a test date, from wake until he leaves, the Deck's resting face is travel, not study: no slide, no study total. One statement: `TEST TODAY · [centre time] · PHONE, MAPS, PHOTOS OPEN · STUDY ROUTINES PAUSED`. The desk clock shows clock time and his rested-set leave-by line. - **HPA-09 — A test day renders as the word TEST.** Never 0 minutes, never a gap, never a broken streak, in any surviving record surface. A test is not an absence.
- **HPA-10 — Conflict sentence on the Deck at arm time.** Any manual session overlapping a routine window prints its resolution on the Deck bar, in human words (`ENDS 20:00 — SLEEP TAKES OVER, NOTHING TO DO`). The Shield-row conflict line stays for rested auditing; a precedence question may never reach him unanswered at the sealing screen.

### Sick day + the morning after (Wieder W-1…W-13)

The plan's worst accusation: **the chrome prints `TODAY 0H00 / 12H` over a fever.** Structurally, absence in the plan is inferred and rendered as zero — and R5.31's rendering has no tri-state day model beneath it, so implementers will render 0.

- **HPA-11 — Absence is never a number.** A nothing-sealed day renders only as his declared word (REST / ILL / FAMILY) or, undeclared, the exact sentence `NOTHING SEALED · NOTHING DECLARED`. - **HPA-12 — The record speaks at closes, never at opens.** No total, war line, or attendance fact is rendered at a day's opening or a return's first screen. The first screen after any gap is the Deck and the slide — nothing else is owed a rendering.
- **HPA-13 — Re-entry is a door, not a debt.** After one day away the first offered block is 25M; after three days, 10M; full size returns from the second session. - **HPA-14 — The 01:00 night-waking is specced.** Nobody's plan owns it today. Minimum: during SLEEP the phone shows a cloister of exactly two facts — the time, and his wake time. The valve is inert during SLEEP. No night data of any kind is displayed inside weak windows.

### Family day (Freund F1–F8, L1–L2)

## §3 The banked valve — how it must FEEL (Barriere definitive, Humans merged)

Full motor spec: `humans-plan/barriere-valve.md` — it rules where numbers conflict. The user's-side summary the master plan must not lose:

- **Shape:** full-width bar × 112dp, bottom-anchored in the thumb arc, with a 16dp spill apron — under load the thumb rolls off the *top* edge first; the apron turns slips into slides. Entry strip 56dp at identical coordinates: the hand never relocates between deciding and holding.
- **Contact law:** any contact counts — any finger, knuckle, palm edge; centroid ignored; presence, never position. `STAY LOCKED` sits ≥120dp above the bar and is inert while any contact is on the bar.
- **Banks at 15/30/45, felt as bolts seating:** pitch falls 560 → 470 → 390Hz while haptic weight rises (waveforms per bank in Barriere §1.2). Chambers turn **solid + an 8dp tooth drops** — progress reads as *how much of the bar has turned to metal*, a shape judgment that survives covered numerals, 10% brightness, blurred eyes. Owed seconds at 56sp; one verb-free state line: **`BANKED 30 · HOLDING 7`**. (Panik's "YOU CAN LET GO" sentence is killed: a verb is a fresh decision. The state word is the whole message.)
- **Ticks a shaking hand can feel:** per-second 10ms @ 0.55 (wave-1's 4ms ticks sit under the tremor noise floor); a 5s metronome at 12ms @ 0.8 — twelve pulses, countable in the thumb. Final leg doubles the tick from 55s.
- **The drain — the trust number:** on a slip, **800ms of no drain** (the longest common involuntary gap: tremor micro-lifts and elbow bumps run 200–600ms), per-slip and **uncapped** — grace never accrues a held millisecond, so a cap would punish only the involuntary case. Then **1.0× for the first 3.0s** of drain (re-grip lands at ~2.5–3.5s; a hand back at 3.0s has lost ≈2.2s, a breath) **→ 2.0× thereafter**, easing as the floor approaches. **Floor = the last bank, full stop; banked seconds never drain in-session.** Worst tail (≈14.9s) reaches the floor in ≈9.7s. The floor's arrival is a catch-click in the thumb — *the floor is felt, not watched.* Dead by all three valve seats: Zug-II's 3× (≈5s — outruns a sweaty re-grip and re-introduces the grip test), any 2,000ms grace (a deliberate set-down becomes indistinguishable from a bump), any zeroing clause.
- **The Bank Beat (HPA-27):** at each bank crossing, the bolt knock, then a beat in which grace restarts in full — if he releases inside the beat, nothing drains. The valve proves at 15s that stopping loses nothing; a man who learns that can *decide* at 15s to press again. Permission, not friction. *(Panik P-9.)*
- **Anti-grief (HPA-28):** starting takes two deliberate acts — the entry tap, then a contact that *begins* inside the bar. A pocket cannot perform that sequence (Bus BM-1: the banked valve as loosely specced could be spent by a thigh through a bus ride — the two-act start is the lock). Contacts sliding in from outside never accrue. OS cancels (call UI, system gesture) are slips, never zeroes. **Nothing is recorded before the first bank** — an accidental touch leaves no trace, touches no token. The token is spent only at 60.0 held seconds, in the same frame as the completion seal.
- **The first 3 seconds decide it:** the lawful 60s door is slower than the Settings→uninstall exit (~30s), so the valve wins on certainty in its first 3 seconds or not at all — the entry must read as already-his: state word present, first tick felt, no surprise. *(Panik.)*

**HPA-23 — ARM amendments AM-01…AM-10 (Barriere, adopted wholesale):** honest grab-offset on the slide (no teleport-to-finger) · commit geometry final (below) · 250ms contact-bounce grace past 0.40 travel · velocity commit >1,200dp/s at ≥0.50 · **the wheel goes relative or it doesn't ship** (±3° tremor dead zone, skid freezes value, tap-to-set on printed majors) — one radial skid at 09:30 currently teleports the session length across the scale and the slide then arms the wrong session perfectly, inside budget; until AM-05/06 land, ≤10s must not be claimed for any wheel path · no gestures on placeholder state · sled/cap tap law (≥56×56dp hit areas, commit on release by centroid, 80ms dwell on drag pass-through, SHUT→OPEN tap-only) · the ≤10s budget priced with one failed slide included: **8.0s worst hand** · any finger, any hand.

**HPA-24 — One gate.** The slide commit threshold is a single number everywhere: **0.65**, with magnet-complete from 0.40 (drive-to-seat 140ms + seal). The plan currently carries two rival gates (0.65 vs 0.82 + velocity throw). A hand in motion — or shaking on Friday eve — cannot learn two gates for one gesture. *(Bus BM-5, Panik P-4, Barriere AM-02 converge.)*
