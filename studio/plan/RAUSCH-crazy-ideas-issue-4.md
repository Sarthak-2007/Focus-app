# RAUSCH — CRAZY IDEAS for Issue #4: what else the Deck top strip could BE

*RAUSCH, crazy-ideas seat, dedicated-100 programme. Posted as comments on
issue #4 in waves; this file is the full set in one place.*
*Ground rules I held: an object, not text. No green. No AI images. Monolith
is a blocker, not a tracker — nothing below counts, scores, or tallies him.
OFF must read instantly; the end time must survive as numerals somewhere
honest. Fonts only where a number must live.*

The Bolt is good. This file is not a vote against it — it is the room
*outside* the Bolt: twelve full objects, a bench of spares, and a graft
list of organs the Bolt (or any winner) can steal. Every object below was
chosen because it **cannot fake its state** — each one has a pose that is
physically impossible to counterfeit, which is the whole disease of the
old strip (`PROOF: POSITIVE` printed beside `BIND NOT HELD`) cured by
physics instead of by law.

The five facts the strip must carry, in every pitch: **BLOCK** on/off ·
**session running** or idle · **END** time · **BIND** (the seal act
happened) · **PROOF** (the hold is true *now*, not at seal time).

## FAMILY A — OBJECTS THAT CANNOT FAKE "HELD"

### A1 · THE ONE WIRE (a string under tension)

The codebase already calls the thing "the wire" — five derivations of one
wire, the audits said. So: show *the wire*. A single string stretched the
width of the strip between two posts, over a dark recess.

- **HELD:** the wire is dead straight and taut — a ruled line with tension
  in it. At the seal throw it *arrives* straight with one visible shiver
  that dies in ~200ms (the pluck of being tensioned — feel, not loop).
- **OFF:** the wire sags in a catenary curve, ends loose at the posts.
  A sagging line is the oldest "nothing is holding" picture there is.
- **UNPROVEN / CAUGHT:** the wire is strung but slack-curved — present,
  attached, holding nothing. Nobody misreads a loose string as a tight one.
- **REVOKED MID-SESSION:** the wire is *snapped* — two curled ends sprung
  back toward the posts, a gap in the middle. The only state with a break
  in the line. It cannot be confused with OFF (sagging ≠ broken) and that
  distinction is exactly the one the text strip could never draw.
- **END:** a small brass bead threaded on the wire sits at the post while
  idle; while running, the end numerals live in a glass window at the
  right post (Bolt-style, Anonymous Pro). Or wilder: the bead *is* the
  marker — it rests against a printed tick etched `07:28`… no. Numerals
  stay numerals; the bead is decoration that dies in review. Window it is.
- **Why it's crazy-good:** tension IS bind. A typed claim can lie; a
  drawn curve can't — sag, straight, and snapped are three different
  *shapes*, readable in greyscale, in blur, at 1 AM, peripherally.
- **Price:** one custom-drawn curve + state machine. Cheapest idea in
  this file to build, hardest to unsee once seen.

### A2 · THE RELAY (an electromagnetic armature)

A small open-frame relay, drawn side-on: coil block at left, armature arm
hinged above a contact pair at right.

- **HELD:** the armature is *pulled down* onto the contact — and the pose
  itself argues proof: a relay armature only stays down **while current
  flows**. Kill the current and physics springs it open. It is the one
  mechanism whose held pose is continuous evidence, not a memory of the
  seal act — the frozen-verdict problem (§2 of the Bolt design) has no
  analogue here; the object refuses to remember.
- **OFF:** armature sprung up at rest angle, contact gap wide and visible.
- **UNPROVEN:** armature down but the contact faces drawn *not touching* —
  a hairline of daylight between them. Came close, never closed.
- **PROOF lamp, re-wired:** the jewel is not beside the relay, it is *in
  series* with it — the lamp can only light through the closed contact.
  A blown/blocked path = dark lamp by circuit, not by software decision.
- **Price:** side-on mechanism drawing is fiddly; readability at 60dp is
  the risk. The *idea* (held = continuously powered pose) is graftable
  onto anything even if the drawing dies — see graft G2.

### A3 · THE CARABINER (gate + locking sleeve)

Every climber's two-stage truth machine, laid horizontal:

- **Session sealed (BIND):** the gate is shut. Necessary — **not
  sufficient**, and the object knows it.
- **PROOF:** the locking sleeve is screwed down over the gate hinge.
  Gate shut + sleeve down = the full hold. Gate shut + sleeve parked up
  the spine = *closed but not locked* — the exact RUNNING_UNPROVEN face,
  drawn by an object whose entire culture is that distinction.
- **OFF:** gate standing open, spring pose, the opening a clear dark
  wedge. Unmistakable across a room.
- **ENDING SOON:** the sleeve backs off two thread-turns — no, pose is
  truth (Bolt law) and the sleeve hasn't moved in reality. Ending-soon
  stays in the window inversion, house style. The carabiner keeps its
  hands clean.
- **Why:** it's the only common object with *two independent physical
  stages* that map 1:1 onto BIND vs PROOF — the pair the old strip
  mangled worst. Climbers die on that distinction; the iconography is
  already trained into the culture.

### A4 · THE FUSE (a glass cartridge fuse in clips)

- **HELD / RUNNING:** filament intact, a fine unbroken hairline through
  glass, seated in both clips.
- **BIND LOST MID-SESSION:** filament *blown* — a gap in the hairline,
  a smoke smudge inside the glass. Failure leaves a corpse; you can see
  what died. Nothing else in this file gives fault a *body*.
- **OFF:** the fuse is **out** — empty clips, the cartridge resting in a
  little tray notch beneath its socket. Absence as a state.
- **UNPROVEN:** fuse seated, filament intact, but the glass is *frosted*
  — untested, unreadable. Present, plausible, unproven.
- **The tap writes itself:** OFF-with-fuse-out is the one face that begs
  a finger — tap = the fix route (reseat/replace), which is the Deck's
  existing tap-to-fix language wearing a body.
- **Price:** tiny drawing, huge metaphor. Risk: reads "electrical fault"
  more than "blocking" — pair it with the seal act elsewhere (graft G4).

## FAMILY B — OBJECTS EVERY HAND ALREADY KNOWS

### B1 · THE ZIP

A zipper running the strip's width, teeth up, over the recess. The Deck
below it is *inside the jacket*.

- **HELD:** teeth interlocked the full width, slider parked at the right
  stop. A closed zip is the most-read "shut" in daily life.
- **OFF:** zip fully open — two separated tooth rows, a dark V of
  *inside* showing (paint the revealed wedge the warning orange: open =
  exposed, same law as the Bolt's bed, bigger canvas).
- **UNPROVEN / CAUGHT:** slider stopped at ⅓ with **fabric jammed in the
  teeth** — a little bunched knot at the slider. Everyone has lived this
  exact failure; it reads "tried, didn't close" in zero milliseconds.
- **RUNNING vs IDLE:** the parked slider carries the jewel in its pull
  tab — brass when armed-idle, magenta when a session runs.
- **Why:** zero learning curve on Earth. And a jammed zip is the only
  UNPROVEN face in this file that makes people *wince* — correct emotion.

### B2 · THE ECLIPSE

A brass sun disc at the strip's right end; a black stone disc (the
Monolith itself, shrunk to a coin) slides across it.

- **HELD:** totality — the black disc covers the sun exactly, and a thin
  **corona ring** (the house magenta) rims it. Totality is RUNNING.
- **ARMED IDLE:** the disc covers the sun but the corona is brass —
  covered, proven, nothing running.
- **UNPROVEN:** partial eclipse — a crescent of sun still showing.
  Beautiful and obviously *not covered*.
- **OFF:** the disc is parked clear; the sun stands bare and flat.
  Maximum exposed light = maximum exposure. (Light as vulnerability —
  the inverse of lamp logic, and it works because the *sun* is the
  thing being covered, not a status light.)
- **END:** numerals in the dark field the disc vacates — the window is
  literally uncovered by the covering object.
- **Why:** Monolith is a stone that blocks light. This is the app's
  name drawn as a mechanism. If any idea here could become *the icon*
  people recognise the app by, it's this one.
- **Risk:** coverage % must stay binary-bucketed ( Bolt's CAUGHT law):
  parked / crescent / total — never a continuous slide that invites
  "63% blocked" readings.
