## 8. FORKS FOR REN (both change a law; both carry my recommendation)

**FORK 1 — The sum and the fence (the corrected sum breaks the
filed fence's floor).** 826 is forced by elements the merge itself
mandated; at 844 the reserve is 18 against a floor of 20, and at

**My recommendation: (a).** The slot is truth furniture — a refusal

fence was a device assumption; the slot is a law of the gesture.
Re-fence, and let handsets under 846 be named honestly as rooms
this Deck does not fit — the alternative is a third silent squeeze,
and silent squeezes are how 796 happened.

**FORK 2 — The datum (the filed charge moves the wheel).**

readout→wheel beat) translates the wheel to centre (206, 392) and
re-bases every datum registration −12 (Running arc, AOD anchors,
Common Floor band). Options: (a) ratify the re-base — a translation,

(b) relocate the −12 to the slot→nav beat (S20→S8, a lawful token),

**My recommendation: (a).** The charge's location was settled twice
