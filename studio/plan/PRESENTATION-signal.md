# SIGNAL — PRESENTATION, WHOLE

**World:** the poster. Flat colour fields, zero shadows, zero gradients, zero radius — ink on paper at poster scale. Giant grotesque type IS the image. Day and night are the same poster: paper stock by day, black stock by night.
**Lane:** builds/signal (off post-Slice-E trunk, DB v10). Visual + presentation only; no mechanism, no DB, no behaviour changed. Every word the screens print today stays printable and honest.
**Proof on disk:** builds/signal/shots-own/ — 01_deck, 02_shield (day), night/n01_deck, night/n02_shield, 08_receipt (receiptSlipDay), night receipt, 09_held_door, night/n09_desk_clock.
**Palette (zero-green audited, G < max(R,B) for every value):** paper #F4F1EA · white slip #FBFAF7 · pale blue-grey print tone #D9DDE8 · poster black #101014 · SIGNAL red #E2001A (night #F5261B) · poster blue #1B2FA8 (night #3150D8). Night ground #0E0E10.

## §0 The grammar — five laws every element obeys

1. **Print hierarchy is information hierarchy.** The one thing that matters now is the headline; everything else is body copy or mono microprint. If two things shout, one of them is lying about its importance.
2. **Colour is spent like ink.** Black = the machine and the facts. Red = marks and the one handle (the commit). Blue = the middle, conditional state. A fourth ink family does not exist.
3. **One fact, one home.** No fact prints twice on a screen. Duplication is noise a tired eye pays for.
4. **Silence is a state.** What is whole prints nothing. The poster speaks when there is something to say; absence is the signal that all is well.
5. **Every chooser is visible.** If a hand can choose it, the chooser is on the page, at print scale, in its current state. No hidden gestures, no unlabelled tap-to-expand.

## §3 SELECTION — one system

Every choice in SIGNAL is made one of four ways, and no fifth way exists:

1. **THE WHEEL** — continuous quantity (duration). Ticks at detents; the headline reprints as you turn.
2. **THE CAP** — one of a few named states (loadout, app tier, routine ON/OFF, FLOW mode). A block travels; its colour is the state.
3. **THE HANDLE** — commit an act (the slide). One handle per screen at most; it is red or it is nothing.
4. **DIRECT ENTRY** — exact values (a routine's 05:40, a duration typed). **The exact-value law: a value the hand already knows is typed or wheeled, never stepped.** ±30 steppers for a known minute are the poster equivalent of spelling a word by alphabet-walking; they are banned from this world on every surface, present and future. (This law is the routines lesson generalised — Sarthak's charge against the shipped Shield, answered as grammar.)

Selection states are always printable: any screenshot of any choice reads correctly. If a choice cannot be read from a still image, it is not finished.

## §4 TASKS — sketch

A printed running order. Numbered slips in sequence; the NOW slip inked black with paper print; the rest on paper. **Order is the only information** — a task prints its name and its place, nothing else: tasks carry NO minutes (his verdict, Oct 29) — no estimates, no durations, no points, ever. Reorder = drag the slip; the sheet reprints. Finishing a task (DONE on the Deck, or on Tasks) tears its slip out of the order and files it in the Drawer — the tear is the same paper haptic as every finish (§8). Adding is a blank slip at the foot of the order. The empty queue prints the blank leaf: a ruled sheet with nothing on it is an honest page, not an error state.

## §5 THE DRAWER — sketch

The archive of counterfoils. Every finished task is stored forever, scoreless (his law): no totals, no counts of oneself, no day grades — the archive does not count itself (a totals line was considered and rejected on exactly this ground). Day heads print at headline scale as archive dividers; entries print as slips in the receipt's voice (mono microprint, rules, № where the machinery issued one). HELD messages live here as the sealed section — full peek, his ruling. Search is the archive's index: a working verb, printed as an open line like every input in this world. The Drawer is the one screen designed to be opened rarely and believed completely.

## §6 THE DESK CLOCK — sketch

One number in the dark. Elapsed and remaining at poster scale (104sp) on the black field; the plates register at low alpha (A6) so the night poster carries its signature into the room it lights. Nothing else prints — no date, no words beyond the units. Its position drifts against burn-in (machinery, kept). Its job: answer "how long, how much left" to a half-asleep glance from across a room, at the meditation hour and through the fasted block. It never asks for a touch. ## §7 THE HELD DOOR — the interception, designed

Wetter's charge stands: this is the surface he meets most — dozens of times a week, a SHUT app opened mid-block — and no world had designed it. SIGNAL's door is a closed sign with perfect typography:

- **Field:** the ground, full-bleed — paper by day, the one black field by night. No card, no sheet: a door is a wall, not a window.
- **First print:** a red square, then the app's name at headline scale (the name is the biggest word — the hand must know what it knocked on before it reads anything else).
- **Second print:** the remaining time as a flat block — the session's truth at statement size ("1H 12M LEFT" as a black block, paper print).
- **The verbs, both working:** PEEK (the black plate — the 2-minute airlock, machinery unchanged) and BACK (a slip — return). No third verb. A door with three handles is a puzzle at the exact moment of weakness.
- Nothing else prints: no streaks, no counts of past knocks, no scolding copy. The door does not argue; it states and offers.

**The truth baseline (Ren, from Una's intercept audit — binding on this design):**
1. *Remaining time is never stated by today's intercept.* This door states it (above) — the first fact the surface owes him.
2. *The peek's cooldown is never printed.* A taken peek starts a mandatory 20-minute per-app cooldown; the state carries the remaining seconds and renders them nowhere. **This door prints the cooldown on the PEEK plate itself** — while an app cools down, its plate reads PEEK · 12:40 (the true remaining), inert; a peek withheld BY cooldown prints its reason. A withheld peek that vanishes silently is a lie of silence, and this world does not print lies of any kind.
3. *The peek offer is honest on one of four doors (UNA2-01, S1).* The offer is computed blind to which intercept path fired; on the Uninstall Guard door it defaults ON and its lease writes to no row — a peek that protects nothing. **Rule for this face: the PEEK plate appears ONLY on doors where a lease can actually be honoured.** On a door that can't keep it, the door prints its state and RETURN, and no offer — unless Truth Car 3's repair makes every door honour the lease before this face ships. The poster never paints an offer the machine can't keep.
4. *Counts:* any count this door or any SIGNAL surface prints splits SHUT from PEEK (UNA2-02) — as the Shield head already does (7 SHUT • 3 PEEK) and the Deck strip now must (§1 D8).

Rendered in-lane: 09_held_door.png (driven through the ViewModel's own intercept trigger, the accessibility service's path). Airlock internals are machinery and unchanged; only the face is the poster's.

**Render truth, stated plainly:** the captured door is the SHIPPED airlock overlay wearing this skin — flat black field, red commit bar (RETURN TO FOCUS), paper type, its card squared in-lane. Its structure (badge line, countdown numeral, intention line) is the machinery's own furniture. The wall-form above — app name as the headline, remaining time as a block, exactly two verbs — is the phase-2 presentation build on the existing overlay: ~1 day of composable work, no mechanism change. The render proves the skin fits the door; it does not prove the wall-form, and this spec does not claim it does.

## §8 FEEL — the sensory signature

Print is a still medium, and SIGNAL does not apologise for it: **this world promises precision, not richness** (Haut's framing, accepted). Its feel is concentrated at the two moments that must be believed — the arm and the finish — and is otherwise the cheapest in the wave to live inside: at idle it costs a dark room nothing.

**Sound — a closed vocabulary of one.** The seal throw, only at proven t=0, bit-identical for hand and routine arms (shipped law; the Throw is never re-voiced — Haut). Everything else is silence, including faults: a jam speaks in the hand and in red print, never in the ear. Reason: the ear's budget is spent once, on the one moment that must be believed; a poster that beeps is a poster that cries wolf.

**Haptics — a closed vocabulary of four, all stamps.** Per Haut's sheet, adopted: single-segment, full-amplitude, no envelopes — a poster does not fade.
- **TICK** — wheel detents and cap stops: 6ms @ 255. The press's ratchet.
- **THOCK** — the latch at proof: one deep stamp, simultaneous with the seal voice and the plates snapping true (§9). Three senses, one instant, one fact.
- **PAPER** — finish/detain: three EQUAL stamps (Haut's correction — the shipped pulse softens, and softening is off this grammar).
- **JAM** — the shipped triple-buzz: the alarm lives in the hand.
No vibration for navigation, scrolling, or selection-by-tap. If it isn't an event, it doesn't buzz.

**Motion — hard cuts and travelling blocks, nothing else.** Digits reprint (no fades, no rolls — a new number is a new impression). Caps travel (the engine's 260ms). The sled returns (650ms, shipped). The plates converge (§9). **The wheel's physics are machinery, not skin** — Haut's cheaper lawful reading, taken: SIGNAL claims no zero-overshoot fork, no ζ amendment, no re-proof. Its flatness lives in chrome motion (Curve A, no decorative overshoot anywhere the skin owns motion). Above all: *shape by time; colour by amplitude* (Haut's law) — meaning rides timing patterns, amplitude only shades.

**Atmosphere.** Day: fresh newsprint — the only world that gets better in harsh sun (ink doesn't wash out; Zunder's note, and true). Night: the same sheet with the power cut (§10) — true darks, so a 03:30 room stays dark. Idle: zero motion, zero sound, zero glow. A poster on a wall.

**Moment by moment.** ARM — ticks under the thumb, the headline reprinting with every detent; the slide drags; at the latch, silence while the machine proves; at proof, THOCK + voice + register (§9). HOLD — nothing moves but the digits; no sound; the poster hangs. FINISH — PAPER stamps; the receipt prints (the loved slip's voice, §5); the Deck reprints to idle. JAM — the sentence prints red at statement size, the hand buzzes triple, the ear hears nothing, the slide comes home: the machine admits it in the open, in print.

## §9 REGISTRATION IS THE SEAL (Ren-ruled: the fingerprint is SIGNAL's)

Adopted whole from Zunder's push, and ruled mine: the offset/registration fingerprint belongs to this world exclusively; no other direction enters judging wearing it.

The headline is printed from three plates, out of register (blue −2dp, red +2dp). **The offset is the proof meter.** While the proving cascade witnesses its links, each witnessed link steps the plates closer to register; at the latch — with the THOCK and the seal voice — the type snaps into perfect register and stays registered for the session's life. Sharpness itself becomes the proof: readable across a room, no words needed, and impossible to fake — the fringe position renders a real, witnessed state (the link count), never a decoration. An annulled arm leaves the plates out of register as the slide returns: the failed print is visibly a misprint. Registration is printing's own word for truth; no other world has plates to align.

Cost: the ghost offsets bind to cascade state (3 steps + snap) — presentation-layer animation on two Text layers, running parallel to the cascade, never gating the seal sound (the sound fires on the cascade's own t=0, as shipped). ~60 lines + harness frames for the intermediate states (A3).

**Sibling rule — the Warning Takes the Wall (Zunder, adopted with one boundary).** A poster's grammar is hierarchy: when anything is unbound, BLOCKING IS OFF is the largest type on the Deck — bigger than the time, which demotes to the sub-headline — printed as a red field with white print (the ratchet banner's treatment at headline scale). When proof is positive, the warning shrinks to the proof strip and the time takes the whole wall. Boundary, stated plainly: this re-ranking governs the IDLE and ARMING Deck. A running session whose blocking dies stays silent and the clock runs (his verdict) — the session books BROKEN and the receipt says so; the poster does not shout mid-session, ever.
