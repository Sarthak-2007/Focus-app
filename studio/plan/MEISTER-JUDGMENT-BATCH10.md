## 1. What BELONGS in this build — keep, these last

- **The one composed MIGRATION_12_13 (the discipline, not the text).** Two lanes
  privately advancing the same database is how products die in year three; one
  migration, composed once, is the oldest boring decision in this epic and the
  one everything else stands on.
- **The BASE-WINS LAW (§0).** A base tree that lanes contribute named deltas to,
  hunk by hunk, never wholesale overwrites — this is the difference between a
  merge and a collision, and it will still be right at batch 30.
- **Wache — no-fire F1–F5.** A routine that silently does not fire is the one
  failure this user already lived on his own phone; machinery that tells the
  truth about whether it fired is not a feature, it is the product's honour.
- **Stempel — `provePipelineNow()` / `notePipelineProof()`.** "CAN'T CHECK
  BLOCKING — TAP TO RETRY" is a machine confessing it does not know itself;
  the cure is proof at arm-time, and proof machinery never goes out of fashion.
- **Frist — editable times, WITH the provider rewire in the same sitting.**
  Columns that land unread are the old disease in a new coat (the plan's own
  words, and correct); a number the user can edit and the machine actually
  obeys is felt every single day for years.
- **Voss — routine editing, delta-only.** Editing the machine's schedule is
  daily-use surface, not decoration; admitted on its confirmed delta inventory
  (R3: Konto's proposal stands — PARTIAL on inventory, never on promises).
- **W-43 — the receipt footer (`THE MACHINE KEEPS ITS OWN LOG · YOUR STUDY
  HOURS LIVE IN YPT`).** The printed word outlives every skin — this user
  already loved the receipt slip once, on sight, and print character is the
  cheapest immortality a product can buy.
- **Step 0 baselines + the symbol survival census (gate M1).** Capturing the
  "pre" state before composition destroys it is the kind of unglamorous proof
  discipline teams still thank themselves for a decade later.

## 2. What is being SMUGGLED — hold or kill, these rot

- **HOLD — migration rows 7–8, `heldShutAtSeal` / `heldPeekAtSeal` (answers R1:
  ship 6, hold 2).** These columns exist in no code anywhere — no writer, no
  reader, a DEFAULT −1 "by analogy only" — and a schema column nobody writes
  is a lie the database tells every future reader; when Suki's Car-3 is built,
  its migration will be one honest page long.
- **KILL from this build — Schall's theatre, all of it (waves 1–2).** The
  warrant reading the room, the pill's three mood-physics, the day visibly
  re-settling when a time is dragged: lovely ideas, every one a NEW feature
  wearing the merge's coat, with no lane, no tests, and no seat at the
  checklist — a merge that admits unlisted passengers stops being a merge and
  becomes a hope. They belong to the next programme, judged against the merged
  machine once it exists. (One exception in kind, not in vehicle: his idea 5,
  the arm-time pre-flight, is truth machinery, not theatre — file it as its
  own issue; it still does not ride this build.)
- **KILL from this build — "The Room IS the Launcher."** The launcher block
  screen is Sarthak's own Oct 30 design ruling and it deserves a programme,
  not a stowaway berth inside a merge epic whose checklist never named it.
- **HOLD WHOLE or SHIP WHOLE — W-41 (the R1 companion).** Columns + writer
  without the enforcer-derived sites figure, the restore reader, and the print
  is a false cure shipped under a true name — the live defect (VM live recount
  vs its own doc) survives untouched while the checklist claims the fix.
- **KILL — F3's denied-start stamp landing voiceless (answers R4).** A stamp
  no surface ever shows is machinery the user pays for in complexity and never
  meets; either the Park consumer is composed in this build or the stamp waits
  for the build that gives it a voice.
- **SPLIT the checklist line — "editable times" is two promises, not one.**
  Pastille's correction stands in the record: Flag B wires peeks only, breaks
  need their own call-site wiring and the `breakSeconds` stamp; signing the
  line off whole would let half a promise ship wearing a full one's name.
- **KILL before anything ships — the C13 ghost ticker.** A constant-seeded
  ticker that silently truncates any peek past 120 s is a known defect with a
  ticket number riding inside the merge; known-and-shipped is the one category
  of bug a 29-year record teaches you never to sign.

## 3. The small seams — cheap now, expensive forever

- **VOICE the pill's hand-back (answers R5: voice it).** One machine, one
  accent: a door that seals silently in one place and sounds in another feels
  broken in year five precisely because nobody can name why — sound at a seam
  costs a line now and an archaeology dig later.
- **RULE R6 before step 4 — extension: current-block-only.** Per-block
  extension arithmetic is the kind of cleverness that generates a decade of
  "why did it add time THERE?" — the plain reading (this block, this slip,
  this wording) is the one that survives contact with a tired user at 21:00.
- **R2 — Konto's shield reconciliation stands.** A header that never moves
  while lanes settle onto it in order is composition; re-sequencing it now
  would be novelty for its own sake.

## 4. The decades test, applied once, plainly

In year five, what remains of batch 10 is: one honest migration, a machine
that proves it is armed, routines that fire or confess, times the user edits
and the machine obeys, and a printed slip that tells the truth. Everything
else in this epic is either scaffolding for those five (keep it, it earns its
keep) or theatre and speculation riding their coat (hold it, it will not earn
its keep). Compose the five. Ship nothing that merely witnessed them.
