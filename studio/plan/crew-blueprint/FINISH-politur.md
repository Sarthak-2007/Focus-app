appear. The two named exceptions are shared machinery, not

## 3. SOUND BENCH — the pitch ladder, sited

**The instrument (Haut §0, binding):** procedural synth, fixed
dual-exponential envelope family, queue voices ≤160 ms, chord
ratio 1.0 in this world (dry — no shimmer), fundamentals
mid-high. Amplitude is best-effort across the fleet; **timing
carries every meaning** — every Blueprint shape below survives
a motor with no amplitude control, because every shape is
timing-led. **The seal is shared law:** the 1-second
pre-rendered Throw buffer, bit-identical at every seal (golden
SHA-256 `d204b95a…`, one call site, `playSealThrow()`).
**There is NO Blueprint seal sound. Any build that re-voices,
layers, or "indigo-flavours" the seal FAILS outright** — worlds
speak in the approach: the latch (`playMechanicalLatch`, two-
stage strike-gap-seat, 760 Hz engaging / 520 Hz releasing)
before it, silence at it.

**Haptics, one shape:** the detent tick is the shipped
`playRotaryDetent` waveform — **7 ms strike** (timings
[0, 7, 5], amplitudes [0, 180, 70]) — **the same 7 ms shape at
every tooth of every family**, flat amplitude within a family.
A spec does not swell. The single lawful exception is mass:
the seat knock scales **16→24 ms** with block count (§3.2),
because mass is a fact and the drawing says why.

the value, same formula family, so the ear reads any dial

`playRotaryDetent(freq)` (6 ms transient, gain 0.38, freq
clamped 280–1480 Hz by the engine). Where the trunk already
pitches by value, the bench adopts the shipped formula and
names it; where it does not, the gap is a RULING (§5), never
a builder's improvisation.

### 3.2 The three lawful mid-session voices

The running session is near-silent (§3.3). Exactly three
events may speak mid-session, each in an existing engine
voice, each one firm click of information — never a chime
(Noir's rejected chime stays rejected; a measurement does
not jingle):

| Event | Engine voice | Shape | Why this voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Break flip** (block → Safe Break, and break → next block) | `playFirmClick()` | 620 Hz, chord 1.1, 12 ms; haptic 13/20/8 ms @ 225/0/135 — hard strike, breath, softer seat | A state boundary crossed by the machinery, not the hand: the click's two-part shape (strike, seat) IS the flip — old state struck, new state seated. |
| **Peek end** (the 2-minute lease expires, the door re-seals) | `playFirmClick()` | identical | The peek ending is the same kind of fact as the break flipping: a lease boundary. Same voice, because the ear must learn ONE boundary sound, not a phrasebook. Its fact is also printed where it was spent (the Pill's cooldown frame, spec §4.6). |
| **Stamp knock** (Held Door HOLD stamp lands) | `playSeatKnock(...)` | single knock at the stamp's landing frame (§2), mid-weight of the family's range | The draughtsman setting his pencil down (spec §5). One knock, no alarm, no buzz phrase. On amplitude-less motors the timing carries it (Haut §0). |

**Bench honesty note:** in the current trunk these three
events are *state transitions in the repository/session layer
with no engine call sites of their own* — the voices above are
the spec's law (§5: "Break start/end and peek end are the only
mid-session voices: one firm click each") assigned to the
engine's existing voices, not a description of shipped wiring.
Wiring them is build work; inventing different voices for
them is not permitted. **→ R5.**

**Also lawful, and not mid-session:** the approach — hub
knocks and ladder detents while arming; the latch at the
slide; the tear pulse (`playTearPulse`, 240 Hz, chord 1.21)
at the tear; the completion pulse (`playCompletionPulse`,
185 Hz, chord 1.33, three softening ticks) as the slip
settles; the nav-refusal knock (`playSeatKnock(6, 0.2, 320)`)
when navigation is refused mid-proof — tries and stays, never
the detent that means travel happened.

## 4. SURFACE BENCH — line, frame, mark, stamp, tear

### 4.1 Line-weight discipline (spec §0 law 1, made judgable)

| Weight | Rank | Carries | Never carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1.5 dp**, full ink | Structure | the plate frame, the Commit Plate's edge family, the figure's extension lines, the dimension lines | decoration; a 1.5 line around anything that is not structure is a lie about rank |
| **1 dp**, full ink | Working lines | rows, wells' rims, tracks, sled bodies, tolerance-block rules, day-scale line | — |
| **0.75 dp @ 0x75 alpha** | Hairline | separators, the tick rule, leader hairlines, recess closures' inner rule | **A FACT, ALONE — ever.** |

**The hairline law, in its judgable form:** no fact may be
carried by a hairline alone. A boundary that *means* something
(a recess's edge, a segment boundary on the day-scale, a
collision on the editor's scale) must be readable if every
hairline on the plate were deleted — by a working-or-heavier
line, by a figure, or by position. §8 N1 is the standing
example: the recess step measures 1.08:1 (unreadable) and is
lawful only because its closing outline measures 3.17:1. At
the gallery pass I will delete-hairlines mentally on every
render; any fact that vanishes with them FAILS.

### 4.2 The full-weight frame rule

Every plate wears a **double hairline frame** (§0) — and the
one raised sheet wears more: **the editor sheet and every
loose sheet are framed at FULL outline weight** (the
#55649F token, measuring 2.69:1 against raised — Salz §2's
supplied figure). This is not taste: at a 1.10 sheet step the
frame IS the sheet's identity (the fill cannot carry it), and
the 0x75-alpha hairline variant measures only 1.55:1 over
raised — a sheet framed in the variant is a sheet that
dissolves into the plate. **A render whose sheet frame is the
hairline variant FAILS N3 on sight**, and the cure is the
frame, not a brighter fill (the fallback — deepening the
sheet token one step — is the register's, invoked only if
L05 captures kill the framed reading).

### 4.3 Registration marks + the stamp

- **Registration marks** sit at the frame's four corners,
  drawn in working ink (1 dp), *outside* the double frame,
  centred on the frame's corner intersection extended.
  Geometry beyond this is underspecified by the spec — my
  proposed dimensions are **R7**; until Riss rules, builders
  hold: crosshair arms 8 dp, centre gap 2 dp, inset aligned
  to the frame corner, identical at all four corners, day
  and night, on every plate including the desk clock's
  erased plate (where they survive at hairline — the one
  lawful hairline-only ornament, because they carry no fact).
- **The stamp:** annotation red — **#C8102E day / #FF6B7A
  night** — rotated **−8°**, always. HOLD on the Held Door;
  the revision stamp in the title block at the seal. The
  angle is a printed constant, not an animation (§2): it is
  −8° in the first frame and the last. A stamp at 0° is a
  label; a stamp at any other angle is another world's
  stamp. Red appears here and at corrections — nowhere else
  (§1.1 light law applies to the drawn stamp equally: lines
  and letters, never a red field behind them).

### 4.4 The torn edge (receipt / revision slip)

The slip is the one surface allowed to feel like paper
(spec §4.3 — his ❤️ sets its bar: narrow measure, mono
figures, torn edge, one fact per line — that character
survives WHOLE). The bench for its edge:

- The tear edge is a **deterministic zigzag perforation**
  along the slip's foot (and the counterfoil's matching head,
  where both render): tooth pitch and depth fixed constants
  of the build, identical on every slip — a torn edge that
  differs per slip is a random number wearing paper's clothes.
  Exact tooth dimensions are underspecified — **R8**.
- The edge is **drawn, never shadowed** (flat law): no drop
  shadow, no curl gradient, no paper texture bitmap. The slip
  reads as paper by its measure, its mono, its edge geometry
  and its tear sound — four honest channels, zero fakery.
- Bracketed derived figures (§4.3 of the spec: `ENDED [09:41]`)
  print on the slip in the same mono, same weight — the
  bracket is the honesty; it is never "cleaned up" into a
  bare figure, and never reddened (a reconstruction is a
  notation, not a correction).

### 4.5 Surface — forbidden, by name

Shadows and elevation anywhere · rounded Material corners
(the lane's flattened radius scale stands, spec §6.12) ·
colour app icons drawn over in fake line-ink where the OS
icon must sit in its ruled cell unaltered (spec §2.7 — the
cell does the drafting, the icon is not forged) · texture,
grain, or noise overlays · any line drawn for decoration
that a judge could mistake for rank.

## 5. RULINGS HANDED TO RISS

stated as: the gap, my bench recommendation, what builders
hold until you rule. I judge by your ruling, not my
recommendation, once it lands.*

- **R8 — Torn-edge tooth geometry.** *Recommend: pitch 6 dp,
