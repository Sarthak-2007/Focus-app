## 0. THE GRAMMAR, BEFORE THE MOMENTS

Blueprint does not celebrate a state change. A state change is the drawing
being **revised, stamped, or filed** — in that vocabulary, and no other:

- **Motion:** plate turns 180 ms lateral, near-linear easing (Curve A),
  durations from the small vocabulary {110–280 ms}; **no overshoot on
  sheets** — the wheel integrator's two ζ=0.38 overshoots survive only as
  the hand's physics on the drafted circle, named in spec §5, never
  extended to a stamp, a plate, or a line (§5 of the spec binds this lane).
- **Sound:** the seal's shared 1-second buffer is **untouched** — Blueprint
  proposes no seal sound of its own, at any moment below. Voices here are
  approach voices only: the latch strike-gap-seat, one firm click at break
  boundaries, one seat knock at a stamp's landing, the tear pulse at the
  finish. Pitch = f(value) where a value moves; nothing jingles.
- **The stamp** is this world's only event-mark: a fact, witnessed, printed
  in the revision cell — or nothing. A stamp never replays for show.
- **The sheet is the same sheet.** Frame, title block, figure seat and ink
  do not move house at a seal. What that claim can and cannot carry is
  argued head-on at FL-01, because Scharf will — and should — attack it.

Every item below closes with exactly one verdict:
**BUILT-IN-LANE** (Blueprint lane builder + the surface named) /
**PRICED PROPOSAL** (machinery outside the lane, priced honestly) /
**ADOPTED-AS-IS** (the register's design, taken unchanged).

### The argument Scharf is owed — does a stamp satisfy "the app just turned black"?

**The weak answer, stated so it can be killed:** "the seal is a stamp on
the drawing he already holds; continuity IS the transformation." Killed:
Sarthak's complaint has two separable parts — (a) *nothing visibly
becomes anything else*, and (b) *the session has no face of its own once
the hand leaves*. A stamp + a repaint answers neither. A stamp is a
140 ms mark in a bottom-margin cell; twenty seconds later the screen is
the arming sheet in black, which is the complaint verbatim with better
typography. If Blueprint shipped beat 4 and stopped, Scharf's charge —
"you annotated the complaint, you didn't answer it" — would be true.

**The answer this lane actually files** is that Blueprint meets the
complaint at its two joints, in its own grammar, and the stamp is only
the smallest of the three moves:

1. **Re-issue, not repaint (answers a, in-hand).** At the shot's landing
   the sheet's *composition* changes: composer out, gauge in; the
   instrument he was setting becomes the instrument that measures him.
   Same sheet, same figure seat — different revision, and a revision on
   a drawing is not a recolour: a REV C sheet is a different instruction
   than REV B, and anyone who has worked from drawings knows the sheet
   he holds after the stamp is not the sheet he held before it. The
   continuity is the point *and* the limit: Blueprint refuses to pretend
   a destination change is honesty — the Deck was the workshop, the
   running Deck is the gauge, and faking a journey between them would
   be theatre this world doesn't do.
2. **The room change (answers b, and it is the register's, adopted).**
   The session's own face is the night plate, auto-raised at the settle
   (FL-02). *That* is "what the app becomes": a clock on a desk. A look
   that claims its in-hand revision exempts it from the settle has not
   understood the complaint — the complaint's second half is about the
   phone he is *not* holding.
3. **What Blueprint will not do:** invent a transitional spectacle at
   t=0 (a sheet does not flip, fold, or fly — the Throw and the stamp
   are the event), or move the figure's seat for novelty (an anchor
   that moves is not an anchor).

Verdict on the argument: the stamp alone — **insufficient, and this file
says so before Scharf has to**. Stamp + re-issue + settle-raise —
sufficient, because each part of the complaint gets a structural answer,
not a decorative one.

### FL-03 · The idle face's honest time (ST-01)

### FL-08 · Break start — the broken dimension (B-3)

Today no event exists: a break is a derived region of the span, and the
Deck's eyebrow merely reads differently next time he looks. Blueprint's
moment is Zunder's B-3, adopted as this lane's break grammar (it was
invented *for* this world, in its own convention):

- **At the flip** (the ticker's first tick inside the break region —
  derived state, both fields already on the row: `isInSafeBreak`,
  `safeBreakSecondsRemaining`): the running gauge's dimension line
  **breaks at its centre with the draughtsman's zigzag break symbol**.
  The elapsed chain stands left of the break, the remaining chain right
  of it, and the break's own remaining minutes are figured **beneath the
  line, in brackets** — `[BREAK · n MIN]` — the bracket being this
  world's mark for a span held off the main line (the same honesty
  grammar as the receipt's bracketed reconstructed figures, §4.3).
  The eyebrow keeps its trunk sentence `BREAK · n MIN` as the figure's
  caption. One firm click sounds — an event, not a chime (spec §5;
  ST-03's boundary voice, in this world's single-click form).
- **The transition:** the break symbol draws in over 180 ms (the plate
  vocabulary's standard beat) — a line being broken by hand, not a bar
  changing colour. Nothing else on the sheet moves; the figure above
  the line keeps counting the *span* (Nacht's law: the line is broken,
  the span is not).
- **The pill tag** (outside the app) takes its break frame at the same
  flip: `BREAK · n MIN LEFT` (§4.6 state 2). He learns the doors are
  open where he is standing, not only where the sheet is.

**The night-plate dissent, filed in the open:** register §2.3 asks the
clock's banner to gain `BREAK — DOORS OPEN · n MIN LEFT` on a mid-break
pickup. Blueprint **dissents in part**: the night plate refuses the break
(§4.4, Nacht's law — the plate tells session time; the break is figured
on the sheet and carried out of the app by the pill, the two surfaces
built for it). A man who picks the phone up mid-break meets the pill's
fact in the other app and the broken dimension on the Deck one tap away.
**Pre-registered fallback, so Ren's ruling costs nobody a redesign:** if
the register's line is held as law over looks, it lands on the night
plate's margin seat — the same seat as FL-03's wall time, alternating
never, break line above the hour while a break runs — at the cured
composite, and §4.4's two-figure law survives because the margin is not
a figure. Either way the fact is printable; the argument is only about
its seat, and it is Ren's to close.

**Verdict FL-08: BUILT-IN-LANE** — Blueprint lane builder; surfaces: Deck
dimension-line composable (Zunder prices B-3 at ~25 lines, one draw
