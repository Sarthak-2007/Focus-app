### 04:00–05:00 · The desk clock across the room, during meditation

**What crosses the room, with the night cure applied

- **The two figures cross; nothing else does.** A 96sp-class
  digit stands ~11 mm cap-height; at 4 m that subtends
  ~10 arcmin — twice the 5-arcmin letter a fully awake eye
  resolves at 20/20 (geometry as filed in the Temple day-fit
  pass, same panel class). At the cured contrast of 4.92:1
  the figure reads as a number, not as texture — with roughly
  2× size reserve, and that reserve is honestly thinner than
  it looks: mesopic, half-adapted acuity falls before contrast
  does, so 2× is the margin, not a surplus.
- **The hairline dimension line does not cross.** 0.75–1.5dp
  is 0.12–0.24 mm — 0.1–0.2 arcmin at 4 m, five to ten times
  below the 1-arcmin resolution floor. Nor do the extension
  lines, the eyebrow, or the exit line (11sp, muted, ≤0.25):
  at ~1 arcmin and whisper luminance they are texture at
  best. This is not a loss. The meditation hour needs exactly
  one thing across the room — *the span, checkable* — and the
  cured face sends exactly that and nothing else.
- **The plate rectangle does not cross, because it stops
  existing.** Collapsing plate to ground takes the plate step

  rectangle, no lit window floating in the room. The ground's

- **The frame stops brightening.** Salz-AOD's shipped frame
  sum grows 0.0305 → 0.0425 across a span (+39%) because the
