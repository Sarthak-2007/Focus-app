# UMSICHT — ISSUE #11 (HELD DOOR) · THE WORST-DAY PASS

*Seat: Umsicht, thoughtful. Lens: consequence and care — for every
decision, who it affects, what it costs them, and what happens on
their worst day, not their best one. Read: AUDIT-held-door.md whole,
ISSUE-11-WAVE2-REPORT.md whole. Paper only — nobody compiled.*

## WHO IS STANDING AT THIS DOOR

Not the man who armed the session. He was calm, fed, resolved.
The man at the door is him at 1 AM, mid-craving, willpower spent,
possibly ashamed he is there at all. Every print on this face is
read by the weakest reader the app will ever have, in the worst
five seconds of his day. That reader is the design target. The
calm man needs nothing from this face; the weak man needs it to
be incapable of lying to him, tripping him, or shaming him.

## THE CHARGES, BY COST TO THAT MAN

**1. The moving RETURN (FIX-6 / F-2) was the cruellest defect in
the audit, and it is a care defect, not a polish defect.**
On his best day a 55dp jump is an annoyance. On his worst day his
thumb is already travelling — he decided, with what will he has
left, to press RETURN — and the face moved his chosen button and
put EMERGENCY PEEK under his finger. The layout converted an act
of will into its exact opposite, and the ledger would have booked
it as his choice. Wave-2's stationarity proof (RETURN centre
136dp above the card's bottom edge on every branch, pixel-equal
bounds across counting / offer / held / never-owed frames) is
therefore the single most caring line of work on this issue.
Umsicht's verdict: **this cure must never regress silently.** The
frame tests added in wave-2 (memory line, errand sub-line,
homecoming) are the right fence. Keep them sacred.

**2. The fabricated ledger (FIX-3) harms the future self, not
the present one.** MUSCLE_MEMORY booked a reason nobody gave;
"Stayed Focused" booked a sequel nobody observed; "Habit" was a
third default of the same kind. Cost, on a later worst day: he
reviews his own history to understand himself and finds motives
invented for him and victories he did not earn. A man who cannot
trust his own record stops consulting it — and the Drawer/Ledger
downstream (Blank's NEXT-b rider) inherits the poison at the
source. Wave-2 verified the cure clause by clause (UNSTATED,
RETURNED AT DOOR, blank books blank, enum deleted whole).
Umsicht: **acquitted and closed, with the principle stated for
every future face — the app may record what a man did; it may
never record why he did it, or what he did next, unless he said
so or it was measured.**

**3. SESSION ENDS reading the wrong clock (FIX-2) is a trust
injury with a long tail.** Worst case in the audit's own words:
the sniper/site doors fire inside a Safe Break and print
"SESSION ENDS {now}" for ten minutes while the session runs.
One printed lie at the craving second teaches the weak man that
this face's prints are negotiable — and then the TRUE prints
(the price, the gate, the end instant) lose their authority on
every later night. The cure (spanRemainingSeconds, Deck TIME
cell in the same pass) protects more than a timestamp: it
protects the credibility every other line spends.

**4. The honest exit (FIX-8, Halbwach I-4) is the difference
between a door and a trap, and the trap's cost is the whole
app.** Picture the worst day honestly: not a craving — a real
emergency. A family call he must take, panic, illness. A door
with no honest exit offers that man exactly three moves: burn
a peek he doesn't want, fight the phone (force-stop, uninstall),
or sit in the valve for 60 seconds while his life waits. Each
of those teaches resentment; the uninstall ends the entire
system because one night had no lawful way out. "END THE SESSION
INSTEAD" — a route, not a spend, its gravity carried by the
Disarm face — means the worst night costs him a session, not
the app, and not his trust in the door on ordinary nights.
Umsicht: **strongest possible endorsement; this line is care
infrastructure, not a feature.**

**5. The taper's memory — "THIRD TIME THIS SESSION" — is the
one cure on this face that can itself wound.** The count is a
fact, and facts are this app's law. But who reads it? The man
at his third craving of the night — the one already most likely
to hear a count as a verdict. Care conditions, all cheap, all
print-level: (a) the count stays in the gate line's family, at
statement rank, never hero rank; (b) its ink never escalates —
no colour, weight, or size change as the count climbs (a count
that reddens is a score); (c) it is never carried into the
Ledger as a grade — the door may remember tonight, the record
must not judge him with it tomorrow. Wave-2's rejection of
"TONIGHT" for "THIS SESSION" (the function's own doc) is the
right instinct: the smaller, truer scope is also the kinder
one. With (a)–(c) held, Umsicht passes the taper. Without them
it becomes P-5's witness line in disguise — which this audit
rightly struck as "a score in witness clothing."

**6. `peekCooldownPrint(0)` — harden the law where it lives.**
Wave-2's Thoughtful seat flagged it and Umsicht adopts it as
this pass's one new charge: the pure function still returns
"AGAIN IN 0 MIN"; the truth holds only because the composable
guards it. Worst-day reasoning: the guard is exactly the kind
of line a tired future refactor deletes, and the failure mode
is a nonsense print appearing at the weakest reader's weakest
moment, on the face whose entire authority is that it never
prints nonsense. Price: make the impossible unrepresentable in
the function itself (no valid 0 print), test at the function,
not only at the surface. Small. Do it before batch-10 proofs
close, alongside the already-owed cooldown-at-0 frame test —
they are the same seam.

**7. The nameless hero (N-4 cure).** Package-verbatim is truth,
and Umsicht backs truth over the fabricated "Short-Form Feed"
without hesitation. The care cost is real and should be named:
at 1 AM, `com.zhiliaoapp.musically` is friction in the fact
slot — a half-second of "what is this?" in the second he can
least afford it. That cost buys the only thing that matters
(the slot never invents), and the filed resolved-label lookup
is the correct later polish. Recorded so nobody "fixes" the
ugliness by re-inventing a name.

**8. What this face already gets right, in care terms — do not
trade these away in the Leaf recomposition:** RETURN never
gated, live from frame one (the good choice is never the one
that waits); the 5-second wall gates only the offer, never the
stay, and now says so ("PEEK OPENS IN 0:0X"); the price printed
before the act, re-timed to when the cooldown actually starts
(FIX-8) — a man deciding deserves the full cost, lease +
cooldown, before he pays any of it; the withheld lines printing
from second 0 (a known refusal is never withheld — suspense is
a cruelty on this face, information is care).

## VERDICT

Wave-2's Held Door passes the worst-day test on every charge
except the two named above: harden `peekCooldownPrint` at the
function (charge 6), and hold the taper's three print
conditions (charge 5) as binding on whoever composes the Leaf.
The door the audit found was "a wall with good manners." The
door wave-2 describes is one a man can meet at his weakest and
be told the truth, be tripped by nothing, be shamed by nothing,
and leave honestly if his life demands it. That is the whole
job.

— Umsicht, thoughtful, issue #11
