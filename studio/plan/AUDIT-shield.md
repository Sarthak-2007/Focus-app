## THE ONE JOB — as ruled

Dens's sentence, amended by Fremd's founding charge (C1):

**Set — in seconds — what is SHUT, what PEEKs, and what stays OPEN;
prove at a glance the wall is as he left it; and say, in the same
glance, whether the wall is standing right now.**

Every verdict below is held to that sentence. The third clause is new
and it is the audit's headline: the screen never prints it today.

## THE HEADLINE FINDINGS (all six seats, converged)

1. **THE MISSING SENTENCE (Fremd C1; Muede F6).** SHUT prints as a
   state; the code enforces it only while a session is live
   (MonolithAccessibilityService L194–195 returns otherwise). The
   screen receives isSessionActive and spends it on palette and
   ratchet gating — never on a printed word. The one fact the screen
   exists for — *is my phone protected at this moment* — is absent.
2. **THE DUPLICATE BOOK (Dens lead; Plan §3; Fremd C5).** Batch 9
   added a second complete enumeration of ruled apps (the Standing
   Orders slip) above the catalogue that already enumerates them.
   Pre-catalogue scroll ≈1,230dp (~1.6 viewports) at the tally's own
   example size, +52dp per new ruling — the slip punishes its own
   use. Search sits ~1,100dp deep; BLOCKED WEBSITES ~6,100dp deep.
3. **THE UNREACHABLE TIME (Vega; Muede Walk 2).** The ±30 chips put
   routine times on a 30-minute lattice: SLEEP 20:00 → 20:40 does
   not exist in any number of gestures. The routine-edit lane's
   wheels cure it exactly — and were never merged into batch 9.
4. **THE SILENT REWRITE (Muede T2/F2; Vega S-5; Fremd C4).** One tap
   on the LOADOUT cap rewrites every app's tier by category
   (Repository applySmartLoadout) — no preview, no confirm, no undo —
   and the cap doesn't even show live state: it shows the last HAND
   pick while a routine's loadout may be the one running. STUDY also
   silently flips the Shorts sniper + browser blocker (C4d).
5. **THE INVISIBLE REFUSAL (Muede T1; Fremd C12).** A ratchet-refused
   flip during a session prints its refusal at item 0 of the column,
   off-screen from where the man stands. At his eyes: nothing happens.
6. **DEAD WIRES (Dens §22; Fremd C8; verified by Vogt).** Seven
   signature parameters occur once each — declaration only. The
   sniper + URL-blocker switches' real home is PARK (ParkDrawer
   park_shorts_sled :584, park_social_sled :753); Shield's contract
   carries ghosts.

## THE BAR — honest placement

Below the bar today, and below Blockit's discipline (Fremd's
placement, adopted): not for want of making — the sled is a better
object than most commercial switches, the tally's manners and the
slip's choreography are machined — but because the making serves
three overlapping representations of one decision while the one
sentence that matters is missing, and the screen's most destructive
control wears a tab's clothes. Blockit's tower spends nothing
twice and knows what it is in one glance. This merge list is the
distance: after it, the screen has one home per fact, a status
sentence, an honest cap, a reachable editor, and a finish that is
one construction system. Then it can be re-measured.
