**Condition 3 — vermilion placement: still open, uncharged.** No

### The night — the charge, pixel-proven

**The awake blue field is real, and it is the plate, not the
ground.** The token ground is what was filed: the night frame's
corner samples #0C1533 (rel-lum 0.083). But the surface the screen
actually spends its area on — the plate the drawing sits on —

**Does the line-work's quiet save it? In the hand, partly; across
the room, no.** The lines are genuinely quiet — hairlines, no
bloom, no signage, nothing that pulls the eye the way Noir pulls.
But at meditation distance the fine line-work is below what the
eye resolves; what crosses the room is not the drawing, it is a
glowing blue rectangle with one huge cream number on it. The
number reads across the room *because* the field is lit — that
is the trap, not the defence. At 20:00 the same face is the
brightest object in a dark bedroom short of a lamp, beside a man

This is a tax, not a fight: nothing here argues with the hour,
grabs at attention, or argues against calm. It simply stays lit
at both dark ends of a deliberately dark day — Dawn's class of
