## PART 1 — FINISHERS: every gap between Slice 1 and gallery standard

### The dial and the home room (highest visibility first)

- **F-01 · HOME/DIAL — the hero numeral is 36% under size and off-ladder.** `LauncherHome` dial numeral is Anonymous Pro Bold **46sp** (`LauncherBlockScreen.kt`, `LauncherHome`, dial `Column`) against DESIGN §A4's sole display tier **72sp / 72sp line-height, −1.0sp tracking, tabular** (also the ladder's H2). At 46sp the one object the room exists for reads as a large label, not an instrument. **Fix:** set the numeral to `72.sp`, `lineHeight = 72.sp`, `letterSpacing = (-1).sp`; grow the dial `Canvas` from `248.dp` to `288.dp` so the 72sp numeral + its label tier sit inside the ring with the §A3 well clearance (≥16dp from numeral edge to ring inner edge at the widest string `1:59:59`).
- **F-02 · HOME/DIAL — no page transition exists at all.** `LauncherBlockScreen` switches rooms with a bare `when (page)` (`LauncherBlockScreen.kt`, root `Box`) — HOME, ALLOWED and PEEK hard-cut with zero motion, against INTERACTION §0.1's grid-open (content translates 14dp → 0 and fades in over 240ms on `MonolithCurves.EasingA`, = `MonolithKin` TAB_IN) and grid-close (160ms, accelerating out / THROW). A launcher whose rooms snap is a settings page, not a machine. **Fix:** wrap the `when` in `AnimatedContent` keyed on `page` with `fadeIn(tween(240, EasingA)) + slideInVertically(tween(240, EasingA)) { 14.dp.roundToPx() }` together with `fadeOut(tween(120))` for HOME→grid, and the reverse pair at 160ms with `MonolithKin.EasingThrow` for grid→HOME; no other motion is added.
- **F-03 · HOME/DIAL — the intention is in the wrong place, wrong font, wrong size, and unbounded.** As built it prints *below and outside* the dial, in **Sora SemiBold 17sp** (`LauncherHome`, under the dial `Box`), with no `maxLines` and no ellipsis. DESIGN §A3/§A4 put it *inside the dial's well, under the numeral*, in **Atkinson Hyperlegible 400, 15sp / 22sp**, verbatim, **at most 2 lines then ellipsis**. As built, a long intention grows the column without limit and pushes both door plates and the foot row down (see F-11). **Fix:** move the intention `Text` inside the dial `Box`'s centred `Column`, below the `REMAINING` label; set `AtkinsonHyperlegibleFamily`, `FontWeight.Normal`, `15.sp`, `lineHeight = 22.sp`, `maxLines = 2`, `overflow = Ellipsis`, horizontal padding 36dp inside the well; delete the outside-dial copy.
- **F-04 · HOME/DIAL — the dial is flat: no ticks, no bezel, no groove, no fixed light.** The `Canvas` in `LauncherHome` draws exactly two arcs (track + progress) and nothing else. INTERACTION §2.3 requires the machined face: 60 minute ticks as one path (every 5th 10dp × 2.5dp, others 5dp × 1.5dp, each with a 0.75dp highlight offset toward the fixed 135° light), a two-stroke bezel (outer 2dp sheet, inner 1dp black @ 0.55), a 12dp recessed groove the progress arc sits *in*, and a fixed specular gradient (white @ 0.10 → transparent at 55%) clipped to the circle that never rotates with progress. Without them the screenshot test in §2.3 cannot pass and the dial reads as drawn, not machined. **Fix:** rebuild the dial as §2.3's 3 layers — Layer 1 static face in `Modifier.drawWithCache` (tick path + bezel + groove + fixed specular, remembered on size/palette), Layer 2 progress arc read only in the draw phase, Layer 3 numeral/intention recomposed at 1Hz (see also F-31).
- **F-05 · HOME/DIAL — the progress track uses the wrong token, so the unburned remainder lies about its material.** Track arc is `NightColorSurfaceBright` (#362C27, raised-card step 1) (`LauncherHome`, first `drawArc`). DESIGN §A3: track = **sheet `#4A423C`** at the full circle, so the remainder is always visible as hardware; §A2 reserves raised-card for Raised objects. **Fix:** track `color = NightColorSurfaceSheet`; keep the progress arc on `NightColorPrimaryContainer` (the ARMED verb — correct as built) and, per F-04, seat it in the recessed groove at **7dp** stroke with `StrokeCap.Butt` (machined ends), not the current 10dp stroke shared with the track.
- **F-06 · HOME/DIAL — stroke is 10dp for both arcs; the ring has no hierarchy.** Same `LauncherHome` `Canvas`: `val stroke = 10.dp.toPx()` drives track and progress identically. Spec: groove 12dp, progress 7dp centred in it (INTERACTION §2.3); DESIGN §A3's standalone figure is 6dp — either way the progress must be visibly *narrower than its housing*. **Fix:** per F-04/F-05 — groove stroke 12dp in the recessed token (#100E0C, `NightColorSurfaceVariant`), progress stroke 7dp, both `StrokeCap.Butt`, start −90°, sweep clockwise from the single session-end timestamp.
- **F-07 · HOME — the accent budget is blown on the home room alone.** DESIGN §B1 caps accent at the ring arc + one label word (<4% of S1 by area). As built, accent (`NightColorPrimaryContainer`) appears on: the `SEALED` state line (Atkinson Bold 11sp), both door-plate counts at **30sp** (`LauncherDoorPlate`), the Safe Break eyebrow, and `EMERGENCY BREAK` in the foot row (`LauncherHome`) — five masses, two of them the largest type on the screen after the numeral. The ARMED verb stops meaning *sealed*. **Fix:** door counts → cream `NightColorPrimary` (they are data, not state); `SEALED` line → `NightTextColorSecondary`; Safe Break eyebrow → cream; `EMERGENCY BREAK` → cream at rest (alert/accent only *while the 60s hold is being performed*, per DESIGN §A4's own exception). Accent remains only on: progress arc + the dial's state label word (F-08).
- **F-08 · HOME/DIAL — there is no state label in the dial, and the word that exists is the wrong word in the wrong voice.** DESIGN §A3/§A4 require the state word (`ARMED` / `ENDED` / …) in **Anonymous Pro 700, 10sp / 12sp, +2.0sp tracking, caps**, as one of TP-7's three legs, seated with the dial. As built the only state print is `SEALED` / `NO SESSION SEALED` at the top of the room in Atkinson Bold 11sp (off-ladder) in accent (see F-07), and the dial's own sub-label is `REMAINING` / `ELAPSED · NO SET END` in Atkinson **10.5sp** (off-ladder; 10.5 exists nowhere in the ladder). **Fix:** inside the dial well, above the numeral, add the state label `Text` — Anonymous Pro 700, `10.sp`, `lineHeight = 12.sp`, `letterSpacing = 2.sp`, text `ARMED` while a timed or stopwatch session is live, absent when `session == null`; delete the `REMAINING` sub-label entirely (DESIGN §A3: no "remaining" inside the well — the state label carries state, the end-time in F-09 carries the clock fact), and for stopwatch print `ELAPSED` in the same new state-label tier (Anonymous Pro 700, 10sp, +2.0sp) directly under the numeral, so no Atkinson 10.5sp text remains in the dial.
- **F-09 · HOME/DIAL — end-time is off-ladder and unseated.** As built: `ENDS HH:mm` in Anonymous Pro **13sp**, no letter-spacing, floating in the conditional stack *below* the dial (`LauncherHome`). DESIGN §A3/§A4: Anonymous Pro **400, 15sp / 20sp, +0.5sp**, format `ENDS 19:42`, seated at the dial's 6 o'clock rim, computed once from the same session-end timestamp as the numeral and never ticking. **Fix:** move the end-time `Text` to directly under the dial `Box` (rim seat, 8dp below the ring), set `15.sp`, `lineHeight = 20.sp`, `letterSpacing = 0.5.sp`; keep the existing single-timestamp derivation (`endsAtMs`) — that part is correct and is acquitted here.
- **F-10 · HOME — the brand line is three violations in one line.** `M O N O L I T H` is hand-spaced with literal spaces, in **Sora Bold 13sp** (`LauncherHome`, first `Text`). The app's nameplate law (header band, `MonolithCustomComponents.kt`) is **Anonymous Pro 700, 10sp, +2.0sp tracking**, the word unspaced. 13sp is off-ladder; hand spaces break under any tracking change and misalign the optical centre (trailing space). **Fix:** text `"MONOLITH"`, `AnonymousProFamily`, Bold, `10.sp`, `letterSpacing = 2.sp`, colour `NightTextColorSecondary` (unchanged); add `Spacer(Modifier.height(6.dp))` between brand and state line — as built the two `Text`s touch with no spacer at all, so the header block has no internal rhythm.
- **F-11 · HOME — the home column cannot overflow safely: no scroll, no insets, and the Emergency Break can be pushed off the phone.** `LauncherHome` is a non-scrolling `Column` with fixed `padding(horizontal = 24.dp, vertical = 28.dp)`, a fixed 248dp dial, two plates, and a `Spacer(Modifier.weight(1f))` foot row. The conditional stack between dial and plates (Safe Break eyebrow + intention + end-time + shut count) varies the dial-to-doors distance by up to ~64dp between sessions, so the doors have no fixed address (DESIGN §D5/INTERACTION §3.1: plates at a fixed address in the lower two-thirds, every time); on a short device or at 200% font scale the column simply clips and the foot row — including `EMERGENCY BREAK` — leaves the screen, and the fixed 28dp vertical padding ignores the system gesture zone (INTERACTION §3.2(c): no control inside the gesture insets, plate bottom margin ≥24dp above the gesture bar). **Fix:** (1) apply `.windowInsetsPadding(WindowInsets.safeDrawing)` to the home `Column` before its own padding; (2) give the conditional stack a fixed-height slot — reserve `64.dp` (`heightIn(min = 64.dp, max = 64.dp)`) between dial and plates in all states, content centred in it, so the plates never move; (3) wrap the region above the foot row in `verticalScroll(rememberScrollState())` so at 200% scale the foot row stays pinned and reachable; foot row itself stays outside the scroll.
- **F-12 · HOME/DOOR — door-plate press physics are a fraction of the spec.** `LauncherDoorPlate` animates a single uniform `scale` 1 → 0.975 with `spring(dampingRatio = 0.55f, stiffness = Spring.StiffnessMedium)` and shadow 6dp → 1dp. INTERACTION §0.1 (normative) requires, all lerped from one `pressProgress`: HeavySpring `SpringSpec(0.86f, 260f)`, scale **X 0.970 / Y 0.945** (non-uniform squish = mass; uniform reads as zoom), corner radius **20dp → 27dp**, elevation 6dp → 1dp, **translation Y +3.0dp**, top highlight alpha 0.16 → 0.03, plus a 120ms SEATED beat before the grid transition runs. As built there is no radius morph, no translation, no highlight, no seated beat, and the spring (0.55 damping) bounces like a tile's should. **Fix:** rebuild `LauncherDoorPlate`'s press on one `Animatable` `pressProgress` with `LauncherMotion.HeavySpring` added to `MonolithCurves.kt` beside the existing vocabulary (`SpringSpec<Float>(0.86f, 260f)`), lerping scaleX/scaleY/radius/elevation/translationY/highlightAlpha per the §0.1 table; hold SEATED (progress = 1, elevation 0) for exactly 120ms on commit before invoking `onClick`'s navigation.
- **F-13 · HOME/DOOR — the door plate's construction breaks the depth grammar.** As built (`LauncherDoorPlate`): fill `NightColorSurfaceBright` with a **1dp full-perimeter border in `NightColorSurfaceSheet`** and radius **18dp**. DESIGN §A2: a Raised plate carries a **1dp cream hairline on the top edge only** (light catches the top of a machined plate, never all four sides), radius **16dp** for plates in §A2 / **20dp** rest in INTERACTION §0.1 — the build matches neither — and Pressed must **step the fill up to sheet** and extinguish the hairline; as built the fill never changes on press. A full-perimeter sheet border on a bright fill is also near-zero contrast (#4A423C on #362C27) and reads as a dirty edge, not a hairline. **Fix:** radius `20.dp`; delete the full-perimeter `border`; draw the top hairline as a 1dp cream line (`NightColorPrimary` @ alpha `0.16` at rest → `0.03` pressed, from the same `pressProgress` as F-12) inset at the plate's top edge inside the clip; pressed fill lerps `NightColorSurfaceBright` → `NightColorSurfaceSheet`.
- **F-14 · HOME/DOOR — a door with zero apps is still Raised, sprung and tappable, and opens an empty room.** `LauncherHome` composes both `LauncherDoorPlate`s unconditionally with `allowedCount` / `peekCount` possibly 0. INTERACTION §0.1 defines the DISABLED plate: flat, elevation 0, no highlight, no spring, no haptic. **Fix:** when `count == 0`, render the plate FLAT — elevation 0dp, fill = ground `NightColorBackground`, no hairline, title/sub/count in `NightColorOnSurfaceVariant`, `clickable` removed, no haptic on the (non-)press; the grid behind it is then unreachable from HOME, matching the Airlock rule in F-44.
- **F-15 · HOME — the foot row's two texts are undersized tap targets with the wrong physics.** `RETURN TO THE DECK` and `EMERGENCY BREAK` (`LauncherHome` foot `Row`) are bare `Text`s with `clickable` (default ripple indication) and `padding(10.dp, 10.dp)` around 12sp type — a ~36dp-tall target, under the 48dp minimum, and they *ripple* while the plates above them suppress ripple (`indication = null`) under the metal law (DESIGN §A2: no ripple — ripples are paper soaking). Two physics in one room. The 12sp size is also off-ladder. **Fix:** give each foot action a fixed `height(48.dp)` `Box` with `contentAlignment = Center`, the same no-indication press treatment as the plates (scale/highlight from a shared press state, or at minimum `indication = null`), and set the words in Atkinson Bold **15.sp** (B1 tier) — `RETURN TO THE DECK` in cream, `EMERGENCY BREAK` in cream per F-07.
- **F-16 · HOME/DIAL — the dial has no spoken existence.** There is not one `semantics` block in `LauncherBlockScreen.kt`; the dial `Canvas` carries no `contentDescription`, so TalkBack gets nothing for time or state — TP-7's text leg and INTERACTION §3.2(a) both fail (focus order must start with the dial as one labelled display: "2 hours 14 minutes remaining, intention: …"). Door plates and tiles are `clickable` layouts with no `Role.Button` and no state description. **Fix:** add `Modifier.semantics(mergeDescendants = true)` on the dial `Box` with `contentDescription` built from the same derivations ("${remainingPrint} remaining, intention: ${session.intention}" / stopwatch and no-session variants); add `role = Role.Button` + `stateDescription`/`contentDescription` ("Allowed apps, $count apps") to `LauncherDoorPlate` and `LauncherAppTile` via their `clickable(role = …)` overloads; foot-row actions get `Role.Button` in F-15's `Box`es.
- **F-17 · HOME — vertical rhythm is off the 8dp grid in three places.** `LauncherHome` spacers are **30dp** (brand→dial), **14dp** (dial→stack), **30dp** (stack→plates), **14dp** (plate→plate). INTERACTION §0.1 fixes the plate-to-plate channel at **12dp** of bare ground ("two plates with darkness between them is an airlock" — DESIGN §B3), and 30/14 belong to no rhythm in the app. **Fix:** brand→dial `32.dp`; dial→slot (F-11) `16.dp`; slot→first plate `24.dp`; plate→plate `12.dp`; all other gaps in the room in multiples of 8dp.
- **F-18 · HOME/DIAL — stopwatch and no-session dials are unfinished faces.** With `session == null` the dial prints `—` over an empty sub-label string (`""`) and a full track ring — a hero object saying nothing, with dead air inside it; with a stopwatch the ring is (correctly) unswept but the rim is still the *timed* track construction, so the two modes are indistinguishable at a glance except by reading the small sub-label. DESIGN §A1's ENDED collapse and §D3's end anatomy have no equivalent here: no-session is this surface's cold state and is undesigned. **Fix:** no-session face — track drawn in raised-card (#362C27, the ENDED/cold tone) instead of sheet, numeral replaced by the plate-tier word `NO SESSION` (Anonymous Pro 700, 10sp, +2.0sp) centred in the well, doors rendered FLAT per F-14 (both counts are necessarily from no live seal), foot row keeps only `RETURN TO THE DECK`; stopwatch face — keep the unswept rim but add the §2.3 tick ring (F-04) so elapsed time still has a scale to be read against.

### The ALLOWED grid (S2)

- **F-19 · ALLOWED — tile press physics are under-spec and the wrong shape of wrong.** `LauncherAppTile` animates one uniform `scale` 1 → 0.93, `spring(0.5f, StiffnessMedium)`, inner-well shadow 4dp → 1dp. INTERACTION §0.2 requires the SNAPPY spring `SpringSpec(0.58f, 850f)`, uniform scale to **0.920**, radius **16dp → 23dp** morph, elevation **4dp → 0dp** (full collapse — the key bottoms out), translation Y **+2.0dp**, top highlight 0.12 → 0.02, all from one `pressProgress`. **Fix:** rebuild on one `Animatable` with `LauncherMotion.SnappySpring` (`SpringSpec<Float>(0.58f, 850f)`) lerping scale/radius/elevation/translation/highlight per §0.2; on OPEN commit add §0.2's hand-off beat — a 1dp accent edge around the well for exactly 120ms, then the launch fires (the tile never goes inset; it is leaving, not seating).
- **F-20 · ALLOWED — tiles cast a plate's shadow at rest, inverting the hierarchy.** The tile's icon well sits at **4dp shadow at rest** (`LauncherAppTile` inner `Box`). DESIGN §A2: tiles are **Seated** at rest — flush, **no shadow, no top hairline**, drawn as an engraved outline (hairline, all four sides, sheet tone); a tile becomes Raised only under the finger. A grid of 4dp shadows shouts over the door plates the user came through. **Fix:** at rest — shadow 0dp, well fill = ground on the grid's raised field (or raised-card directly on ground, one choice made once in `LauncherAppsPage`), 1dp sheet-tone outline on all four sides, radius 12dp (DESIGN §A2 tiles) — reconcile with F-19's morph by morphing 12dp → 19dp if §A2's radius wins, or keep §0.2's 16 → 23dp and strike §A2's 12dp in the design file; the builder applies §0.2 (the later, normative spec) and flags the §A2 line for the design bundle. Shadow appears only as `pressProgress` lifts the tile (highlight + outline brighten), never at rest.
- **F-21 · ALLOWED — app names are 10.5sp, truncate, and break the rack's shared baseline.** Tile label is Atkinson **10.5sp** (`LauncherAppTile`), `maxLines = 2`, ellipsis, no fixed cell height. DESIGN §A4 sets tile names at **Atkinson 700, 15sp / 20sp**; INTERACTION §3.1 sets 12sp as its floor *with the rule that names are never truncated — shorten the grid before truncating a name*; TYPE-RAMP allows only 15sp for this job. 10.5sp satisfies none of the three. Worse, 1-line and 2-line labels in the same `LazyVerticalGrid` row give that row two icon baselines — the key-rack alignment (DESIGN §A5/S2) is broken by every long name ("Samsung Internet", "Google Play Services"). **Fix:** label → Atkinson Bold, `15.sp`, `lineHeight = 20.sp` is too large for a 4-column cell at this width, so apply INTERACTION §3.1's own escape: drop the grid to **3 columns** (F-25) *and* fix every cell to a constant height (F-22) with the label slot reserved at exactly 2 lines (`height(40.dp)` for two 20sp lines at 12sp — see the reconciled numbers in F-22); names ellipsise only after 2 full lines at the wider column.
- **F-22 · ALLOWED — no fixed cell geometry: rows breathe.** `LauncherAppTile`'s `Column` has no height; the well is 52dp, the gap 5dp, the label variable. INTERACTION §0.2 fixes the cell: **84dp tall including label, icon 40dp, touch target ≥ 64 × 64dp**. **Fix:** tile root `Modifier.height(96.dp)` at 3 columns (84dp spec cell + the wider column's 2-line label slot: well 52dp + 5dp gap + label slot 40dp − 1dp rounding = 96dp — set exactly `96.dp`), well stays `52.dp` only if the icon inside is 40dp (F-23); grid `verticalArrangement = spacedBy(14.dp)` is kept, `horizontalArrangement` becomes `12.dp` at 3 columns.
- **F-23 · ALLOWED — icons are rasterised once at 96×96px on the composition thread and will blur and jank.** `LauncherAppTile` does `remember(packageName) { Bitmap.createBitmap(96, 96, …); drawable.draw(canvas) }` synchronously during composition — on a 100+ app grid this allocates and draws on the main thread as tiles enter, and 96px upscaled into a 40dp well on an xxhdpi device (≈120px needed) is visibly soft; adaptive icons are also force-fit into a square with no mask, so their shapes fight the well's 14dp radius. **Fix:** rasterise at `40.dp` in px read from `LocalDensity` (×1.5 headroom = the well's pressed scale), on `Dispatchers.Default` into a process-level `LruCache<String, Bitmap>` (cap 120 entries) shared by both grids, composed via `produceState` with the F-40 fallback letter shown until the bitmap lands; clip the drawn icon to the well's shape before rasterising so adaptive-icon masks survive.
- **F-24 · ALLOWED — no `OPEN` tag on any tile; permission must be decoded, not read.** DESIGN §A4 requires a tile tag `OPEN` — **Anonymous Pro 700, 10sp / 12sp, +2.0sp, cream** — on S2 tiles. As built the only permission signal is which room you are standing in. **Fix:** add the tag as a fixed slot under the label (inside F-22's cell height — extend the label slot, do not grow the cell ad hoc: cell becomes `108.dp`, tag slot `12.dp`), text `OPEN`, Anonymous Pro Bold, `10.sp`, `letterSpacing = 2.sp`, colour cream `NightColorPrimary`.
- **F-25 · ALLOWED — the grid is hard-coded to 4 columns at every width and density.** `GridCells.Fixed(4)` (`LauncherAppsPage`) with page padding 20dp — on a narrow device or at 200% font scale the columns crush (feeding F-21's truncation); on a wide/tablet device they stretch and the rack looks lost. **Fix:** `GridCells.Fixed(3)` as the phone default per F-21, computed once from `BoxWithConstraints` in `LauncherAppsPage`: `maxWidth < 340.dp → 3`, `340–600.dp → 4` *only if* labels are at the §0.2 12sp tier — since F-21 fixes labels at the reconciled size, ship **3 columns below 600dp, 5 columns at ≥600dp**, and state the choice in one `when` so both grids inherit it.
- **F-26 · ALLOWED — once inside the grid, time disappears.** Neither grid page carries any session readout. DESIGN §A4/S2 requires a session strip (remaining, mini — **Anonymous Pro 700, 15sp / 20sp, +0.5sp**) on S2. A user browsing allowed apps at hour nine must go back to HOME to learn the one fact the app exists to protect. **Fix:** add the strip to `LauncherAppsPage`'s header row, right-aligned: the same `launcherRemainingPrint(remainingSeconds)` derivation the dial uses, Anonymous Pro Bold, `15.sp`, `letterSpacing = 0.5.sp`, cream; `LauncherAppsPage` gains `remainingSeconds: Int?` and `isStopwatch: Boolean` parameters fed from `LauncherBlockScreen`'s existing tick (no second clock).
- **F-27 · ALLOWED — the empty state states a fact and nothing else, in an off-ladder size, with no object in the room.** Empty ALLOWED prints `NOTHING IS OPEN IN THIS SESSION` centred in a `weight(1f)` box, Atkinson **13sp** (`LauncherAppsPage`). DESIGN §A4: the empty line is **Atkinson 400, 15sp / 22sp, one line, stating the fact *and the lawful way to change it***. DESIGN §A2 also gives empty grid slots an Inset construction — an empty room should still be a room. **Fix:** text → `NOTHING IS OPEN. OPEN APPS ARE SET ON THE SHIELD, BEFORE THE SEAL.` set in Atkinson 400, `15.sp`, `lineHeight = 22.sp`, centred, max width 280dp; above it, one Inset well (ground fill on the raised field, inner top shadow, bottom-edge hairline only) at 52dp — the tile well with nothing seated in it.
- **F-28 · ALLOWED — one app looks like a loading error.** With a single allowed app, the grid shows one tile at top-left over a full screen of bare ground, with the footer marooned at the bottom — indistinguishable from a grid that failed to finish loading. **Fix:** when `apps.size == 1`, centre the single tile horizontally in the first row (wrap the grid in a `Box` with the tile at `Alignment.TopCenter` at the 3-column cell width) and print F-27's Inset wells in the remaining two slots of that row, so one reads as *a rack with one key*, not a broken list. No copy is added.
- **F-29 · ALLOWED — 100+ apps have no position cue and no scroll affordance.** The `LazyVerticalGrid` has no scrollbar, no index, no overscroll treatment; alphabetical order is correct (DESIGN §D5: never reorder — acquitted) but a user hunting in 120 tiles has no idea where they are in the alphabet or the list. **Fix:** attach a thin draw-phase scrollbar to the grid's `LazyGridState` (4dp wide, sheet tone, right edge, fades to 0 alpha 800ms after scroll stops) and print the first letter of the first fully-visible item as a plate-tier label (Anonymous Pro 700, 10sp, +2.0sp) at the grid's top-right, updating from `layoutInfo.visibleItemsInfo` — no search bar, no sort chips (DESIGN §B5/S2 forbids them; this is a position readout, not chrome).

### The PEEK grid (S3) — the room that must feel different, and doesn't

- **F-30 · PEEK — S3 is pixel-identical in construction to S2; the tier cannot be felt.** `LauncherAppsPage` is shared with only `title` / `sub` / `emptyText` differing — same ground, same well fill (`NightColorSurfaceBright`), same header, same tile. DESIGN §A1 locks PEEK's verb to **sheet `#4A423C` as a material**, §A5/S3 requires *the whole surface to sit one material step lower* ("you feel you have opened a door onto a darker room"), and §B1 defends the dimness by name. As built, a user who cannot read the 19sp title cannot tell which room they are in. **Fix:** give `LauncherAppsPage` a `room: LauncherRoom` parameter; for PEEK — page ground stays night ground, tile wells take **sheet fill** (`NightColorSurfaceSheet`) instead of bright, the header title plate sits on sheet, and all non-essential ink steps down one tone (sub-label and foot row in `NightTextColorSecondary`); ALLOWED keeps the current bright wells. One parameter, both rooms still one composable.
- **F-31 · PEEK — no peeks-remaining readout exists anywhere on S3.** DESIGN §A4/S3 requires `2 PEEKS LEFT` — **Anonymous Pro 700, 15sp / 20sp, +0.5sp** — and §D5/§E5 make the honest count the surface's daily proof of TELL-THE-TRUTH. The launcher prints no peek economy at all: not remaining, not spent, not per-app cooldown. **Fix:** S3's header gains the readout in the F-26 strip's slot (right-aligned, same tier): text derived from the same lease/cooldown store the Held Door reads — `"${n} PEEKS LEFT"` when the machinery can state a count, otherwise the per-app state in F-32 and *no invented total* (if no honest total exists in the store, print nothing rather than a fabricated one, and file the missing derivation as the blocker in the PR — never hard-code a number on this surface).
- **F-32 · PEEK — every peek tile claims the same promise; withheld/cooldown/active states have no tile face.** `LauncherAppTile` has one construction for all apps. On S3 an app in cooldown, an app whose peek is already running, and an app freely peekable render identically, and the tap raises the Held Door to deliver news the tile could have printed. INTERACTION's score even reserves F-DENIED (silence + the printed reason) for withheld targets — the tile is where that reason belongs first. **Fix:** `LauncherAppTile` gains a `badge` slot (the F-24 tag slot, reused): PEEK tiles print `PEEK` (Anonymous Pro 700, 10sp, +2.0sp, on sheet fill per §A4) when offerable; when the row's own state says otherwise, the tag prints the reason in the same tier — `HELD · {cooldownPrint}` / `RUNNING` — the tile's press becomes FLAT (no spring, no haptic, per §0.1's disabled construction) and the tap prints the reason in place instead of raising the door.
- **F-33 · PEEK — the peek tile's commit beat is missing: the tile must stay seated while the door rises.** As built, a PEEK tap fires `SealHaptics.tileSeat` (a single TICK — see F-41) and `onPeekApp` immediately; the tile springs back as if nothing happened. INTERACTION §0.2: on PEEK commit the tile goes **SEATED/inset** (elevation 0, well fill drops to recessed #100E0C, radius held at the pressed 23dp) and *stays* inset while the Held Door raises over 320ms — "the inset tile is the last thing seen." **Fix:** `LauncherAppsPage` (PEEK room only) holds `seatedPackage: String?` state; on tap, set it, drive that tile to the inset construction and hold it; fire `onPeekApp` in the same frame (the door's own raise is the Held Door's machinery, untouched); clear the seat when the launcher next becomes visible.
- **F-34 · PEEK — empty PEEK has the same defect as F-27, with its own lawful way missing.** Empty text is `NO PEEK APPS ARE SET`, Atkinson 13sp, fact only. **Fix:** *(shared scaffold with F-27 — same construction, this room's words)* text → `NO PEEK APPS ARE SET. PEEK APPS ARE SET ON THE SHIELD, BEFORE THE SEAL.` in Atkinson 400, `15.sp / 22.sp`, with the same single Inset well above it, sheet-toned per F-30.
- **F-35 · PEEK — during a Safe Break the room still sells what is already free, and its tiles don't say so.** The design bundle's own critic file (C-10) established that PEEK apps are simply open during a Safe Break; the as-built S3 routes every tap through `onPeekApp` (the Held Door) in all states, and no tile or header marks the break. Even where the machinery cure belongs to another slice, the *finish* belongs here: a room that looks identical in and out of a break is mislabelled in one of them. **Fix (finish layer only):** when `session.isInSafeBreak`, S3's sub-label becomes `SAFE BREAK — THESE APPS ARE OPEN UNTIL THE BREAK ENDS` (Atkinson 400, 15sp tier in the header's sub slot), tile tags print `OPEN` instead of `PEEK`, and tiles use the ALLOWED room's bright wells for the break's duration; the tap routing change itself is C-10's machinery cure and is *not* smuggled into this finish.
- **F-36 · PEEK + ALLOWED — the grid header and footer duplicate the same back action at two addresses with two sizes.** `LauncherAppsPage` prints `‹ LAUNCHER` at the top (Atkinson Bold **13sp**, padding 8dp) *and* again in the foot row (Atkinson Bold **12sp**, padding 10dp) — the same verb, twice on one screen, at two off-ladder sizes, with two different target heights (~33dp and ~36dp, both under 48dp). **Fix:** keep exactly one back affordance — the header one — rebuilt as a 48dp-tall `Box` (F-15's construction), Atkinson Bold `15.sp`, text `‹ LAUNCHER`; the foot row keeps only `EMERGENCY BREAK` (right-aligned, 48dp box, 15sp). One verb, one address, per room.
- **F-37 · PEEK + ALLOWED — grid-page margins disagree with the home room's margins, so the house has two widths.** HOME pads `horizontal = 24.dp, vertical = 28.dp` (`LauncherHome`); grid pages pad `horizontal = 20.dp, vertical = 24.dp` (`LauncherAppsPage`), plus the grid's own `contentPadding = 4.dp` — the door plates (24dp inset) and the tiles below them (24dp inset only by accident of 20+4) align vertically but the headers (20dp) do not align with the brand line (24dp). **Fix:** one room inset for all three surfaces: `horizontal = 24.dp` everywhere, grid `contentPadding` horizontal `0.dp` (vertical `4.dp` kept), header back box starting at the same 24dp line as HOME's brand. *(Shared scaffold.)*
- **F-38 · PEEK + ALLOWED — grid header type is off-ladder in both tiers.** Page title is Sora Bold **19sp** and sub is Atkinson **11sp** (`LauncherAppsPage` header). DESIGN §A4 sets both grid titles at **Sora 700, 22sp / 26sp, −0.3sp** (the ladder's T1), with the sub in the body tier. **Fix:** title → Sora Bold, `22.sp`, `lineHeight = 26.sp`, `letterSpacing = (-0.3).sp`; sub → Atkinson 400, `15.sp`, `lineHeight = 20.sp`. The back word and the title then share one baseline row at a fixed 48dp height (F-36). *(Shared scaffold.)*
- **F-39 · PEEK + ALLOWED — no entrance for the grid's own content.** Beyond the room transition (F-02), tiles appear fully formed in one frame, including on first composition of a 100+ grid where F-23's rasterisation makes the pop visible tile by tile in scroll order — the worst possible stagger, an accidental one. **Fix:** tiles fade in with the room transition only (alpha from the `AnimatedContent` transition's progress — no per-tile delay, no stagger; a rack does not cascade), and until F-23's bitmap lands the tile shows its fallback letter, so first paint is complete in one frame. *(Shared scaffold.)*
- **F-40 · ALLOWED + PEEK — the fallback letter and the icon well fight the icons' own colours.** When an icon is missing, the tile prints its first letter in Sora Bold 20sp, cream on bright — fine — but when an icon *is* present it sits at 40dp inside a 52dp bright well with 6dp of bright showing on every side: a cream-bright frame around every foreign-coloured icon, which *does* change how the icon's colours read (simultaneous contrast) even though no surface samples the icon. Gallery standard is that the icon is the object; the well is its socket, not a frame. **Fix:** icon fills the well edge-to-edge (well 52dp, icon 52dp clipped to the well's 14dp radius, rasterised per F-23); the bright fill shows only behind the F-40 fallback letter and during F-39's first paint. Foreign colours stay foreign — the socket stops framing them.

### Cross-cutting feel, the Airlock door chips, and the last polish

- **F-41 · ALL LAUNCHER — the haptic score is a different, smaller score than the one specified.** `SealHaptics` implements exactly three single-primitive calls: `sealWeight` (THUD 0.8, fired from `LaunchedEffect` on *every* launcher open), `doorPress` (CLICK 0.9, fired on click/release), `tileSeat` (TICK 0.6, fired on tile click). INTERACTION §0.3 scores: F-ENTER = CLICK **0.70** on the frame the surface becomes visible; F-PLATE = CLICK **1.00** at `pressProgress` crossing 0.35 on the way *down* + THUD **0.60** when the grid lands; F-OPEN = CLICK 1.00 down + QUICK_FALL **0.80** at hand-off; F-PEEK = CLICK 1.00 + one composition SLOW_RISE 0.70 @0ms / THUD 0.80 @+300ms; F-BACK = LOW_TICK 0.70; F-END = QUICK_FALL 0.90 + THUD 0.70 @+90ms; F-DENIED = 0 events. As built: the enter event is the wrong primitive at the wrong weight, every commit fires on *release* instead of the down-crossing (violating Law 1, same-frame), the land/hand-off/back/end events do not exist, and the tile's TICK 0.6 spends the navigation voice on a commit (THUD/CLICK discipline, §4.2). The fallback ladder itself (Composition → predefined `EFFECT_CLICK`) is correctly built and is acquitted. **Fix:** replace `SealHaptics` with `LauncherHaptics` exposing `enter() / plateDown() / plateLand() / tileDown() / openHandoff() / peekRaise() / back() / sessionEnd()` per §0.3's table and composition shapes, fired from the `pressProgress` snapshotFlow crossing (INTERACTION §2.1) and the transition/hand-off frames named there; delete `sealWeight`'s open-time THUD; keep the existing rung ladder as the delivery mechanism inside each named event.
- **F-42 · AIRLOCK — the door chips are a different species from the doors they open.** In the Airlock, ALLOWED/PEEK are `BlockitActionChip`s — 42dp tall, 12dp radius, `surfaceVariant` fill, 12sp mixed-type label with the count as an inline same-size suffix (`MonolithCustomComponents.kt:170`, `BlockitActionChip`) — while one tap later the same two doors are 18sp Sora plates with 30sp counts (`LauncherDoorPlate`). Same verb, two unrelated costumes, two sizes, two physics. **Fix:** the Airlock chips keep the chip *size* (they live inside the Airlock's card — that context is correct) but adopt the doors' voice: label in Atkinson Bold 15sp is the chip's own tier and stays; the count badge moves out of the inline suffix into a right-aligned Anonymous Pro Bold **15sp** numeral in its own 24dp slot, and the chip gains the plate's top hairline (1dp cream @ 0.16) so the eye files it as the same hardware in a smaller casting. No other Airlock element changes.
- **F-43 · AIRLOCK — the chips pop into existence and the whole card jumps.** The two chips are inside `if (countdownSecs == 0)` with no `AnimatedVisibility` (`AirlockAndDisarmOverlays.kt`, `AirlockInterceptOverlay`, the `countdownSecs == 0` door block, ~L731–747): at the countdown's end the card's `Column` (`spacedBy(16.dp)`) grows by two chips + two gaps (≈116dp) in one frame and recentres — content moves under the user's finger at the exact moment the wall opens. **Fix:** wrap the door block in `AnimatedVisibility(visible = countdownSecs == 0, enter = expandVertically(tween(240, EasingA)) + fadeIn(tween(240, EasingA)), exit = fadeOut(tween(120)))` so the room grows at TAB_IN speed; the card's other contents do not move (anchor the card's column to the top of its `Box` during the expansion — `contentAlignment = TopCenter` on the outer box for this overlay).
- **F-44 · AIRLOCK — zero-count doors are live chips to empty rooms.** The chips print `allowedAppsCount` / `peekAppsCount` verbatim and stay tappable at 0, the Airlock twin of F-14. **Fix:** when a count is 0, that chip is not composed at all (absent, not disabled — the Airlock's own law for the peek chip above it); if both are 0, neither is composed and the Airlock is exactly its pre-revamp self.
- **F-45 · AIRLOCK — chip press has no physics and no haptic at all.** `BlockitActionChip` is a plain `clickable` `Box` — default ripple, no press state, no haptic — while its launcher twins at least scale (F-12). A door that ripples like a link, inside the app's most serious room, breaks the metal law at the threshold. **Fix:** inside `AirlockInterceptOverlay` only, wrap each door chip's `onClick` with `SealHaptics`' successor `plateDown()` (F-41) fired on press-down via a local `MutableInteractionSource` observing `PressInteraction.Press`, and pass `indication = null` through a new optional parameter on `BlockitActionChip` for these two call sites (default unchanged for every other chip in the app).
- **F-46 · AIRLOCK — the door chips inherit the theme scheme, not the night tokens, so they can drift from the room they stand in.** `BlockitActionChip` colours come from `MaterialTheme.colorScheme` (`surfaceVariant` / `onBackground` / `outlineVariant`) while the Airlock card around them is built from explicit `NightColor*` tokens (`NightColorSurfaceBright` card, `NightColorPrimary` ink). Today they happen to agree at night; any theme re-map silently splits the doors from the room. **Fix:** at these two call sites only, no code change to the chip — instead pin the Airlock's `MaterialTheme` night scheme as the tested contract with one screenshot assertion per mode in the existing test style (day scheme never composes this overlay while sealed: `effectiveNight = darkTheme || sealed` in `MainActivity` already forces night) — the buildable finish is the *test*, filed here so the drift becomes a failure instead of a surprise. If the test cannot be made to pass by scheme alone, the fallback fix is an explicit `nightTokens = true` variant parameter on the chip; the builder tries the test first.
- **F-47 · HOME/DIAL — the dial recomposes the whole room every second.** `nowMs` is composition state in `LauncherBlockScreen` read by `LauncherHome` during composition, so each 1s tick recomposes the numeral, the plates, the foot row — and can restart a plate's press spring mid-press. INTERACTION §2.3: the arc is draw-phase state, the numeral recomposes at 1Hz in its *own* composable, and neither invalidates the other. **Fix:** split `LauncherHome` — `DialFace` (draw-phase progress via `withFrameMillis` writing a `MutableFloatState` read only in the draw block), `DialNumeral` (the existing 1s tick, scoped to the numeral + end-time only), and the static remainder (brand, plates, foot row) outside both, so a tick invalidates exactly two `Text`s and one arc.
- **F-48 · TASTE-ONLY — the home room's proportions are worthy but not yet inevitable.** Dial 248dp (288dp after F-01) over two plates over a foot row is a correct stack that still reads as *a column of things* rather than one composed object; the optical centring DESIGN §B3 asks for (dial centre +4dp above geometric centre) is not attempted. No number in the specs fully determines the remaining air, so this is filed as taste, short, per the bundle's rule: after F-01/F-11/F-17 land, re-render and judge the stack once, on the phone, before inventing new spacing. — *TASTE-ONLY*

## PART 2 — FRESH-EYES SOUL TEST (blind protocol, colours mentally stripped)

**Protocol:** palette ignored entirely. Only layout structure, navigation
shape, and object inventory are compared — what objects exist, how the hand
moves, what carries state. Pre-revamp world = the Deck/Airlock app described
in the design docs and still present in this tree: a rail-navigated app
(DECK / SHIELD / TASKS / LEDGER + PARK drawer) whose Deck is an *instrument
panel* — a rotary wheel you drag to set time, a readout, a slide-to-arm
commit bar you drag to seal, a proof strip (BLOCK / APPS / TIME) — plus the
Airlock intercept overlay (5-second breathing countdown, peek offer, Held
Door) and the 60-second Emergency Break valve overlay. Sealed, the Deck
stays the app's centre; blocking is something that happens *around* it.

**The as-built launcher, structurally:** a full-screen overlay *on top of*
that same app, containing three pages held in one local `remember`ed state
(`LauncherPage.HOME / ALLOWED / PEEK`): page one is a display dial (ring +
numeral + intention) over two full-width buttons over a two-text foot row;
pages two and three are the *same* 4-column icon grid composable
instantiated twice with different lists. Entry is a tap on the Guard line
or a tap on a chip inside the Airlock. Exit from the launcher is a text
tap — `RETURN TO THE DECK` — back to the unchanged app underneath.

### Evidence (structural only)

1. **Object inventory: one new silhouette, zero new object kinds.** The
   dial is the Deck readout's silhouette (circle + big numeral + intention)
   with its *input removed* — the Deck's wheel is dragged; the launcher's
   dial is looked at. The door plates are the app's existing action chip
   (`BlockitActionChip`) enlarged into a row with a count. The grids are
   the Shield's per-app rows (same `BlockedAppEntity` rows, same
   `resolvedTier` partition, same alphabetical order) re-laid from a list
   into tiles. Every object in the launcher has a direct ancestor in the
   old app; none does a job no old object did.
2. **Navigation shape: a branch, not a root.** The old app's shape is a
   rail with four stops plus overlays. The launcher does not join or
   replace that shape — it hangs off it as a modal branch (Guard-line tap
   in, `RETURN TO THE DECK` out, Back swallowed only *inside* the branch).
   While sealed — the very state the launcher exists for — the Deck, the
   rail, Shield, Tasks and Ledger all still exist underneath, composed
   around it. A new app would have a new centre; this has a new *room off
   the old centre*.
3. **The hand moves exactly as it did.** The old app's two signature
   movements — drag the wheel in a circle, drag the slide in a line to
   commit — are both *absent* inside the launcher. Inside it, the hand
   only taps rectangles in a vertical column and taps tiles in a grid:
   the generic Android movement vocabulary the old app's *menus* already
   used. Nothing in the launcher is operated the way a launcher is
   operated (no home surface you arrange, no dock, no widgets, no
   persistent personal layout) — it is operated the way a dialog is.
4. **State: the launcher carries none of its own.** Session state still
   lives entirely in `FocusSessionEntity`; app state entirely in the
   Shield's rows; the launcher's only owned state is which of its three
   pages is showing, held in `remember` and lost on recomposition of the
   host. Delete `LauncherBlockScreen.kt` and the app loses no capability
   and no data — OPEN apps were always reachable from the system
   launcher, PEEK was always reachable through the Airlock — it loses
   only a second front door to verbs that already existed. A surface
   whose deletion changes nothing structural is, structurally, a coat
   surface — however well the room itself is built.
5. **What *is* genuinely new (and why this is not a flat NEW COAT):** the
   Back law (grid → home, home swallowed) is a navigation rule the old
   app never had; the tier partition as a *spatial* fact — two doors,
   two rooms, permission as geography — is a real structural idea, not a
   re-skin; and the sealed phone gaining an interior (somewhere to *be*
   while blocked, instead of a wall to stare at) changes what a session
   *is*, even though the furniture inside is inherited.

### Verdict: **IN BETWEEN** — a new room built inside the old app, furnished entirely with the old app's furniture.

Not NEW COAT: the two-doors/two-rooms geography and the un-exitable Back
law are structural, and a user living inside a sealed session will feel
the difference daily. Not NEW APP: the centre of gravity never moved —
the Deck is still home, the launcher is a branch off it, every object is
an inherited object with its input or its list re-laid, and the launcher
owns no state, no gesture, and no navigation root of its own.

### The three structural changes that would most push it toward unmistakably new

1. **Move the root: while sealed, the launcher *is* the app — the Deck and
   the rail leave composition entirely.** Today the launcher is an overlay
   with a `RETURN TO THE DECK` door, which is precisely what makes it a
   branch. Structurally: the seal swaps the navigation root (launcher
   becomes the sealed app's only destination; the Deck exists only
   unsealed), the foot row's return door is deleted, and session end /
   Emergency Break are the only ways the old app's surfaces ever reappear.
   The hand learns one fact — *sealed means a different app is running* —
   and every other surface in the product starts telling the same story.
2. **Give the dial its input back: the launcher's rooms and tiles are
   operated by turning, not by tapping down a column.** The dial is
   currently the Deck wheel's silhouette with the gesture amputated.
   Restore a rotary movement as the launcher's primary navigation —
   turn to move between the two doors and through the grid's rows, tap
   only to commit (open / raise the Held Door) — and the launcher gains
   the one thing no coat can fake: a movement vocabulary that exists
   nowhere in the old app's menus and that the hand recognises as *this
   place's* way of moving. (Machinery untouched: the gesture selects; the
   existing launch and peek verbs still fire the commit.)
3. **Give the launcher state of its own: a sealed arrangement that
   persists.** Let the user, while sealed, pin apps to a fixed dock row
   on the launcher home (and re-order nothing else), stored as
   launcher-owned data that survives sessions and that neither Shield
   nor the Deck reads or writes. The moment the launcher remembers
   something the old app never knew — *how this person arranges their
   sealed phone* — deletion of the surface would lose something real,
   the home page becomes *theirs* rather than a readout, and the surface
   crosses the line this test actually measures: from a view of the old
   app's state to a place with a life of its own.
