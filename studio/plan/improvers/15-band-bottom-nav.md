**What the 4→3 cut does to the instrument (the geometry this ladder is built on):**
Removing a stop is not deleting a quarter. At the reference width (360dp; slab 336dp wide):

- **Detent spacing grows ~50%.** Four stops sat ≈82dp apart (wave-1 PEAK centres); three stops on the same span sit ≈119–131dp apart depending on variant (§5). Each hop is a longer, more deliberate travel — the switch gets *slower-feeling and more certain*, which suits a machine with three rooms and no fourth to confuse it with.
- **Pass-through collapses to at most one tick.** FOCUS→HORIZON crosses exactly one middle stop (SYSTEM). The wave-1 two-tick flourish is gone; the single crossing tick becomes *the* signature sound of a full traverse.
- **SYSTEM becomes the only middle stop** — therefore the only stop that may overshoot (end stops are walls; a wall never overshoots). The whole overshoot budget of the band concentrates on one detent.
- **The printed scale must be re-ruled, not amputated.** Numerals become `01 / 02 / 03`. If the `04` were simply deleted, the scale would end in a visible absence — a gap the eye reads as damage. Every rung that prints a scale prints a *complete three-stop scale*: ruled end-to-end for three, end tick heavier than the intermediates, no ghost space where a fourth stop was.
- **Touch zones widen to ≈112dp each** (midpoints between centres) — the fattest, most forgiving targets in the app. Three stops is a one-handed, half-asleep, 03:30 control. The ladder keeps it that way: no rung may shrink a zone below 100dp.

### RUNG 3 — THE HAND AND THE SEAL (drag, and the Cloister state)

*Mechanism:* everything in Rung 2, plus the cap becomes grabbable and the band gains the state HOLD demands. This rung is where the standards stop being aspirations.

- **Drag.** DOWN within the cap rect ±6dp slop grabs it (`requestDisallowInterceptTouchEvent(true)`, animator cancelled, grab offset preserved — a fader is grabbed, not teleported). MOVE is 1:1; within **12dp** of a stop centre the drawn position magnetises — `drawn = centre + (raw − centre) × 0.55` — the cap hesitates in each groove without the finger stopping. Beyond the end centres, excess travel rubber-bands ×0.25, clamped to 9dp. UP commits the nearest stop; remainder ≤ ½ span animates **180ms Curve A** (a click-shut, not a glide). CANCEL returns to the committed stop, **260ms Curve A**, no callback. Selection (emphasis + callback) commits **only on release**; during a scrub the committed stop stays lit — preview ≠ selection, stated honestly by the light.
- **Drag feel.** Grab: `fireFeel(4L)` latch `[0,11,26,9]ms @ [0,215,0,150]`. Scrub crossing a stop's nearest-boundary: that stop's 1L detent (500/590/680Hz). End-wall entry while dragging: `fireFeel(4L)` once per entry — a dull stop, distinct from the seat. Seat on release-commit: `fireFeel(8L)` firm click as Rung 2. While dragging, pool offsets grow +1.5dp and the contact core drops to `0x2E000000` — the cap *lifts* in the hand; the cap body never scales.
- **THE SEALED STATE (Session Cloister) — new, complete.** When a session is active (`isSessionArmed == true`, renamed in intent to *sealed*), the band locks down and offers **no decisions**:
  - *Engage:* on the session-seal event, a **locking pin** drops through the cap: a 3dp-wide bar, `tokTextPrimary()`, drawn over the cap across the slit, `slotY − 14dp … slotY + 14dp` at the cap's centre — a bolt through a fader. Pin drop: **180ms Curve A**. Simultaneously the printed scale dims: numerals/words of *unselected* stops drop to 45% alpha, the selected stop's word stays full (where you are remains legible; where you could go stops advertising). No text is added. No colour is spent.
  - *Behaviour:* all touch is swallowed. DOWN anywhere on the slab returns exactly one dull acknowledgement — `fireFeel(4L)` latch, throttled to once per press, no repeat, no ripple, no callback, no navigation, no drag. The cap does not move. `contentDescription = "Navigation sealed — session running"`.
  - *Release:* only on session end. Pin lifts **180ms Curve A**, scale restores over **260ms Curve A**. There is no in-band exit, no long-press, no secret gesture — a sealed band that can be talked out of sealing is not sealed. (The emergency break lives on the Deck, under his banked-hold rule; the chrome neither duplicates it nor advertises it — TRUTH once, HOLD absolute.)
  - *Why a pin and not a word:* the app states state by position and form everywhere else; "SEALED" printed on the chrome would be a label doing a mechanism's job, and a word can be ignored in a way a bolt through the cap cannot.
- **ARM check at this rung:** destination switching is on the arm path only *before* arming (he arms from FOCUS; if he starts on SYSTEM, one tap — callback fires on UP/commit, never on animator end — puts him on the Deck in ≤280ms of chrome time). The seal engages *after* the arm gesture, so the seal can never tax the ≤10s path. Drag is a rested-user pleasure; the tired user's path is tap-and-go, unchanged.
- **Depth / tokens:** as Rung 2; pin in `tokTextPrimary()` (day `#2C2A29`, night `#D2C6BB`); dimming by paint alpha only, restored to 255 after every use. Zero orange — including in the sealed state, where a warning colour would be the easiest lie to reach for.
- **Price:** one view file, ≈200 lines (touch state machine + seal rendering). Host: one boolean already exists (`isSessionArmed` feed); no new wiring. Risk: medium — the seal's touch-swallowing must be tested against the host's pull-down header gesture so a sealed band never eats a swipe meant for the screen above it (slab bounds only; outside `cardRect` the view returns false).

## §5 — THE END OF TRAVEL: THE PREPARED FORK (his pick lands here)

Both variants share: HORIZON is the last *named* stop; the cap's travel *to* a non-band state (whatever Guard becomes) is **420ms Curve C** `cubic-bezier(0.3,0,0.8,0.15)` with the `fireFeel(10L)` completion pulse — parking is the machine winding down, never a band switch, in either variant. Neither variant gives the end a word, a numeral, an icon, or a tap target. The difference is purely whether the rail *admits* a position beyond the scale.

**Variant A — PLAIN END WALL after HORIZON.**
The scale is complete at three. `travelRight = slabRight − 26dp`; HORIZON's centre sits ≈25dp from the wall; the end tick is drawn **double-weight** (2.8dp) — a ruled full stop. A non-band state (Guard, if it survives as a screen) parks the Rung 1 way, upgraded to peak grammar: the cap slides **14dp past HORIZON's centre** against the wall, narrows 58dp → 40dp, marker drawn in `tokHairline()` at 60% — docked *against* the end, visibly not *on* a stop.

HORIZON also carries double duty forever: last band *and* the wall everything parks against.

**Variant B — GENERIC END DETENT, TENANT UNNAMED.**
The rail reserves its last 52dp: a **lead hairline** (1dp `0x2E000000`) runs from `travelRight` to the detent centre at `slabRight − 17dp`; a **notch tick** (1.4dp wide, `slotY ± 4dp`, `tokTextSecondary()`) marks the position. No numeral, no word, no label — the detent is a *place*, not a destination; it is never a tap target (taps in its segment resolve to HORIZON). If and only if the host enters a non-band state, the cap travels there (420ms Curve C), narrows 58dp → **24dp**, marker dimmed to `tokHairline()`, and ticks once at 770Hz on arrival — the reserved fourth note, spent on a position, not a name.
*Price:* +≈30 lines over A (lead line, notch, width-lerp on the 3→park segment); spacing tightens to ≈119dp (still +45% over the old four-stop band). *Cost:* an unexplained notch is a question the rail asks every day — mitigated, not eliminated, by the off-scale grammar (hairline, not slit; notch, not numeral).
