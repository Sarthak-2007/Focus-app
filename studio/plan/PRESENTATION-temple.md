# PRESENTATION — TEMPLE (carved stone & brass), phase 2

*Temple, designer. Lane: builds/temple/. Phase-1 renders:
shots-own/ (Deck, Shield, Tasks, Vault, Cabinet — day + night).
This spec is the presentation design whole: what information is
presented and what is not, how each thing is used at its moment,
how every choice a hand makes is selected, and the feel of it —
moment by moment. Lane-applied items are marked [LANE]; everything
else is design-filed for the winner's merge [MERGE]. Nothing here
touches mechanism, data, or the seal's voice.*

## 0. The world, in one breath

A sanctum object: carved sandstone by day, lamplight on bronze by
night. Ink is deep bronze; brass rules every line; gold leaf is
the single accent and is spent like ritual metal — on commits,
never as paint. Vermilion is the temple pigment: filled, it means
the day is wounded; outlined, it is the seal — authentication,
never alarm. The staircase (sRGB relative luminance):

- **Day:** recess #D8C69C (0.58) → ground #E8DCC0 (0.72) → slab
  #E3C184 (0.56, *below* ground — a bronze-stone tablet sitting on
  sand, separated by its cast shadow, §1.4) → sheet #EFE5CB (0.79).
- **Night:** recess #0E0906 → ground #17100A → raised #392A16 →
  sheet #4E3A20; text parchment #E7D5AB at 13.01:1 on ground.

## 1. THE DECK

### 1.1 Information audit — every element, kept or cut, with its job

- **Header — "MONOLITH · GUARD 1/4 — ACCESSIBILITY".** Job: the
  one standing lack, named, at every glance. Fremd (fresh eyes)
  could not decode "1/4" cold. Temple files [MERGE]: print it as
  words a tired man reads in five seconds — "GUARD — 1 OF 4 SET ·
  ACCESSIBILITY MISSING" — same fact, no cipher. The counted four
  are trunk law; only the spelling is presentation.
- **TOTAL (eyebrow).** Job: names what the wheel sets — the total
  block. Kept. Its pair is FLOW on the stopwatch face; the two
  words are the two modes, printed where the number lives.
- **The hero number.** Protected, untouchable (the finish-list's
  ruling stands). In Temple it is the inscription on the slab:
  bronze ink by day, parchment by night, at monument scale.
- **The dial.** The selection instrument (§1.2). Its wells (the
  eight dots Fremd asked after) are the block-count register:
  filled wells = blocks set, in two tetrads of four; tapping the
  hub cycles the count. They are print, not ornament — and by day
  they now read (§1.4).
- **NOW line + DONE + QUEUE 2.** The current task, its tear-off,
  the count waiting. Kept whole; it is the Deck's only sentence
  about work, and it earns its line.
- **The status band — BLOCK OFF · APPS 10 · TIME ENDS 16:27, with
  BIND NOT HELD / PROOF: POSITIVE beneath.** Job: the machine's
  own state, stated, never implied. Fremd stumbled on both
  verdicts; Temple decodes them in grammar [MERGE]: the band is
  the slab's inscription strip, and PROOF: POSITIVE takes the
  vermilion *outline* seal beside it when positive — the seal is
  how this world says "witnessed" (§5 uses the same mark on the
  receipt's №). NOT HELD stays words; a held bind prints HELD and
  the seal. No new fact is printed; the mark is the state's own.
- **The slide — "SLIDE OR TAP TO START".** His verdict stands:
  tap can commit. The cap is the day's one gold spend (night:
  leaf gold struck brighter, #DFAF3F). Ruth judged this arm
  instruction the best in the wave; it is not touched.
- **"BLOCKING IS OFF — TAP TO FIX".** The refusal sentence. In
  Temple it prints in full ink at full voice (contrast §10);
  vermilion does *not* take it — a lack is a fact, not a wound.
  The filled vermilion is fenced to §8.

### 1.2 Selection — how a hand chooses, on the Deck

- **Duration: the wheel.** Temple's read of the shared wheel: a
  great dial settling — ζ=0.38's two overshoots are a heavy

  across a black-bronze track; the tap is the same bolt struck.
  Both paths run the one proving cascade — presentation adds
  nothing to the arm but its light (§1.3).

## 2. THE SHIELD

The Shield is the temple register: loadout, routines, the app
list — entries in a kept book. Its phase-1 fault is the one
Wetter named: sections are found by *reading*. Two adopted
organs cure that (§11):

- **The seal stamp [MERGE].** Each section header (LOADOUT /
  ROUTINES / ALL APPS) is preceded by a small vermilion *outline*
  seal — a stroked square, the section stamped, not labelled.
  The eye navigates by marks, confirms by words. The fence is
  absolute: vermilion FILLED stays alert-only (§8); the outline
  is authentication. One glyph + the existing hairlines; view,
  trivial.
- **The frieze of the day [MERGE].** Beneath the routine rows,
  one engraved scale: a brass rule ticked 03–21, each routine's
  span cut in as a filled notch — FASTED / DAY / SLEEP left to
  right. His whole guarded day as a band of inscription. Data
  already exists (routine rows carry start/end); one canvas
  strip ~28dp + read-only state. View only.
- **Loadout selection.** The chosen loadout's cap is deep bronze;
  the offers sit frosted on the sled track. Temple files the
  metal law plainly: *bronze chooses, gold commits* — the
  loadout never takes gold, because gold is the slide's one
  spend and a colour that means two things means neither.
- **App rows.** SHUT is a bronze tablet; OPEN plain words; the
  peek state is a bronze chip. Fremd: "2m" never says what it
  is. [MERGE] the chip prints **PEEK 2M** — same state, spelled.
  The sled's three positions are the whole truth of an app's
  state; nothing else prints.
- **Toggles.** ON/OFF as bronze tablets — a switch you can see
  from the doorway. Kept as rendered.
- **Filter + category tabs.** Ruth's residue: the tab row is dim
  in both states. [MERGE] tabs take the secondary ink tier by
  day (4.84:1 on ground) and the glyph tier by night; the
  selected tab keeps its bronze underline — a mark, not a glow.

## 5. THE RECEIPT — the loved slip, in bronze and stone

The printed-slip character is the proven keeper — his ❤️ on the
batch-7 render is this world's founding evidence — and Temple
does not redesign it; Temple *materials* it. The slip is a
parchment strip (sheet tone) pinned under a brass rule at its
head. Its laws, as filed for the merge:

- **The № is sealed.** The receipt's number is stamped with the
  vermilion *outline* seal beside it — the register's mark that
  this entry was witnessed and entered. Same mark as the Deck's
  proof strip (§1.1): one authentication grammar across the app.
- **Fact rows in primary ink only.** The receipt is a sheet, and
  Nacht's N4 law binds sheets (§10): Temple's secondary tier
  measures 3.49:1 on its sheet — unlawful for body text. Rows
  print in text-1; the one quiet line (the WITHIN window) takes
  the glyph tier (8.24:1 on sheet). Nothing on the slip is set
  in a tier that cannot carry it.
- **Witnessed vs reconstructed is printed, never blended**
  (Hale's receipt law, RC-01). A finish the machine witnessed
  carries the seal. A recovered finish prints its ENDED as what
  it is — the recovery's own instant, labelled — and its seal
  is drawn in *brass outline*, unstruck: entered, not witnessed.
  The slip never lets a reconstruction wear the witness's mark.
- **FLOW slips omit PLANNED** (RC-02): a stopwatch session had
  no plan; the slip prints the lived span and nothing invented.
- **State of the render:** ReceiptSheet reads the colour scheme,
  so the slip already wears Temple's tokens in the lane build;
  its full in-world render (with the seal at the №) is a
  finalist-set debt, owed with the desk clock — named, not

## 9. FEEL — answering Haut (REFINE-haut.md §5), moment by moment

Haut's constitution is adopted verbatim as this world's feel
law: **shape by time, colour by amplitude**; the seal's voice is
bit-identical across worlds and Temple claims *nothing* at it;
worlds speak in the approach. His verdict — Temple the richest
feel-carrier of the six, its grammar coinciding with the
engine's parameter space — is cashed in below, at call-site
prices, with the fraud line drawn where he drew it.

- **The arm (prime):** the seat knock at the floor of its
  range — pitch toward 160–220 Hz, 24–32 ms, full amplitude: a
  bronze bolt dropping into its socket. Lawful at call-site
  parameters; no machinery.
- **Detents (the wheel settling):** sparse and deep — a bead of
  stone in a groove. The majors speak; the fine tier is felt in
  the finger, not chattered in the ear. Same engine detent
  family, thinned by class at the call sites [MERGE, trivial].
- **The latch:** two-stage, as shipped — and the released latch
  lands *heavier* than the engaged: a temple door is unbarred,
  not clicked open. The shipped shapes already lean this way
  (Haut); Temple keeps the lean and names it law.
- **The seal (t=0):** the Throw is the Throw — golden hash,
  single call site, another world's hands off it. Temple's
  consecration at that instant is **light** (§1.3's lamp rake),
  the same division Noir's bloom flare was ruled lawful under:
  worlds may shine at the seal; they may not sing at it. And
  the Throw's own room is already this world's acoustics — the
  synth's two-comb space (1310/1372 samples, −46 dB, ≈900 ms)
  is, literally, a stone room. Temple requests no change to it
  and says why: the machinery was speaking Temple first.
- **The fraud line, confessed in advance:** this spec claims
  **no temple bell**. A long decay through the sound queue is
  impossible — fixed decay constants, a 160 ms buffer cap,
  ~150 ms of audible tail whatever any spec says. Temple's
  resonance is MASS — low pitch, hard knock, the imagined room
  — never duration. Any future Temple text that promises a ring
  is fraud and should be charged as such.
- **The press:** the dip may deepen in *travel* (a key that
  sinks like a stone seal into clay); its 90 ms answer on DOWN
  never moves. A key that answers late is lying about being
  pressed (Haut's line, adopted).
- **Motion:** the slowest house — Curve C and the 520 ms spec
  as defaults; the wheel's ζ=0.38 coast as shipped. Nothing in
  this world hurries, and nothing in it lags: slow is a weight,
  not a delay.
- **The Held Door:** one low knock on arrival; silence and
  instant dismissal on exit. **The finish:** the tear's own
  voice as shipped; the receipt arrives like a slip under a
  door — motion down, once, no flourish. **Idle:** silence.
  Ambient beds are fraud (zero-loop charter + his soundscape
  verdict); this world's atmosphere is carried by colour
  temperature and event sparsity — the smell of cool stone and
  lamp oil, stated as what it lawfully is: the lowest
  fundamentals, the sparsest events, the heaviest knocks of the
  six worlds (Haut §5's cross-sensory read, adopted as spec).
- **Hardware honesty:** amplitude envelopes are best-effort
  across the fleet (the engine never promised otherwise), so
  every Temple shape is timing-led — two stages, sparse knocks,
  heavy-then-heavier — and degrades lawfully on a cheap motor:
  the meaning survives the amplitude's loss.
