## D2 — THE DISC GETS A CHAMFER, NEVER A BORDER (Sana, from Stoff + Uhr)

**Exceeds:** §3.2 (disc = recess tone + full catch hairline, Nacht N1).
**Spec:** the disc's catch treatment becomes an **arc**: a 2dp arc at
**R133**, spanning azimuth **250°→350°** through 315° (the specular
rig's fixed azimuth), tone day **#FAF6F0 at 80%** / night **#4A423C at
60%**. The rest of the circumference carries no line. The full
catch-hairline loop is deleted from the wheel.
**Why:** §1.4 bans border loops — a full loop reads as a resurrected
card. But a machined disc in a dark room is not uniformly edged: light
catches the chamfer where the window is, and the rest of the rim falls
away. A partial arc is how a real turned edge looks; a loop is how a UI
card looks. The arc also agrees with the rig (same 315° light) without
joining it — the arc is static print, the rig is the moving light on
teeth and caps; two crafts, one sun.
**Budget:** static. No motion. (If knurl's gate fails at build and the
rim ships clean, the arc moves to R140 and becomes the rim's only light
answer — see S1.)

## D5 — THE RESERVE, COMPOSED (Sana, from Vau + Raster)

**Exceeds:** §3.1 (readout→wheel gap fixed at S32; reserve = fenced
minimum 48dp).
**Spec:** the readout block and the wheel are composed as one optical
pair. Gap law: readout→wheel gap is **S32** as filed whenever the flex
reserve (the free height between wheel bottom and the task line) is
< 96dp; when flex reserve ≥ 96dp the gap becomes **S48** and the pair
breathes — the extra air goes between the number and the instrument,
never above the number, never into the fence. All other beats exactly
as filed (header 48 · S32 · readout 88 · S8 · NEXT 20 · wheel 288 ·
reserve min 48 · task 20 · S20 · slide 72 · S20 · nav).
**Why:** the filed reserve is a fence around nothing — correct, and
dead. Air in a composition is not the absence of elements; it is the
spacing of the two that matter. On a tall screen the filed Deck
stretches its emptiness at the bottom where nobody looks; the pair law
moves the breath to the one relationship the screen is about: the
number you are making, and the instrument that makes it.
**Budget:** layout only. Both gap values are Raster tokens; the closed
dp list is untouched.
