## 0. The confession — what was still skeleton

Phase-2 Temple was the shared skeleton wearing sandstone. The
arrangement never changed: a header bar, a hero slab, a round
dial floating on flat ground, a task line, a three-box status
strip, a slide, a rail. I changed every token, the geometry,
the metal law — and kept the furniture plan of the dead app.
Ruth's condition 2 was the daylight price of exactly that:
a selection wheel floating on a pale flat ground is the
skeleton's centrepiece, and pale ink on pale ground is what a
flat composition has left to read by. The token cure (§1.4)
treated the symptom in the only currency a flat layout holds.
This document redesigns in the building's own currency:
**relief**. A temple is not arranged; it is *built up* —
steps, courses, a standing stone, a beam. What follows is the
elevation the building generates, element by element: what of
the skeleton dies, what replaces it, and why the replacement
could belong to no other world.

## 1. The generating logic

A temple front, read top to bottom, is: **a lintel** (the beam
that spans and carries), **a standing stone** (the stele, its
face inscribed), **courses** (the wall is read course by
course; each course is one register of fact, separated by the
shadow of its joint), **a frieze** (the band where the day is
carved as spans), **a threshold on steps** (the crepidoma —
you climb to the gate, and the gate's bolt is in the top
step), and **doorways along the base** (the colonnade — the
rooms you may enter). Every Temple surface in this deepening
is one of these parts, doing its architectural job:

- **Selection lives in the wall's fabric**, never on furniture
  placed against it: duration in a ring *incised into the
  stele's face*, block count in a *course of set stones* at
  the stele's base, loadout in a *keystone travelling its
  course* on the Shield wall.
- **The arm is an approach**: the eye climbs the steps to the
  threshold, where the bolt is drawn. Arming a block is
  barring the gate of a building you then inhabit.
- **Facts are inscriptions**: they are cut into courses, in
  order, each course one fact deep. Nothing floats; nothing
  is a card; nothing is a box among boxes.

## 3. The daylight answer — in structure, not only in tokens

Ruth's condition 2, restated honestly: the §1.4 token cure
stands in code (Noir-verified, CURE-VERIFY §2) and its render
is still owed — that debt is not discharged by this document.
But the deepening answers the charge in the building's own
medium, so the next render proves a structure, not a shade:

- **Incision becomes the daylight legibility system.** Every
  mark that must be read in glare — ring ticks at all tiers,
  the set stones' seats, course joints, the frieze's notches —
  is drawn as a *chisel pair*: a 1dp dark edge on the
  shadow side and a 1dp light edge on the lit side (day: dark
  edge = the text ink at the §1.4 depths; light edge = the
  sheet tone). An incised mark encodes *shape*, and shape
  survives any palette lightness; pale-on-pale failed because
  it asked tone difference to do shape's job. A groove in
  stone is legible at glancing light precisely when ink
  washes out — the temple's oldest fact, now the Deck's
  reading system.
- **Shadow becomes a system, not an incident.** Phase 2 had
  one shadow (the slab's). The deepening budgets depth in
  three sizes, everywhere, consistently: the stele casts
  (7dp — a standing stone); a seated stone casts small (2dp —
  a block set into its course); a course joint is a 1dp
  shadow line. Three reliefs, no more — depth is structure,
  not decoration, and it is rationed like the gold.
- **What this costs:** drawing changes at the same sites as
  §1.4 (tick voices, wells, joints) plus the course/steps
  composition — view only, no new machinery, one candidate
  new token (the chisel light edge by night = the raised
  tone; by day it is the existing sheet). The token deepening
  already in code stays as the foundation; the chisel pair
  is the structural layer above it. Night is untouched in
  value: lamplight reads incisions by its rake, which was
  §9's claim from the start.

## 4. THE SHIELD — the wall of inscriptions

The Shield's skeleton form is a scrolling list of section
cards. In the building it is the temple's **outer wall, where
the laws are posted** — read as courses, found by marks:

- **Courses, not cards.** Each register (LOADOUT / ROUTINES /
  ALL APPS) is a course band of the wall, its head closed by
  the vermilion *outline* seal at its left (phase 2 §2's
  stamp, kept — a mason's mark on the course). The eye walks
  marks down the wall; words confirm.
- **The frieze is promoted to the entablature.** Phase 2 put
  the day-frieze *beneath* the routine rows, as an appendix.
  In the wall it sits where a frieze belongs: directly under
  the lintel, the first band the eye meets — the whole
  guarded day carved as spans, before any single routine is
  read. The routine rows hang beneath it as **inscribed
  plaques**: editing a routine (Voss's verbs — machinery,
  untouched) is re-cutting a plaque; in the editor the plaque
  lifts off the wall (raised, shadowed) while its lines are
  re-cut, and seats again when saved.
- **The sled dies; the keystone travels.** Loadout's sled and
  its frosted offers are skeleton switch-furniture — cut.
  The offers are three **stones on the ground course**; the
  chosen stone is *set into the wall* (raised, seated, its
  shadow gap showing). Selection's hand is the **keystone**:
  a bronze capstone that travels the groove cut along the
  course and seats over the chosen inscription — his
  sliding-cap verdict, housed in masonry: the cap still
  slides, the groove is a channel in stone, and what it
  seats over is an inscription, not a chip. *Bronze chooses*
  — the travelling keystone and the set stone are bronze;
  gold never enters this wall (phase 2 §2's metal law).
- **App rows are small gates.** Phase 2's bronze SHUT tablet
  was already a gate leaf without knowing it; the deepening
  names the row a gate in the wall: SHUT = the leaf barred
  (bronze tablet), PEEK = the leaf ajar (the bronze chip,
  spelling the set length — §9.1), OPEN = unbarred (plain
  words). The row's three states are a gate's three states;
  the counts above the wall print the honest split
  (baseline item 4).
- **Toggles are tablets in niches.** A switch is skeleton
  furniture; in the wall, a setting is a small bronze tablet
  seated in its niche — ON seated flush and struck, OFF
  resting loose in the niche. Same affordance, housed.
