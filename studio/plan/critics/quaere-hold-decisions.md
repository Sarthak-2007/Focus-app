## LEDGER — THE PURPOSE QUESTION

*(Addendum for Hale, on Sarthak's reopening: keep rebuilt / demote / kill. The Ledger today = the Vault tab, `CurioVaultScreen`: counter tiles, Streak Tower, 7-day faders, "Verified Session Receipts" list, AES-GCM backup export — plus its echoes: drawer BANKED line, Streak Tower monument overlay, header/dashboard counts.)*

### 1. The one sentence

A record is a claim about the past made to a man deciding about the future. Every other surface's claim is spoken for: the Deck speaks about *now* (how long is left), the Shield speaks about *the rules* (what is shut), the Guard speaks about *the machinery* (what is permitted to run). None of them can say the past tense.

The Ledger's one sentence exists, and I can write it:

> **"You kept your word for N hours this week — the hours you promised yourself are hours that actually happened."**

That is the only fact in the app that is (a) true or false independent of the present moment, and (b) load-bearing for the only future decision that matters — whether the rested man, planning tomorrow at arm time, is planning with a self whose promises mean something. Every plan he makes is priced on that exchange rate. No other surface quotes it.

So the sentence survives. What does not survive is the current Ledger's way of saying it. One fact, one rendering — and the Vault renders that single fact **at least five times**: FOCUSED MIN tile, Tower Bricks (minutes ÷ 25, re-costumed as masonry), 7-day faders (the same minutes, per day), the receipts list (the same minutes, per session), the drawer's "BANKED Nm" line, the Streak Tower monument (the same minutes, again, as an overlay). Five costumes for one number is not emphasis; it is the app insisting — and insistence is what a truth does not need.

Worse, the number itself is not yet true. `totalFocusedMinutes` is floored at 35 minutes in the ViewModel (`.coerceAtLeast(35)`, initial value 75); the Vault's SESSIONS tile is floored at 4 (`coerceAtLeast(4)`); tower bricks are floored at 16 in the Vault and 14 in the monument; each session card carries an "HMAC•…" fragment — cryptographic theatre no human can read, claiming verification it does not explain; and every Airlock intercept is written into the underlying logs with a fabricated cause ("Habit", see the Airlock row above). A Ledger whose founding counts are padded at zero and whose causes are invented fails TELL THE TRUTH before the checking-habit question is even reached. Any form that survives §3 must first be *de-floored*: zero must render as zero.

### 2. The checking-habit charge, at full strength

**The charge.** The three standards exist to remove decisions and friction from his day. A tab whose only verb is LOOK trains a look. The Vault asks for nothing and offers nothing actionable — so every visit is pure checking: the same motor pattern, the same dopamine accounting, as opening the apps the seal exists to shut. It is reachable mid-session *today* (bottom nav, one tap), so the app literally offers a checking surface inside the hold. And the checking is not neutral: a tower and a streak convert the record into an asset to protect, which manufactures a *new* decision the standards never authorised — "arm today or lose the streak?" — asked on sick mornings and family days, priced in guilt. On this account the Ledger contradicts the philosophy that killed the concept in exactly the concept's way: it is a story the app tells about him ("a man with a tower") that he must keep visiting to keep true. Petra's line convicts it: a paragraph, not a gesture.

**The defence.** Witnessing the past is a different species from deciding in the present, and the difference is mechanical, not rhetorical. A decision consumes the resource the seal protects — it demands a comparison, a commitment, a consequence. A *closed* record demands none: it originates no action, offers no fork, and cannot be answered wrong. The receipt at session end is closer to a bank statement arriving than to a notification asking: it closes the session's account so the mind can stop holding it open. There is also a truth argument the charge cannot answer: HOLD without TELL is a seal that could be lying — about elapsed time, about whether the block counted, about the break that silently re-labelled his minutes. Some rendering of the past must exist or the second standard eats the third. And the settled law itself already ruled on this species: *"Ledger KILLED (receipt at session end survives)"* — the distinction between a browsable ledger and a delivered receipt was drawn by Sarthak, not invented by this audit.

**Which side survives.** Both, on different objects — and the objects must not be blurred. The charge **kills the tab**: a persistent, browsable, mid-session-reachable destination whose verb is LOOK is a checking habit with a monument attached, and no purpose argument saves it, because its purpose (the one sentence) does not require a *place* — it requires a *moment*. The defence **saves the witness as an event**: a record that arrives, states, and closes is not a habit, because a habit needs a door he can open at will, and an event has no door. Species distinction, stated plainly: **deciding is a fork; checking is a loop; witnessing, done right, is a full stop.** The current Ledger is a loop wearing a full stop's clothes.

### 3. The form I can defend

Exactly one form survives my audit — the one that originates or closes an action:

**The session-end receipt, and nothing else.**

- **When:** fired by the completion event itself (final block end; also on valve-disarm, stating plainly what was banked before the break — a partial truth, told as partial). Never browsable, never re-openable, never a tab.
- **What:** the one sentence, once, in human language: what was promised, what was kept — minutes banked, blocks completed, reaches stopped — in a single rendering. No tiles, no tower, no faders, no per-session list, no HMAC fragments, no export button. Numbers unfloored: if it was 0, it says 0.
- **Behaviour:** dismiss-only. No choices, no "arm again", no streak to protect, nothing to check later — because there is nowhere to check it. The underlying session rows stay in the database (they are the receipt's evidence and the backup's content), but storage is not a surface.
- **Every other Ledger surface dies:** the Vault tab, the counter tiles, the Streak Tower (screen + monument overlay + drawer chip), the 7-day faders, the receipts list, the drawer's BANKED line, the harmonic-synth / artifact furniture the Vault currently carries. If a weekly truth is ever wanted, it belongs *inside a future receipt* ("…and this week, N hours") as a clause of the sentence — not as a destination.

If Hale's consolidation cannot hold the line at "event, not place" — if the receipt grows a history, a tap-target, a monument — then my position converts to **kill, entire**: a Ledger that can be visited will be visited, and a visited Ledger is the checking habit with better typography.

### My lean

**(b) Demote** — kill the Ledger as a tab and a destination; keep it demoted to the session-end receipt specified in §3, computed from records that remain in storage but are never again rendered as a place. This is, in substance, Sarthak's own earlier ruling ("Ledger KILLED, receipt at session end survives") reached independently from the purpose side: the one sentence in §1 is real and no other surface can say it, but it needs one moment at one boundary, not a room in the app. **The strongest point against my lean:** the receipt is still a look, delivered at the single most depleted moment of his cycle — the end of a block, when a bad number ("23 minutes banked") lands as a verdict he cannot act on until tomorrow, and a good number can license coasting; the purist kill position can honestly say that TELL THE TRUTH is satisfied by the seal's in-the-moment honesty (the ratchet refusals, the Airlock's intention line, the break declared as a break) and that the past, once held, needs no spokesman at all. I hold (b) anyway — barely — because a hold that never reports back leaves him pricing tomorrow's promises on memory, and his memory at 8 PM is the least reliable instrument he owns.
