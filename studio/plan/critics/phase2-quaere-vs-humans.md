### Moench (`moench-ledger.md`) — **DEMOTE UPHELD IN DIRECTION; THREE CLAUSES STRUCK, ONE GAP HE MUST ANSWER**

Converges with the verdict on: tab dies, tower/streak/faders/charts die,
receipt as ephemeral event, storage silent on disk, absence never rendered
as a number. His one-fact-one-rendering inventory is the strictest in the
Humans plan and the merge should steal its denomination discipline
(`1H 40M`, never raw minutes past 59; words for the hold, never a tally).

- **Struck 1 — the declaration machinery (§2).** His absence rule requires
  *him* to declare REST / ILL / FAMILY / AWAY by "an explicit act", with
  controls, a next-morning grace window, and edit-locking at DAY CLOSED.
  That is a check-in system: a decision the sick man owes the record,
  priced in his own file at "one tap" as if a tap at 03:30 with a fever
  were free. It also makes the declared word *buy* something — declared
  days exempt routines from the miss counter — which is V5's corruption
  in monastic dress. **Ruling:** absence is *inferred* (no attended
  session = the word prints where a word must print, or nothing prints);
  a name he later attaches changes the word and nothing else. This also
  answers the question in his letter to me (`ledger-court`): **DAY CLOSED
  is a rendering, not an action's home** — nothing originates or closes
  there that the last session's receipt doesn't already close, and the
  *day* is YPT's transaction, not Monolith's. His day total therefore has
  no independent home; it survives, if at all, as a clause inside the
  final receipt ("…and today, 9H 42M sealed") — a clause, not a ceremony.
  By his own purpose test, the standalone DAY CLOSED fails, and his day
  total folds or dies. He half-knew: it is the crack he mailed me about.
- **Struck 2 — the hero's denomination (§1 row 4).** "`9H 42M OF 12H`" is
  Monolith's sealed minutes wearing YPT's denominator. The 12-hour day is
  the *study* verdict; Monolith cannot state what fraction of it it
  sealed, because unsealed study exists (tests, unsealed blocks) and the
  fraction would be false. Sealed time prints as sealed time, labelled,
  or the file re-opens the two-totals-for-one-day lie Selbst flagged in
  the chrome. **Ruling:** no `/12H` on any Monolith rendering, ever.
- **Struck 3 — the auto-pause sentence (§1 row 9).** "`PAUSED AFTER 3
  MISSES`" endorses the machine editing his routine. Settled law
  (CRITICS-PLAN C3): the lawful form is the printed fact (`MISSED ×3`)
  and his hand. A pause he didn't perform, however honestly worded, is a
  decision taken at his tiredest by a counter.

  weekly line is missing entire: Moench renders routine facts as one word
  for the *most recent firing* only (`KEPT` / `MISSED` on the Shield row)
