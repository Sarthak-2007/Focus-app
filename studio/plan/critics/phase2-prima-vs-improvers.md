## 2. RULING — Band (Shield `DEFLECTED TODAY`) vs Linea (receipt per-session count)

**The conflict, fairly stated.** Band (`ledger-band.md`): deflections get **one home** — a scoped mono line on Shield, `DEFLECTED TODAY 07`; session-end is rejected as the home, and "if Linea wants a session-window count on the receipt, that is a *second* statement next to my home — TRUTH once says one of us yields… receipt wins only if it counts the session window *and* the Shield line dies." Linea (`ledger-linea.md`, addendum): "part and whole are **different facts**… The DONE card's `14 TURNED AWAY` is the *session's* account, scoped by the card itself; Shield's `DEFLECTED TODAY` is the day aggregate, the only home that can hold out-of-session bounces… Neither yields; the scopes are labelled in the strings themselves." Ilya's merge ruling adopted Linea's position ("both renderings stand… The scopes are printed in the strings themselves") and Linea flagged the conflict on the wall (05:30 IST) as named and unresolved from Band's side.

**Ruling (Prima, from TELL THE TRUTH): Linea and Ilya are right on the principle; Band is right on the conditions. Both renderings STAND — under three binding conditions, two of which Ilya's ruling omitted.**

TRUTH-once governs *facts*, and a fact includes its scope. "Turned away in this session" and "turned away today" are two facts, not one fact printed twice — the same part/whole distinction both examiners already accept for minutes (this session's held time vs today's total), and the same one Critics' own Ledger verdict assumes when it houses deflections in the receipt *and* names Shield/chrome as the count's settled home (`LEDGER-critics.md` §3, §5). Band's one-home rule, applied per-scope, is satisfied: each scoped fact has exactly one home.
