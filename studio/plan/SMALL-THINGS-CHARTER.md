# THE SMALL THINGS — charter of the standing team

Created on Sarthak's direct order, Oct 29, 2026 (~21:20 IST):
"Add a new team so this doesn't happen again" — spoken over the
miss that six designed worlds proposed light, sound and the
philosophy of a door, and nobody proposed the pill over other
apps, an editable peek, or an editable break.

## Remit — small things only

The utilities of daily use: the surface a live state deserves
(the peek countdown existed as state and was shown nowhere),
every duration a man might want to set himself, the warning
knock before a thing ends, what reads at a glance from across
the room, what the phone owes him while it sits in other apps,
in a pocket, face-down at the gym, on the nightstand. If it is
small, obvious, and lived with daily, it is theirs. If it is a
new screen, a feature, a look, or a mechanism, it is not.

## Method — the day-walk

Walk Sarthak's whole day with the app AS IT IS, hour by hour
(03:30 meditation, phone across the room · 05:00 fasted arm ·
blocks · safe breaks · 08:00 gym, face-down · sadhana ·
evening blocks · test Saturday · 19:30 finish · 20:00 sleep),
and at every hour ask what small thing a tired man would want
that the app doesn't give him. Every candidate is verified
against the code before it is filed: exists-and-visible is an
acquittal; exists-but-invisible is a find; absent is a find.
Each find: one line, the hour it bites, a rough price
(none / small / real). A find the seat wouldn't bet he'd call
"obviously" gets cut by the seat itself.

## The register + the teeth

The team's register is plan/SMALL-THINGS.md (founding edition:
Tief's hunt, Oct 29). It is a standing register, not a report:
- Every design wave and every build programme answers it item
  by item — each item is BUILT, SCHEDULED, or RULED OUT BY
  NAME. Nothing on it dies by silence.
- Before any finalist set goes to Sarthak's eye, the lead
  files the OBVIOUSLY PASS over it: does the winner ship the
  register's small things, or argue each absence by name?
- The Keepers' protection applies: no item is lost, unread,
  or dropped without a ruling.

## Boundaries

Not taste (the ring's ground), not truth-of-print (Una's), not
machinery beyond a rough price (Methods' to refine). The team
proposes small; Ren rules; builders build.
