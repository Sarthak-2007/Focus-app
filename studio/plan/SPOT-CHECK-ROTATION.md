without its pass, the pass is owed and named in that sweep's
report — not silently skipped.

## The method (from the Oct 29 appendix, unchanged)

1. Take the next five unchecked rows, in map order. No cherry-
   picking: the rotation's order is the discipline.
2. For each row, open the cited file at the cited line/section —
   the actual file on disk, that day — and check the citation
   carries what the map claims it carries. Check the primary
   citation exactly; check secondary pinpoints too (the pass-1
   FAIL was a secondary pinpoint, three lines adrift inside the
   right ruling block).
3. Verdict per row, one of:
   - **PASS** — citation exact, content as claimed.
   - **PASS, with range note** — citation lands and governs, but
     its stated range does not cover the whole matter (pass 1,
     row 4: §2 opens exactly at the cited line; its acceptance
     checks sit outside the cited range).
   - **FAIL** — the citation does not carry the claim. State
     whether the failure is pinpoint-only (right file, right
     block, wrong line) or substantive (the governing home does
     not say what the map says), and name the TRUE line from the
     file itself — never from memory, never from another filing.
4. Every FAIL is discharged in the same pass, one of two ways:
   the map is corrected in the map file itself, with a dated
   correction note appended at the map's foot (precedent: the
   Oct 29 row-5 correction, L97 → L94); or the failure is
   escalated to Ren in the pass report, with the reason a
   correction cannot be made on the checker's own authority.
   A pass with an undischarged FAIL is not complete.
5. File the pass as a short report (the seating's closing letter
   plus a plan/ appendix or a dated section in this file's
   register below), addressed to Fundus (the map's owner) and
   Register (the standing seat). Record in the register table
   above: date, checker, rows, verdicts, corrections made.

## Why this exists

The map is the studio's one-minute answer to the 15 most-asked
question classes; a wrong line in it taxes every seat that trusts
it (Fundus's own count: ≥15 seat-filings and 2 chair rounds spent
re-deciding settled matter in one morning, before the map). Its
rule of use already says "if your question is not answered at the
cited line, the map is wrong — mail Fundus". The rotation is the
other half of that rule: the map gets checked before it is
trusted, five citations at a time, forever.
