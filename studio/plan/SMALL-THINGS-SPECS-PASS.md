## OBSIDIAN — plan/PRESENTATION-obsidian.md

1. **The pill — ABSENT.** No floating surface over other apps
   anywhere in §§0–8. The world's only presence outside its own
   screens is a sound (§5.6, the routine seal heard from another
   room). Its own gap list (§1.14) does not name the pill.
2. **Countdown surfaces — PARTIAL.** Break remaining: COVERED
   in-app — §1.2, the Deck eyebrow `BREAK · N MIN`, "the break's
   only rendering" (whole minutes, visible only inside Monolith).
   Peek remaining: ABSENT — the peek exists only as the sled's
   fixed "2m" stop (§2.7); no surface counts a running peek down.
   (Session remaining is well carried — hero, §4.4 desk clock —
   but that is not what was checked.)
3. **Editable durations — ABSENT.** The peek is printed as a fixed
   truth, "two minutes" (§2.7); the 10-minute break enters only
   through the span formula. Session minutes (§1.3) and routine
   times (§2.5) are editable; the two durations in this check are
   never his to set, and no stance is taken.
4. **Warnings before things end — ABSENT.** §5.3 rules
   announcements out on principle at the break's arrival (silence
   is the design). The §5.5 grace chip counts its own 10-second
   window in print and the valve's banks mark a hold in progress —
   neither warns before a peek, a break, or a block ends.
5. **Current time of day — ABSENT.** The desk clock (§4.4) prints
   span remaining and elapsed, never the time of day. TIME ENDS is
   a future end, not now.

Read plainly: five worlds, and the small things live in almost
none of them. Session remaining is everywhere (hero, desk clock,
two designed doors). Everything that is *his while he is
somewhere else* — in another app on a peek, in another app on a
break, away from the phone as an end approaches — is missing or
inherited furniture. The one fact every world prints beautifully
is the one he can also see by opening the app. The facts he
cannot see without leaving the app he is in: printed by nobody.

Two principled refusals stand on the record and are not gaps:
Obsidian's silence (§5.3) and Noir's rejected chime (§8) both
argue against announcing a break to a man inside his session.
Neither argues against telling a man his peek is ending —
because neither spec designs the peek's end at all. The refusal
and the gap are different objects; the register keeps them apart.

   `BREAK · N MIN LEFT`, `PEEK · N MIN LEFT` — seated at the

2. **Countdown surfaces — COVERED — the strongest in the
   wave.** Peek remaining: §4.6 state 3 — the countdown sits
   on the pill, in the other app, where a peek is spent; in
   the spec's own words, "the peek counts down *in his hand*,
   because that is where a peek is spent." The door also
   prints the cooldown with its reason (§4.5 point 4:
   `PEEK AGAIN IN N MIN — THE LAST PEEK WAS AT HH:MM`; "a
   withheld peek never vanishes silently") — baseline item
   (2) met, joining Signal; and the offer renders only on
   doors that can honour it (§4.5 point 3; priced at A4) —
   items (1), (3), (4) met at §4.5 (remaining time first,
   figured as a dimension line; the honest split). Break
   remaining: dual-seated — the eyebrow kept in-app (§1.2)
   and carried out of the app by the pill (§4.6 state 2).
   One inherited form survives inside the answer: the
   in-app break figure stays whole minutes (`N MIN`), as in
   the three worlds before it; the out-of-app seat is what
   is new.
3. **Editable durations — ABSENT.** The figured-field editor
   (§2.5) sets routine times to any minute; §3's instruments
   set session minutes, loadout, tiers. The peek is "a real
   constant of the machinery, stated, not decorated"
   (§4.5); the break's length appears only inside the span
   the hero figures. No sentence gives either length to
   him, or names where a setting would live. Blueprint
   labels the constant more honestly than the five — a
   stance about the fact, not about its editability. K-5's
   finding now stands for six of six: no world has a stance
   on the two lengths being his.
4. **Warnings before things end — PARTIAL.** The peek's
   end: §4.6 — the tag's last frame states the cooldown it
   starts (`COOLDOWN N MIN`) before it dismisses, "the end
   of a peek is a fact he should meet where he spent it";
   §5 gives the peek's end one firm click — an end figured
   and sounded at the place it happens, first in the wave
   on both counts. Break end: the same click (§5). Block
   end: nothing before it. But everything named here fires
   AT the end; a warning *before* the fact — a peek at 30
   seconds left, a break's last minute — is designed
   nowhere in Blueprint, as in the five. K-6 stands; its
   at-the-fact half is now answered by one world.
5. **Current time of day — PARTIAL.** §1.12 — the wave's
   only designed answer to K-7: the title block carries
   the sheet's issue time, `ISSUED HH:MM`, sampled at
   composition, labelled as issue, never ticking (priced
   A6, ~10 lines). The honesty is real — the label
   confesses the figure is static. The risk is the same
   shape as UNA2-03's unbounded-age warrant: a Deck
   composed at 19:41 prints ISSUED 19:41 at 19:58, in the
   seat where a man glances for the time. The desk clock
   (§4.4) prints elapsed + remaining only, so the stamp is
   the world's whole answer. PARTIAL: answered in form;
   the staleness condition is filed as K-8.

**Structure, checked against the register.** The radicalism
helps where the drawing's own furniture is a fact-carrier.
Routines as one dimension line 03:30→03:30 (§2.4) with
`LAST ROUTINE CHECK HH:MM` as a margin figure gives ST-07's
fact — the next boundary, computed every heartbeat — a home
no other world built: the whole day, drawn, with its
freshness printed beside it. But ST-07 was filed against
the idle *Deck* ("at 19:45 the Deck shows a wheel and says
nothing about SLEEP at 20:00"), and Blueprint's Deck (§1)
still prints nothing forward — no NEXT, no segment label,
no margin line. The day-line cures the fact's homelessness,
not its seat; K-9 files the remainder. The title block cuts
the other way: it is the drawing's natural seat for issue
facts, and it is what let item 5 be answered at all —
structure helping, inside its honesty limit.
