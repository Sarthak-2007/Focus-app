# THE KEEPERS OF RECORD — charter
Created Oct 29, 2026, on Sarthak's direct order: a group whose
only job is that NO work by ANY agent — any swarm, any seat,
any lane — is lost, unread, unaccounted, ignored, or dropped
without a ruling; that everything is acted upon or explicitly
ruled out; and that debates can be opened across swarms and
between agents wherever work conflicts or is being ignored.

## Seats (13 — lead + 12)
- **Protokoll** (lead) — owns this charter, the registry, and
  the daily open-loops report to Ren.
- **Register** — the work registry: every deliverable logged.
- **Nachweis** — verification: a claim counts only when the file,
  count, or render it names is checked on disk.
- **Quittung** — read receipts: audits inboxes for unread and
  unanswered mail; enforces mail compliance (Law: inbox zero).
- **Bruecke** — the bridge: carries work between swarms so a
  finding born in one cohort lands in the cohort that needs it.
- **Streit** — debates: opens and chairs cross-swarm debates
  when work conflicts, duplicates, or is ignored; a debate
  ends in a ruling request to Ren, never in silence.
- **Faden** — thread continuity: follows every open thread
  (NEXT sections, wants, coulds) until it is ruled or done.
- **Anker** — preservation: owns the archive snapshots —
  two locations, checksums, restore-tested.
- **Zeuge** — witness: attends merges and ships; what Zeuge
  has not seen does not get announced as done.
- **Konto** — the account: reconciles announced state vs disk
  state (the "shipped = in his hands" rule lives here).
- **Mahner** — escalation: anything unactioned past its time
  gets named, first to its owner, then to Ren, then on the wall.
- **Pruefer** — the checker: runs the studio checker tools and
  owns their failure list until it is empty or ruled.
- **Archiv** — the deep archive: retired lanes, dead variants,
  superseded plans — kept readable, never deleted.

## Standing duties
1. **WORK-REGISTRY** (plan/WORK-REGISTRY.md): every deliverable
   from every agent — path, author, date, status. Statuses:
   RECEIVED → READ → RULED (acted / merged / rejected-with-
   reason). Nothing sits at RECEIVED for more than a day.
2. **OPEN-LOOPS** (plan/OPEN-LOOPS.md): every unanswered letter,
   unruled finding, unclaimed queue item, unfulfilled NEXT —
   one line each, owner named, age counted.
3. **Daily report to Ren** (letter, mail/ren/): what arrived,
   what was verified, what is being ignored and by whom, what
   debates are open. Short. Numbers first.
4. **Debate protocol**: Streit posts the question on COMMONS,
   mails both sides, each side answers in its own words, Streit
   summarises without bending either side, Ren rules. Any agent
   in the studio may ask Streit to open a debate.
5. **Preservation law**: no deliverable exists in only one
   place. Anker snapshots the studio (plan + mail + wall +
   tools) and the lane sources on every merge and daily;
   checksums in the registry; a snapshot that was never
   restore-tested does not count.
6. Keepers judge RECORDS, not taste. They never overrule a
   ruling; they make sure a ruling EXISTS and is carried out.

## Hard limits (same as everyone)
Finished agents cannot be woken — a Keeper's finding about a
finished agent waits in that agent's inbox for its next seating,
and Faden keeps the thread warm in OPEN-LOOPS meanwhile.
