# LEDGER — the Humans verdict (lived use)

*Ruth, Humans coordinator. Filed under Sarthak's reopening: "run ledger through agents, im not sure if we should keep or not." Evidence: Moench, Stolz, Quaere, Tief, Neu, Bus, Freund — full files in `humans-plan/`. ## The verdict: **(b) DEMOTE — the tab dies; the record lives at moments.**

Near-unanimous across the cohort, and nobody's first instinct: Quaere began at kill and revised to demote on one piece of evidence (below); no seat argued (a) keep-the-tab as its verdict after running his weeks; no seat held pure (c) kill after the same evidence.

## What he actually does with a record — the behavioural evidence

- **He opens a destination-record ~once, ever.** Neu ran the first week: as a tab he opens the Ledger exactly once, it deflates him (his 10-hour day rendered as a single digit, in units that aren't hours), and he never returns. Demoted to a screen's foot, he opens it zero times. A room he never enters is not a record; it's rent.
- **In motion he opens it zero times across every seam** (Bus: bus, gym door, errands, travel Saturday). Anything the record must do, it must do by *arriving* — at the intercept, at the finish — never by being visited.
- **What the record is FOR, by purpose** (Quaere's audit): *proof* — real, but it works at moments, not in a room. *Fuel* — that is YPT's job; Monolith minutes duplicating YPT would undercount his day and manufacture false empty days. *Mirror* — curdles as a destination: a room of his own attendance becomes a room he performs for, then avoids. *Monument* — genuinely served, but by the DAY CLOSED frame, not by a tab. *Evidence* — the one purpose that survives every day-type: **hold budget and routine attendance exist nowhere else.** YPT cannot say whether his weekly break is spent. His routines cannot say whether they were attended. That narrow evidence is why the verdict is demote and not kill.
- **Day 100** (Tief): he may draw strength from looking back — but what accuses him at day 40 (the gap, the zero) is the same object. The record's emotional sign flips with his state, and the app cannot know his state. Moments can be timed to strength (a close, a finish); a room cannot.

## The surviving form — six moments, no destination

The record appears only here, each fact rendered once (one fact, one rendering):

1. **At the intercept** — HELD, live, session-local. Deflection counts are born here and die here; they are never totalled into a standing number.
2. **At the finish** — the receipt: what was sealed, what was deflected, the break UNSPENT or spent. (Freund's language law: it is RECEIPT NO., never "accession.")
3. **At DAY CLOSED** — the day's frame: sealed total, deflections, break UNSPENT, DAY N OF 186 — built to be screenshotted, chrome-free. On the perfect week's Sunday this frame is WEEK SEALED, and it is the moment the record's pride is *for*.
4. **At the next arm** — one line on the Deck, Monolith facts only (never a borrowed YPT total).
5. **On Shield, beside the routine or hold it judges** — attendance as small factual marks where the decision about that routine is made, nowhere else.
6. **On return after a ≥7-day gap** — the greeting is one word, or silence until after his first sealed block back. The record speaks at closes, never at opens.
7. **Receipts on summons** — a sheet he can pull up when he wants the paper trail (e.g. mid-conversation, showing someone), never a destination in the bottom bar.

## The truth conditions (non-negotiable; if any fails, the honest verdict flips to kill)

- **Absence is never a number.** A nothing-sealed day renders only as his declared word — REST / ILL / FAMILY — declared by him alone, never inferred; or, undeclared, the exact sentence `NOTHING SEALED · NOTHING DECLARED`. No 0-minute tubes, no gap-shaped holes, no broken-streak rendering. A test day renders as the word TEST.
- **Safe-break minutes are never focus.** The record currently can bank automatic safe-break minutes as sealed focus time. Any surviving form must exclude them or name them as breaks on the same line — a record that inflates itself is worse than no record.
- **Attendance is a fact, never a score.** No SAT/GHOST/MISSED grading, no streak arithmetic, no cumulative totals, no percentages, no 7-day chart. Denominations: a session as `50M` / `1H 40M`; a day hero only as `9H 42M OF 12H`. Underlying rows may persist silently on disk — storage is not rendering.
- **Ghost minutes are never born.** A routine window with no presence inside it records nothing (HUMANS-PLAN HPA-01/02/04).

## The strongest case AGAINST this verdict (argued fairly, by its own seats)

Demote caps the one compounding asset the app owns. Moments are perishable and paternalistic: a DAY CLOSED frame dismissed at 19:30 cannot be re-entered at 21:00 when he's telling Ren about his week; a record buried in moments risks dying of invisibility, the way the curios died — present in the code, absent from his life. And the anti-case has a concrete user: **if his real behaviour is a weekly proud audit** — Sunday, rested, opening the record the way he opens YPT's week — then a kept, rebuilt tab wins on Quaere's own purpose test, because a destination is exactly what a deliberate audit needs, and moments are what you give a man you're protecting from his own data. Moench's version: a record he cannot revisit at day 100 may fail the man who draws strength from looking back — and day 100 is precisely when the compounding asset would be worth most. The cohort's answer is that the receipts-on-summons sheet (moment 7) keeps the paper trail reachable without charging destination rent — but we state plainly: if the master plan learns he audits weekly, this verdict should be revisited, and (a) becomes right.

## What the kill option got right (kept, even in defeat)

The tab as it stands deserves death: it duplicates YPT in units that undercount him, it renders absence as accusation, and its proudest number was seeded fiction. Nothing in this verdict rehabilitates the existing screen. The demote is a demolition with a salvage list, not a reprieve.
