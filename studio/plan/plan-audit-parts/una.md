Target: `plan/MASTER-PLAN.md` (5,229 lines) read as ONE document.
Cross-checked against `plan/REGISTER.md` and `plan/LIE-REGISTER-2.md`
only where a charge touches them. Every charge carries both passages,
file + line. Where the plan itself flags a pair as HELD/ratification,
the flag is recorded — a flag does not make the two texts buildable
together, and §§ that carry no flag are charged as unflagged.

contradiction that will cost a build or a ruling · **S3** friction.
