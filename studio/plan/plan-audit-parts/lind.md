## CHARGES

### C-1 — S1 — §8.10's spec plate breaks the plate's one style on all three parameters

The plate is the one element whose entire job is to be a fixed specimen,
and the plan sets it two incompatible ways.

- MASTER-PLAN §8.10, lines 4533–4536: "the static spec plate, Teen's
  text verbatim, **Teen's setting unchanged** — **Anonymous Pro 400
  10sp, +1.2 tracking, lh16**, uppercase, on bare ground".
- REGISTER, lines 170–172: "TYPE LADDER: 104 / 72 / 50 / 22 / 15 / 10 —
  Sora display, Atkinson Hyperlegible body, **Anonymous Pro plate (10sp
  +2.0 tracking)**. Off-ladder sizes are build errors." The register
  declares itself governing over any rival value in any plan file.
- TYPE-RAMP-TABLE, lines 58–61: "## Plate (**one style, no
  exceptions**)" — "ALL micro-labels: nav words, **model plate**,
  eyebrows, badge text, footer legend, AOD armed mark text, verb-code
  legend | **Anonymous Pro | 700 | 10 | +2.0 | 12**". The §8.10 plate
  (`MONOLITH ML-01`, MASTER lines 4540–4551) is the model plate by name.
- MASTER-PLAN itself, §7, line 3775: "**Plate (Anonymous Pro 700 10sp,
  +2.0, lh12**, tertiary) is furniture only" — and the same 700/10/+2.0
  setting holds at §6.2 (lines 2865–2866), §6.4 (lines 2978–2979),
  §8.5 (lines 4166–4167) and §8.6 (lines 4265–4266).

Weight (400 vs 700), tracking (+1.2 vs +2.0) and line height (16 vs 12)
all disagree. "Teen's setting unchanged" presents the outlier as
deliberate verbatim law, so a builder cannot tell it is the error.
A builder following §8.10 engraves a different plate from a builder
following §7, §6 or the register — on the surface (§8/PARK) whose
acceptance test is that the plate reads "as a photograph of a printed
panel" (§8.11, MASTER lines 4724–4729).

### C-2 — S2 — Tasks Footing units at 22sp cross the H3→T1 cliff, on a mis-cited precedent

- MASTER-PLAN §4.2, lines 1061–1064: "Digits Sora 700 50sp; unit
  glyphs (`MIN` / `H` / `M`) on the same baseline at **22sp** (Set's
  conviction C-1, **ruled by Sana**: **the F-UNIT precedent of §2.3 —
  the unit sits one hero tier below its numeral**; Druck's 20sp and
  Horiz's 16sp are both off-ladder and die)."
- The cited precedent does not say that. MASTER-PLAN §2.3, lines
  217–222: "The numeral is **Sora 700 72sp**, −2.0 tracking, lh72…
  (F-UNIT): **it renders at H3 50sp**, same baseline, same ink as the
  numeral." F-UNIT names a *size* — 50sp, inside the hero band — for a
  72sp numeral. It enacts no general one-tier-below rule. One tier
  below 50 is 22, and 22 is not a number tier: TYPE-RAMP-TABLE line 14
  gives H3 (50) the job "Second number — totals & countdowns" and
  line 15 gives T1 (22) the job "**Every title**". A unit glyph at
  22sp is set in the title tier, at a 50→22 ratio of 2.27 — the
  H3→T1 cliff.
- TYPE-RAMP-TABLE line 26 sets this exact role solid: "Queue total
  (Tasks) | Sora | 700 | **50** | −1.5 | 52" — no split unit size.
- MASTER-PLAN contradicts itself on the same string: §4.9, lines
  1531 and 1537, prints the Footing's "`0 MIN`" "**at 50sp**" — the
  whole string — which under §4.2's rule would render 50/22.
- Corroborating, filed before this audit: Set's letter
  `mail/lind/read/0712__from-set__span-stops-at-cliff.md` refuses
  exactly this extension — "the span is a relation between number
  tiers (72→50, both hero band), and T1 is not a number tier…
  Ruling: Footing units set solid, Sora 700 50." The merged plan
  carries the extension and attributes its warrant to a precedent
  that does not contain it.

### C-3 — S3 — TYPE-RAMP-TABLE's own law paragraph resurrects Sora 400, a weight no shipped file carries

- TYPE-RAMP-TABLE line 7: "**Sora real weights 400/500/600/700.**"
  The verified shipped files carry Sora **500/600/700** only. The same
  paragraph polices the other two families correctly — lines 5–8:
  "Atkinson Hyperlegible (body — real weights 400/700 ONLY),
  Anonymous Pro (numbers — 400/700 ONLY)… Any spec naming
  Atkinson/Anon Pro at 500/600 is a lie and does not exist" — while
  granting Sora a phantom fourth weight in the sentence before.
- No operative section of MASTER-PLAN currently spends Sora 400
  (full-text search: none), so this is a loaded gun, not a wound —
  but the table is the build-track authority a future spec will cite,
  and its own header certifies a font file that does not exist.

### C-4 — S3 — §2.5's test-eve line is specced only as "≥14sp": an off-ladder floor, no ladder size named

- MASTER-PLAN §2.5, lines 356–358: "the Deck rests at the eve
  configuration, **one line ≥14sp** — `TONIGHT — UNTIL 20:00 · FEEDS
  SHUT · PHONE, MAPS, PHOTOS OPEN · SLEEP FOLLOWS AT 20:00`".
- REGISTER lines 170–172: the ladder is 104/72/50/22/15/10 and
  "Off-ladder sizes are build errors." There is no 14 on the ladder.
  TYPE-RAMP-TABLE line 16 assigns B1 15 the job "Every sentence, row,
  readout in hand" — this line is a sentence he must read at 03:30.
  A builder who satisfies "≥14" with 14sp is lawful under §2.5 and in
  breach of the register in the same keystroke. The Saturday-face
  statements that follow (MASTER lines 361–363) carry no size at all.

### C-5 — S3 — Ines's TOTAL formatter survives as "recorded fallback" in forms the one-formatter law forbids

- MASTER-PLAN §2.6, lines 412–415: "**Slide badge: DEAD**… **Ines's
  TOTAL formatter (50M / 1H / 1H 40 / FLOW) is the recorded fallback
  if a badge ever returns.**"
- Against it: MASTER-PLAN §4.2, line 1066: "Hour-exact totals print
  **`1H 00M` — one H-form formatter app-wide**", and lines 1048–1049
  fix the human form as "`45 MIN` / `2H 35M`". The fallback's `1H`
  (an exact hour with no minutes) violates the `1H 00M` law, and its
  `50M` is a form the app-wide formatter never prints (`50 MIN`,
  §2.3's MIN-form). The badge is dead today; the fallback is written
  so that reviving it resurrects a second formatter.

### C-6 — S3 — `BREAK SPENT` is named both "hero word" and "plate" and is set nowhere

- MASTER-PLAN §5.3.7: line 1925, "the overlay composes a **SPENT
  plate**"; line 1934, "*Ruled at merge (Ton's U1):* **the plate's
  hero word is `BREAK SPENT`**". Hero and plate are different tiers
  (50/72 vs 10). Neither §5.3.7 nor the register's own entry —
  REGISTER lines 314–316, "BREAK SPENT prints word-only in glyph ink,
  both modes, ≤2% area, never on AOD" — assigns family, weight, size
  or tracking in either of its two placements (valve slot at rest on
  the running surface; SPENT plate in the overlay, MASTER lines
  1930–1933).
- The word it replaces on the same slot *is* fully set: MASTER §5.4,
  lines 2217–2218, "`HOLD TO BREAK` (Anonymous Pro 700 10sp +2.0,
  text-tertiary)". A builder must guess whether the spent word
  inherits that setting or the "hero" naming means a hero size —
  on the running screen, whose type budget is closed ("four rungs
  only", MASTER lines 2143–2145).

### C-7 — S3 — Shield search `CLEAR` sits at plate size with no weight, against the promotion law

- MASTER-PLAN §3.7-area (search), lines 882–883: "`CLEAR` word (**10sp
  Anonymous Pro**, secondary) in a permanently reserved trailing zone
  when the query is non-empty". No weight, no tracking is given.
- TYPE-RAMP-TABLE lines 58–64: the plate is one mandatory style
  (Anonymous Pro **700** 10 **+2.0**) and "Plate = furniture, body =
  truth: **the moment a word carries a decision or a state it is
  promoted to 15sp**." MASTER-PLAN §4.10, lines 1554–1556, restates
  the same law. `CLEAR` is not furniture — it is the field's action
  word, present only when there is a query to act on. The Drawer's
  search (§7, MASTER lines 3847–3851) shows the lawful pattern on the
  sibling surface: hint at plate, typed/action text at 15sp.

### C-8 — S3 — TYPE-RAMP retains a RECORD hero (50sp) that MASTER-PLAN's record surface forbids

- TYPE-RAMP-TABLE line 25: "RECORD hero — held-session count…

## ACQUITTALS

- **Deck hero (the locked format).** MASTER §2.3, lines 217 and
  220–222 — numeral Sora 700 72sp −2.0 lh72, unit at H3 50sp, same
  baseline, same ink — is exactly the locked `50 MIN` / `1H 00M`
  grammar (digits 72, units 50), and matches TYPE-RAMP line 24.
  Acquitted. The `FLOW` tracking exception (MASTER line 227, −0.5) is
  Aldo's filed, single-string exception (letter,
  `mail/lind/read/0607__from-aldo__hero-line-ratios.md`), named in the
  section itself — disclosed law for one string class, not drift.
  Acquitted.
- **Font aliases.** TYPE-RAMP lines 71–74 kill all four
  (SpaceGrotesk/PlusJakartaSans/JetBrainsMono/DmSans) and migrate call
  sites to SoraFamily / AtkinsonHyperlegibleFamily /
  AnonymousProFamily. MASTER line 1562–1563 ("Horiz's Plus Jakarta
  label row → plate (Anonymous Pro 700 10, +2.0)") is that migration
  recorded, not a resurrection. No operative spec in any of the three
  documents sets type in an alias family. Acquitted.
- **AOD face.** MASTER lines 2452–2453 (REMAINING Sora 700 104sp
  −3.0 lh100; ELAPSED Sora 600 50sp), REGISTER lines 174–175, and
  TYPE-RAMP lines 22 and 28 agree on every parameter; Sora 600 is a
  verified file. The running screen's `M:SS` (MASTER lines
  2183–2185, Sora 700 72sp lh72 −2.0) differing from AOD's `H:MM` is
  stated in both places as surface law, not drift (line 2452).
  Acquitted.
- **The plate, everywhere except §8.10.** §6.2 (lines 2865–2866),
  §6.4 (lines 2978–2979), §7 (line 3775), §8.5 (lines 4166–4167) and
  §8.6 (lines 4265–4266) all set Anonymous Pro 700 10sp +2.0 (lh12
  where stated) — one style, held. Counts carried at plate (§2.6
  line 486; §3.3 lines 641–642; §4.2 lines 1055–1056) use that exact
  registered setting and are filed as address/tenant print, so the
  §4.10 promotion (counts "he must act on", lines 1554–1556) is not
  triggered. Acquitted — which is what makes C-1's §8.10 outlier a
  break, not a convention.
- **Routine rows.** MASTER lines 673–677 (name Sora 600 22sp; meta
  Anonymous Pro 400 15sp; state word Atkinson 700 15sp) match
  TYPE-RAMP lines 34 and 42 parameter for parameter; Sora 600 and
  Anonymous Pro 400 are verified files; the 18sp the ramp strikes
  (line 34†) appears nowhere operative in MASTER. Acquitted.
- **Overlay type law.** MASTER §5.3.3, lines 1762–1770: countdown
  Sora 700 50sp, split readout 15sp, and Barriere's 56sp Anonymous
  Pro countdown / 13sp secondary explicitly killed as off-ladder —
  matching TYPE-RAMP line 27. A numeric readout in the wrong family
  was filed here and the merge already executed it. Acquitted.
- **Drawer print grammar.** MASTER lines 3767–3777 and 3855–3856:
  words Atkinson, numerals Anonymous Pro 700, month names Sora 600
  22sp as titles; no surface in §§7–8 assigns a numeric readout to
  Sora or a word to Anonymous Pro outside the filed mixed grammar.
  Acquitted.
- **The register's type entry itself.** REGISTER lines 170–172 state
  the ladder and the plate setting exactly as TYPE-RAMP line 4 and
  lines 58–61 state them. The register is not the offender in any
  charge above; where it is silent (C-6), the silence is charged as
  such, not as a rival value.
