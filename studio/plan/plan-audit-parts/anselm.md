## 1. B-55 — THE EXTINGUISHED CLOCK

**Charge as registered:** `plan/MASTER-PLAN.md` L5185–L5191 —

> "B-55 · The Extinguished Clock's internal contradiction — owner:
> Moench/Ruth · OPEN · §5.8.11 files the invention as a carried
> proposal, not spec: its stillness-threshold constant does not exist,
> its kill trial has not run, and its text contradicts itself about
> completion-while-extinguished. Closes when: Moench/Ruth answer the
> contradiction in writing; blocks promotion to spec only."

### VERDICT: SHARPENED — not closed

The governing text narrows the contradiction to one binary (below), and
kills one lazy reading of it — but it cannot close the charge, because
§5.8.11 itself reserves the answer to the author: the invention "does
NOT enter §5 as spec… but not plan law" (MASTER-PLAN.md L2653–L2656)
and promotion needs "the completion-bypass question answered"
(L2693–L2695). A critic substituting inference for the author's stated
intent would be manufacturing the very register truth this audit exists
to prevent. Hale: do not let any seat close B-55 by interpretation.

### The two contradictory passages, side by side

**Source text — Moench's own file**
(`invention/humans-parts/moench-moments.md` §2):

| Passage A — completion BYPASSES | Passage B — completion behaves as today |
|---|---|
| L174–L178: "The intercept overlay, a break, a **completion — every *event* surface — bypasses the extinguished state instantly and at full lawful brightness**: extinction governs the *idle* display only, never a moment where the app has something true to say." | L180–L182: "**Session end while extinguished: completion behaves exactly as it does today** (the receipt waits for his next open; no sound is added or removed by this invention)." |

**As carried into the merged plan** (`plan/MASTER-PLAN.md` §5.8.11):

- Bypass half, L2665–L2666: "Event surfaces bypass extinction
  instantly at full lawful brightness."
- Tension named, L2682–L2691: "the bypass list includes *a completion*
  (\"every event surface bypasses instantly at full lawful
  brightness\"), while its session-end paragraph says completion while
  extinguished \"behaves exactly as it does today\" — and today's
  completion on this face is the §5.8.8 step, which under a bypass
  would fire face-down into a desk at full lawful brightness, and
  under the second reading would run unseen while the face stays
  black. Moench / Ruth must state which completion law the invention
  intends (§9, charge B-55)."

The contradiction is real, not a paraphrase artefact: Passage A makes
completion an *event* that breaks extinction; Passage B's parenthetical
defines "today" only by the receipt and the sound — it never mentions
the AOD's own completion rendering at all. A builder reading B alone
ships a black face at zero; a builder reading A alone ships a lit face
at zero. Both builders are following Moench.

### What the merged plan's governing text DOES settle

Three settled passages lean one way, and they kill the weakest reading
of Passage B — that completion does *nothing* on this face:

1. **§5.8.5, L2507–L2508:** the AOD "never gets brighter on its own,
   for any reason **except the finish step (§5.8.8)**." Finish is the
   face's *sole* licensed brightness exception. Settled law therefore
   already treats completion as the one event allowed to break this
   face's stillness — structurally Passage A's side.
2. **§5.8.7, L2528–L2541 ("Events do nothing"):** the invariance list
   is deflection · blocking death · Safe Break entry/exit · routine
   firing · notifications. **Completion is not on it.** Every other
   mid-session fact is expressly silenced on this face; completion was
   left out of the silence.
3. **§5.8.8, L2543–L2556:** when remaining reaches zero the face *does*
   act — REMAINING holds `0:00`, tones step up to Stage 0, the dot
   steps to `#C74814` (lum 0.168), held exactly 60s. So "behaves
   exactly as it does today," read against the plan's own today, cannot
   mean "stays black and silent": today's completion is a step.

Why this still does not close the charge: §5.8.8 is settled spec and
the Extinguished Clock is expressly *not* spec (L2653–L2656). The
invention is entitled — if its author says so — to override §5.8.8
while extinguished; that override is precisely the design decision
(does the deepest stillness survive even the end of the block, or does
the end of the block break stillness like every other true event?).
Settled law can show which answer is *consistent*; only Moench/Ruth
can show which answer is *intended*. Consistency is not intent, and
B-55's closing evidence as registered is the author's answer in
writing (L5189–L5191).

A second, smaller divergence the answer must also cover: Passage A's
"full lawful brightness" is not §5.8.8's number. The step is an undim
to Stage 0 (REMAINING lum 0.215, dot 0.168, ceiling 0.25 —
L2547–L2551), not a flare to any maximum. If A governs, Moench must
say whether "full lawful" *means* the §5.8.8 Stage-0 step or something
brighter — the two are not the same spec.

### The exact question Moench/Ruth must answer (one sentence)

> **When a sealed block ends while the Extinguished Clock is active,
> does completion break extinction and fire the §5.8.8 finish step
> (Stage-0 undim, dot to `#C74814`, 60s) face-down on the desk — or
> does extinction outrank completion, the face stays pixel-off black,
> the §5.8.8 step is suppressed entirely, and the session's only
> completion rendering is the receipt at his next open?**

**Consequence of each answer:**

- **Bypass/step fires:** extinction governs idle time only; the finish
  step becomes a second bypass class alongside intercept/break, its
  60s of light is spent unseen into the desk on every face-down finish
  (the common case — the invention's own premise is that the phone
  lies face-down for most of every block), and §5.8.8 needs no
  amendment, only an extinction exception clause naming completion.
- **Extinction outranks:** the invention silently amends settled
  §5.8.8 — the one glanceable confirmation the face is allowed
  (§5.8.10 D11, MASTER-PLAN.md L2639–L2645: "An immediate vanish at
  zero would spend the one glanceable confirmation the face is
  allowed") never renders on any still, face-down finish; §5.9's
  ending checks and the "endings never share a silhouette" law must
  then be re-audited for the extinguished case, because FINISH while
  extinguished would share BROKE's AOD silhouette — nothing.

### Collateral finding inside B-55's own source (not a new charge)

Moench's trigger claims a constant that does not exist, in his own
words: "accelerometer variance below **the settled threshold already
used for AOD position-shifting**" (`moench-moments.md` L154–L156).
§5.8.11 reason 1 already catches this correctly (MASTER-PLAN.md
L2671–L2676): the settled patrol is clock-driven (§5.8.4, the 4×3
anchor grid, ≤1 step per minute) and no accelerometer variance
threshold exists in the settled spec or Register. The merged plan is
honest here; the *source file* is not. Ruth's merge should carry that
correction back to the source, not leave it standing in the Humans
file as if a settled threshold were available to borrow.

Severity key: **S1** = presents the unshipped as built / as register
truth — a builder acting on it builds or hunts for what does not exist.
**S2** = present-tense material claim for an unshipped element, partly
mitigated by a spec/build-status frame elsewhere. **S3** = stale tense
or missing per-item flag; the truth is recoverable inside the plan but
not at the passage.

### CHARGE M-1 — S1 — §2.4 presents the swept integral, the 720-entry LUTs, and the dead dual sub-draws as the rig

`plan/MASTER-PLAN.md` L279–L285:

> "**Light (Mara):** the point-sampled specular is convicted… **The rig
> integrates: `specularSwept(θ₀, θ₁)` over each tooth's swept frame
> arc, prefix sums on the 720-entry LUTs, dual sub-draws at
> |Δ| ≥ 1.5°**, tooth contrast attenuated by |sin(πΔ/p)/(πΔ/p)|,
> p = 3°."

Three failures in one paragraph:

1. It presents the swept form in the present tense as the rig's
   mechanism, with no shipped/spec marker — while the shipped rig is
   the very "point-sampled" rest-state form the same sentence convicts
   (0.16 + 0.66·t³, screen-fixed teeth). A reader of §2.4 alone learns
   the opposite of the tree.
2. The dual sub-draws are not merely unshipped — the plan's own §9 has
   already killed them: C-11, L4908–L4912, "The \"dual sub-draws at
   |Δ| ≥ 1.5°\" rendering phrase in MASTER §2.4 / BEYOND W2 is
   superseded by Dario's Amendment 2 (the sinc is a frame-global
   multiplier replacing the dual sub-draws)… The Register stands; the
   filed phrase is the stale interim." §2.4 still prints the stale
   interim as operative spec while §9 files it as superseded. The plan
   contradicts itself across its own sections, not just against code.
3. §0 repeats the elision at the top of the file: "Knurl: kept cheap
   (alternating teeth **lit by the specular rig**)" (L127–L128) — true
   of the rest-state rig, silent about which rig exists. At §0's
   altitude that silence is forgivable; combined with §2.4 it completes
   a file in which no passage ever tells a builder the swept form is
   future-only.

**Closing evidence:** §2.4 amended to carry the shipped/spec split in
the Light paragraph itself (rest-state form = shipped, screen-fixed;
swept integral + LUTs = specced, gated on the rim itself rotating),
and the dual sub-draws phrase struck in favour of C-11's sinc — or a
§9 charge registered against §2.4 in the same form as B-2 (filed text
standing as history under a headstone). Silence is not an option: this
is the exact claim Ren's 0905 correction was filed to stop.

### CHARGE M-2 — S1 — The Register still registers the swept form as register truth

This is the precise offence the 0905 correction names — the Register
claiming the swept specular — and as audited the correction has not
landed in the Register's specular entries: neither entry carries the
words *not shipped*, *rest-state form in the tree*, or *lands when the

"the REGISTER's \"specular: swept form\" line describes a plan state,
not the tree" (`plan/slice-de-parts/mara.md` L37–L38). A register is
the document a builder trusts over prose; an unqualified swept entry

Mitigation, recorded fairly: the Register is a spec register, and its
Dario amendments (sinc replaces dual sub-draws, Δ over drawn frames)
are correctly carried at L90–L92 and L270–L274 — the Register is
current on the *form* of the future rig. The charge is narrowly the
missing shipped/spec split, not the speccing itself. Speccing the
swept form is lawful. Registering it without its status is not.

**Closing evidence:** both Register specular entries amended with the
0905 correction's own sentence — shipped = rest-state
0.16 + 0.66·t³ on screen-fixed material; swept form + LUTs specced,

Against the tree: Mara's code audit, `plan/slice-de-parts/mara.md`

(BlockitCoreViews.kt:686-750) is: contact shadow…, flat cap fill…,

invented number. The dishonesty is tense only: a specced core written

S1 — by §2's build-status footer (L593–L602), which states the fold-in
is in flight and "where a shipped interim differs (pips, badge), the

**Closing evidence:** one status word in §2.4's hub paragraph (core:
specced, unshipped) — or the core's shipment. Either closes it.
