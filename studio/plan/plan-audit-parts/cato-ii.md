## 2. Charges

Severity: **S1** = as printed, breaks standing law or ships a wrong colour ·
**S2** = cross-file contradiction a builder must guess through ·
**S3** = naming / status defect, colour itself sound.

### C-1 · S1 — `#758F88`: the plan prints a G-dominant hex for a token Ren verified as a warm grey

The literal as printed in both files (MASTER §9 B-20; REGISTER, Colour
amendments) is `#758F88` = **R117 G143 B136 — G−R = +26, G−B = +7, hue 164°**.
That is the only G-dominant hex in the 43, and 164° is not near the warm
family (every other saturated literal sits at hue 13–36°). Printed as a
6-digit hex, this colour is a teal-grey, and it breaks the never-green law
on its face.

The token Ren actually verified (29 Oct 2026, 07:27 IST; LIE-REGISTER-2
item 10) is the 8-digit ARGB **`0x758F8888` = A117 R143 G136 B136** — warm
grey, R-dominant. The two readings share the digit string and nothing
else: the leading byte `0x75` is **alpha 117** in the verified token and
**red 117** in the printed literal. The Register itself files this exact
trap two entries earlier ("the byte 0x75 ≈ 46%, not 75%") and then walks
into it: it transcribes an ARGB token as an RGB hex, manufacturing the
green the original never had.

I do **not** re-open Ren's ruling on the shipped token — as ARGB it is a
warm grey, no swap, and my replacement nomination `#8F8375` (R143 G131
B117, warm-ordered) stands as filed dissent only. The charge is against
the *printed record*: the Register is the builders' governing authority
("where any plan file carries a different value… this register's value
governs"), and a builder who builds the Register's literal builds
R117/G143/B136 — a green-leaning outlineVariant Sarthak never approved
and Ren never saw. **Fix shape (for Hale/Ren, not mine to apply): print
the token in its verified ARGB form, or strike the hex and carry the
token name only.**

### C-2 · S2 — Day muted has two hexes: `#5E5A56` vs `#5F5856`

§4's queue ladder (MASTER L1091) prints Queue 3 day ink as `#5E5A56` =
**R94 G90 B86**. Every other day-muted surface in both files — §2 wheel
medium ticks and rim numerals (L324, L326), §6 band stop words (L2984),
§8 board word tones (L4227), PARK spec plate (L4537), and the Register's
day staircase — uses `#5F5856` = **R95 G88 B86**. Δ between the two:
R−1, G+2, B0. One is a typo-variant of the other; the plan never names
the difference, so two builders can ship two muted tones for the same
role and both cite the plan. The one-off occurs exactly once; the
registered form occurs 6 times across the two files.

### C-3 · S2 — Day primary text: the Register's `#2C2A29` exists nowhere in the plan; every section prints `#252323` as text

The Register's day staircase splits two tokens: ink `#252323`
(R37 G35 B35) and text `#2C2A29` (**R44 G42 B41**). `#2C2A29` occurs
**zero times in MASTER-PLAN.md**. Every MASTER section that sets day
body/primary text reaches for `#252323` in the text role: §2 wheel major
ticks (L324), §7/PARK spec plate "lines 1–2 in primary ink (day
`#252323`…)" (L4537), §8 word tones "primary ink (day `#252323`…)"
(L4228). Under the Register's own governance law, all of those sections
are off-register for day text — or the Register's text token is dead on
arrival. One of the two files is wrong on the most-printed day colour
in the app; the audit cannot acquit both.

### C-4 · S2 — AOD accent budget is registered 0, but §5.8 spends two orange-family tones permanently

Register, Accents: "Accent budget per screen: Deck 1 · Tasks 1 · Shield 0
· running 0 · **AOD 0** · overlays 0 · chrome 0." §5.8 then draws, on
every AOD render for the whole session: the armed dot in ember
`#7A3410` = **R122 G52 B16, hue 20°, saturation 0.869**, and at finish
steps it up for 60s to `#C74814` = **R199 G72 B20, hue 17°** — the
*hue-identical* twin of both accents (day `#F55314` hue 17°, night
`#F47442` hue 17°), differing only in value. §5.8.3's defence — "AOD
accent budget is zero; the ember dot is a tone, not the accent" — is a
naming distinction, not an arithmetic one: by channel, hue and
saturation these are oranges, spent on a screen whose registered budget
is zero. The luminance defence is real (0.066 and 0.168, both under the
0.25 ceiling — verified, see A-4) but luminance was the *ceiling* law,
not the *budget* law. Either the Register's AOD 0 is wrong, or §5.8's
dot is an unbudgeted accent. Both cannot stand. (Compounding: neither
hex appears in the Register as a literal — see C-5.)

### C-5 · S2 — Sixteen MASTER hexes have no Register literal; the Register's own law says unregistered numbers don't build

The Register: "A builder who finds a number in a cohort file that is not
in this register does not build from it until it is registered." Yet 16
of MASTER's 37 hexes appear nowhere in the Register:

- **Tasks queue ladder (7):** `#C9BBB0` (201/187/176), `#333130`
  (51/49/48), `#B7A797` (183/167/151), `#474441` (71/68/65), `#887A6E`
  (136/122/110), `#756F6A` (117/111/106), `#877F77` (135/127/119) —
  plus C-2's `#5E5A56` as the eighth ladder hex. The Register carries
  no queue ladder at all.
- **AOD stage ladder (6):** `#8A7E71` (138/126/113), `#6E6359`
  (110/99/89), `#4E463F` (78/70/63), `#7D7268` (125/114/104), `#5C5348`
  (92/83/72), `#554C43` (85/76/67). The Register carries these **by
  reference only** ("AOD stage tones are registered as filed in
  §5.8.5") — no hex printed, so the "single home of every load-bearing
  constant" holds no copy of them.
- **AOD dot pair (2):** `#7A3410`, `#C74814` (see C-4) — not even
  covered by the by-reference line, which names the stage tones of
  §5.8.5, not the §5.8.3 dot or the §5.8.8 finish step.

Vice versa, 6 Register hexes appear nowhere in MASTER: `#2C2A29`
(C-3), `#471602` ON-ACCENT (71/22/2), `#2E2A26`/`#161311` BLOCKBAR
day/night (46/42/38 · 22/19/17 — BLOCKBAR is not named in MASTER at
all), `#CFC7C0`/`#241E1A` knurl tooth B (207/199/192 · 36/30/26).
Register-only constants are lawful (the Register governs); the charge
is the asymmetry: MASTER-only *colour families* that whole sections
are built from, unregistered.

### C-6 · S3 — Shield's legend swatch contradicts Shield's zero-orange acceptance check

§3.8 prints the legend `■ ORANGE = COMMIT · ■ INK = SHUT · ■ GREY =
PEEK · ■ PORCELAIN = OPEN` with 8×8dp swatches, on Shield. §3.9
acceptance check 1, same section: "Zero orange anywhere on Shield, in
every state." The Register: Shield budget 0. The text shields the
*words* ("all words secondary ink — the line explains colour; it
doesn't wear it") but never states what the ORANGE *swatch* renders
in. If it renders accent fill, §3.9-1 fails on the section's own
legend. If it renders in ink, the legend's first swatch is a grey
square labelled ORANGE — the one place the app explains its colour
code would misstate it. One horn or the other; the section must pick.

### C-8 · S3 — Marker-line cream: ratified in standing law, still "provisional" in the Register; absent from MASTER §2

Standing law (this audit's brief): cap marker-line cream `#FFE4CF`
(R255 G228 B207) @ 90% **was ratified**. The Register (L318) still
carries it as "**provisional, flagged for ratification**", with Vau's
and Joss's losing candidates on record. And MASTER §2.4, the section
that specs the slide cap, mentions the marker line only as thickening

ratification, wherever it lives, has not landed in either file a
builder reads.

### C-9 · S3 — "Primary ink" at night names two different hexes, one tier apart

§5.4 (running) and §7 (Drawer) name night primary text `#D2C6BB`
(R210 G198 B187); §7 expressly bars the glyph tier from the Drawer.
§8's word tones (L4228–29) print lacks in "primary ink (day `#252323`
/ night glyph `#EDE3D8`)" = **R237 G227 B216** — a full tier above
primary (ΔR+27, G+29, B+29), and §5.3.7 likewise prints BREAK SPENT in
"glyph ink". The step-up for a lack is plausibly deliberate ("a lack is
printed at full ink"), but the *name* "primary ink" now denotes two
hexes in the same plan. Name §8's tier glyph, as §5.3.7 honestly does,
and the collision dies.
