## 5. RUNNING SESSION + AOD — the cloister and the desk clock

**Floor:** DESIGNERS-PLAN §6 (pit.md + nacht.md, bilaterally settled —
the running screen's five elements at §6.1 are this section's floor),
as amended by WILD-PLAN §2 (completion composite §2.2, the Numbered
Single Strike receipt §2.3), BEYOND-deck W4 (Release Proof, HELD
FRAME, Sled Goes Home — operative at §2.6 of this plan), the Frozen
Digest (HUMANS-INVENTIONS #1, Probe), and the Now Frame
(MAXIMPL-INVENTIONS top-3 #3, Nadia × Freya). Where the filed floor
and a later verdict or a registered constant collide, the verdict and
plan/REGISTER.md govern and the fight is named in place. **One
sentence:** while a session runs, the screen states the time and the
task, offers exactly one operable thing (the banked valve), tells no
news — not even the news that blocking died — and at the end tells
the whole truth exactly once, on the receipt.

### 5.1 Sealed-state law (this screen)

While a session is sealed: this screen is the cloister. The wheel,
hub, and slide are not drawn; navigation is locked and read-only;
Shield, Tasks, and the drawer are inert and refuse at the repository
boundary (§0, standard 2). No element on this screen is editable, no
mid-session decision is offered anywhere on it, and no caution has a
rendering here — cautions live at their owning rows on screens that
are unreachable mid-session. Exactly two exceptions, both his
verdicts (§0): the banked emergency valve (§5.4 — endurance, not
judgement) and the HELD digest, which may be *read* in full
mid-session, bodies included (§5.5) — the blocked apps stay sealed:
no opening, no replying, no acting from the digest. Blocking's death
mid-session changes nothing in this law and nothing on this screen
(§5.6).

### 5.2 The time source — the Now Frame (one authority, stated once)

**Every number on this screen and on the AOD is a field of the Now
Frame and of nothing else.** One pure `derive()` (MAXIMPL-INVENTIONS
top-3 #3) publishes a single immutable frame — verified elapsed,
penalty-aware remaining, block/break state, finishes-at, window
state, weekId — and every surface in this section consumes it: the
running hero, the second reading, the stepped arc's step boundaries,
the AOD's two readings and its progress bar, the digest's `AS OF`
and `It will keep` times, and the receipt's timestamps. No surface
in this section derives, counts, or caches its own time; the
entity's penalty-blind `remainingSeconds` and the AOD's shipped
fabricated progress constant (`0.65f`) are dead by this paragraph.
The publisher sleeps to the next change instant the frame itself
returns (the Deadline, not the Ticker): a stalled wake yields a late
frame, never a wrong one. True span everywhere in this section is
the registered formula, **n × block + (n−1) × 10** (Safe Break 600s,
between blocks only, never after the last block). Session t=0 is
§2.6's PROOF-SEAL FRAME; the seal's voice and shot (cadence
**210ms**, the Throw / the Laid Seal) are specced there and are not
re-specced here — from t=0, input is dead and this section owns what
the screen is.

### 5.3 The running screen — five elements, nothing else drawn

Night scheme at any hour (a running session forces the armed-lockdown
scheme regardless of clock or theme). Locked tones: ground `#171412`
· recess `#100E0C` · raised `#362C27` · text `#D2C6BB` / `#9C8E84` /
`#776A60` · glyph `#EDE3D8`. Type spends four rungs of the registered
ladder only: 72 (hero), 50 (hold-overlay countdown), 15 (every word
and second number), 10 (plates). Margin 20dp; radius 8 only on this
screen. The idle Deck's furniture — nav, wheel, slide, chips, NEXT —
is not dimmed or greyed; it is **not drawn**. A locked panel that
still draws its controls is lying about what it can do. Stack
(584dp before the void; surplus falls as empty ground below zone 5,
nothing added, nothing stretched; on short viewports gaps compress
32→20 then 20→12, zones never):

| Zone | Content | Height | Gap after |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Status strip — armed dot + state word | 48dp | 12 |
| 1 | Current task line (reserves 2 lines) | 40dp | 32 |
| 2 | `REMAINING` plate + hero numerals | 12 + 8 + 72 | 20 |
| 3 | Stepped arc Ø280dp, valve nested in its gap | 280 | 20 |
| 4 | Second reading — elapsed · finishes | 20 | — |
| 5 | Void (ground, defended) | remainder | — |

**Zone 0 — the state, stated once, in two channels.** Armed dot,
Ø6dp, filled, static, never pulsing — **in glyph tone, by form**
(filled centre, ink). *Fight, closed:* Pit's filed clause spent the
accent `#F47442` on this dot and on the warning rule (DESIGNERS-PLAN
§11-R13, escalated as a named exception); Ren's craft ruling (§0)
refuses the exception — no orange in chrome, no exceptions — and the
dot loses nothing it says: state here is carried by form + word, the
redundancy the accessibility floor demands, one fact confirmed in
two channels. Beside it, 8dp gap, the state word `RUNNING`,
Atkinson 700 15sp +0.5, text-primary. Nothing else lives in this
strip: no clock, no counts, no date. **During a Safe Break the word
is the break's:** `BREAK · 9:41` (the string the Shield notification
already computes; §2.1) — the break is a state word in zone 0's
slot, never a second clock, and the hero keeps stating the
session's remaining throughout.

**Zone 1 — the only sentence on the screen.** The current task,
Atkinson 400 15sp / lh20, text-primary, left axis, up to two lines
in a zone that reserves both — a long name, or the next task
arriving at a block boundary, never moves a pixel below it. No
checkbox, no DONE, no steps, no queue count: the line informs, it
offers nothing. The task changes only when the session's own
program advances it (the Now Frame's block state), in one hard cut,
digits and arc untouched.

**Zone 2 — the hero: remaining, and only remaining.** Plate
`REMAINING`, Anonymous Pro 700 10sp +2.0, text-tertiary — the
screen's only plate text (furniture naming the instrument).
Numerals Sora 700 72sp / lh72, tracking −2.0, glyph `#EDE3D8`,
tabular, format **`M:SS` in total minutes** (`47:32`, `107:32`) —
one format at every duration, no H:MM switch at the hour, no layout
shift at 60:00, ever. Update once per second, hard cut, on the Now
Frame's change instant: a measured number may step; no roll, no
flip, no fade — intermediate glyphs would be values the clock never
held. Remaining is stated exactly once in the app at this moment:
not on the arc, not in the strip, not as a percentage. During any
hold attempt the digits **do not freeze** — a frozen clock is a lie
with a delay; the hero keeps counting, dimmed (§5.4).

**Zone 3 — the stepped arc: the escapement, restrained.** Ø280dp,
stroke 10dp, sweep 270° from 135° to 45° clockwise; the 90° gap at
the bottom houses the valve (§5.4). Printed scale: long tick 14×2dp
at 0/25/50/75/100% of total, short tick 7×1dp every 10%, text-tertiary,
inside the rim; no numerals on the arc (the hero owns the number).
Centre empty — no pip, no block count. Step logic, quartz law: the
arc is divided into **N segments, N = total session minutes ÷ 5**
(the wheel's detent quantum, clamp 4–36), 2dp of ground between
segments. A segment fills when its 5 minutes have elapsed — one
frame, hard cut, on the exact boundary frame the Now Frame names.
Between boundaries the arc is frozen; it never sweeps, eases, or
interpolates. Completed segments text-secondary `#9C8E84`;
uncompleted recess `#100E0C`. The beat: on the boundary frame the
newly landed segment renders in glyph for exactly **150ms**, then
settles — one flash per five minutes, no pulse loop, no haptic, no
sound; while a session runs the machine is mute. Truth split, stated
so nobody fixes it later: the arc understates elapsed by <5 minutes
by construction — position is the glance channel, the hero's digits
are the truth channel. That is not a duplicate rendering.

**Zone 4 — the second reading, one line, once each.** Centred,
Anonymous Pro 700 15sp +0.5, text-secondary:
`ELAPSED 12 MIN · FINISHES 16:38`. Elapsed in whole minutes (seconds
live in the hero only — an elapsed with seconds beside the hero
invites arithmetic, and a cloister screen that invites arithmetic
has failed); finishes in 24h wall-clock (the merged floor's form,
DESIGNERS-PLAN §6.1 — Pit's filed AM/PM form is superseded there,
not here by preference). Session total is never stated on this
screen: it was stated at arm, it is elapsed + remaining, printing it
would be its second rendering. Block count and block length: setup
facts, dead here.

**Deflections: NOT on this screen, in any tier, at any size.** A
number with no verb attached is a scoreboard; it pays attention to
the exact behaviour the session exists to starve, during the
session. The count earns exactly one rendering — itemised, on the
receipt (§5.7). Blocking itself stays silent while it works.
**Absent, and why:** nav bar (drawn-dead nav teaches that locks are
cosmetic) · back/close affordance (beside the valve it is an argument
the screen is having with itself) · wall-clock time (remaining is the
time that matters; finishes is its translation) · the peek/airlock
composition (a system surface laid *over* a frozen cloister when a
blocked app summons it — hero and arc keep counting underneath, and
on expiry the cloister is simply there again, pixel-identical).

### 5.4 The valve at rest — reachable, never advertised

Mechanics are not re-specced here: the banked hold's numbers are
REGISTER's, verbatim — banks at **15 / 30 / 45**, full hold **60.0
held seconds**, slip = **800ms grace (per-slip, uncapped) → 1.0×
drain for the first 3.0s → 2.0× thereafter, easing at the floor**;
banked seconds never drain in-session; the spend writes only at
60.0 held seconds, in the repository's `completeEmergencyBreak()`,
one token per week, week anchored Monday 00:00 local. (DESIGNERS-PLAN
§7.2's flat 2:1 drain and Pit's filed references to Ton/Floor
mechanics are superseded by the register where they differ.) This
section owns the valve's *place and announcement* on the running
screen. Resting form: a recessed slot **200dp × 48dp**, nested in the
arc's 90° bottom gap, fill recess `#100E0C`, radius 8, 1dp hairline
text-tertiary at 55%; inside, centred, one grip line 24×2dp and the
plate `HOLD TO BREAK` (Anonymous Pro 700 10sp +2.0, text-tertiary).
No orange, no weekly count, no `EMERGENCY`: the valve is the dimmest
legible object on the screen, findable eyes-off because it is the
only operable element the screen contains. When the week's break is
spent, the valve is not composed at all — a SPENT plate renders in
its place (REGISTER, Ledger-Direct: the valve receives no count).
On finger-DOWN, in that frame: every other zone dims one full tier,
the hero keeps counting dimmed, and the slot expands in place to the
hold overlay — full-width × **112dp** touch field (the registered
valve floor is ≥88dp; the field exceeds it), countdown Sora 700
50sp above the bar, split readout BANKED / HOLDING under it at 15sp,
four chambers, banks landing on one frame. On release before
completion the overlay collapses in one cut, tiers restore in one
cut, banked progress stays banked and is **not** summarised on the
resting slot — the screen returns pixel-identical; a failed attempt
leaves no mark. On completion the session ends as BROKE (§5.7); the
valve does not reset, pulse, or celebrate.

### 5.5 HELD mid-session — the Frozen Digest (his verdict: FULL PEEK)

The one read the seal permits, specced as a snapshot, never a feed
(HUMANS-INVENTIONS #1, Probe — the walk that closed Quaere's HELD
charge). On a HELD tap while sealed, the digest opens on the list
**as it stood at the moment of opening**:

- **Header, two lines and no more:** the plate `HELD · AS OF {time}`
  (Anonymous Pro 700 10sp, quiet tier — the time is the Now Frame's
  wall time at the open) over one plain sentence, Atkinson 400 15sp:
  `Read everything here. Nothing here opens.` The first second of
  the surface belongs to the app, not to the most charged sender.
- **Full bodies, read-only.** Complete, scrollable, in arrival
  order — full peek stands in every line; nothing is hidden,
  delayed, or blurred. Rows carry no chevron, no open affordance, no
  tap target: a row tap fires nothing and animates nothing, and null
  taps write nothing.
- **No live appends.** Arrivals while the digest is open are held by
  the pipeline and appear on the next open or after the session —
  never appended under his eyes. There is no pull-to-refresh and no
  spinner, because there is nothing to wait for. The snapshot's
  `AS OF` is the only clock the digest owns.
- **CLEAR ALL does not exist while sealed** — repository-gated OFF,
  not hidden and not greyed (a greyed act is still an offered act).
  An irreversible, destructive act inside the reading surface
  contradicts the verdict's own terms (*reading is free, acting
  stays sealed*) and is a mid-session decision, which HOLD prices at
  zero. Clearing returns post-session, where it is a rested act,
  through the same repository boundary that refuses loadout and
  profile changes while sealed.
- **It Will Keep — on the close gesture, with its precondition
  flagged.** As the sheet dismisses (any close gesture, session
  still sealed), one line at the sheet's foot, Atkinson 400 15sp,
  full ink: `It will keep until {finish}.` — the finish is the Now
  Frame's finishes-at, a time, never a duration or a count. No
  button, no tap target, pointer-through throughout; it fades in
  with the dismiss (~280ms), holds ~900ms, fades in 200ms, and fires
  **once per session at most** (first close only; later closes are
  silent — repetition rots it into wallpaper). **⚑ Precondition,
  stated as a gate, not a hope: this line ships only if held
  captures provably persist to session end across process death.**
  If captures are in-memory only at build time, the persistence work
  is the price and the line does not ship before it — a sentence a
  dead process falsifies is a lie with good type.
- **The Landing Line — the way back in.** When the sheet has fully
  closed and the session is still sealed: for **2,500ms** the task
  line (zone 1) and the remaining time (zone 2) hold full ink while
  every other zone recedes to recess tone; the valve is never dimmed
  (an exit does not participate in anyone's composition). Then a
  400ms fade returns the screen to exactly its pre-digest state —
  pixel-identical apart from the clock fact itself. It yields
  instantly to any touch. No sound, no haptic: neither a second seal
  nor a reward happened. What it says, without saying it: *you left
  from a task; here is the task; the block did not move.*

No count of his reading exists anywhere — no "you read N messages"
line, no record of opens. Reading is free means its frequency is his
too: no digest lockout, no reads-per-block ration, no re-open tax.

### 5.6 Blocking death — the silent law (his verdicts Q7 + Q8, one law)

If blocking dies mid-session — through no fault of his — **nothing
is signalled in the moment: no knock, no word, no screen change, no
sound, no AOD change.** The session clock keeps running to its
scheduled end; nothing ends early; there is no BROKE screen at the
death. The session runs its course in silence, and the truth is told
**once, on the finish receipt** (§5.7): the receipt carries the gap
line (`THE SHIELD WAS OFF FOR PART OF THAT SESSION`, in the
receipt's provenance block), and **the record books the session as
BROKEN, never as a clean seal** — the booking follows the receipt,
into the rebuilt Ledger tab (§7) and the Shield Verdict Line's
COMPROMISED sentence at the next rested visit (§3.3). *Fight,
closed by his word:* Pit's filed WARNING state (a 2dp accent rule +
`UNGUARDED — …` line burning mid-session, the armed dot dimming
while it burns) is **struck entire** — it was written before the
07:22 verdicts and cannot survive them; a warning that knocks
mid-session is a decision demanded at the door HOLD welded shut.
What survives of Pit's alarm discipline is its negative half, now
the whole law: CAUTION has no rendering in the cloister, and neither
does WARNING. Deflections likewise change nothing on any surface in
this section — a machine doing its job does not file progress
reports, and a machine that has stopped doing its job does not file
them either, until the receipt.

### 5.7 Endings — FINISH, BROKE, and the receipt

Completion and emergency never share an exit form: symmetric
treatment renders a broken session as a finished one, which is a lie
told by form. The two silhouettes are carried by geometry first,
word second, number third.

**FINISH (the timer reaches zero — the completion composite,
WILD-PLAN §2.2 × BEYOND-deck W4).** The composite is keyed to the
persisted `RUNNING → COMPLETED` transition by `sessionId`, written
before any animation, so process death or reopen cannot re-fire it —
the completion happens once, ever. Sequence: the hero steps to
`00:00` on its normal per-second cut and the arc's final segment
lands with its standard 150ms beat, the arc standing **full**; then
the **HELD FRAME — 1200ms** whole (REGISTER): the Room Stops (bed
out 300ms), 400ms of true silence, then the **completion pulse alone**
— reserved strictly for verified fully-elapsed sessions — against an
unmoving picture. At E1200 a **0ms CUT** (deliberately unsoftened; no
reverse Vertigo — rewinding would claim a restoration, and nothing
was restored) to the defocused idle Deck, RACK 280 to sharp, while
**Release Proof** proves the opening: the pawl lifts only on
per-link CONFIRMED-OPEN via the reverse cascade, faults print once
as `STILL BLOCKED`, silent retry, zero decisions. The receipt
(§below) is detained **≤1.8s**; its own t=0 lands at E1480, inside
the ceiling. **The Sled Goes Home:** at receipt +1000ms the sled
returns powered over **650ms** — silent, accent-free, interruptible
(specced at §2.6; cross-referenced here, not re-specced). *Fight,
closed:* Pit's filed FINISHED state (400ms stillness → hard cut to a
composed finish screen: total as hero, `TURNED AWAY N`, a `RELEASE`
tap gating navigation) is superseded by this composite — its 400ms
stillness survives inside the HELD FRAME's 1200ms, its full-arc
silhouette survives as the held picture, and its RELEASE gate dies:
after Release Proof the Deck is idle and navigation is the Deck's
own law again, not a toll the finish charges.

**BROKE (the banked hold completes).** A **0ms splice cut** — never
the completion's held frame, never a run-down, **no completion
pulse**. The silhouette: the arc stays **partial**, frozen at the
segments it had earned; the status word reads `BROKE`; the hero
states the elapsed-at-break once (`32 MIN IN`); the single action
reads `OPEN`. The Deck then states the spend plainly in human words
(`EMERGENCY BREAK USED — NEXT AVAILABLE {weekday}`, WILD-PLAN §3.3).
A session that ends by the valve is booked as ended-by-break — true,
and not the same booking as §5.6's death-broken record.

**The receipt — the Numbered Single Strike (WILD-PLAN §2.3), the
one telling.** One flat slip, inline on the Deck's end state — no
overlay, no archive. The strike: 220ms empty beat → the sheet rises
24dp over 380ms `CubicBezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.25, 1)` → all type arrives
in one 110ms blow. It carries, and only carries:

- **Uma's provenance, from an arm-time frozen snapshot:** task title
  as it stood at sealing, loadout counts as actually sealed, arm and
  seal timestamps — later edits can never rewrite a finished
  session. The routine line says *within* his routine, never *by*
  it. Banked-break seconds print separately, never folded into
  sealed time.
- **Deflections itemised** per app with timestamps (break-window
  openings excluded, 30s dedupe), headed `AS LOGGED BY THE SHIELD`.
  **No deflection total is printed anywhere on the receipt** — the
  itemisation is the fact; a total would be its second, score-shaped
  rendering. (Pit/Sana's `TURNED AWAY N` line belonged to the
  superseded FINISHED state; on the receipt the items stand in its
  place, and §3.3's Verdict Line carries the rested one-sentence
  form. One fact, one rendering per surface, no surface carrying
  both.)
- **The death line, when true:** `THE SHIELD WAS OFF FOR PART OF
  THAT SESSION` (§5.6) — the receipt is its only mid-flow rendering;
  the record's BROKEN booking is its permanent form (§7).
- **The break line, when true:** a receipt for a valve-ended session
  is labelled `ENDED BY EMERGENCY BREAK`.
- **Permanence, honestly:** a monotonic sequence counter, incremented
  only at genuine completion, is the only permanent artefact —
  `№ 048` proves `№ 047` existed; nothing of 047 is stored. A single
  `lastReceipt` slot, overwritten by the next session. The footer
  states the slip's terms under his Ledger verdict (KEEP, rebuilt):
  `THE MACHINE KEEPS ITS OWN LOG · YOUR STUDY RECORD LIVES IN YPT`.
  No day total (YPT owns the day; Monolith would undercount), no
  streak, no orange — the committing is over.

### 5.8 AOD — the desk clock (Nacht's terms, Pit countersigned)

A separate instrument, not the Deck dimmed: true `#000000`, no
panels, hairlines, nav, brand, date, or wall-clock while running.
All its numbers are Now Frame fields (§5.2); its progress is
frame-derived — the shipped fabricated constant is dead. **Two
standing readings, rigid hierarchy, never swaps:** REMAINING `H:MM`,
Sora 700 **104sp**, tracking −3.0 (the format differs from the
running screen's `M:SS` by surface — law, not drift), colon at 45%
of numeral tone, never blinking; ELAPSED `H:MM`, Sora 600 **50sp**,
below it on the same axis. **Progress bar 120dp × 3dp** under the
elapsed numeral — geometry, not a reading: fraction by fill length,
no digits, ever. **Armed dot Ø6dp, ember `#7A3410`** — the face's
only warm element; it never pulses, animates, or dims across stages.
`OF total` never prints. **Task line transient only:** first 60s of
a session and 10s after any wake-touch or pickup (Atkinson 400
15sp), in a reserved slot so the cluster never reflows; NEXT lives
on the idle face only. **Patrol:** the rigid cluster moves on the
registered 4×3 anchor grid (±36dp × −64/0/+64dp), ≤1 grid step per
minute on the minute tick, all 12 anchors before any repeat, ≥24dp
from edges, repositioning a 1-frame cut, never an animation; updates
once per minute on the minute boundary — update and patrol step are
one wakeup. **Staged dimming (REGISTER luminance):** REMAINING
0.215 (0–20min) → 0.174 (20–50, labels drop) → 0.130 (50+), ceiling
0.25 for every element, transitions crossfading 2s on a minute
boundary; the dot never dims. **Events do nothing:** a deflection
changes nothing on the face; a routine firing changes nothing
visible; **blocking death changes nothing** (§5.6 — the face stays
silent with the room). **Finish is exactly one step, for exactly
60s:** REMAINING holds `0:00` at Stage-0 tones, the dot steps up to
`#C74814` (lum 0.168, under the ceiling), then the face hands over
to idle. No receipt on the AOD — the receipt lives on the Deck.
Wake: pickup is the 10s transient only; unlock lands on the running
screen or the Deck with a night first frame — no day-flash, ever.
The AOD is unconditionally silent.

3. **Span truth:** hero, finishes, and receipt all derive from
   n × block + (n−1) × 10; at 3 × 50 the span is 170 everywhere it

11. **Valve:** banks at 15/30/45 hold against the register's drain
