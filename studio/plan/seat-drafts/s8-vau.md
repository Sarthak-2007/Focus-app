## 8. PARK — the utility end-detent

### 8.1 Sealed-state law (this surface)

While a session is sealed: **PARK cannot open.** Both entry paths
(§8.2) are inert — the band's end-detent answers a press or a drag
with total stillness, and the chrome Guard line is print, not a
target. Every Android Settings launcher that lives in PARK —
in practice the FIX key — renders in its exact unsealed form and
answers nothing: no press state, no ripple, no navigation (Hale's
clause, carried by the ruling as a repository-level gate,
`fixEnabled = !isSessionArmed ∧ missingExists` — the second lock
on a door the sealed rail already closes). A permission that dies
mid-session changes nothing here and nothing in chrome: no knock,
no word, no screen change (§0, his 07:22 verdicts — STAY SILENT /
THE CLOCK RUNS); the clock runs to its scheduled end, the finish
receipt tells the truth once, and the session is booked broken,
never clean. *This supersedes Uhr §5.3's dead-beat cut to a BROKE
screen, filed before those verdicts existed; Uhr's detection
discipline survives, his mid-session rendering does not.* PARK
holds no slide and no seal path: a session can only be armed from
the Deck, so PARK never has to be closed *by* a seal — it has to
be left before one is reachable.

### 8.2 What PARK is, and how he gets in

**A utility end-detent, not a destination.** PARK is a position
inside the band's track, after LEDGER: **44dp zone + 4dp notch,
a flat 48dp cost under every variant** (REGISTER). It carries no
stop word of its own in the rail's rhythm and earns no badge,
count, or dot. The drawer it invokes is a panel (radius 16,
raised tone — day `#FAF6F0` / night `#362C27`, parting hairline,
interior on the frozen 20dp margin) with exactly **two rest
heights, closed and open** — no half-detents; its grab strip's
touch height is 48dp (REGISTER touch floor). Travel, filed and
kept from the invention wave: open 280ms SEAT, close 160ms THROW
(Ton's Drawer Slide, −24dB open / −26dB close). While the drawer
is open the band cap stays parked in the zone — the drawer is
visibly the cap's cargo. **Leaving PARK and dismissing the drawer
are one gesture**, never two: any stop press, a cap drag out of
the zone, a drag-down past 40% of open height, or a tap on the
scrim dismiss to the same closed seat (§6.7's contract, stated
here because this section owns what the gesture opens).

**Entry paths — exactly two, plus the first run:**

1. **The end-detent.** The cap is dragged past LEDGER into the
   zone (rising resistance in the last 12dp, the grip notch
   stepping ink-35 → ink-70 at the zone's inner edge, a detent
   tick pitched *below* the stop family — PARK must not sound
   like a stop), or the zone is tapped (every detent is also
   tappable). **Arrival IS invocation:** on the cap's seat
   against the end-wall the drawer rises on that same frame. No
   second tap, no handle tap, no confirm.
2. **The chrome Guard line, while unsealed.** Tapping the line
   (§6.2) opens PARK. It is a readout with one door; the door is
   this drawer, and there is no other.
3. **First run.** Setup opens PARK directly — before the Deck is
   ever armed from — and walks the permissions there (§8.5's
   walk, in board order, one lack at a time). Discoverability is
   designed into the first run and into every slide refusal
   (§2.6's reserved sentence: tapping `… — TAP TO FIX` opens PARK
   on the board); it does not need, and does not get, a permanent
   fifth stop.

No other surface invokes PARK. Shield in particular does not:
Shield keeps the working lists (apps, sites, loadouts) and gains
**no second permission board** — PARK is the sole detailed Guard
home, and the CHAIN question is settled for this branch (ruling
§1). A build that renders the board anywhere else fails §8.10-11.

### 8.3 Header — `GUARD`, and the instant's only home

The drawer's header is two lines, and nothing else rides in it:

- **Line 1: `GUARD`** — Sora 700 22sp (the title tier Shield's
  header already spends, §3.3), left on the margin. The room
  died; the name survives as the repair section's name, exactly
  as the ruling keeps it.
- **Line 2, beneath it: the telltale.** Left, `LAST PROVED` in
  plate style (Anonymous Pro 700 10sp, +2.0). Right-aligned on
  the same line, the instant itself: **Anonymous Pro 700 15sp,
  +0.5**, wall-clock, seconds printed — `LAST PROVED 14:02:31` —
  followed by its evidence grade, part of the same fact:
  `· END-TO-END` when the canary last proved the chain end-to-end,
  `· SELF-REPORT` when the proof is the OS's word only (§2.6's
  grades, Rosa's device gate binding: the canary dies on one
  flash, an unkeepable path, or a non-rare UNKNOWN rate, and the
  grade then prints SELF-REPORT permanently).

**The instant is an instant, never a duration.** No `AGO`, no
`LAST SYNCED`, no age string exists in PARK's copy (Uhr §2.1: a
duration begins lying the second after it is struck; an instant
ages in the reader's eye and condemns itself against his clock).
**The instant lives here and nowhere else** — not in chrome
(§6.2: the Guard line carries no instant), not on the Deck strip
(its seam stamp is a freshness age, a different fact), not on the
plate (§8.8: the plate carries no date, ever). While PARK is
foreground the Watch Store sweeps on its 5,000ms cadence, and
**each completed sweep re-strikes the instant — a hard cut, 0ms,
no fade, no roll**: a computed fact re-strikes; it never performs
freshness. A partial or failed sweep advances nothing: not the
instant, and not any line it did not measure (Uhr §2.2). Before
the first complete sweep has ever landed, the instant's slot is
**blank** — the name prints, no time does; absence renders as
absence, never as `00:00` or a default.

### 8.4 The Permissions board — the full board, one source

**One source, stated as build law:** every row below renders
from the **Watch Store only** — per item, its value, when it was
measured, and its evidence class. No row, and no code path behind
a row, runs its own OS query or keeps its own copy (Uhr §1). A
builder who finds a second poll has built a second truth.

**Form — Teen's board, unchanged.** Ruled lines, not rows: pitch
**48dp**, 1dp hairlines between lines at ink 18%; the name in
plate style (Anonymous Pro 700 10sp, +2.0) left on the margin;
the state word at the right margin in **Atkinson 700 15sp, +0.5**
— truth is never whispered at plate size. No fill, no radius, no
press state, no ripple, no chevron, no per-line action, no
description under a name: the name *is* the description or the
name is wrong. A line you cannot press is print, and print is
the point — with exactly one exception, the FIX key (§8.5).

**The board, in fixed order** (order never changes; a line is
never added without amending this table and the REGISTER):

| # | Line | Family | State words (Watch Store) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `ACCESSIBILITY` | permission | `GRANTED` / `MISSING` / `STALE` |
| 2 | `USAGE ACCESS` | permission | `GRANTED` / `MISSING` / `STALE` |
| 3 | `DEVICE ADMIN` | permission | `GRANTED` / `MISSING` / `STALE` |
| 4 | `BATTERY` | permission | `GRANTED` / `MISSING` / `STALE` |
| — | *16dp doubled gap, no eyebrow* | | |
| 5 | `SHORTS + REELS` | interceptor | `RUNNING` / `PAUSED` / `OFF` |
| 6 | `SOCIAL SITES` | interceptor | `RUNNING` / `PAUSED` / `OFF` |
| 7 | `NOTIFICATIONS` | interceptor | `RUNNING` / `PAUSED` / `OFF` |

- **Permissions** are the OS grants the seal depends on, measured
  by Uhr's four queries (enabled-services list · app-op mode ·
  admin-active · battery-optimisation exemption). `BATTERY`
  reports the exemption grant itself — the shipped screen's
  conflated `BATTERY & ADMIN ACCESS` row, which read admin state
  while opening the battery pane, is split by this table into
  lines 3 and 4, each with its own word and its own pane (§8.5).
- **The count runs over the permission family only.** Chrome's
  `GUARD 4/4` and its first-lack naming (§6.2) count these four
  lines and no others — the denominator is 4, board-defined
  (REGISTER). Interceptors never enter the count: an interceptor
  at `OFF` because he turned it off is his rested choice, not a
  lack, and chrome does not name his choices as failures.
- **Word meanings are Uhr's split, exact (§3 of his file, and
  already the REGISTER's vocabulary).** For a permission:
  `GRANTED` = measured granted, age within warrant; `MISSING` =
  measured lacking, including a canary breach (a breach is a
  measurement, not an inference); `STALE` = was measured, warrant
  expired, current value unknown — *never* a synonym for
  `MISSING`. For an interceptor, the word renders the *effect*,
  not the setting: `RUNNING` = setting on ∧ every parent
  permission GRANTED-warranted ∧ no suspension — it is
  intercepting now; `PAUSED` = setting on ∧ permissions warranted
  ∧ a suspension he chose is in force (digest held while sealed,
  outside its routine window) — it resumes without his hand;
  `OFF` = setting off, **or** a parent permission MISSING/STALE
  — it will not resume without his hand. PAUSED vs OFF is the
  load-bearing distinction: both are "not intercepting," and they
  are opposite facts. `BLOCKED` never appears on this board — it
  is the word for what the machine does to apps, exiled from
  self-description.
- **Warrant decay (Uhr §2.3).** A board word is warranted for
  **15,000ms** from its line's own measurement (three PARK
  sweeps). Past warrant, on the next render, the word re-strikes
  to `STALE` — in form, not prose; no sentence is spent on
  staleness anywhere in PARK. One complete sweep re-warrants
  every line it measured; words re-strike on the frame their
  query lands (§8.5). Recovery prints no "recovered" line.
- **Blank-until-proven (Uhr §3.3).** On entry, a line with no
  in-warrant value prints its name and **no state word** until
  its first sweep resolves (deadline 1,500ms, the arm path's
  truth deadline reused). Past deadline unresolved: a prior
  value exists → `STALE`; none exists → the word slot stays
  blank and the header instant does not advance. No spinner
  exists in PARK, in any state.
- **No toggles on this board.** An interceptor's *setting* is
  flipped with Shield's working lists, in Shield's sled grammar;
  PARK reports the effect of that setting and changes nothing.
  The board's only key is FIX.

### 8.5 FIX, and the repair return — the walk's proof law

**The FIX key — the board's one interactive thing.**

- **Exists only in lack.** If any *permission* line reads
  `MISSING`, exactly one key exists in PARK. If none does, there
  is no key and the Permissions section contains zero interactive
  elements. A `STALE` line earns no key: the action it needs is a
  fresh sweep, and PARK's foreground cadence is already running
  it; a pane cannot fix an unmeasurable state. An interceptor at
  `OFF` earns no key of its own: if a parent permission is the
  cause, that permission's lack already owns the key; if his
  setting is the cause, there is nothing to repair.
- **Form, filed and kept:** full content width, **48dp tall,
  radius 8** (a key, so it obeys key law), ink fill — day ink
  `#252323`, word `#FAF6F0`; night fill cap `#4A423C`, word glyph
  `#EDE3D8`. **Never orange:** FIX commits nothing inside the
  app — it escorts him to the OS and back, and orange would spend
  the COMMIT signal on a trip to Settings.
- **Exact text:** `FIX — <first MISSING in board order>`, Sora
  700 15sp, +0.5. One lack at a time, in the order the board
  prints them. It sits **under the board**, as Teen filed it —
  the board moves into PARK unchanged, and this is its filed
  place. *(Placement conflict, reconciled in the open: Uhr §8A
  and Dieter-II both put the key directly under the first
  MISSING row. The ruling's "the complete board, the one FIX
  path … moves into PARK unchanged" is the stronger text, and
  Teen's under-the-board position is what "unchanged" carries;
  Sana may flip this at merge with one word, and the walk below
  does not depend on it.)*
- **Behaviour:** answers on **DOWN**. Opens the **exact** Android
  pane for the named grant — accessibility settings for
  `ACCESSIBILITY`, usage-access settings for `USAGE ACCESS`, the
  device-admin pane for `DEVICE ADMIN`, the battery-optimisation
  pane for `BATTERY` — never Settings home, never a list he must
  hunt. On DOWN the key dips 1dp (PRESS curve, ≤90ms) and *stays
  dipped* for the whole absence, and the board keeps the form he
  left: his place is held open while he is in the pane. Voice:
  Ton's Nav Key at its quietest (−16dB; night ×0.6 sound, ×0.85
  haptic) — repair is navigation to a bench, not a commit, and
  never borrows the seal's thock.
- **While sealed the key is print (§8.1):** same form, same ink,
  no press state, no navigation — Hale's gate, repository-level.

**The return — PROVING.** On return to the app with a repair
outstanding, all four permission queries dispatch **in parallel**
at t=0 of the return (queries are lawful pre-commit; nothing
here commands anything). Budget: the sweep lands ≤**300ms**.

1. **Each line re-strikes only when its own fresh query lands** —
   on that frame, a hard cut, no crossfade, no intermediate
   glyph. Lines land in *query order, not board order, and not
   on a timer*. **A fixed stagger is banned by name:** a line
   that flips because 80ms elapsed is Rosa's timer-driven
   bulb-check lie — choreography claiming to be a measurement.
   If the queries land in one frame, the board re-seats in one
   frame and the walk is invisible. That is correct: the walk is
   what proof looks like when it is not instantaneous, not a show
   laid over it.
2. **The repaired line alone gets the firm click.** When — and
   only when — the line that sent him out re-seats `GRANTED`:
   one `playFirmClick` (−8dB against the seal; haptic inside the
   same 100ms). Every other line re-seats silently. If he fixed
   nothing, there is no sound at all, the key springs back
   (PRESS-BACK, settled ≤150ms), and the board looks exactly as
   it did when he left — stillness is the refusal, here as on
   the slide.
3. **Stability beat: 500ms.** After the last proof lands, the
   board holds untouched for 500ms of confirmed stability. Its
   stillness is the difference between "the query said yes once"
   and "the lock is holding."
4. **Hand-back: no restored banner, ever.** No `FIXED`, no
   `RESTORED`, no `ALL GOOD`, no checkmark, no banner of any
   kind, in any state — the machine owed him a working lock, and
   delivering what was owed is not an achievement. What happens
   instead: the FIX key **absents itself** — its 48dp plus the
   gap above it collapse over 160ms THROW, one mover only, and
   the sections below rise to close the space; the chrome Guard
   line (§6.2) simply stops printing the lack, because it stopped
   being true. **Total hand-back budget ≤890ms** from return
   (300 sweep + 500 stability + 90 cross-fade of the healthy
   tenant), inherited whole from §2.6's imprint law. If a query
   exceeds the sweep budget, its line holds and the stability
   beat waits for *it*, not for a deadline — on this surface
   nothing seals, so proof is never refused by a clock.
5. **The second lack inherits, on the proof frame.** If another
   permission still reads `MISSING` when the first is proven,
   the key does not collapse — it re-prints in place for the
   next lack (`FIX — <next MISSING>`), word re-striking on the
   same frame. One lack at a time, until the board is whole or
   he leaves.

**The first-run walk is this same walk**, walked from the top:
PARK opens on the board, the first MISSING owns the key, each
repair returns and proves as above. He may leave mid-walk with
the one dismissal gesture — nothing traps him in setup — and an
unfinished board is not hidden from him later: the Deck's slide
refuses under §2.6's interlock and its sentence routes him back
here. There is no second setup path, no modal, and no summary
screen at the end of the walk; a whole board is its own ending.

  farm regrows. If a control is ever added to this section, it

### 8.7 Theme — an AUTO readout, naming its owner

Below Sound, one ruled line, pitch 48dp, same print grammar as
the board — because this line *reports*, and reports are print:

- **Form:** left, `THEME` in plate style. Right, the readout in
  Atkinson 700 15sp, exactly one of these forms:
  - `AUTO · NIGHT — SESSION ACTIVE`
  - `AUTO · NIGHT — SLEEP 20:00` (the SLEEP routine's name and
    start, as printed on its Shield row)
  - `AUTO · DAY — NO ROUTINE OWNS IT`
- **The source named is Sten's precedence, whole** (routines
  rebuild §7, the theme's one rule): (1) a session is active →
  NIGHT, the session owns it; (2) else an enabled, unpaused
  SLEEP window is active → NIGHT, that routine owns it and is
  named; (3) else DAY, owned by nothing — and the readout says
  so rather than inventing an owner. The readout is computed
  from the same evaluation the theme itself uses; a readout
  that could disagree with the scheme on screen is a second
  rendering and is banned by construction.
- **No manual pill is resurrected — here or anywhere.** The line
  has no press state, no target, no toggle behind it. The
  shipped manual override (`_darkTheme` / `toggleDarkTheme` and
  the DAY/NIGHT pill) is dead by Sten's kill order, and PARK —
  the one drawer where a tired user would go looking for it —
  is where its absence is designed, not accidental. His honest
  lever is the schedule itself: pause SLEEP on Shield and the
  app stays in day, because his day says so.
- The theme transition itself is a 0ms cut at the boundary
  (Sten §7); the readout re-strikes on the same frame the scheme
  swaps. While sealed, the readout would read `SESSION ACTIVE`
  — and is unreachable, because PARK is shut (§8.1). Both facts
  are stated so no builder invents a sealed exception for it.

### 8.10 Acceptance checks (this surface)

1. **Entry.** One drag to the end-detent invokes the drawer on
   the seat frame — no second tap, no confirm; a tap on the
   PARK zone does the same; tapping the chrome Guard line while
   unsealed opens PARK; no other surface invokes it. The drawer
   has exactly two rest heights; leaving PARK and dismissing it
   are one gesture, verified from every dismissal path in §8.2.
2. **Header.** The header prints `GUARD` and, beneath it,
   `LAST PROVED <instant>` — and the instant appears **nowhere
   in chrome** and nowhere else in PARK (render audit, every
   screen). Before any complete sweep: the instant's slot is
   blank. No `AGO` / `LAST SYNCED` string exists in PARK copy
   (grep).
3. **The instant steps only on proof.** With PARK foreground,
   kill the sweep source: the instant freezes at the last
   complete sweep and does not advance across 60s; restore it
   and the instant re-strikes on the next completed sweep's
   frame, hard cut. A partial sweep (one query withheld)
   advances neither the instant nor any unmeasured line.
4. **Board truth.** Exactly 7 lines, in §8.4's order, rendering
   from the Watch Store only — grep: no PARK-side OS permission
   query exists outside the store. Permission words ∈
   {GRANTED, MISSING, STALE}; interceptor words ∈ {RUNNING,
   PAUSED, OFF}; `BLOCKED` appears nowhere. A word whose
   measurement ages past 15,000ms re-strikes to `STALE`; a
   MISSING never renders as STALE and a STALE never as MISSING.
