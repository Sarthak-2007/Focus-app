## 8.P.1 Sealed-state law (this drawer)

While a session is sealed: PARK cannot open. The chrome Guard line
is inert, and every Android Settings launcher that PARK would
otherwise offer renders as print and answers nothing (Hale's clause,
carried into the dissolved branch verbatim by the ruling). A
permission that dies mid-session is **not** announced by a chrome
change, a knock, or any change inside PARK; the session clock runs
and the finish receipt tells the truth once (his death verdicts,
§0). The board below is a rested-user surface; it is never a
mid-session signal.

## 8.P.2 The material grammar — one ground, print, one object

PARK is the flattest surface in the app, and that is its identity.
The kept room's discipline was *print, not settings*; the drawer
keeps the discipline by having almost no material at all:

- **Exactly one surface tone: Ground.** The drawer's interior is the
  slab itself — day Ground `#E4DFDA`, night Ground `#171412`, matte,
  flat fill, 0dp elevation, no shadow, no gloss. There is **no recess
  and no raised zone anywhere in PARK**: no wells, no tracks, no
  sleds, no cards, no panels-within-the-panel. My one-step law
  (§1.4) is satisfied trivially — no depth change ever occurs, so
  **no catch hairline and no parting hairline exists in PARK**. The
  1dp lines that do appear are *print ruling* (ruled lines between
  board lines, the hairline above the plate), not depth lines; they
  claim no depth and follow Teen's board setting, below.
- **Everything on the ground is printed ink** (rule 10: print, don't
  float): names, state words, the `LAST PROVED` instant, the Sound
  and Theme readouts, and the plate are letterpress on the ground —
  never on a chip, a backing plate, or a fill of their own.
- **Exactly one object:** the FIX key (§8.P.4), present only when a
  lack exists. It is the drawer's only seated piece, its only fill
  other than the ground, and its only radius other than the drawer
  panel's own.
- **The drawer panel itself** obeys the radius freeze: **16dp**
  (panels), top corners per sheet form. No other radius exists in
  PARK except the FIX key's 8dp. No pills, ever; no text sits inside
  a pill.
- **Orange: zero, everywhere in PARK, no exceptions.** PARK's accent
  budget is 0 (Shield's budget, inherited — this is a filing
  surface, not a committing one). FIX is ink, never orange: it
  commits nothing inside the product (§8.P.4). Orange is never a
  surface tone, a hairline, or type (rule 10).
- **Locked tokens only.** Every tone named in this part is a locked
  staircase or ink token from §1.3 / REGISTER. No new hex is
  introduced, and the specular exception (§8.P.6) may shade a locked
  token but may never mint one.

## 8.P.3 The board — ruled print, truth never whispered

The full board from the ruling, in the ruling's PARK order. The
board is **ruled lines, not rows**: no fill, no radius, no press
state, no ripple, no chevron. A line you cannot press is print, and
print is the point (Teen §3.3, kept-branch discipline, moved into
PARK unchanged per the ruling).

**Composition, top to bottom:**

1. **Header:** `GUARD` (Sora 700 22sp, the title tier, as Shield's
   title) on the ground. Beneath it, `LAST PROVED <instant>` —
   the instant lives here, not in chrome (ruling). 24h format only.
   *(Setting assignment, mine, flagged in the merge letter: plate
   style, Anonymous Pro 700 10sp +2.0, secondary ink — the instant
   is the drawer's furniture line; its size is not fixed by the
   ruling or the register, and Set/Sana may overrule the assignment
   without touching anything else in this part.)*
2. **Permissions board** — one line per permission or interceptor,
   fixed order, never reordered, a line never added without
   amending this table:
   - Permissions, in board order: `ACCESSIBILITY` · `USAGE ACCESS` ·
     `DEVICE ADMIN` · `BATTERY`
   - Interceptors, in board order: `SHORTS + REELS` ·
     `SOCIAL SITES` · `NOTIFICATIONS`
3. **Sound** — its own section below the board (ruling's PARK order,
   item 2), in print form here: the line `SOUND` with its state
   word. Its interactivity is **not settled** — see §8.P.7, open
   conflict C-2. This part specs only how the line *prints*.
4. **Theme** — its own section (ruling, item 3): an `AUTO` readout
   naming the routine or schedule that owns the theme. No manual
   theme pill is resurrected, ever.
5. **The FIX key**, when a lack exists (§8.P.4) — below the board.
6. **The spec plate**, last (§8.P.5). Nothing below the plate
   except the scroll inset.

**Line setting (Teen §3.3 verbatim discipline):** name in plate
style — Anonymous Pro 700 10sp, +2.0 tracking — left on the margin;
**state word at the right margin at Atkinson 700 15sp. Truth is
never whispered:** a word that carries a state is body-tier truth,
never plate size, never a lighter weight to make a lack look
smaller. Hierarchy inside the tier is tone only, never size and
never weight — every state word on the board is the same 15sp at
the same 700. Line height 48dp, the state word's baseline centred
(but see open conflict C-3 on pitch). No descriptions under names —
the name *is* the description or the name is wrong; no prose
anywhere on the board.

**State words (REGISTER governs):**

| Family | Words | Tone of the word |
|---|---|---|
| Permissions | `GRANTED` / `MISSING` / `STALE` | `GRANTED` in secondary ink (day muted `#5F5856` / night `#9C8E84`); `MISSING` and `STALE` in primary ink (day `#252323` / night glyph `#EDE3D8`) — a lack is printed at full ink, a wholeness quietly, at identical size and weight |
| Interceptors | `RUNNING` / `PAUSED` / `OFF` | `RUNNING` in secondary ink; `PAUSED` / `OFF` in primary ink, same rule |
| Sound | `ON` / `OFF` | same rule (`ON` secondary, `OFF` primary) |
| Theme | `AUTO` + the owning source named | readout in secondary ink; it reports, it never states a lack |

All state renders from the Watch Store only — PARK runs no check
of its own and keeps no copy (ruling). **Re-strike law:** a state
change is a *re-print* — the word re-strikes in place, instantly,
no animation, no flip, no stagger; on return from a FIX trip, each
line re-strikes only when its own fresh query lands, and the
repaired line alone gets the firm click (ruling). There is no
restored banner, ever. **No count reprints in PARK:** the `n/4`
fact's one home is the chrome Guard line (REGISTER: `GUARD 4/4`;
a lack names the first lack there). Teen's kept-branch board
partner (`3 OF 4 GRANTED` beside the eyebrow) does **not** move
into PARK — it would be the same fact in a second rendering
(§8.P.7, reconciled R-2).

## 8.P.4 The FIX key — the drawer's one interactive object

- **Exists only in lack.** If any permission line reads `MISSING`,
  one FIX key appears below the board. If none does, there is no
  key and the board + plate contain **zero** interactive elements —
  a complete panel, fully printed. Absence renders as absence: the
  space closes up (Teen's filed exception to the reserved-slot law,
  kept: the key sits below the board, and its closing moves only
  the plate, which is print, not measurement).
- **Form:** one full-content-width key, **48dp tall, radius 8dp**
  — it is a key, so it obeys key law and the radius freeze (8dp
  keys / 16dp panels; no third radius is invented for it). Fill:
  ink `#252323` by day, Cap `#4A423C` by night. Word in porcelain /
  cream. No border loop, no shadow, no skirt — a flat ink key on
  the ground.
- **Word:** `FIX — <name>`, Sora 700 15sp, +0.5 — the verb, an em
  dash, the name of the **first** `MISSING` line in board order.
  One lack at a time, in the order the board prints them. On
  return from the Android pane the board re-strikes (§8.P.3); if
  another lack remains, the key re-prints for that one. The panel
  has one moving part; it moves one step at a time.
- **Colour: ink, never orange.** FIX commits nothing inside the
  product — it escorts him to the exact Android pane for the named
  grant and back (never a generic Settings home, never a list he
  must hunt). Orange on this panel would spend the app's one COMMIT
  signal on a trip to Settings.
- **Behaviour:** answers on DOWN. While sealed it is unreachable
  with the whole drawer (§8.P.1) — and if PARK is ever rendered in
  a sealed context, the key renders as print and answers nothing.
- **Scope note:** "one interactive object" is stated for the
  board-and-plate panel this part specs. Whether the Sound section
  below the board carries an operable control inside PARK is an
  open conflict (§8.P.7, C-2) — this draft does not smuggle a
  second interactive object in, and does not kill Sound's either.

## 8.P.5 The spec plate — furniture at the foot

The plate sits at the drawer's foot as identity (ruling: "the
plate is furniture, never a reason to keep a destination"). Its
discipline is Teen's, verbatim, carried through §8.5:

- **Setting:** after the board (and the FIX key, when present) —
  32dp section break, one 1dp hairline at full content width, 16dp,
  then the plate. The plate is **not a card: no fill, no border, no
  radius — print directly on the bare ground** (surface #1 in
  §8.P.2, day `#E4DFDA` / night `#171412`). **No orange anywhere on
  the plate.** No line wraps.
- **Type:** plate body in Anonymous Pro 400 10sp, tracking +1.2,
  lineHeight 16sp, UPPERCASE, secondary ink (day `#5F5856` / night
  secondary `#9C8E84`); lines 1–2 in primary ink (day `#252323` /
  night text `#D2C6BB`). This is the type ladder's one print
  exception (Teen's, granted §11-R14): labels are engraved at +2.0;
  the plate is *printed* at +1.2 — no seventh size is invented.
- **Text, verbatim (teen.md §3.5 — reprint in the same release if
  any mechanism line stops being literally true; a false plate is
  worse than no plate):**

```
MONOLITH ML-01
UNIT Nº 01 · BUILT FOR ONE USER · NOT FOR SALE
WHEEL SETS TIME · SLIDE ARMS · THE HOLD BREAKS
HOLD: 60 SECONDS, BANKED EVERY 15 · ONE BREAK A WEEK
PEEK: 2 MINUTES, THEN SHUT AGAIN
COLOUR IS A VERB: ORANGE = COMMIT · INK = SHUT · GREY = PEEK · PORCELAIN = OPEN
ROUTINES: SET BY HAND ON SHIELD · FIRED AS SET
SITES: KEPT WITH APPS ON SHIELD · DATA: ON THIS PHONE ONLY
STUDY RECORD: IN YPT · SESSION RECORD: THE LEDGER
SOUND: FLOAT-PCM, 4 PARTIALS · POWER: YOUR PHONE
```

- **Invariance:** the plate carries **no count, no state, no
  date** — it is **identical on day 1 and day 100**, in day mode
  and night, whether the board above it is whole or broken. State
  lives on the board; identity lives on the plate; the two never
  trade jobs. `UNIT Nº 01` never increments and never localises.
  The plate is always the last thing in the drawer and always
  reachable.

## 8.P.6 The specular-dimming exception inside PARK — what it may and may not touch

Ren's ruling stands and is restated here so §8 carries it whole:
my §1.4 exception is **GRANTED as shading of a locked token** —
a seated specular peak may scale **×0.50 / ×0.75 / ×1.00** — and
**dynamic light sampling stays dead** (it would cross Mara's law:
light must carry no data; the rig's fixed 315° azimuth, core ±9°,
cos² falloff to zero at ±34°, peak lift 10% day / 8% night are
unchanged everywhere, PARK included).

**Inside PARK, the exception's territory is nearly empty, and this
part keeps it that way:**

- **MAY touch:** only the specular peak of a surface that is
  *already* specular territory under §1.4 and *already* filled
  with a locked token. In PARK that set contains exactly one
  candidate: **the FIX key, while pressed** (pressed key caps are
  territory, §1.4). If the FIX key carries the rig at all, its peak
  may be shaded ×0.50 / ×0.75 / ×1.00 **as a pure function of its
  drawn press displacement only** — of where the cap physically is
  in its press travel, never of anything it means.
- **MAY NOT touch:**
  - any printed surface — the board lines, state words, names,
    `LAST PROVED`, the Sound and Theme readouts, the ruled lines,
    and the plate, in whole or in part. Print never shines; a
    dimmed state word would be truth whispered by light, and
    §8.P.3 forbids it in type and forbids it here in light;
  - the drawer ground, any recess (there are none), the drawer
    panel, or the scrim — ground and sheets are forbidden
    territory (§1.4);
  - the FIX key's **fill or word**: the multipliers shade the
    specular *peak only*, never the locked ink/cap fill beneath it
    and never the legibility of `FIX — <name>`;
  - anything **as a function of state**: not of `GRANTED` /
    `MISSING` / `STALE`, not of the presence or absence of a lack,
    not of which permission lacks, not of the count. The moment
    dimming reads a state variable instead of a drawn position, it
    is light-as-data and it is cut on sight (Mara's law);
  - no new tone may result as a *named* surface: shading is an
    operation over a locked token's specular excess, never a fifth
    staircase step and never a hex a builder writes down as a fill.
- **No topography is imported to give it work.** PARK has no wells,
  no seats, no sled caps; the graded-well grammar that earned the
  exception lives on Shield. If the FIX key is built flat — flat
  ink fill, printed word, no specular claim, exactly as Teen's
  form describes it — then the exception has **zero application
  inside PARK and nothing is lost**. The exception survives in
  PARK as law, not as decoration looking for a surface.

## 8.P.7 Conflicts — reconciled and unresolvable

### Reconciled (a governing source exists; the loser is named)

- **R-1 — The plate in the dissolved branch.** Razor's invention
  file kills the plate when Guard dissolves ("do not invent a
  drawer footer to house nostalgia") and would re-home `PEEK: 2
  MINUTES` in the Shield legend. **The ruling overrules it:**
  GUARD-RULING §1 places the static spec plate at the drawer's
  foot, and its closing argument carries the plate into PARK
  unchanged. Razor's kill is the loser; the plate stands at
  §8.P.5, and peek's duration keeps its only printed home there.
- **R-2 — The board's count partner.** Teen's kept board prints
  `3 OF 4 GRANTED` beside the eyebrow as the count's only home,
  the chrome pill being dead in that branch. In PARK the count's
  home is the chrome Guard line (REGISTER: `GUARD 4/4`, lack
  named there). Reprinting it in the drawer would duplicate the
  fact. The count does not move into PARK; Teen's line is the
  loser *in this branch only*.
- **R-3 — Interceptor state words.** Teen's board prints
  interceptors `ON` / `OFF`; Uhr's amendment asked for `RUNNING` /
  `PAUSED` / `OFF`. **REGISTER settles it:** interceptors render
  `RUNNING / PAUSED / OFF`, permissions `GRANTED / MISSING /
  STALE`, from the Watch Store only. Teen's `ON/OFF` survives
  only on the Sound line. Uhr's fallback negotiation over the
  word `RUNNING` is moot under the register.
- **R-4 — Theme's board word.** Teen's kept board reports Theme
  as `DAY / NIGHT`. The ruling's PARK form is an **AUTO readout
  naming its owning routine/schedule**, no pill. PARK follows the
  ruling (§8.P.3 item 4); `DAY/NIGHT` as the PARK Theme word is
  superseded.

### Unresolvable here (flagged for Sana — no resolution invented)

- **C-1 — `STALE`'s entry behaviour.** REGISTER gives permissions
  a third word, `STALE`, but no filed source settles how it
  *arrives* in PARK: Teen's board is struck from live state on
  entry with no freshness theatre, while Uhr's invention file
  specs blank-until-proven (a line prints its name and **no**
  state word until its first sweep resolves; past deadline, a
  prior value decays to `STALE`). Uhr's behaviour is invention-
  wave, not ruling or register. This draft prints `STALE` at full
  ink when the Watch Store writes it (§8.P.3) and takes **no**
  position on blank-until-proven. Sana/Ren must rule it; a word
  printed before its measurement exists and a word withheld past
  its deadline are both truth questions, not materials questions.
- **C-2 — Sound's interactivity inside PARK.** The ruling carries
  "the existing sound controls/readouts" into PARK as section 2;
  Teen's dissolved-branch hatch gives Sound a launcher that opens
  its control *inside* the drawer; Razor's sheet reduces Sound to
  a word-state row. These cannot all be true beside §8.P.4's
  one-interactive-object discipline and the §8.P.8 photograph
  test. This draft specs Sound's **print form only** and flags
  the rest: if Sound is operable inside PARK, the photograph test
  must be scoped to the board + plate, or Sound's control must
  live behind its line as a separate workshop layer — Sana rules,
  I do not.
- **C-3 — Board line pitch: 48 vs 52 vs 64.** Teen's board lines
  are 48dp; Razor's drawer sub-rows are 52dp; Uhr's file claims
  drawer rows are fixed P64. REGISTER registers no board pitch.
  This draft is written to Teen's 48dp because §8.5's kept-branch
  discipline is the form the ruling moves into PARK — but the
  disagreement is recorded, not erased: if Sana merges 52 or 64,
  only §8.P.3's line-height number changes; no tone, word, or
  setting in this part moves with it.
- **C-4 — The drawer's sheet tone.** Razor's Repair Sheet specs a
  sheet on day Raised `#FAF6F0` / night Ground `#171412`. Teen's
  plate discipline — which the task and the ruling both carry
  verbatim — sets the plate on **bare ground** (day `#E4DFDA`).
  A plate printed on a Raised day sheet is not on bare ground.
  This draft follows Teen + the ruling (the whole PARK interior
  is Ground, §8.P.2); if the build implements PARK as an overlay
  sheet in Razor's tones, the plate's ground and Razor's sheet
  cannot both stand. Flagged, not forked.

## 8.P.8 Acceptance checks (this part)

1. **The photograph test (the acceptance form, verbatim from the
   kept branch):** cover the FIX line. The remainder must read as
   a photograph of a printed panel — names, state words,
   specification, unit number, and nothing that invites a finger.
   If any element outside the FIX key looks pressable, it moves
   or dies. *(Scope is subject to C-2: if Sana rules Sound
   operable inside PARK, this test applies to the board + plate
   as printed, and Sound's layer is tested separately.)*
2. **Truth never whispered:** every state word on the board
   measures Atkinson 700 15sp — `GRANTED`, `MISSING`, `STALE`,
   `RUNNING`, `PAUSED`, `OFF` alike. Any state word found at
   plate size or below 700 fails the drawer.
3. **Board whole ⇒ zero interactive elements.** With every
   permission `GRANTED`, a full accessibility/action sweep of
   PARK finds no tappable element in the board or the plate.
   With a lack, exactly one: the FIX key, 48dp, radius 8dp,
   naming the first `MISSING` in board order.
4. **Plate invariance:** screenshot-diff of the plate region
   between day 1 and day 100, whole board and broken board, day
   and night settings — empty, excepting the day/night token
   change itself. The plate text matches teen.md §3.5 verbatim.
5. **Zero orange:** no pixel of accent tone anywhere in PARK in
   any state, including the FIX key's press state.
6. **Radius audit:** the only radii in PARK are 16dp (drawer
   panel) and 8dp (FIX key). Any third radius fails.
7. **One ground:** every non-FIX surface in PARK samples as
   Ground in both modes; any recess, raised card, or fill found
   on the board or the plate fails.
8. **Specular audit:** no specular rendering on any printed
   surface, the ground, or the panel. If the FIX key carries the
   rig, its dimming multiplier is a function of drawn press
   displacement only — code inspection: the dimming input list
   contains no state variable (no tier, no permission, no lack,
   no count, no clock). Any state input → the rig is cut from
   the key, not retuned.
9. **Sealed PARK:** while sealed, PARK does not open; any
   rendered PARK surface answers no tap, including FIX and any
   Settings launcher (§8.P.1).
10. **Re-strike:** on return from a FIX trip, each board line
    re-strikes only on its own fresh Watch Store value; no line
    moves (Δy = 0 across the board in every state — the FIX key's
    appearance/disappearance below the board is the one filed
    exception, and it moves only the plate).
