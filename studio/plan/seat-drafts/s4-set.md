# §4 TASKS — TYPE DISCIPLINE (seat draft, Set, for Sana to merge)

*Drafted by Set (Designers, typography) to merge with Druck's body
draft (§4's floor: DESIGNERS-PLAN §5, which is druck.md as amended).
This draft governs TYPE ONLY — every size, family, weight, tracking
and line height on the Tasks screen. Geometry, tone, motion, sound
and vocabulary are Druck's and the Improvers', except where a size
forces a word below. Where this draft and a source collide, the
fight is named and the ladder rules — except the convictions in
§4.T5, which are Sana's to rule and are flagged, not settled.*

**The ladder, stated once for this screen:** 104 / 72 / 50 / 22 /
15 / 10 (REGISTER: TYPE LADDER). Sora display, Atkinson Hyperlegible
body (real weights 400/700 ONLY), Anonymous Pro plate and numbers
(400/700 ONLY). Off-ladder sizes are build errors (DESIGNERS-PLAN
§10). **Tasks uses exactly four of the six rungs: 50 / 22 / 15 /
10.** 104 is AOD-only; 72 is the one big number *in hand* on Deck
and Running — Tasks has no duration hero of its own to wear it, and
importing 72 here would make the queue total compete with the
session total it feeds. The screen's largest type is its Footing.

**The discipline in one sentence:** one size per slot, hierarchy
inside the 15sp body tier by weight, family and tone — never by
size — and the plate carries furniture only: the moment a word
carries a decision, a state, or a count he must act on, it is 15sp.

### 4.T1 Sealed-state law (this section's type)

While a session is sealed: the Footing, the well, the ACTIVE block's
title and steps, the queue rows and the DONE rows still print, at
exactly the sizes in §4.T2 — facts stay readable, and readability
does not shrink under HOLD. Verbs leave (Druck §9): START, DONE and
CUT are absent, not greyed and not set smaller; the composer is
absent entirely. The one type change HOLD makes on this screen is a
word, not a size: the ACTIVE block's label reads its sealed state
at body size (see conviction C-3). No size in the table below has a
sealed variant.

### 4.T2 The type table — as it lands

#### Header / the Footing

| Slot | Family | Weight | Size | Tracking | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plate row — `TASKS` / `N TASKS` | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 10 | +2.0 | 12 |
| **The Footing — queue total numeral** | Sora | 700 | **50** | −1.5 | 52 |
| Footing unit (`MIN` / `H` / `M`) | Sora | 700 | **22** ⚑ C-1 | −0.3 | on the numeral's baseline |
| Projection label — `IF STARTED NOW` | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 10 | +2.0 | 12 |
| Projection value — `ENDS 18:40` | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |

The Footing is a computed number: it re-strikes in place (88→100%
ink over 120ms), never rolls, never counts through intermediates
(Aldo's law; Horiz Rung 2, adopted). At zero it prints a real
`0 MIN` at the same 50 — a printed zero is a mark, not an absence.
The `ENDS` stack is absent at zero; in the all-done state its value
slot carries `ALL OF IT DONE` at the same 15 (Druck §8.2), under
the plate label `QUEUE TOTAL`.

#### Queue rows (one module, every depth)

| Slot | Family | Weight | Size | Tracking | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Index — `#02` | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |
| Title | Atkinson | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |
| Duration — `25M` | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |

One baseline, one size. Depth is carried by tone and indent
(Druck's Light Well) — never by shrinking the row's type: a queue
title at depth 5 is the same 15sp as at depth 1, only paler.
No verbs, no fraction, no second numeral column on these rows.

#### The ACTIVE block (the screen's one panel)

| Slot | Family | Weight | Size | Tracking | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plate row — `ACTIVE` | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 10 | +2.0 | 12 |
| Plate row, right — `#01 · 25M` | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |
| **Title — ACTIVE task only** | Atkinson | 700 | **22** | 0 | 27 |
| Action words — `DONE` · `CUT` | Sora | 700 | 15 | +0.5 | 20 |
| `START` — label on the one orange key | Sora | 700 | 15 | +0.5 | 20 |
| Sealed state word — `RUNNING` (HOLD only) | Atkinson | 700 | 15 | +0.5 | 20 ⚑ C-3 |

The ACTIVE title is the screen's only 22: it is T1, the one title
tier, in Atkinson Bold because the title is a sentence he wrote,
not a display numeral — Sora stays for section titles and button
words. The duration is NOT reprinted on the START key (the plate
row's `25M` states it; a second printing is a second rendering).
Druck's `DEL` does not land: the vocabulary law names the verb
`CUT` (Teen's closed list), set in the same action-word style.

#### Sub-steps (inside ACTIVE only)

| Slot | Family | Weight | Size | Tracking | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Step numeral — `01` (`%02d`, never `010`) | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |
| Step text | Atkinson | 400 | 15 | 0 | 20 |
| `ADD STEP` — the create line | Atkinson | 400 | 15 | 0 | 20 ⚑ C-2 |
| Add-step field (while entering a step) | Atkinson | 400 | 15 | 0 | 22 |

Completion changes no size: a done step keeps its 15, steps to
ink-3, its numeral is replaced by the tick, and the strike is
*drawn* (1.5dp, 180ms — Floor §10: strikethrough wins as the
step-done shape). The parent title is struck only by DONE itself,
at its full 22 — steps-done and task-done are two facts, and type
does not blur them (Luz's correction, held on this screen too).

#### Composer (collapsed line + focused bank)

| Slot | Family | Weight | Size | Tracking | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Field text (typed) | Atkinson | 400 | 15 | 0 | 22 |
| Field placeholder | Atkinson | 400 | 15 | 0 | 22 |
| Duration echo — `· 25M` | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |
| `ADD` — word, collapsed line | Sora | 700 | 15 | +0.5 | 20 |
| Duration caps — `15M` `25M` `45M` `90M` | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |
| `ADD` — key, focused bank | Sora | 700 | 15 | +0.5 | 20 |
| Queueing stamp — `QUEUED · 25M · #04` | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |

Placeholder and input are the same size *and* the same weight
(400): the first keystroke changes glyphs only — no weight pop,
no width pop (Fach's finding, kept; his 14sp is not). A cap label
is one quantity at one size: numeral and `M` together at 15, never
split (see audit, Taste). The stamp is a confirmation he may act
on, so it is body, not plate. ADD is a button word in both its
forms — Sora 700 15 +0.5, per the ramp's button row — in ink,
never orange: only START commits a session.

#### DONE rows (below the foundation rule)

| Slot | Family | Weight | Size | Tracking | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Section plate row — `DONE` | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 10 | +2.0 | 12 |
| Done count (plate row, right) | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |
| Title (struck, at rest) | Atkinson | 400 | 15 | 0 | 20 |
| Index / duration | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |

Done rows wear the queue module at Regular weight, ink-3, struck —
finished is read by weight, tone and the strike, never by a
smaller size. Zero done → the section, its plate row and its rule
do not exist; absence renders as absence, at no size at all.

#### Empty states

| Slot | Family | Weight | Size | Tracking | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Virgin empty — the Footing | Sora | 700 | 50 | −1.5 | 52 |
| Virgin empty — the one sentence | Atkinson | 400 | 15 | 0 | 22 |
| All-done — `ALL OF IT DONE` | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |

No illustration, no icon, no new size for emptiness. An empty
screen is the same page with less on it: the Footing prints its
zero at full hero size, one hairline, one sentence at reading
line height (22 — it is body copy, not a row).

### 4.T4 What the audit leaves untouched

Every lawful size in the sources lands as filed: Druck's plate,
body, mono and word styles; DESIGNERS-PLAN §5 in full; Horiz's
re-strike mechanics (a motion law, size-free); Zeile's axis as

### 4.T5 Convictions — flagged for Sana, not settled here

- **C-1 · The Footing's unit size.** The ramp table names the
  queue total (50) and no unit slot for it. Three lawful mappings
  compete: **22** (Druck's nearest — his 20 → 22; and the F-UNIT
  precedent in §2.3, where the Deck's unit sits one hero tier
  below its numeral: 72 → 50, so here 50 → 22, ratio 2.27, the
  ladder's own cliff) · **50** (a whole-string reading of
  DESIGNERS-PLAN §5: "Sora 700 50sp in human form") · **15**
  (Horiz's nearest — his 16 → 15). *Set's recommendation: 22*,
  on the F-UNIT precedent this seat authored for §2 — but the
  ramp table's silence means this is a conviction, not law.
  §4.T2 prints 22 as the interim; Sana rules before build.
- **C-2 · `ADD STEP` — plate or body?** Druck sets it as plate
  text (10/+2.0). It is the only step-creator: a verb he taps,
  carrying an act. Set's law (plate = furniture, body = truth)
  and the Shield precedent (`+ NEW ROUTINE` at Atkinson 400 15,
  §3.4 — a create line is body, not plate) both say **15**.
  §4.T2 prints 15. But Druck's file is the body draft this merges
  with, so the strike is flagged, not buried: Sana confirms.
- **C-3 · `RUNNING` in the ACTIVE plate row.** Druck §9: under
  HOLD the slab's plate word reads `RUNNING`. A state word on
  the plate rung contradicts the state-word law (Atkinson 700
  15 +0.5) that Floor's audit and §3 both hold. §4.T2 therefore
  carries RUNNING at 15 as its own slot, demoting the plate
  label while sealed. If Sana holds the plate row inviolable,
  the alternative is that RUNNING does not print on Tasks at
  all (Running screen owns the state) — either is lawful;
  a 10sp RUNNING is not.
- **C-4 · No seventh size was bought.** Three sources reach for
  a size between body and plate (11, 12, 13, 14 — four different
  reaches for the same rung). The arithmetic in this seat's
  filed ladder stands: with the plate locked at 10, that rung
  cannot exist lawfully. If the merge wants it, the burden is
  the filed one: name which rung dies. None of the four sources
  names one. Recorded as a standing conviction, not a request.
