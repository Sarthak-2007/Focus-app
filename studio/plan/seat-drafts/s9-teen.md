## 9-V.2  GHOST — the boundary (§0.1-7; Critics merge condition 7)

**Disposition: SURVIVES IN ONE SENSE ONLY, and that sense never prints as an
attendance word.**

- **Lawful sense (spec / code / audit language only):** GHOST is the technical
  term for **a session that never happened** — a session booked, minted or
  counted without his slide and without presence evidence (the thing Sten's
  no-ghost law, filed §3.4, prevents at the source: attended minute = inside
  the real window ∩ session active ∩ evidence within trailing 5 min, evidence
  = his acts only, never the machine's).
- **Never lawful:** GHOST as a printed attendance grade or category
  (`SAT · GHOST · MISSED` rows — LEDGER-designers' form is dead), GHOST as a
  count on any surface, GHOST as an adjective for a routine, a day, or the
  man. "Ghost minutes" name a *prevented* thing in specs; no such minutes are
  ever minted, so there is never a quantity to print.
- **The printed replacements already in the sources:** attendance outcomes in
  spec are ATTENDED / PARTIAL / MISSED / HELD / LATE_FIRE (§3.4); on a surface,
  only a true sentence prints — `LAST · ATTENDED 2H 50M OF 3H`. Absence
  renders as absence.
- New sections (§§4–8) may use GHOST only inside a sentence that defines or
  forbids the never-happened session. Any other use is a §9 violation on
  sight.
