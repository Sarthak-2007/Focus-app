## 6. CHROME — header · band switch · PARK

### 6.1 Sealed-state law (this surface)

While a session is sealed: the band switch is locked and the header
band is inert. Stops accept no press and answer with total stillness
— no ink answer, no cap departure (Mikro §7, refusal form). PARK
cannot be opened; the Guard line is print, not a target; every
Android Settings launcher renders as print and answers nothing
(Hale's clause, carried by the ruling). The Drawer, if already open
at the seal moment, stays readable and nothing in it is editable —
search, reopening and every edit are inert (§7). The HELD digest may
be read in full mid-session (his verdict, §0) — that exception lives
on its own surface and opens nothing in chrome. **Blocking death is
not announced in chrome:** if blocking dies mid-session the Guard
line does not change, nothing knocks, no word appears (§0, his
07:22 verdicts) — the clock runs, and the finish receipt tells the
truth once; the session is booked broken, never clean. Geometry
under seal is invariant (§6.5): sealing moves no frame in chrome by
a single dp.

### 6.2 Header band — the maker's mark and the Guard line

**One band, 48dp, top of every screen, hairline at y48.** No screen
name (the band switch names the screen), no pills, no theme toggle —
theme follows routine/schedule and is reported in PARK, never
switched here. The nameplate pills (HELD count, GUARD n/4 as a pill,
DAY/NIGHT) are dead — chrome states nothing twice.

**Left: the maker's mark as furniture.** `MONOLITH`, plate style
(Anonymous Pro 700 10sp, +2.0 tracking), left-anchored at the 20dp
margin. It is furniture, not a fact (§8.1 as filed; §11-R11 — the
census governs facts, not the maker's mark): it carries no state,
changes with no state, and is the only thing in the band allowed to
say nothing. On the Deck this is also the health silence of §2.5 —
the left end prints no permission fact, no total, no count.

**Right: the Guard line, in words.** Right-aligned to the 20dp
margin, Anonymous Pro 700 **15sp** — information at body size, not
plate (Floor's fix, adopted in §8.5's dissolved form). Whole, it
prints exactly:

`GUARD 4/4`

When something lacks, the same line names the **first lack in board
order**, and nothing else changes:

`GUARD 3/4 — ACCESSIBILITY OFF`

- **Denominator is 4, board-defined** (REGISTER). The n/4 charge
  (§0.1-10) closes with the Guard ruling: the counted set is the
  PARK board's set, defined there once; no surface recounts.
- **One source:** the Watch Store only — per item its value, when it
  was measured, and its evidence class. Permissions read
  GRANTED / MISSING / STALE; interceptors read RUNNING / PAUSED /
  OFF. No surface in chrome runs its own check or keeps its own
  copy. A reading the store has not proved does not print as whole:
  `4/4` is a measured fact, never a default.
- **Quiet by law:** no colour alarm, no motion, no sound, no badge,
  no fill, no pip. (Razor's filed Ø6dp hollow/filled pip is
  superseded by the ruling's words-only line — the REGISTER specs
  the line as words, and a pip is the killed pill in miniature,
  Vera & Una's V-07 charge answered by removal, not defence.) Form
  is the whole signal: the words change, the band does not.
- **It is a readout with one door:** while unsealed, tapping the
  Guard line opens PARK (the ruling's second invocation path,
  §6.7). While sealed it is inert (§6.1). It never carries a FIX
  key, a count of anything else, or the `LAST PROVED` instant —
  that instant lives in PARK's header, here it would be a second
  rendering.
- **Failure on the Deck is not duplicated here.** A dead permission
  at arm time is named by the Guard line *and* refused in the
  slide's own path (§2.6: the reserved sentence under the track,
  the inert slide) — two homes because they are two facts: the
  standing state (chrome) and the refusal of this gesture (Deck).
  Neither borrows the other's words.

### 6.3 The war line — STRUCK

The band carries **no data line**. Razor's filed war line,
`186D TO NEET · TODAY 2H30 / 12H`, is struck in both segments
(§8.2, §11-R11; Ren's ruling, §0): `186D` is computed from an
assumed, unannounced exam date — an assumption is not a fact, and a
masthead is the worst place to print one; `TODAY`'s hours are YPT's
fact, and any Monolith rendering of them undercounts and so lies.
Chrome carries Monolith's own state, in words — that state is the
Guard line (§6.2) and the Deck chrome sentence (§6.6), nothing else.

**Fallback, filed as dissent only:** if Sarthak orders the count
knowing it is an assumption, Razor's convention is the fallback —
one date constant, count = (exam − today) + 1, computed at render,
never hand-edited — and the `TODAY` segment still never returns.
Until that order exists, the convention is a dissent on record,
not a spec: no build renders the line, in any state, on any screen.

### 6.4 The band switch — four stops + PARK

**The band is DECK · SHIELD · TASKS · LEDGER + PARK.** Four stops —
the Ledger stop is KEPT by his verdict (§0, 05:41: "Keep the tab,
rebuilt"), and the kept tab opens the Drawer (§7). PARK is a
utility end-detent after LEDGER, not a fifth stop and not a
destination (§6.7).

**Superseded text, named:** DESIGNERS-PLAN §8.3's operative line —
"3 stops, segment 98dp, + PARK" — assumed the Ledger demote and is
dead. The demote's geometry (segment 98dp, cap 90dp) does not carry
into this band in any form. Likewise Razor's §4b addendum target
(V-DEMOTE × G-DISSOLVED, segment 98dp) is superseded by the same
two verdicts that produce this section.

**Rail and cap (Razor §0.4 base, §8.3 as amended):** a flush
recessed rail at the screen foot, full margin width, **52dp** tall,
radius 16, recess fill, a 1dp letterpress light-line on its top edge
so the recess reads pressed in. Track inset 4dp on all sides →
usable track 342 × 44dp at the 390dp reference width. The cap is
the rail's only raised object: radius 8 (a key), cap fill, its lower
2dp band one step below cap fill — the rail has no wells, so the
skirt is drawn as the cap's own bottom band. No shadow, no
elevation anywhere in chrome; depth is tonal only.

**Operative geometry (REGISTER, V-TAB figures):** **segment
85.5dp, cap 77.5dp** — step travel 85.5dp, DECK→LEDGER end-to-end
256.5dp. PARK costs a **flat 48dp** under every variant (44dp zone
+ 4dp notch, §6.7).

> **Geometry flag — stated, not forked.** 4 × 85.5dp = 342dp fills
> the usable track exactly; a further flat 48dp for PARK does not
> also fit inside the same 342dp. Razor's filed arithmetic for
> four stops + PARK (§3.1 of razor.md; Mikro §12) resolved this by
> shrinking the segment to 73.5dp *after* subtracting PARK. The
> REGISTER instead registers both the 85.5/77.5 figures **and** the
> flat 48dp, and §1.2's stacking law says rail stops are
> start-anchored and a verdict changes the trailing zone's content,
> never its spacing. This section drafts to the REGISTER's values
> as ordered and does **not** silently choose 73.5dp, drop PARK's
> 48dp, or widen the rail. The reconciliation — which surface the
> 48dp is taken from — is owed as a REGISTER amendment by
> Sana/Ren and is carried as unresolvable in my letter to Sana.

**Stop words:** printed on the recess, plate style (Anonymous Pro
700 10sp, +2.0 tracking), centred in each segment — printed stops
on a strip; selection is carried by cap + position + word together,
three channels. No icons, no badges, no counts on any stop; the
Ledger stop in particular carries no badge, count, dot or "new"
marker (the ruling: the Drawer never summons him). Unselected words
rest at the recess's verified muted tone (day #5F5856 / night
#9C8E84 — Floor's contrast fix; hierarchy comes from the cap's
presence, not from starved words). Floor's formal dissent stands
with its reversal condition (§11-R18): one observed
mis-tap-by-destination on device → the words promote to 15sp.

**Touch:** the target is the full segment × the rail's 52dp; the
44dp cap is visual only (the §11-R17 touch/visual split). No
touchable in chrome is under the 48dp floor in its touch area.

**Travel (Mikro §7, behaviour on Razor's layout):** everything
answers on DOWN — the pressed word inks 55→100 the same frame; if
the pressed stop is already selected, the cap's bottom band steps
one tone down for the press duration (the cap cannot translate
inside a flush rail). The cap does **not** depart on DOWN: it
departs on release (tap) or follows the finger 1:1 (drag,
grabbable, detent tick 1ms at each stop centre crossed). Travel is
KIN-NAV — 180ms + 60ms per stop crossed (adjacent 240ms, four-stop
end-to-end 360ms), critically damped, **zero overshoot past the
target stop**. The departing word stays full ink until the cap has
fully left its segment; the target word stays at rest ink on the
recess until arrival. Content (KIN-TAB-IN) starts **on the seat
frame, never mid-flight** — for the whole travel the old screen is
simply still there. A drag ending between stops seats to the
nearest stop; the cap never parks between words. On the ARRIVED
frame, together: target word re-inks in cap-content tone, origin
word drops to rest ink on the recess, content begins, feel 4ms —
navigation is not a throw and never takes the 8ms size.

**Armed marker — form, never orange.** Ren's ruling stands (§0):
no orange in chrome, no exceptions; the R13 exception request is
refused and does not reopen here. While a session is armed/running,
armed state reads by **form**: a filled-centre marker in ink/glyph
tone, printed at the DECK segment's position in chrome — it does
**not** ride the cap (Mikro) and is visible whatever stop the cap
rests on. Steady, never blinking or pulsing; it fires on at the arm
commit's arrival frame and off at session end's arrival frame.
Absent when no session — absent, not dimmed: absence renders as
absence. (Mikro's Ø3dp orange lamp and Razor's filed lamp are
superseded by this paragraph; the lamp's position and timing law
survive, its colour does not.)

**Dead branches, one line each:**
- **V-KILL — dead:** three stops, no record surface anywhere, and
  chrome carries nothing of the record; killed by his keep-the-tab
  verdict (§0, 05:41).
- **G-KEPT — dead:** Guard as a pushed spec plate off the Shield
  word `GUARD ›`, never a rail stop; killed by the dissolution
  ruling — its board, FIX path and plate survive inside PARK
  (§6.7), only the room dies.
- The named collapse is not a branch: Guard as a fifth rail stop
  (5 stops + PARK, 58.8dp segments, the longest word not fitting
  its own segment) was never buildable and is not priced again.

### 6.5 Chrome under session

The rail **locks mid-session** (HOLD absolute, §6.1) and the header
band persists unchanged — **geometry never jumps**: the rail's block
swaps its content to the hold state, the frame does not move, the
band does not reflow, first content does not shift by a dp
(Raster's chrome-invariance). The cap rests at the stop the session
was sealed from; the armed marker (§6.4) is the only chrome element
whose state differs from rest, and it differs by form alone. No
drawer, no detour, no mid-session decision is offered by any part
of chrome, and a refused press is answered with total stillness —
chrome does not flinch when asked. Chrome unlocks on session end,
on that event's arrival frame, not before.

### 6.6 Next-firing — in chrome only

**Law (§0.1-7, adopted):** the next-firing fact has exactly one
home in the app, and that home is chrome. No screen body renders
it, no row repeats it, PARK does not carry it.

**Its home is the Deck chrome sentence (§2.5):** on the Deck, the
chrome carries the live state word — in the FASTED prime,
`FASTED BLOCK · 05:00` at 15sp — the loadout's name and its firing
time stated once, in words. Everything else that looks like this
fact is a different fact and stays in its own home: the Deck's
NEXT slot (§2.5) carries the primed loadout's word alone, with no
time; Shield's routine rows (§3.4) print days · loadout · duration
and the in-window state word, and **never** the next firing time;
Tasks and Ledger print no firing at all. Attendance vocabulary
around firings is neutral (§3.4, §4): GHOST survives only as the
technical term for a session that never happened, never as a word
printed at him.

**One slot, two tenses, never both (§2.5):** on failure, the
failure sentence evicts the firing sentence — the slot prints the
refusal (`BLOCKING IS OFF — TAP TO FIX`, in its §2.6 form at the
slide) or the firing, never the two stacked, never the firing
carried as a subtitle under bad news.

> **Slot flag — stated, not resolved.** §2.5 assigns the Deck
> header's right end to this live state/firing sentence; §6.2,
> under the Guard ruling, assigns the header's right end to the
> Guard line. Both texts are filed, neither yields, and the 48dp
> band has one right end. This section does not stack them, shrink
> either to plate size, or give one of them a second line — each of
> those would be an invented resolution. The claim is carried as
> unresolvable in my letter to Sana; until Ren/Sana rule, a builder
> implements §6.2's Guard line as the band's standing right end
> (it is the all-screens fact) and §2.5's sentence as the Deck's
> chrome sentence in its §2.5 slot, and treats any render that
> puts both in the band at once as a build error.

### 6.7 PARK — the invocation contract

*This subsection states the contract. The detail mechanics —
resistance curve, notch behaviour, drawer micro-states — are
Mikro's draft (mikro.md §§8–9), which governs where this contract
is silent; nothing here re-specs them.*

**What PARK is:** a utility end-detent inside the track at the
rail's right end, after LEDGER — a **position, not a stop**: no
segment rhythm, no destination word printed in the zone (Mikro §8:
the zone reads as rail — recess fill, a 1dp end-wall line, and the
single 6dp grip notch at ink-35 are its only marks). It is where
the rested user puts the machine down between runs: the repair
board, sound and theme live behind it, and nothing behind it is a
daily journey. PARK costs a flat **48dp** under every variant
(44dp zone + 4dp notch; REGISTER) — subject to the §6.4 geometry
flag, which this contract does not settle.

**Invocation — arrival IS the invocation.** The cap is dragged
past LEDGER into the zone: rising resistance in the last 12dp, the
grip notch stepping ink-35 → ink-70 as the cap crosses the zone's
inner edge, a detent tick at zone entry pitched *below* the stop
family — it is not a stop and must not sound like one. On the
cap's seat against the end-wall (soft detent, 4ms, duller than a
stop seat), the drawer rises on that same frame. **There is no
second tap, no handle tap, no confirm.** A tap on the PARK zone
does the same (cap glides, drawer rises). The second lawful path
is the Guard line's tap while unsealed (§6.2). No other surface
invokes PARK, and in any branch without the dissolution PARK does
not exist at runtime — build-time absence, never a greyed zone.

**While open:** the cap stays parked in the zone for as long as
the drawer is open — the drawer is visibly the cap's cargo. The
drawer has exactly **two rest heights, closed and open** — no
half-detents; a drag released above 40% of open height seats open,
below seats closed. Its grab strip's *touch* height is 48dp
(REGISTER touch floor; Floor's fix) — the visual score line is
unchanged by the taller target.

**Dismissal is one gesture with leaving:** any stop press or a cap
drag out of PARK dismisses the drawer and travels the cap on the
same input; drag-down past 40% and a tap on the scrim above the
sheet dismiss to the same closed seat. Leaving PARK and dismissing
the drawer are never two gestures, two animations owned by two
systems, or a state where the cap is home and the drawer still up.

**Contents — the contract (detail in the ruling, §GUARD-RULING 1):**
the drawer's header is `GUARD`, with `LAST PROVED <instant>`
beneath it — the instant's only home. Then, in order:
1. **Permissions** — the full board, one row per permission or
   interceptor: its state word from the Watch Store
   (GRANTED / MISSING / STALE · RUNNING / PAUSED / OFF) and a FIX
   key **only** where action is needed. FIX opens the exact Android
   pane. On return, each line re-strikes only when its own fresh
   query lands; the repaired line alone gets the firm click. No
   fixed stagger, no restored banner.
2. **Sound** — the existing sound controls/readouts that used to
   sit in Guard.
3. **Theme** — an AUTO readout naming the routine or schedule
   that owns it. No manual theme pill is resurrected, here or
   anywhere.
4. The static spec plate may sit at the drawer's foot as identity
   — furniture, never a reason to visit.

PARK is the **sole detailed Guard home**: Shield keeps the working
lists (apps, sites, loadouts) and gains no second permission board
— the CHAIN question is settled for this branch. **First run**
opens PARK directly and walks the permissions there;
discoverability is designed into first run and into every slide
refusal, and needs no permanent fifth stop. **While sealed, PARK
cannot open** (§6.1): no invocation path answers, and the drawer
does not tease a closed state over a running session.

### 6.8 Acceptance checks (this surface)

1. Header band is 48dp with the hairline at y48 on every screen,
   sealed and unsealed; sealing, opening PARK, and a Guard lack
   each move no frame in chrome by a single dp.
2. Left end of the band prints the maker's mark and nothing else,
   in every state. No pill, badge, theme word, HELD count or screen
   name ever renders in the band.
3. Guard line: whole state prints exactly `GUARD 4/4`; a lack
   prints the first lack in board order in the form
   `GUARD 3/4 — ACCESSIBILITY OFF`; the denominator is the PARK
   board's 4 everywhere. Removing colour from a render loses no
   Guard information — the words carry it alone.
4. Guard state traces to the Watch Store only: no chrome code path
   runs its own permission or interceptor check, and an unproved
   or stale reading never renders as `4/4`.
5. War line: `186D`, any NEET day-count, and any `TODAY` hours
   total appear nowhere in chrome, in any state — grep-clean. The
   fallback convention renders only on Sarthak's explicit order.
6. Band geometry as registered: four stops DECK · SHIELD · TASKS ·
   LEDGER, segment 85.5dp, cap 77.5dp, PARK flat 48dp — with the
   §6.4 flag on record; a build that silently substitutes 73.5dp
   segments or drops the 48dp fails this check until the REGISTER
   is amended. Stop words fit their segments; the Ledger stop
   carries no badge, count, dot or marker.
7. Travel: DOWN answers in-frame by word-ink only; the cap departs
   on release-or-drag, overshoots no target stop, and content
   starts on the seat frame and never mid-flight; no cap ever
   parks between words.
8. Armed marker: present only while a session is armed/running,
   steady, at the DECK segment position, independent of cap
   position — and orange appears nowhere in chrome in any state,
   marker included (pixel-audit the band in day and night).
9. Sealed chrome: stops, PARK and the Guard line answer a press
   with total stillness; PARK cannot be opened; a mid-session
   blocking death changes nothing in chrome — the finish receipt
   is its only telling, and the session books broken.
10. Next-firing renders in the Deck chrome sentence only: Shield
    rows, Tasks, Ledger and PARK print no firing time; on failure
    the failure sentence evicts the firing sentence — never both.
11. PARK: one drag to the end-detent invokes the drawer on the
    seat frame with no second tap; the drawer has exactly two
    rest heights; leaving PARK and dismissing it are one gesture;
    FIX opens the exact Android pane and a repaired line
    re-strikes only on its own fresh query; `LAST PROVED` prints
    in PARK and nowhere in chrome.
12. Shield carries no permission board: the detailed Guard board
    exists in PARK only, in renders of every screen.
