> (DESIGNERS-PLAN §9, "Conditions carried"; my part file, liv.md

*existence was his verdict; permanence is the ruling; handsomeness

## 1. THE VISUAL DISCIPLINE — WHAT SURVIVES OF THE BOUND BOOK

The ruling names the survivors in one line — *flat ruled surfaces,
real words, no orange, no charts* — and kills the book's judgement
machinery. Taken piece by piece, from the book's actual craft:

### 1.1 Surfaces — one ground, no furniture

- **The Drawer is printed on the ground, everywhere.** Landing,
  month index, month view, search results, detail, empty states —
  all on ground (day `#E4DFDA` / night `#171412`), directly, the way
  the Bound Book printed its leaves on ground. There is no raised
  surface, no panel, no card, no fill anywhere in the Drawer.
- **The one carved object is the search field** (§3) — a true recess,
  because finding is the Drawer's one act and the hand needs one
  place to land. Everything else is flat print separated by hairlines
  and air. Borders are punctuation; a drawer of bordered boxes is a
  form, and forms are where beauty goes to be processed (my Guard
  audit, liv.md §1.5 — the sentence survives its screen).
- **Night is the locked staircase, unlit.** Text tiers only: primary
  `#D2C6BB`, secondary `#9C8E84`, tertiary `#776A60`. The glyph tier
  `#EDE3D8` is **not used** in the Drawer (Old Tom's tab law, carried:
  glyph is for instants — the Deck hero, the receipt's strike; a
  standing surface that shouts all day is a scoreboard warming up).
  Cream `#FFE4CF` never appears, in any role. No surface rises above
  ground by any step — the old Ledger's night face (fifteen cream
  bricks on a brown card, a radiator at midnight) is this surface's
  founding anti-render, and §4-K1 is written to catch its return.
- **Accent: zero.** Orange is COMMIT fill, and the Drawer commits
  nothing — not the caret, not a link, not the `EARLIER` word, not
  the selected month. Zero orange in every state, including focus
  and drag, verified the Shield way: with colour removed, nothing
  about this surface changes its meaning.

### 1.2 Rules — the ruling is the furniture

- **One rule weight:** 1dp hairlines, full content width, in the
  Bound Book's filed furniture tones — day recess `#D8D1CB`, night
  cap `#4A423C`, both at 100%. Rules are furniture; they sit in the
  furniture register, never in text-primary. (This is a deliberate
  narrowing of the book: its *double* rule was struck in primary
  tone because it was a closing mark. The Drawer has no closing
  marks — see §1.4.)
- **Baseline law (the book's, verbatim in effect):** every slip's
  last text baseline sits **12dp above its closing rule**. Entries
  are read *on* the ruling, like handwriting on a ruled line — the
  rule belongs to the entry above it, never floats between entries
  as a generic divider.
- **Where rules fall:** under each date heading (§2.2 — the shelf
  edge), under each slip, under each month row (§3.2), above the
  `EARLIER` foot row. Nowhere else. A rule with no entry above it
  is wallpaper and is not drawn.

### 1.3 Print — the mixed grammar, ladder only

Three voices, all from the locked ladder (104/72/50/22/15/10 — no
Drawer element invents a size):

- **Words are Atkinson; numerals are Anonymous Pro; month names are
  Sora.** The Bound Book's mixed grammar, kept whole: inside a
  B1 line, word matter sets Atkinson, number matter (finish times,
  durations, dates) sets Anonymous Pro 700 — a slip reads as print,
  not as UI text.
- **B1 15sp is the Drawer's body size** — titles, meta lines, the
  `ALL MONTHS` and `EARLIER` words, empty-state sentences. Hierarchy
  inside the surface is weight, family, and tone — never size
  (Set's law). Title Atkinson 400 primary; meta: words Atkinson 400
  secondary, numerals Anonymous Pro 700 primary.
- **Plate (Anonymous Pro 700 10sp, +2.0, lh12, tertiary) is
  furniture only:** the `STORED` header and the date headings
  (§2.2). Plate carries no fact he needs to *read* — a heading is
  an address, and addresses are furniture; the tasks under it are
  the truth, at 15sp. This is the plate law's own distinction
  (furniture vs truth), applied, not an exception to it.
- **Real words, whole sentences, no codes.** No `DONE n`, no
  tallies, no abbreviations invented for width. The two empty
  states print exactly as Floor files them — `NOTHING STORED YET`,
  `NOTHING STORED UNDER “<query>”` — set Atkinson 400 15sp
  secondary on bare ground at the first-content beat, with nothing
  else on the screen but the header and the field. Absence gets a
  sentence and a page of air, never an icon, an illustration, or a
  zero. (No AI imagery anywhere, per standing law — and none is
  needed: an empty drawer is drawn by its ruling, not pictured.)

### 1.4 What does NOT survive — killed by name, so it cannot creep back

- **The Double Rule — DIES.** In the book it was bookkeeping's
  closing mark (1dp·4dp·1dp, primary tone): *this day is finished
  and bound.* The Drawer closes no days. A double rule under a
  date group would grade that date as complete — the day-book's
  judgement machinery wearing a hairline. Every rule in the Drawer
  is single, furniture-toned, identical in form over every date,
  forever. My own Second Line (liv-ledger.md §2) is therefore
  **not** carried into this surface, and I kill it here myself
  rather than leave it for the merge to find: DAY CLOSED is not a
  Drawer entry (Floor §7.8), and its mark does not enter by the
  back door of craft.
- **The Bound Leaf and the fore-edge — DIE.** Leaves turned; the
  Drawer scrolls and pages (Floor §7.3: 50 + `EARLIER`). No
  fore-edge strip, no leaf-turn travel, no binding line at x=40 —
  a spine with an empty gutter is book cosplay on a surface his
  words made a drawer, and the margin stays the app's plain 20dp.
  The fore-edge's one honest organ — a printed foot row you act on
  to go further back — survives transposed as the `EARLIER` row
  (§3.3), a word on the ground, not a strip.
- **The Press — NOT THIS SURFACE.** My press (liv-ledger.md §1)
  belongs to the finish receipt, a moment in §5's territory. The
  Drawer receives no press, no deboss, no strike frame, no knock.
  Storage does not celebrate being stored into. (The receipt's own
  fate is §5's merge, not §7's; nothing here rules on it.)
- **Heroes — NONE.** No 50sp anything in the Drawer: no total to
  hero, no count to crown, no month name above T1. The biggest
  type in the Drawer is a month's name at 22sp (§3.2), and it is
  big because it is a *title of a shelf*, not a number.

### 1.5 Motion, sound, stillness

- **Record class, whole surface:** entrances 0ms; no stagger, no
  fade-in of rows, no count-up (there is nothing to count), no
  idle motion of any kind. Paging appends at final values. The
  destination itself travels under the app's Panel Travel Law and
  nothing inside it travels at all.
- **Sound: none. Haptics: none.** Reading and finding have no
  moving part and earn no voice (Fenja's silence argument, adopted
  for this field's whole surface). A drawer does not speak when
  opened; it is simply open.
- **Sealed face = the same face, as print.** While sealed (Floor
  §7.1) the search field renders exactly as at rest — same recess,
  same hint — and answers nothing: no dimming, no lock glyph, no
  greyed theatre. Inert things in this app render as print (the
  PARK law), and the Drawer's sealed rendering is that law applied
  to a field.

## 2. THE ONE MOMENT — *THE KEPT SHELF*

Every screen in this plan carries one moment that is beautiful *at
rest* (my standing law: a moment that needs motion will be friction
by month two). §3.5's Printed Day was the Shield strip's — a 24h ink
skyline whose familiarity compounds instead of decaying. The Drawer
needs its sibling, and the ruling's own phrase names its material:
*a quiet drawer of ruled slips.* Made concrete:

### 2.1 What it looks like

He opens the Drawer — a month later, per his words — and the screen
is **a shelf of his own finished work, ruled like a ledger page and
labelled like a cabinet:**

- The current month's slips stand newest-first under their date
  headings, each slip the same form — title in Atkinson, one meta
  line in the mixed grammar, a hairline 12dp under its last
  baseline — so a full month reads as *ruled handwriting*: line
  after line of print he recognises because he wrote every word of
  it when the tasks were alive.
- The ornament is **repetition with variation**, the quality my
  audit found in the bag's typeset tray (liv.md §1.2): identical
  form, different words, mass accumulating downward. Nothing on the
  surface decorates. The accumulation *is* the decoration — the
  only screen in the app that gets handsomer the more it is used,
  and plainer the less, in exact proportion to his life in it.
- At the top, the newest shelf label; at the foot, one word —
  `EARLIER` — and more shelf below it. The Drawer never ends in a
  summary. It ends in an offer to keep looking.

### 2.2 Date headings as shelf labels — the composition

- A heading is **a label sitting on its shelf edge:** the date in
  plate — Anonymous Pro 700 10sp, +2.0, tertiary — `THU 29 OCT`,
  left-aligned at the margin; **S8** below it a full-width 1dp rule
  in furniture tone (the shelf edge); **S12** below the rule, the
  first slip. The label is small *on purpose*: it is the tab on a
  drawer divider, not a headline — his eye passes it the way a
  hand passes a labelled divider, by recognition, not by reading.
- In **search results**, the same label carries the full address —
  `THU 29 OCT 2026` (Floor §7.5: year on every result) — same
  plate, same rule. A found slip must be datable at a glance or it
  is a rumour (Old Tom's §1, agreed in his own words).
- A **declared word** (REST / ILL / FAMILY, when its storage lands —
  Floor §7.7) prints beside its heading, right-aligned on the
  label's line, **Atkinson 700 15sp primary** — his authored word
  at body size in full ink, while the date stays plate. Truth is
  never whispered (the Guard board's law): the furniture whispers,
  his word does not. The word gets no rule, no box, no second line.
- A date with no finished tasks prints **no label and no rule** —
  there is no shelf for a day that holds nothing (Floor §7.6).
  The shelf has gaps the way a real cabinet does: only where a
  divider would label nothing.

### 2.3 Why it survives month six — and month sixty

The Printed Day compounds by *fluency*: a map read daily becomes a
street he knows. The Kept Shelf compounds by *authorship*: every
line in it is a thing he did, in words he chose. There is no trick
to wear out, no novelty to decay — the face on day 1 (one shelf,
two slips) and the face in year three (shelves of shelves, reached
by search and the month index) are the same face at different
fullness, and fullness is the reward his own sentence described:
*"maybe after a month and look at what i did."* The moment is that
look, landing. If the form is right, opening the Drawer in month
six feels like opening a drawer in a well-made desk and finding it
exactly as kept as he left it — that feeling is the retention
mechanism, at storage scale.

## 3. THE WORKING PARTS, AS PRINT

### 3.1 The search field — the Drawer's one recess

- **Geometry:** full content width, **48dp** tall (Floor's touch
  floor is the visual height here — this field is not on the ARM
  path and does not borrow the sticky-field's 36/48 split), radius
  **8dp** (key radius; panels are 16 and this is not a panel),
  recess fill (day `#D8D1CB` / night `#100E0C`).
- **The recess is true, not claimed:** the app's recess grammar —
  catch hairline on the bottom inner edge in raised tone, per
  §1.4 materials law; at night the recess sits a true step below
  ground (`#100E0C` under `#171412`), so the field sinks after dark
  instead of smudging. No border loop, no shadow, no focus ring,
  no glow: on focus the field does not change its face at all —
  the caret is the only focus signal, because this surface does
  not perform its states.
- **Print:** the hint `TYPE TO FIND A TASK` (the ruling's exact
  words) sets in **plate species** — Anonymous Pro 700 10sp, +2.0,
  tertiary — so the hint can never be mistaken for typed text in
  either mode (Fenja's species separation, Rung 3, adopted; her
  11.5sp size is off-ladder and the ladder wins: plate is 10sp).
  Typed text sets **Atkinson 700 15sp** ink/text primary — body
  size, real weight, same species as the slip titles it searches,
  so eye flicking field ↔ results never changes voice. **Caret:
  ink, both modes, always** — a caret is a piece of text, it
  commits nothing, and orange appears nowhere in this element in
  any state (Fenja's caret ruling, adopted whole).
- **No count, no badge, no match numeral ever enters the field**
  (Floor §7.5/§7.6; the field finds, it does not tally).

### 3.2 The month index — addresses, set as titles

- The `ALL MONTHS` entry row on the landing (Floor §7.3) prints
  Atkinson 700 15sp, primary, on the ground, closed by a furniture
  rule — a word you act on, set at the size words you act on get.
- Each month row: the month's name as a **title** — **Sora 600
  22sp (T1)**, primary, year never omitted: `OCTOBER 2026`. P64
  pitch, full-row touch, closed by its furniture rule. No count,
  no total, no chevron pill, no trailing anything: the row is an
  address, and an address is complete when it names the place.
  Newest month first (Floor §7.3).
- Why titles, not plate: the index is the one Drawer surface he
  *reads as a list of places* — a year of his life in twelve
  lines. Set at T1, a long index reads as a contents page; set in
  plate it would read as telemetry. The contents page is the
  handsomer object and the truer one: these are chapter names,
  not labels on telemetry. (The date headings stay plate because
  they recur daily and must whisper; a month occurs once and may
  speak.)

### 3.3 The foot — `EARLIER`

The paging word prints as a **foot row**: furniture rule above it,
Atkinson 700 15sp secondary, P48, centred on nothing — left at the
margin like every other word in the Drawer. It is present only
when an earlier page exists (Brief B's full-page rule, per Floor
§7.3). No spinner, no progress fraction, no "showing n of m" —
the Drawer does not report its own size, to him or to itself.

### 3.4 Detail — the slip, pulled out

Floor's detail content (§7.4), in print form:

- Still the ground, still flat — a pulled slip is laid on the same
  desk, not raised onto a tray. `BACK` prints Atkinson 700 15sp at
  the head; it is the page's only action (Brief B).
- **Title: Atkinson 700 22sp (T1)** — the active-task rung; the one
  place in the Drawer a single task speaks at title size, because
  here it is the whole page's subject.
- Facts below in B1 mixed grammar, estimate and actual as two
  separate ruled lines (never a delta — Floor §7.4), then the
  stored notes in Atkinson 400 15sp, and the substeps — **done
  steps struck, exactly as Tasks strikes them.** The Struck Line,
  my Tasks moment, survives lawfully here and only here in the
  Drawer: the detail is where a finished task shows its evidence,
  struck step by struck step, and it is the detail page's whole
  ornament. Nothing else on the page decorates.

## 4. ACCEPTANCE — THE CONDITION IN RENDER FORM

My condition (§0) is enforceable only as render evidence. Sana
folds these into §7.11 beside Floor's seven and Old Tom's tests;
they grade the **face only** and block nothing about storage.

**The evidence set (all six, day and night where stated):**
(R1) landing, current month, ≥3 date groups incl. one heavy day
(8–10 slips) · (R2) landing at night · (R3) month index, ≥12
months · (R4) a full month view (≥50 slips, one `EARLIER` spent) ·
(R5) search results across two years + the failed-search state ·
(R6) detail of a task with struck substeps.

**Kill conditions — any ONE kills the face (not the Drawer):**

- **K1 — the radiator returns.** Any night render shows a fill
  lighter than ground anywhere except the search recess's single
  step down; cream used as a fill; the glyph tier used; a surface
  raised above ground. The old Ledger died of exactly this light.
- **K2 — the card relapse.** Any border loop, panel, chip, pill,
  or raised container around a slip, a group, a month, or the
  detail in any render. If the build needs boxes to look
  organised, the composition has failed and boxes are the
  confession.
- **K3 — hierarchy collapse.** With colour removed and at arm's
  length: shelf labels, slip titles, and meta lines must still
  separate by family, weight, and tone alone, in that eye order —
  label recognised, title read, meta last. If everything sits at
  one visual weight (my wave-1 Ledger charge: a spreadsheet with
  good manners), the face is TIDY, and TIDY fails §4 outright.
- **K4 — the cold photograph test.** R1 shown cold, unnamed: if it
  reads as *a settings list* or *a dashboard* rather than as a
  drawer of ruled slips — a kept, printed, physical thing — the
  One Moment is not landing and the face fails.
- **K5 — judgement creep in render.** Any total, count, chart,
  percentage, streak, orange, or "new" marker visible in any
  render. (Floor's law too — double-locked on purpose: it is a
  truth failure and a beauty failure in the same pixel.)
- **K6 — fullness does not compound.** R4 (a full month) must be
  *handsomer* than R1's sparse top — mass reading as a kept shelf,
  not as clutter or hoarding. If full reads worse than empty, the
  form decays with use, which is the one failure a storage face
  cannot survive: its whole life is filling up.

**Verdict words are my audit's, strictly:** BEAUTIFUL passes;
TIDY fails; UGLY fails. I grade the renders myself when they
exist, in the open, on the wall — the condition is mine and I do
not delegate its eye.

## 5. THE FALLBACK — IF THE FACE FAILS

The face dies; **nothing else does.** Pre-specced so a §4 failure
is a swap, not a crisis:

**THE RESIDUE FACE** — the cheapest honest rendering of Floor's
§§7.2–7.6, with no composed moment claimed: `STORED` plate header,
the §3.1 recess field (finding is function, not composition, and
it stays), date headings as bare plate labels with **no** shelf
composition, slips as single B1 lines over furniture rules, month
index in B1 instead of T1, detail unornamented. It is lawful,
permanent, findable — and it makes no beauty claim, so it cannot
lie about one. The Drawer ships in residue form while a new face
is designed against the named §4 failure.

What the fallback is **not**: not a return to the Shield foot
(the demote is dead by his verdict), not a plainer *tab* with the
storage trimmed to fit it, and never a reason to delay, prune, or
hide a single stored row. **The face is what's at risk. The
permanence is not.** If the residue face is what ships, §7 prints
that plainly — a storage drawer in a plain face, honestly labelled
— and my condition stands open against the next attempt.
