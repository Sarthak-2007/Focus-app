## 4. TASKS — the daily screen

**Floor:** DESIGNERS-PLAN §5 (druck.md as amended by §11), as amended by
Improvers ladders 11–14 (Horiz, Fach, Taste, Zeile), the composer
settlement (IMPROVERS-INVENTIONS §4: the Vernier composite), and MaxImpl

Register's value governs and the disagreement is flagged to Sana, never
forked. **One sentence:** Tasks is where the rested user writes the day
down — one total at the head, one task in the light, a queue that
descends in ink, finished work dropped below a ruled floor into storage
— and it offers the sealed user nothing at all.

### 4.1 Sealed-state law (this screen)

While a session is sealed: Tasks offers no verb and no composer. The
ACTIVE block's `DONE` and `CUT` are **absent**, `START` is **absent**,
the composer is **absent entirely** — not greyed, not disabled-looking,
absent; a dead control is a mid-session decision wearing a costume.
Step rows and queue rows are inert (no toggle, no promotion). The
slab's plate word reads `RUNNING` — a state word, flipped on arrival —
and **no session time prints anywhere on this screen, ever**: Zeile's
filed `RUNNING · 12:04 LEFT · ENDS 14:35` line is dead; the Deck owns
time, from its own source (§2.5), and Tasks does not borrow it. The
Footing, the Light Well, the ACTIVE block's facts and the foundation
with its done rows still print: facts stay readable, verbs leave.
Everything returns on session end, in place, with no animation
ceremony. HOLD is enforced below the UI as everywhere: completion,
promotion and filing are repository operations, and the repository is
the boundary the seal closes (§0, standard 2).

### 4.3 The Light Well and the queue rows (F-ROW, ruled)

The well is not an object. It is a descent: the ACTIVE task sits in
the light (the one raised panel, §4.4), and each queued row steps
down in **ink tone and left offset only** — no fills, no bands, no
cards in the queue. Both channels agree, always, from the same depth
value. Tone = position in queue, nothing else; no progress is encoded
in it. Done tasks do not continue the descent — they leave the well
entirely (§4.5).

**Tone ladder (title ink; duration/index ink = one step further down
the same ladder):** Queue 1 night `#C9BBB0` / day `#333130`, indent
4dp · Queue 2 `#B7A797` / `#474441`, 8dp · Queue 3 `#9C8E84` (ink2) /
`#5E5A56` (ink2), 12dp · Queue 4 `#887A6E` / `#756F6A`, 16dp · Queue
5+ `#776A60` (ink3) / `#877F77` (ink3) — **floor**, indent capped at
20dp. The floor is a legibility law, not a style: below ink3 a 15sp
title stops being readable at 3:30 AM, so depth past 5 is carried by
the row's index numeral alone. The descent is capped, stated, and
never faked darker.

**FIGHT, ruled:** queue row height — Floor's **48dp** over Zeile's
44dp one-liners (ladder 14, Rung 3). Floor's annex convicted the 44dp
row by name (a tappable row 4dp under the touch floor); the Register
fixes the touch floor at 48dp everywhere. **48dp stands.** Zeile's
density model otherwise survives whole: one-liners, sub-steps only
inside ACTIVE, promotion by tap, CUE dead.

- **Row: 48dp exactly, at every depth.** One module, learned once:
  index `#02` (Anonymous Pro 700 15sp, ink one step below the title,
  32dp slot at the indented edge) · title Atkinson 700 15sp, **one
  line**, ellipsis · duration `25M` (Anonymous Pro, right-aligned to
  the right margin, ink one step below the title). All three share
  one baseline. Hairlines between rows only — none above the first,
  none below the last.
- **Zero verbs.** No DONE, no CUT, no START, no step text, no
  fraction, no progress mark. A queue row states three facts —
  address, name, length — and offers one gesture.
- **The one gesture:** tap anywhere on the row → it becomes ACTIVE
  (§4.4); the previous active task returns to depth 1 as a queue row.
  Height animates 280ms, Curve A. There is never a second expanded
  block, in any state, ever. Press state: on DOWN the row's title and
  duration ink step one tone *up* the ladder for the duration of the
  press — no fill, no ripple, no scale. Letterpress does not bounce.
- **The fallback lie, killed (Linea + Zeile, Arbitration 6):** when
  nothing is active, the head of the queue is **not** labelled ACTIVE
  and carries no START. Index 0 renders as what it is — the next
  task — under the joint rename to `NEXT`, made on this screen and
  the Deck's task line in one commit; a rename one surface makes
  alone is a worse lie than the shared one. Nothing is active until
  he arms it.
- **Row minutes stay raw** — no rounding, clamping, or display floor
  anywhere in the rows, so the Footing = Σ printed rows holds exactly
  (Horiz ↔ Zeile, closed by mail). Row duration form is raw `{minutes}M`;
  the Footing alone splits to H/M at ≥60 — the same number spoken by
  a person reconciles to the minute.

### 4.4 The ACTIVE block — the screen's one panel

Exactly one exists at any time. Raised fill, radius **16dp** (panel
freeze), full measure, padding 20dp. Plate row: left `ACTIVE` (plate,
ink2) — or `NEXT` under the fallback law above, `RUNNING` while
sealed (§4.1) — right `#01 · 25M` (Anonymous Pro, ink2): position +
this task's duration, their only home inside the block. Title:
Atkinson 700 **22sp**, ink1, max 2 lines, ellipsis on line 2 (the
merged floor's 22sp stands over Druck's filed 20sp and Zeile's Sora
SemiBold 20sp — 22 is the ladder step, and the title is body text,
not display).

**Sub-step rows (inside ACTIVE only, nowhere else on the screen):**
40dp rows, full panel inner width — the whole row is the tap target.
Numeral `01` Anonymous Pro 700 15sp, ink2, right-aligned in a 24dp
column; gap 12dp; step text Atkinson 400 15sp, ink1, single line,
ellipsis; all elements on one baseline. No hairlines between steps —
air and the 40dp rhythm separate them. `ADD STEP` line after the last
step, same geometry (`+` in the numeral column, ink3; `ADD STEP`
plate, ink3): tap → the line becomes a single-line field for one
step's text; return adds the step and restores the line, keyboard
staying up. A task with zero steps shows only the `ADD STEP` line —
no empty scaffolding, no placeholder steps.

**The strike (completion language, spent once per fact):** tapping a
step row completes that step — the strike is **drawn**, a 1.5dp rule
in ink2 drawn left→right across the step text's exact measure in
180ms, Curve A, with the hit-stop: the strike pauses at ~70% for
**40ms** before completing (Zeile Rung 2; the hand feels the step
*land*). On the same frame the text steps ink1 → ink3 and the numeral
is replaced by a 10×2dp tick in ink2 in the same column. Haptic 8ms;
un-tapping reverses all of it (strike erases right→left 120ms,
haptic 4ms). **Steps-done is not task-done:** completing the last
step escalates everything *except* the title strike — the **50ms
hit-stop freeze** (the Register's spectacle ledger: final sub-step
50ms — Druck's filed 110ms escalation freeze is superseded by the
ledger), the step's strike drawn at 2dp, a hairline *bracket*, a
third haptic pulse (20ms), and the action row's DONE word inks
ink2 → ink1. The parent title is NOT struck. The strike is the
strongest completion mark this screen owns and it is spent once per
fact.

**Steps are rows (Rurik, MaxImpl — the plumbing under the strike):**
steps are child rows with identity (`task_sub_steps`: id, taskId,
ordinal, title, isDone), never parsed from the task's notes text.
The fraction is counted from rows (`done / size`); with zero step
rows the fraction is 0f for every status — a step-less completed task
never pretends it did steps. Notes are freed: free text that parses
as nothing; writing a note can never move a fraction. Toggle is by
step **id**, not position. `updateTaskDetails` loses its power over
steps entirely. **SPLIT is killed outright as a verb** (a task is
split by composing two); the repository guard survives as plumbing —
auto-split is refused on a task with any done row, in the layer every
caller shares. Steps-as-rows lands in migration **9→10** (Register,
Data — append/child tables only; Rurik's filed v7→v8 packaging is
superseded by Voss's arbitration as registered, flagged to Sana).

**Action row: 48dp,** baseline-aligned, inside the slab. `DONE` left
and `CUT` second (word style, Sora 700 15sp +0.5; DONE ink1, CUT
ink3; 48dp targets) — the merged floor's `CUT` replaces Druck's filed
`DEL`; deletion is clerical, it gets no ceremony and no colour.

**FIGHT, ruled (F-START):** the START key — Druck's **124×48dp**
orange key against Zeile's re-argued filled key at 44dp tall, min
width 96dp (ladder 14 peak; Improvers Arbitration 5 ratified the
*filled* form — orange is a fill, never text — but filed it at 44dp).
**Ruling:** the filled key stands; its height is Druck's 48dp,
because the Register's touch floor is 48dp in both dimensions and
44dp is the exact violation Floor convicted in Zeile's rows. Zeile's
re-argue wins the fill and loses the height. The key: right edge at
the panel's inner right edge, **124×48dp, radius 8dp**, fill `accent`
(day `#F55314` / night `#C74814`), label `START` in `accentLabel`,
centred. Skirt: 3dp band of `accentSkirt` under the face; press
answers on DOWN, face travels down 2dp (skirt 3→1dp) in 70ms, fires
on release, slide-off cancels, haptic 10ms on fire. The duration is
**not** printed on the key — the plate row's `25M` already states it;
a second printing is a second rendering. START is the screen's
single orange, its only filled element in the action row, and it
exists only inside ACTIVE. No sound is played here — the arm owns
its own sound.

**START is a ticket, not a pair of callbacks (Rurik's Arm Ticket):**
the tap calls one repository operation, `cueTask(taskId)`, which
promotes-to-ACTIVE and returns a single-use Arm Ticket (taskId,
frozen title, estimatedMinutes, issuedAt); navigation to the Deck is
a *consequence* of the ticket appearing, never a second callback.
The Deck's task line reads the ticket, not three racing flows. The
arm consumes it: `startSession` gains a real `taskId` — the session
binds to the task by id, not by a copied title string; a second arm
attempt with a consumed ticket is a no-op. Sessions started without
a ticket carry `taskId = null` — honestly unbound. The per-second
tick credits the session's `taskId` and does nothing when there is
none; its silent promotion of the first QUEUED task is deleted —
queue order changes only through queue operations. The `taskId`
column rides migration **8→9** (Register, Data).

**DONE is an event (Rurik — DONE semantics):** the DONE press fires
one `@Transaction` on DOWN — it does not wait for the choreography.
The transaction inserts an append-only **completion event**
(taskId, sessionId — the session active inside the transaction, null
never 0 when none — completedAtMillis, titleSnapshot,
estimatedMinutes, actualSecondsSpent, stepsDone/stepsTotal counted
from the step rows at that moment), flips the task to COMPLETED, and
promotes the next QUEUED task. A unique index on the event's taskId
makes the double-DONE race (Deck DONE and Tasks DONE wired to the
same call, plus a double-tap) a constraint absorbed by the
transaction, not a double count. Kill the app mid-drop and on return
the task is done — no resurrection, no half-seal. The choreography
plays over a fact already durable: the title's drawn strike (1.5dp,
ink2, 180ms, **only** DONE ever strikes the title) concurrent with
the **drop** — the slab translates down, height collapsing, the
completed row landing below the foundation line (§4.5); the fall is
**ease-in only**, scaled by duration (260/320/400/480ms, 1–3dp
compression on landing; a 90-minute landing lands twice). An
ease-out fall is the cheap failure. The DONE landing freeze is
**40ms** — one of the Register's four freezes; a fifth requires
naming which dies. Landing haptic tiered by minutes (≤25M 14ms ·
45M 18ms · ≥90M 24ms). The next queued row rises into the slab in
the same window; if the queue is empty, the slab is simply absent
afterwards. **Un-complete is symmetric:** it deletes that task's
completion event and restores status in the same transaction —
every downstream surface drops the task automatically, because none
of them stored it. Completion-events land in migration **9→10**.

### 4.5 The foundation line and DONE below it — and the Drawer feed

The floor of the well, drawn as print draws a floor: a **2dp rule,
full measure** — the heaviest line on the screen, used exactly once.
Above it, 8dp of air and a plate row: left `DONE`, nothing at the
right — **no done count prints on this screen** (Zeile Rung 4 kills
the header's `DONE n`; the merged floor carries no count here, and
the Drawer law below forbids turning storage into a score). Below
the rule, the country of finished things. If there are no done
tasks, the plate row, the rule and the section **do not exist** —
absence renders as absence; no empty foundation is drawn under a
well that has never dropped anything.

- **Done rows:** 48dp (the row module's height, §4.3 — Druck's filed
  44dp done rows rise with the module), same geometry — index slot,
  one baseline, duration right — so the eye reads them with the
  module it already learned. Title Atkinson 400 15sp (**Regular** —
  done tasks lose the bold), ink3, with the drawn strike through its
  measure at rest (1.5dp, ink2 at 60%) — the same mark as §4.4, at
  rest: this is where struck titles live. Index and duration ink3.
  Indent 0 at every position — below the foundation there is no
  depth; finished is finished. Hairlines between done rows only.
  Done rows are inert on this screen: no tap, no verbs, no un-done
  here. The record does not negotiate.
- **What this section is:** today's completions, derived from the
  completion-event stream — tasks whose latest event is a
  completion, filtered to the event's calendar day. It is a working
  surface, not the record.
- **The Drawer feed (his verdict, §0 — the record side):** DONE's
  completion is what **feeds the Drawer**. The same completion event
  that drops the row below the foundation is the one pipeline by
  which the finished task is **stored permanently** in the Drawer
  (the kept Ledger tab, §7): title, category, notes and step rows
  exactly as stored, estimate, actual seconds, completion timestamp
  — never auto-deleted, browsable by month (labels as addresses,
  never totals), searchable across all years. The Drawer reads the
  completed task rows themselves, never a session summary string.
  Tasks prints no total, count, streak or grade over any of this:
  finishing a task here is filing it, not scoring it. If a task is
  deliberately reopened elsewhere, it leaves the Drawer; completed
  again, it returns under the new timestamp (the symmetric
  un-complete, §4.4). **No test-day apparatus touches this feed:**
  Zeile's Day Sheet GATE species — a pinned test bulkhead with
  attested completion — does **not** enter this spec. TEST-day
  marking is dead (§0 verdicts; the Drawer ruling): a task finished
  on a test date is stored under its date like any other task, and
  nothing more is inferred.

### 4.6 The composer — the Vernier composite (settlement, §4 of IMPROVERS-INVENTIONS)

**The settlement, stated whole:** the composer slot was fought
between Fach's Row Zero (the composer as the queue's unborn row) and
Taste's Vernier (the duration bank as a measuring rail). **The
Vernier composite survives; the Row Zero branch is dead.** Row Zero
as a complete branch dies on TRUTH (its solidify flat-beat performed
"task became this row here" while the add could land off-screen) and
on its push gesture (undisambiguated from scroll, no affordance).
**Carried from Row Zero into the composite, binding:** the
**proto-row** (the field built as a queue row, on Zeile's axis grid —
axis column + gap, first glyph at the same x as every queued row),
the **ink block caret**, the **ghost→stamp sequential hand-over**
(ramp- and contrast-cured), the **focus guarantee**, and the **card
punch**. **Not carried:** the push, the flat-beat, the Thumb Reel and
its pitch set, the embalmed fallback flag. The Vernier carries the
minutes instrument and the slot. The composite is cheaper than
either complete branch — Row Zero's R0-2/R0-3 never build.

**Task #0 (Alba, MaxImpl — the plumbing under the field):** the
draft is not two `remember` cells. It is one permanent singleton
**DRAFT row** in `focus_tasks` (status `DRAFT`, queueOrder −1) — the
composer is *editing task #0*. The status column is a String: **no
migration, no DB version bump** (Register, Data). Field and rail
render the draft row; every keystroke and gate move writes through
to it, so the draft survives process death, rotation, navigation and
collapse — durability as a storage fact, not a composition promise.
Submit is a flip, not an insert: `fileDraft()` — one `@Transaction`
— flips the draft (status → ACTIVE if no ACTIVE exists, else QUEUED;
queueOrder computed inside the transaction), respawns a fresh empty
draft **born with the filed row's minutes** (duration persists
across adds because the new draft inherits it), and returns a
`FiledReceipt` from inside the transaction — the stamp and the
single commit sound render the committed fact, never a prediction.
The leaks are plugged by name: the DAO ordering gains a DRAFT arm
(harmless last, never interleaved); the Deck checklist flow filters
`resolvedStatus != DRAFT` (the one real leak — a permanent draft
must never appear on the Deck or suppress its empty state; this is
acceptance test, not footnote); seeding runs before `ensureDraft()`
and its emptiness test ignores DRAFT. A DRAFT can never be promoted,
ticked, completed, counted in the Footing, or enter the queue's
summed set.

**The proto-row (collapsed state — one sticky line, 56dp):** sticky
at the foot of the page, full measure, on `ground`, separated from
the list by a 1dp hairline across the full measure at its top. It
never floats, never carries a fill of its own. Inside: the field —
48dp, radius 8dp, fill `recess` — carrying the ghost position in its
axis cell: `#04`, the position this task *will* occupy
(`orderedQueue.size + 1`, %02d), computed from the same list the row
will join; if the queue changes, the ghost re-strikes instantly, no
animation. Placeholder `Add a focus task…` (Atkinson 400, ink3);
typed text Atkinson, ink1. **Caret: the ink block caret** — 7×18dp
block, radius 1dp, fill ink, drawn from the real layout result on a
520ms blink; a frame with no layout result draws no caret that
frame. The caret is never orange, in any state, ever — the screen's
one orange is START's. Duration echo right inside the field: `· 25M`
(Anonymous Pro, ink2) — the selected duration, stated where it will
act; **the echo lives in the collapsed line only** (Arbitration 1 +
the settlement: in the expanded state the gate's position is the
rendering, and the echo hides — one fact, one rendering, per state).
**ADD as a word** right of the field (52dp, 48dp target, word
style): field empty → ink3, **disarmed** — tapping it requests focus
and does nothing else (an ADD that fires on nothing is a lie; its
paleness is the statement, with Fach's empty-submit signal: a low
thud, never silence, never a message); field non-empty → ink1,
armed, answers on DOWN, fires on release. Focus, keyboard and
duration persist across adds — batch filing is type → ADD → type
with no re-tap of field or gate between tasks. Blur keeps the draft
(it is a row; blur cannot throw it away). The keyboard is never
summoned uninvited.

**Focus treatment (drawn on the field, nowhere else):** three
agreeing changes, reversible on blur over 180ms — fill deepens
`recess` → `recessFocus`; the field's bottom catch-line steps
ink3 → ink1; the duration echo inks ink2 → ink1. No outline ring, no
glow, no orange. If you cannot tell the field is focused by looking
*at the field*, the treatment has failed. **The focus guarantee:**
the rail and its zones are non-focusable for input/IME focus and
never call `clearFocus` — selecting a duration can never collapse
the composer mid-entry.

**The Vernier (focused state — the slot appears):** on focus the
composer grows upward into the list's inset: field line 56dp · gap
8dp · **the slot, 52dp** = **116dp total** (the filed 128dp bank
form — 56 + 8 + 64dp well — is superseded by the settlement; the
well, its four caps, and Druck's UNPLANNED cap-touch gap die with

### 4.8 OPEN — not spec: Zeile's queue re-planner

**Filed OPEN, deliberately unbuilt.** Zeile's invention (Day Sheet
§3) proposes a queue that re-plans itself at session end —
deterministic, **order-only**: it may permute, never edit — never
titles, never minutes, never content, never across a Gate — with
its changes arriving as timed slips in a closed vocabulary stating
what moved and why; an unchanged queue earns silence. The
examination verified the order-only charge (no prescription hides
in it) and attached two cures (PROMOTE becomes a pin the sort
honours; the pre-plan order is stored against day-roll starvation).
**None of that is spec here.** The re-planner's authority is
Sarthak's question, filed to Ren in protocol form (Zeile, mail/ren
0715): *"When a sealed session ends, should the queue re-plan
itself — started work moving up, your estimates and titles never
touched — and just show you a slip saying what moved and why?"* —
options: re-plan itself / propose it / never. It waits on his word.
Until his answer lands, the queue order on this screen changes only
through his own operations (file, promote, complete, cut), each of
which already exists — and this section builds no engine, no slip,
and no sort key in anticipation of an answer he has not given.
