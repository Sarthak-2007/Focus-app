## 1. The ladder — six sizes in the entire app

Locked foundations, honoured literally:
- **Families:** Sora = display · Atkinson Hyperlegible = body/interface ·
  Anonymous Pro = numbers/telemetry.
- **Real weights only:** Sora 400/500/600/700 exist. Atkinson exists ONLY at
  400/700. Anonymous Pro exists ONLY at 400/700. Any spec below naming
  Atkinson or Anonymous Pro at 500/600 is a lie and is not in this table.
- **Bands:** heroes 44–112sp · titles 18–26sp · body 13–15sp · labels 9–11sp tracked.
- **Separation law:** no two tiers within 1.4× of each other.
- **Micro-label plate (wave-1 C8, locked):** Anonymous Pro Bold 10sp,
  +2.0 tracking — everywhere, no exceptions.

### Why only six sizes — the arithmetic, not a taste call

The plate locks the label tier at **10sp**. Work upward under the 1.4× law:

- Body ÷ 10 must be ≥1.4 → body must be ≥14sp. Body band caps at 15.
  **Two body sizes are mathematically impossible** (15/13 = 1.15).
  One body tier only.
- Title-low ÷ body must be ≥1.4. With body at 15, title-low would need
  ≥21sp, and a second title tier would need ≥29.4sp — outside the title
  band (max 26). **Two title sizes are mathematically impossible.**
  One title tier only.
- Heroes can hold at most three tiers inside 44–112 at ≥1.4× steps
  (112 → 80 → 57 → 41✗). Three hero tiers, no more.

So the maximum lawful system is **3 heroes + 1 title + 1 body + 1 label
= 6 sizes**. Every role in the app lives on one of these six rungs.
Roles are separated by family, weight, case and tracking — never by
inventing a seventh size. That is the whole system, and it is frozen.

### The ladder

| Tier | Size | Band | Ratio to tier above | Jobs it carries |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** | **104sp** | hero | — (top) | Across-the-room time. AOD only. |
| **H2** | **72sp** | hero | 104/72 = **1.44** | The one big number in hand. |
| **H3** | **50sp** | hero | 72/50 = **1.44** | The second number — totals & countdowns. |
| **T1** | **22sp** | title | 50/22 = **2.27** | Every title in the app. |
| **B1** | **15sp** | body | 22/15 = **1.47** | Every sentence, row and readout in hand. |
| **L1** | **10sp** | label | 15/10 = **1.50** | The engraved plate. Nothing else. |

**Minimum ratio in the ladder: 1.44 — every joint clears the 1.40 law
with margin.** No joint sits exactly on the floor, so rounding, platform
scaling or a future weight change cannot quietly push a pair illegal.

The deliberate jump is H3→T1 (2.27×). That cliff is the design: heroes are
numbers you read at a glance or across a room; titles are words you read
in hand. Nothing lives between them, so the eye never has to decide
whether a 30-something is "a big title or a small number." It is always
obviously one or the other. Beauty here is that certainty.

## 2. The complete type ramp table — every text role in the app

Tracking in sp as written in Compose styles. Line height given where it
matters (display numerals and reading text); single-line plate/row styles
are trim-both centred and carry no meaningful line height.

### Heroes

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Tracking | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AOD remaining | Sora | 700 | 104 | −3.0 | 100 |
| Running time (in-session clock) | Sora | 700 | 72 | −2.0 | 72 |
| Deck total readout | Sora | 700 | 72 | −2.0 | 72 |
| Ledger hero stat | Sora | 700 | 72 | −2.0 | 72 |
| Queue total (Tasks header numeral) | Sora | 700 | 50 | −1.5 | 52 |
| Overlay countdown numerals (hold-to-break, airlock) | Sora | 700 | 50 | −1.5 | 52 |
| AOD elapsed (second reading) | Sora | 600 | 50 | −1.5 | 52 |

One duration hero in hand is **always 72** — Deck total, running time and
the Ledger's hero are the same fact-shape ("how long"), so they wear the
same size; the tired eye learns one place and one scale for it. 104 is
reserved for the one screen he is not holding (AOD, across a dark room).
50 is for numbers that are important but are *not* the session: a queue
sum, a countdown he is acting on, elapsed time riding second on the AOD.

### Titles — one tier, three weights/cases do the separating

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Tracking | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Section titles | Sora | 700 | 22 | −0.3 | 26 |
| Routine names | Sora | 600 | 22 | −0.3 | 26 |
| Task title — ACTIVE task only | Atkinson | 700 | 22 | 0 | 27 |

### Body tier — rows, sentences, readouts (all 15sp)

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Tracking | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Body / explanatory copy | Atkinson | 400 | 15 | 0 | 22 |
| App names (Shield rows) | Atkinson | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |
| Routine meta line (`MON–FRI · STUDY · 4H 00M`) | Anonymous Pro | 400 | 15 | 0 | 20 |
| Task titles — queued (non-active) rows | Atkinson | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |
| Meta lines (all other secondary lines) | Atkinson | 400 | 15 | 0 | 20 |
| Header war line (`186D TO NEET · TODAY 2H30 / 12H`) | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | +0.5 | 20 |
| NEXT ribbon line (`NEXT 09:00 · STUDY`) | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | +0.5 | 20 |
| Deck rim numerals (major detents) | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | 0 | — (canvas, centred) |
| Button words (START, ADD, DONE, slide label) | Sora | 700 | 15 | +0.5 | 20 |
| State words (SHUT / PEEK / OPEN, ON / OFF, HELD, LIVE) | Atkinson | 700 | 15 | +0.5 | 20 |
| Composer field text (what he types) | Atkinson | 400 | 15 | 0 | 22 |
| Composer duration readout | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 15 | 0 | 20 |
| Search field text | Atkinson | 400 | 15 | 0 | 22 |
| Ledger receipt lines (when · how long · loadout) | Atkinson / Anon Pro | 400 | 15 | 0 | 21 |
| AOD task line (third reading, when present) | Atkinson | 400 | 15 | 0 | 20 |

Two rulings baked into this tier, stated plainly because they are fights:

1. **Meta lines and state words do NOT shrink.** The instinct to whisper
   secondary text at 11–13sp is dead under this ladder — 13/10 = 1.30 and
   11 is not a tier at all. It is also right: state words are the second
   channel for every position/colour state, and meta lines carry truth
   (routine durations, the war line). Essential text sits at 15sp or it
   does not ship. Hierarchy inside the body tier comes from weight
   (400 vs 700), family (Atkinson vs Anonymous Pro) and ink tone — never
   from size. That restraint is what makes the screen look calm instead
   of speckled.
2. **Rim numerals are 15, not a dial-only size.** A 28 or 32sp rim would
   need its own tier and would break the ladder (32/22 = 1.45 but
   50/32 = 1.56, 32 sits in no band — heroes start at 44). At 15sp
   Anonymous Pro Bold on the major detents only, the rim reads as a
   printed instrument scale, which is what it is. Minor detents carry
   no numerals at all — one fact, one rendering: the readout at 72 says
   the total; the rim only orients the hand.

### The plate — one style, no exceptions

| Role | Family | Weight | Size | Tracking | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Micro-labels (ALL: nav words, model plate `MONOLITH ML-01`, section eyebrows, badge text, footer legend, AOD armed mark text) | Anonymous Pro | 700 | 10 | **+2.0** | 12 |

Nav words are plate text. There is no separate "nav size," no 11sp badge
size, no 12sp caption anywhere in this app. If a word is set in the plate
style it is decoration-grade furniture (a printed stop, a model number);
the moment a word carries a decision or a state, it is promoted to the
15sp body tier. Plate = furniture, body = truth. That one sentence is
the enforcement rule for every future screen.

## 3. Wave-1's frozen scale {84, 32, 20, 16, 13, 11, 10} — SUPERSEDED

Explicitly, size by size, so nobody resurrects it as precedent:

| Wave-1 size | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 84 | **Kill** | Rogue hero. AOD already owns 104; 84/72 = 1.17 and 104/84 = 1.24 — illegal against both neighbours. |
| 32 | **Kill** | In no band (titles end 26, heroes start 44). It was a number pretending to be a title. |
| 20 | **Superseded by 22** | Lawful only against a 14sp body (20/14 = 1.43) whose own joint to the plate sits exactly on the floor (14/10 = 1.40). The 22/15/10 chain buys margin at every joint (1.47 / 1.50) for the same visual step. |
| 16 | **Kill** | In no band, and 16/13 = 1.23 against its own body size. The original violation. |
| 13 | **Superseded by 15** | 13/10 = 1.30 against the locked plate — illegal the day the plate was frozen; wave-1 never checked its own scale against its own plate. |
| 11 | **Kill** | Not the plate (plate is 10, locked). Two label sizes 11/10 = 1.10 apart is the exact disease the law exists to kill. |
| 10 | **Keep** | The plate. The one survivor, unchanged: Anonymous Pro Bold, +2.0. |

Net: wave-1 froze seven sizes of which **one** survives contact with the
locked bands, the plate, and the 1.4× law applied simultaneously. This
ladder {104, 72, 50, 22, 15, 10} replaces it in full.

Also superseded in passing: the Synthesis nominal sizes that predate this
ladder — queue total 44sp → **50** (44 was lawful alone but leaves no
room for the countdown tier beside it: 50/44 = 1.14 if both lived),
Ledger hero 64sp → **72** (64 is illegal against both 72 and 50), ACTIVE
task title 20sp → **22**. One ladder, no local exceptions per screen.

## 5. Audit of Type.kt's existing styles — keep / re-spec / kill

One line each. Families below are post-Round-0 truth (real TTFs are now
in the tree); the style *specs* are what is on trial here.

| Style in Type.kt | Verdict | Line |
|---|---|---|
| `DeskClockTextStyle` — Sora 700, 104/100, −3.0 | **KEEP** | The one fully lawful style in the file; becomes H1, AOD remaining, unchanged. |
| `DialClockTextStyle` — Sora 700, 58/56, −2.0 | **RE-SPEC → 72/72, −2.0** | 58 is a rogue hero (72/58 = 1.24); at 72 it becomes H2 — Deck total + running time, one style for both. |
| `BentoMetricTextStyle` — Sora 700, 36/38, −1.2 | **KILL** | 36sp sits in no band (27–43 is empty by law) and its tile-grid screen role is already dead; Ledger secondary stats move to the 15sp meta line, hero to H2. |
| `PillBadgeTextStyle` — Anon Pro 700, 11.5/15, +0.3 | **RE-SPEC → plate: 10sp, +2.0, lh 12** | 11.5 exceeds the label band and ignores the locked plate; re-specced, this style IS the micro-label plate and the only label style. |
| `DrawerHeaderTextStyle` — Sora 700, 21/25, −0.5 | **RE-SPEC → 22/26, −0.3** | 21 is in-band but is a second title tier in waiting (22/21 = 1.05); folded into T1. |
| `PrimaryButtonTextStyle` — Sora 700, 15/20, −0.2 | **RE-SPEC → 15/20, +0.5** | Size was already lawful (B1); negative tracking on capped button words is wrong — buttons get the +0.5 caps treatment. |
| `AppTitleTextStyle` — Atkinson 700, 15/20 | **KEEP** | Exactly B1-bold; it is the row-name style (app names, queued tasks) as specced above. |
| `SubtitleExplanationTextStyle` — Atkinson **Medium**, 13/18 | **RE-SPEC → Atkinson 400, 15/22 — and the fraud, named:** | **This style requests `FontWeight.Medium` from a family that has no Medium. Atkinson Hyperlegible exists only at 400 and 700; the registration in this same file maps only those two, so "Medium" silently resolves to Regular while the code tells every reader it is a medium-weight style. The style lies about its own weight.** Its 13sp is also a rogue second body size (13/10 = 1.30 vs the plate). Re-specced, it becomes the single body style; the name should die with the lie — call it `BodyTextStyle`. |
| `Typography.headlineLarge` — Sora 700, 24/28 | **KILL** | A second, competing title size (24 vs T1 22 = 1.09). Section titles use T1 via DrawerHeader's successor; this slot maps to it or dies. |
| `Typography.titleLarge` — Sora 700, 18/22 | **KILL** | 18/15 = 1.20 against the body tier — illegal; no role in the app needs a small title, titles are 22 or they are body-bold. |
| `Typography` mapping — displayLarge = 58 style, displayMedium = 104 style | **RE-SPEC** | The Material slots are inverted (the "Large" display is the smaller number); re-map after the re-specs above so slot names stop lying too. |
| `Typography` bodyMedium + bodySmall — both = the same Subtitle style | **RE-SPEC** | Two slots pretending to be two tiers while rendering one; bodySmall has no lawful size to hold (next rung down is the plate) — map both to the re-specced 15sp body and document them as one tier, or leave bodySmall unassigned. |
| `Typography` labelLarge/Medium/Small — all = PillBadge style | **KEEP (as one)** | Honest in effect — there IS only one label tier; after the plate re-spec all three slots point at the plate, which is the truth. |
| Legacy aliases `SpaceGroteskFamily`, `PlusJakartaSansFamily`, `JetBrainsMonoFamily`, `DmSansFamily` | **KILL (names)** | Four names claiming fonts that are not in this app (DmSans even aliases Atkinson). Call sites migrate to `SoraFamily` / `AtkinsonHyperlegibleFamily` / `AnonymousProFamily`; Type.kt should tell the truth about the product it typesets. |
| `TightNumericPlatformStyle` + `CenteredNumericLineHeightStyle` | **KEEP** | Honest plumbing; applies to hero tiers H1–H3 only, never to reading text. |

**Audit summary:** 13 style objects audited — 3 keep, 6 re-spec, 4 kill —
plus 4 lying alias names. After this pass Type.kt holds exactly six sizes,
three families, and no weight that does not exist as a real font file.

## 6. The fight I picked

With the plate, not against it — against **shrink-as-hierarchy**, the
habit (in wave-1's scale, in Type.kt's 13sp "subtitle," in every 11.5sp
badge) of making secondary text smaller instead of quieter. Under the
locked law it was always illegal arithmetic; I am saying it is also bad
design for this user: at 3:30 AM, tired, the difference between 15sp bold
and 15sp regular is legible, and the difference between 15sp and 11sp is
a squint. This system separates by weight, family and tone on a single
body size, and lets exactly three hero sizes carry all the drama. If a
future seat wants a seventh size, the burden is theirs: name which rung
dies to make room, because the bands and the 1.4× law say one must.
