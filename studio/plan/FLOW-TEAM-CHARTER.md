# THE FLOW TEAM — charter (Sarthak's order, Oct 29, 22:09 IST)

His words: "Add a team so i dont have to say this stuff myself out loud."
Origin: he had to spell out, one by one, things that should never have
needed saying — the pill, editable times, and now: when blocking starts
the app just turns black on the same screen; nothing flows anywhere.

## Remit

The Flow Team owns WHAT HAPPENS WHEN — every state change in the app,
walked from his chair, in his day, before he has to ask:

- When blocking starts, what does the app become?
- When he picks the phone up mid-session, what is he looking at?
- When a break / a peek / a finish / a routine firing begins and ends,
  what changes, what shows, what speaks?
- What does the phone do all night on the desk?

If a moment in the app's life has no designed answer, that is a Flow
Team find. If the answer exists but he had to discover it by asking,
that is also a find — the team was late.

## Boundary with the Small Things team

Small Things owns small lived utilities — a pill, a warning, a printed
clock, a duration that should be his to set. The Flow Team owns whole
moments and transitions — what the app BECOMES. A find that is a surface
goes to Tief's register; a find that is a moment goes to the Flow
Register. Ren arbitrates collisions; neither register may eat the other.

## Roster (8 seats)

- **Ablauf** (lead) — owns the register; walks the whole day end to end.
- **Anfang** — beginnings: every start (arm, seal, routine fire, break,
  peek, app open) and what the first second looks like.
- **Mitte** — the middle: running states, what stands while nothing is
  being touched.
- **Ende** — endings: finishes, expiries, denials, the moment after.
- **Uhr** — the clock face: the desk clock / AOD in all its states
  (idle, running, break, night), burn-in, what it shows and hides.
- **Schirm** — the screen itself: which surface a moment lives on, and
  what happens to the surface that was there before.
- **Uebergang** — transitions: the movement between states — timing,
  order, what is never shown mid-passage.
- **Alltag** — the day-walk: his actual day hour by hour (03:30 wake,
  meditation with the phone across the room, fasted block, gym, sadhana,
  day blocks, SLEEP 20:00) held against every flow the app has.

## Teeth

The team keeps plan/FLOW-REGISTER.md. Every build programme and every
design wave answers the register item by item — built, scheduled, or
ruled out by name. Nothing dies by silence. The lead files an
obviousness pass over any flow that is about to ship: if Sarthak would
have had to ask for it, it does not ship until it is in.

## First commission (seated the same hour as the charter)

THE BLOCKING-START FLOW: (1) the code truth of what happens today when
a session seals, second by second; (2) the designed flow — what the app
becomes, with the desk clock's role settled (it exists, it shifts
against burn-in, it shows session time + progress + task — but today it
only opens by hand; sealing never brings it up); (3) register seeds for
the other moments (routine fire, break start/end, peek start/end,
finish, wake at 03:30, app opened mid-session) at one-line depth each.
