## 0. The laws that govern every bench

1. **NO RENDER, NO CURE.** A cure claimed without a render is not
   a cure. Code verification (Noir's, CURE-VERIFY §2) proves the
   code half and nothing else. Every verdict below is one of
   three words: **PASS** (seen in a render / capture / recording),
   **FAIL** (seen, and a charge is named), **UNPROVEN** (the owed
   evidence has not landed). There is no fourth word.
2. **Stills judge pixels. Nothing else.** A still can pass or fail
   LIGHT (composition) and SURFACE. It cannot pass MOTION — that
   needs a frame sequence or a capture with timestamps. It cannot
   pass SOUND — that needs a recording or a call-site audit read
   against §3's table. Any motion or sound "pass" issued from a
   still is struck.
3. **Shape by time, colour by amplitude** (Haut §7, the one feel
   law). Meaning lives in timing — segment lengths, gaps, order,
   duration — because timing is the only channel every phone
   renders faithfully. Amplitude and pitch-shading may colour a
   meaning, never carry it. A Temple shape that reads wrong on a
   motor with no amplitude control is a rendering bug, not a feel.
4. **The Throw is bit-identical.** The seal's voice is the shipped
   1-second buffer (360 Hz body + deterministic-noise bolt at
   +30 ms + two-comb stone room, golden SHA-256 `d204b95a…`, one
   call site). Temple claims *nothing* at the seal's voice, in
   either direction: no Temple seal sound, and no complaint that
   the seal does not sound Temple. Worlds speak in the approach.
5. **The Fremd test, on every render.** Colours stripped, names
   covered: what remains must read as a stepped stone front —
   beam, standing slab with a ring cut into it, bands of
   inscription between shadow joints, a notched frieze, three
   steps with a bar across the top one, doorways along the base.
   If a render could be another world in sandstone paint, it
   FAILS, however pretty. A second Fremd question runs beside it:
   can a tired man, cold, read every fact on the surface in five
   seconds, in words, with no cipher?
6. **The slowest house, never a lagging one.** Slow is a weight,
   not a delay. Any surface that makes him wait for decoration
   FAILS on that fact alone.
7. **I do not compile, and I do not build.** I judge what lands.
   A FAIL names the bench, the fact, and the source line it
   breaks; the fix is the builders' (Maurer / Setzer, disjoint
   partitions), the re-render is the lane's, the re-judgment is
   mine.

## 1. LIGHT bench

*Temple's atmosphere is carried by light doing exactly two jobs:
one pass at the seal, and one lamp spending itself across a night
session. Everything else is stillness. Light that moves for any
other reason is a charge.*

### 1.1 The lamp rake at t=0 — once, then absolute stillness

- At the seal instant (t=0, the existing hook, the Throw
  bit-identical and untouched by it) a warm gradient rakes
  **left to right, ~450 ms, once**, masked to the hero's
  hairlines and gold — light passing an inscription, nothing
  more of the screen lit than the strokes it crosses.
- After the pass: **absolute stillness.** No loop, no second
  pass, no breathing, no shimmer on the gold, no residual glow
  animation. A rake that repeats, lingers, or moves any pixel
  after ~450 ms FAILS.
- The rake is **light, never sound** (PRESENTATION §9, the Noir
  flare ruling): it adds no voice at t=0, delays nothing at t=0,
  and does not touch the seal cascade before or after it.
- FAIL facts: rake covering the whole hero as a wash (unmasked);
  rake on any non-seal event; rake direction right-to-left;
  rake present in a still claimed as its proof (a still shows
  the mask's home, never the pass — the pass is judged from a
  capture, §0.2).

### 1.2 T-2 — the lamp burns down (night, running)

The night radial glow is keyed to the sealed session's
**remaining fraction** (the Now Frame already derives it):

| At remaining | Centre | Radius | Alpha |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 (at the seal) | high on the slab, offset 0 | 1.0 × hero diagonal | 1.0 |
| → 0 (at the finish) | sunk **0 → 18 dp** | contracts **1.0 → 0.55** | settles **1.0 → 0.72** |

- **Stepping on the minute cadence, never continuous.** The lamp
  changes between glances, on the app's existing minute ticks.
  Continuous interpolation — a glow visibly crawling in a
  capture, a per-second step, any animation between steps —
  FAILS. This is the stillness law kept while the oil is spent.
- The verb is *spending*, and it is fenced: Dawn's light grows;
  Temple's is spent. A Temple night glow that brightens, rises,
  or widens as the session elapses has the wrong world's verb
  and FAILS.
- At the finish the glow settles to a **low ember ring behind
  the receipt's parchment**, then the desk clock's true black
  takes the room. The ember ring lives behind the slip only —
  it is not a frame, a halo on the screen edge, or a second
  receipt decoration.
- Judging a sealed-running still: the glow's centre, radius and
  alpha must be *consistent with the remaining fraction stated
  in that same render* (read off its own numbers). A full lamp
  over a nearly-spent session — or a sunk lamp over a fresh one
  — FAILS on its own arithmetic.

### 1.3 Day — incisions read by shape, not by tone

Daylight legibility is the chisel system (§4.1), and its token
foundation is fixed by PRESENTATION §1.4 / CURE-VERIFY §2:

- Dial fine tier at the ink's own depth: **mids = text primary
  #2B2013; minors = the same ink at 85 %; unfilled set-stone /
  well seats = the text ink at 45 %.** Any return to the old
  voices (secondary ink for mids, text-2 at 55 % for minors,
  recess tone for unfilled wells) FAILS — that wash is Ruth's
  condition 2, and it does not come back.
- The stele **stands**: slab **#E3C184** (bronze-stone), a
  measured luminance step of **1.26** off the ground #E8DCC0
  (was 1.00 — no step at all), **plus its real cast shadow**
  (§4.2, the 7 dp relief). A day render where the slab is a
  border drawn on the ground's own colour FAILS, shadow or not;
  a render with the tone step and no shadow reads as a stain,
  not a stone, and FAILS.
- The day ground, sheet and recess are **not** repainted
  (Ruth acquitted the working screens on this ground; ink on
  ground is 11.70:1, ink on the cured slab 9.27:1). A "cure"
  that repaints the acquitted ground to fix the slab is a new
  charge, not a cure.
- Night branches are **byte-identical in value** to the honoured
  lamplit read (cure values 0xD9 / 0.45 exist only behind day
  arms). Any night pixel moved by a day cure FAILS.

### 1.4 The desk clock — the lamp turned to the wall

- True black ground (the overlay's own black — not the bronze
  room, not a darkened day face).
- Numerals (elapsed + remaining, Anonymous Pro) in night
  parchment **composited to 55 %**: measured **0.201 relative
  luminance against the ≤ 0.25 ceiling**. Full-alpha numerals
  measure 0.750 and FAIL on that number alone. Anything above
  0.25 FAILS; anything at token value claimed as passing without
  the composite FAILS as an unproven claim (§0.1).
- The composite law covers the stand's hour too (DEEPEN §9.3):
  the hour inscribed at the brass stand's right end is
  glyph-sized and under the same 55 %.
- The **one brass hairline** beneath the numerals — the lamp's
  stand — is the only furniture in the dark. Any second object
  (a rule, a seal, a glow, a course) in the clock render FAILS.
- **Idle, the clock prints the device's time** — never the
  composer's dial setting dressed as a time (ST-01). An idle
  render whose hour matches a dial setting instead of the
  capture's clock FAILS as a printed lie.
- Burn-in: the glyphs shift position (the machinery already
  moves them). Judged across the idle + running pair: identical
  glyph positions in both captures is a FAIL fact to report —
  the patrol is machinery, but its absence is visible here first.

### 1.5 Night staircase — the filed numbers are the floor

PRESENTATION §10 stands as filed and is not re-negotiated at
judging: text-1 on ground **13.01:1**; leaf gold #DFAF3F on
ground **9.28:1** and the only bright warm thing in the room;
recesses read by their brass hairline (**5.06:1** on ground),
never by a luminance leap (N1/N2 steps are 1.05:1 by design);
ground→raised **1.36:1**, raised→sheet **1.29:1**; secondary text
lawful on ground (**6.10:1**) and recess (**6.41:1**) only —
**on sheet it measures 3.49:1 and is unlawful for body text**
(the sheet fence, §4.4). A render that asks me to re-derive a
filed number instead of showing the fact has not landed yet.

low pitch, hard knock, the imagined room — never duration**

every voice below is two-stage, sparse, or single-knock timing
that survives an amplitude-less motor with its meaning intact.*

## 4. SURFACE bench

*Relief is the legibility system (DEEPEN §3, §12): a world that
reads by its relief reads in any light, including none. Surface
law here is rationing — of the chisel, of depth, of gold, of
vermilion — and a ration broken anywhere is broken everywhere,
because the eye learns the ration in a day and trusts it.*

### 4.1 The chisel pair — on every incised mark, no exceptions

Every mark that must be read is **incised**, and an incision is
a **pair**:

- **1 dp dark edge on the shadow side** + **1 dp light edge on
  the lit side.**
- By day: dark edge = the text ink at the §1.3 depths (the
  mark's own tier depth); light edge = the sheet tone.
- By night: light edge = the raised tone; the dark edge holds —
  lamplight reads incisions by its rake.

Lawful homes (the list the pair is *owed* on): ring ticks at
**all tiers** (majors, mids, minors); the set stones' seats in
the course at the stele's base; course joints; the frieze's
notches and its rule; the tear's scored groove on Tasks; the
pill tablet's incised ✕; the Held Door's engraved name and its
double rules. **A mark drawn flat — one tone, one edge, a fill
doing shape's job — FAILS on any of these.** A chisel pair found
on a mark that is *not* incised by law (a button face, a toggle
tablet, printed body text) is decoration wearing the system's
clothes and also FAILS: the pair means *cut*, and it may not
mean anything else.

### 4.2 Three relief depths only — 7 / 2 / 1 dp

| Depth | Its one job |
|---|---|
| **7 dp** | the stele casts — a standing stone's shadow off the wall behind it (the hero, day and night) |
| **2 dp** | a seated stone casts small — a block set into its course; the lifted plaque in the routine editor (M-3) wears this same depth while it is off the wall |
| **1 dp** | a course joint — the shadow line between courses, and between the threshold steps |

**No fourth depth exists.** Any other shadow size, any soft
card shadow, any glow-as-elevation, any Material default
elevation leaking onto a Temple surface FAILS. Depth is
structure, rationed like the gold; a surface floating at an
unbudgeted height is furniture, and furniture is what this
world cut. (The container-leak corollary: every surface's tone
is its token's — a stock Material grey anywhere on a render is
an automatic FAIL, per PRESENTATION §12.)

### 4.3 Gold — spent only on commits: the audit list

Metal law: **bronze chooses, gold commits** (DEEPEN §13).
Gold leaf is **#BC8F26 by day, #DFAF3F by night**. The complete
list of lawful gold pixels — **any gold pixel not on this list
is a charge**, and I will audit renders against it pixel-class
by pixel-class:

1. **The bolt's grip on the Deck's slide** — the day's one gold
   spend (phase 1: the one piece of ritual metal on the screen;
   night: the leaf struck brighter on the same cap). The bolt's
   bar, channel and threshold are bronze / black-bronze — gold
   is the grip only.
2. **START on Tasks**, when a task is picked up — that screen's
   one gold spend, as rendered in phase 2 and kept.
3. **The frieze's NOW marker, with its printed hour** ("NOW
   16:04") — Deck frieze course, Shield entablature frieze, and
   the Held Door's miniature. The marker is a *position in the
   day*, spent at the one place the day is committed to print;
   the rule, the notches and the span fills are brass / bronze,
   never gold.
4. **The T-3 keystone fill at the final seal** (M-8) — one block
   left, the day's last commit: the outline keystone fills gold
   leaf and the inner double rule completes across its base.
   Before that seal it is bronze outline; a gold keystone on a
   non-final block, or gold anywhere else in the cap course,
   FAILS.

**Named non-gold, so the audit cannot drift:** the Shield
keystone and set stones (bronze — gold never enters that wall);

gold and words coincide, the words are umber / ink on the gold
fill at full contrast.

### 4.4 Vermilion — outline is the seal, filled is the wound

The pigment is **#D63E06**. The fence is absolute and has two
sides:

**OUTLINE — authentication, never alarm.** A stroked square:
the seal stone's mark, unstruck. Its lawful homes, all of them:

1. **The Deck's witness course** — beside PROOF: POSITIVE, only
   when the proof is positive. NOT HELD prints words and no
   seal; a seal beside a negative or absent proof is a forged
   witness and the gravest surface charge on this bench.
2. **The Shield's course heads** — one small outline seal before
   each register head (LOADOUT / ROUTINES / ALL APPS): the
   section stamped, not labelled.
3. **The receipt's №** — beside the number, on a *witnessed*
   finish only (§4.5).
4. **The Held Door** — one large outline seal, roughly the
   name's height, centred over the engraved name: the gate is
   sealed, not alarmed. This is the pigment's largest lawful
   area anywhere; anything larger, anywhere, FAILS.

**FILLED — the wound, and true alerts.** Full-body pigment.
Its lawful homes, all of them:

1. **The Confession, on the Deck** — one filled square before
   the broken-day line ("DAY BROKEN — …", the fact and its hour
   in primary ink, the hero slab's inner rule drawn with its
   6 dp break above the line).
2. **True alerts** — the alert states the trunk itself raises.
   Nothing Temple invents may promote itself to an alert to
   earn the fill.

**Never, under either side of the fence:** the blocking-off
sentence (a lack is a fact — full ink, no vermilion, §1 of
PRESENTATION §1.1); the Held Door's cooldown (a fact — bronze
and glyph parchment only, DEEPEN §6); warnings, counts, headers
as decoration, navigation, the colonnade, toggles, or any
surface "for emphasis". Vermilion found anywhere outside the
lists above is a charge — and outline/filled swapped (a filled
seal, an outlined wound) is a double charge, because it teaches
the eye the wrong grammar in both directions at once.

### 4.5 The sheet fence and the receipt's paper

- **Sheets carry words in primary ink and the glyph tier only**
  (night: primary 13.01:1 family, glyph 8.24:1 on sheet).
  Secondary on sheet is 3.49:1 and unlawful for body text —
  any secondary-tier word on any sheet-toned surface FAILS
  (PRESENTATION §10, N4, filed as a fence, not a failure).
- **The receipt stays paper, by argument** (DEEPEN §5): the one
  object that leaves the building — parchment strip under its
  brass rule at the head, its printed-slip character untouched
  (it is the one surface Sarthak has loved on sight; his ❤️
  outranks purity). Temple materials it; nobody redesigns it.
- On the slip: **fact rows in primary ink only**; the one quiet
  line (the WITHIN window) in the glyph tier; **FLOW slips omit
  PLANNED** (RC-02); an extension, when the session carries
  one, prints as its own fact row — "EXTENDED +7 MIN", primary
  ink (DEEPEN §10, ST-04).
- **Witnessed vs reconstructed is printed, never blended**
  (RC-01): witnessed → the vermilion *outline* seal at the №;
  recovered → the seal drawn in **brass outline, unstruck**
  (entered, not witnessed); broken → the seal **struck through
  by one brass hairline**. A reconstruction wearing the
  witness's vermilion FAILS as a printed lie, however small.

### 4.6 The palette fence

No green, and nothing close: every Temple colour passes
**G < max(R, B)** — the brass stays gold, no verdigris creeps
(phase-1 census, kept as a standing check). A single
green-leaning pixel-class in a render — a tint in a shadow, a
grey with a green cast from an unmapped slot (§4.2) — FAILS
the render until its source token is named.
