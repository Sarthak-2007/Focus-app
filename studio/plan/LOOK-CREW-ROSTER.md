# THE LOOK CREW — explicit roster for every style coordinator
(Sarthak's order, Oct 29, 22:15 IST: "tell coordinators explicitly what
subagents they need to have like the original ones and newer ones too
like finishers innovators maximiser and what not")

Every look coordinator (Kern, Morgen, Tinte, Fahne, Stein, Riss, Werk)
seats a FULL crew under them — at minimum ONE named agent of EACH type
below. Headcount beyond the minimum is the coordinator's call; an
unseated type is a breach of this order, reported to Ren, not silently
skipped. The coordinator's first letter to Ren lists their crew by name
and type.

## The original cohorts
1. **Designers** — the world's design seats: composition, surfaces,
   selection, the look's grammar carried into every screen.
2. **Innovators (the Wild)** — invention: new mechanisms AND new
   surface ideas, priced with spec; the world's soul is partly theirs.
3. **Critics** — unrestricted scope (anything: design, code, words,
   process, the coordinator's own calls); a charge files with proof
   or not at all.
4. **Improvers** — take what exists in the world and make it better,
   edge by edge; they own the second pass over everything.
5. **Maximisers (MaxImpl)** — the builders: the lane's code, written
   whole, compiled once at completion under the queue law.
6. **Concept seat** — holds the world's one-sentence idea against
   every new element: does this belong to THIS world or is it borrowed
   furniture?
7. **Humans / day-fit seat** — judges everything against his actual
   day (03:30 wake, meditation with the phone across the room, fasted
   block, gym, sadhana, day blocks, SLEEP 20:00): FITS or TAXES, on
   evidence.

## The newer cohorts
8. **Finishers** — the art team: finishing benches for light, motion,
   sound, and surface; every screen leaves their bench at gallery
   standard or it doesn't leave.
9. **Methods** — the crew's own pipeline: lane kit, compile speed,
   waste audit, merge-readiness; nothing the crew makes is wasted.
10. **Keepers of Record** — one registry seat per crew: no agent's
    work is lost, unread, unaccounted, or dropped without a ruling;
    the crew's registry lives with the coordinator's manifest.
11. **Small Things seat** — the world's lived utilities: the pill,
    warnings before things end, durations that are his to set,
    glanceable facts (works from plan/SMALL-THINGS.md).
12. **Flow seat** — the world's moments: what happens when blocking
    starts, the desk clock's behaviour, transitions, endings (works
    from plan/FLOW-REGISTER.md).

## Standing laws for every crew
- Mail compliance: every agent clears its inbox to zero; closing
  letters open with the inbox count and end with a NEXT section.
- No idle agents: a finished agent pulls the next piece of the
  coordinator's programme; sleeping with work available is a failure.
- Compile at completion; compiles only through the queue
  (tools/queue.sh v2); never gradle --stop in a lane.
- NO RENDER, NO CURE; the Fremd test on every printed word.
- Everything the crew produces is filed (manifest, registry, wall) —
  if it isn't on disk, it didn't happen.
