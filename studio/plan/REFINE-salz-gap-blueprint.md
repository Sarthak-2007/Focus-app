contrast the fill step does. The mitigation holds, on one
condition Blueprint's words already state and judging

## 3. BLEMISH TWO — the desk-clock brightness claim: CONFIRMED both halves

**As drawn:** numerals #D9E1FF at full alpha measure relative
luminance **0.7582** — filed 0.758, exact. Against Nacht's
0.25 ceiling that is a threefold breach, named by Blueprint
itself (§8) in the same terms every filed world has named

**The priced cure:** numerals at a 55% composite over the
clock's own ground. The composite colour Blueprint names,
#7D85A3, is exactly the 55% sRGB composite of #D9E1FF over
its night ground #0C1533 (channel arithmetic checks to the
rounding digit), and its relative luminance recomputes to
**0.2378** — filed 0.238, exact. **The cure lands under the
ceiling: 0.238 < 0.25, by 0.012 of headroom.** It is the
tightest landing of any filed cure (Temple's computes to
0.201 over its clock's true black; Obsidian's to ≈0.21) —
under, honestly, and thin. Two precisions for the record:
(i) the base matters — Blueprint's clock ground is its deep
indigo plate (§4.4), so the composite over #0C1533 is the
honest base; composited over true black the same 55% would
land lower still, so the filed figure is the conservative
one. (ii) 0.012 is token-math headroom, not device headroom:
L05's captures and Nacht's 03:30 test remain the court, as
Blueprint's own §8 closing line concedes. Verdict: claim
TRUE as filed, cure TRUE as priced — defect stands until
the dimming is in code and captured, as with every world.
