# SCHALL — CRAZY IDEAS FOR THE MERGED WHOLE (Batch 10, issue #15)

**Author:** Schall, crazy-ideas seat, dedicated-100 programme. Oct 30, 2026.
**Scope law:** every idea here needs TWO OR MORE Batch-10 parts (editable times,
routine editing, Session Room, no-fire fixes, the Pill, the Warrant, onboarding)
to exist at all. Single-feature ideas were somebody else's job.
**Binding limits honoured:** blocker not tracker (no scores/streaks/grades
anywhere below), no green, no AI images, nothing compiled by this seat.

## A. THE ONE-MACHINE IDEAS

1. **Session Warrant.** Entering the Session Room mints a stamped warrant card
   for that session: routine, edited times, pill armed, pre-flight passed.
   Clean finish = stamp stays whole in the Drawer. Early exit = stamp cracks.
   (Warrant + Room + Drawer)
2. **Onboarding Is A Session.** The final onboarding step arms a real 10-minute
   routine with the pill live. First-run success = the machine demonstrably
   working, not a tutorial read. (Onboarding + Routines + Pill + No-fire)
3. **Three-Physics Pill.** Session = time remaining; break = draining fill;
   peek = burns at visible 4x rate. A peek should feel expensive in the hand.
   (Pill + Editable Times)
4. **The Re-Settle.** Any time edit triggers a visible whole-machine re-settle:
   desk clock recomputes, pill re-plans, warrant re-stamps, Room breathes once.
   Silent saves are settings-app behaviour; re-settling is machine behaviour.
   (Routine Editing + Room + Pill + Warrant)
5. **Pre-Flight Warrant.** Arm runs a ~600ms self-check (accessibility alive,
   shield ticking, overlay granted). Stamp only lands if all three answer.
   Kills "CAN'T CHECK BLOCKING — TAP TO RETRY" as a category.
   (No-fire + Warrant + Onboarding)

## B. THE LAUNCHER CROSSOVER (Sarthak's Oct 30 ruling: block screen = a
## launcher you can't exit, separate screen for allowed + peek apps)

6. **The Room IS The Launcher.** When a session arms, the Session Room becomes
   the phone's home: allowed apps on one face, peek apps behind the airlock
   on another. There is no separate "block screen" anymore — being in session
   means living inside Monolith. (Room + Launcher ruling + Shield)
7. **Peek From The Launcher Costs Pill-Time.** Peeking an app from the launcher
   face spends from the same clock the pill shows outside apps — one economy,
   visible from both sides of the wall. (Pill + Peek + Launcher)
8. **Routine Change = Launcher Change.** Editing a routine re-deals the
   launcher's allowed face for that routine's hours. Monday's launcher and
   Sunday's launcher are different rooms of the same house. (Routine Editing
   + Launcher)

## C. WILDEST, STILL BUILDABLE

9. **The Machine Sleeps Audibly.** When the last routine of the day ends, the
   app plays its one power-down ritual: pill retracts, warrant files itself,
   Room dims to the AOD study clock. Day boundary as a physical event.
   (Routines + Pill + Warrant + AOD clock)
10. **Break Amnesty Window.** Editable break time unused at session end is NOT
    banked, scored, or shown as a number ever — instead the next session's
    Room simply opens one breath slower, calmer. Reward as feel, never as
    points. Blocker, not tracker — enforced in the design, not a settings toggle.
    (Editable Times + Room)
