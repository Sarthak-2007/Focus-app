## 0. Cross-cutting systems (chosen once, applied everywhere)

### 0.1 The sound engine — Orson (synths)

One `TactileSoundEngine`, two layers under treaty (Orson ↔ Ivo, agreed by
letter): Orson owns the room/engine layer; Ivo owns Tasks-local acoustics.
No event borrows another's voice.

- **Reference level:** the harmonic seal at 0 dB (gain 0.62). Every other
  sound is placed relative to it on a fixed gain ladder and takes the one
  shared room tail.
- **Silence Stack (chosen over a single binary switch):** five classes —
  S0 Absolute (AOD, proximity, DND, system mute, SLEEP loadout: no audio
  at all, not even completion), S1 Session (all non-engine sounds
  suppressed while sealed), S2 Surface, S3 the three scheduled silences,
  S4 no ambient sound ever. Queue depth 1; late events are dropped, never
  delayed. Every sound has a non-audio twin (haptic or visual), so
  silencing the engine never silences the truth.
- **Guard fork:** the SOUND EFFECTS switch changes address if Guard
  dissolves; the engine is identical under both outcomes. **His call.**

### 0.2 The light rig — Mara (light)

One source, fixed in space forever: `L = normalize(−0.42, −0.91)`. Two
pure functions only: rotary `specularAlpha(absoluteAngle)` and linear
`bandOffset = −0.35 × travelFromRest`. **Core law: light must never carry
data.** Specular encodes no duration, readiness, progress, or armed-ness —
which is why it can be trusted on every surface. It has no motion, haptic,
or sound of its own; it rides the host controls' springs and detents.

### 0.3 Numeral motion law — Aldo (typography)

Three motion classes, assigned by a figure's origin, never by taste:
**still** (facts at rest — re-strike or hard cut only), **computed**
(figures the machine derives — settle, never roll), **live** (the one
running clock family). Roll-up / count-up / split-flap as decoration is
banned under every Ledger outcome: a reel passes through values the
machine never held, and that is a lie in motion. Runner-up — uniform
split-flap everywhere — lost on exactly that ground.

### 0.4 Transition grammar — Wren (cinema)

Chrome is the camera body: the nameplate rail, nav rail, and viewport
hairline live outside every transition container and never blur, slide,
or fade. Body-text changes are hard cuts. Shared tokens: RACK 280 ms ·
VERTIGO 1050/850 ms · HELD FRAME 1200 ms · SHEET 260/190 ms · CUT 0 ms.
One blur-curve family; defocus + tone steps only; no shadows; zero orange
in chrome.

## 1. DECK

### 1.1 Duration wheel — Vega (physics)

**Chosen: the Stepped Flywheel.** 36 stops over a 315° sweep (9°/stop):
stop 0 is `FLOW`, printed as a word; stops 1–35 are 10–180 min in 5-min
steps, with a printed axis-break between FLOW and 10 so the one
non-linear jump is marked instead of hidden. One physics loop (θ, ω):
1:1 drag; viscous + Coulomb coast; magnetic detent wells that catch
below ~540°/s and blow through above it; two-overshoot seat in ≤520 ms;
the hardest fling seats inside 1,400 ms. Haptic ladder: 5 ms @ 0.35
detent ticks rising to a 20 ms @ 0.95 seat knock. Shipped silent (the
per-stop pitch ladder is a sound-on fragment, §7). One-tone recess;
knurl lit by Mara's fixed-in-space light, so a fast spin aliases into a
shimmer that reads as speed. Zero orange.

**Runner-up: drag-only ratchet wheel** — lost on arm-path cost across a
36-stop scale; a wheel you cannot fling taxes every arm.

**Standards:** worst-case arm cost ≈1.4 s of the 10 s budget; wheel input
dies on seal (HOLD); the axis-break and FLOW-as-word keep the scale
honest (TRUTH).

### 1.2 Hub / block count — Vega (physics)

**Chosen: the Counterweight Hub.** Count 1–4 rendered **only as pips** —
subitising range, no numerals, no `×N` anywhere in the app. Static
letterpress `BLOCKS` label. Press-and-release commits; drag-off cancels.
The hub is a second body on a weak torsional spring: it lags the rim ~6°,
counter-rotates, and settles upright. Under FLOW the hub visibly rests
(pips dimmed, press disabled) rather than pretending to work.

**Runner-up: Rhea's Block Stack** — the hub well as a tray of physical
slabs, one per block, dropped with stack-compression sway. It lost
because the stack re-renders duration the total readout already closes
over (one fact, two renderings), its settle (~900 ms at tall stacks)
taxes the arm path, and the tray competes with the wheel for the Deck's
space. Its tactility is preserved in §7 — it is the first form to revisit
if the hub ever feels abstract on the phone.

### 1.3 Total readout — Vega (physics) + Aldo (typography)

**Chosen: the Still Witness.** Total = block × count, computed live,
rendered in human format (`50 MIN` / `2H 30M` — never fake `:00`
seconds). It changes **only** on detent-crossing / hub-commit frames, as
a hard cut with a 120 ms ink-settle. `FINISHES` dims to ~55% while the
wheel is in motion and recomputes at the seat frame. FLOW prints `FLOW` /
`UNTIL YOU STOP`. Aldo's rim law layers on: **the rim is the block, the
readout is the total** — the wheel's rim scale carries block structure,
the readout carries only the product; neither restates the other.

**Runner-up: rolling mechanical counter** — lost on truth: a reel passes
through minute values the stepped wheel cannot set.

### 1.4 Slide-to-arm — Fen (gesture) + Rosa (checks) + Cato (rendering) + Opal (mechanism)

The centre gesture of the app, specced by four seats as four layers of
one control. The composite is the chosen form:

- **Gesture — Fen's Two-Stage Weighted Slide.** Free 1:1 travel to a
  0.68 gate (tick 6 ms @ 0.45), then a damped zone: grain haptics (3 ms
  @ 0.18 per 12 dp), ≤6 dp critically-damped visual lag. COMMIT orange
  appears only after the gate. Latch at 0.97, 90 ms snap, **120 ms
  dwell**, seal thock pair (18 ms @ 1.0 + echo @ +55 ms). Weight is
  rendered, never enforced — fast and slow arms both pass. Whole slide
  ≈ ≤1.7 s. (Fen's wave-1 velocity-gated Kindling Slide is explicitly
  killed: a pace tax is a toll on a tired user.)
- **Truth layer — Rosa's Parallel Bulb-Check.** A 3-cell check strip
  (`BLOCK` / `APPS` / `TIME`) fed by live queries that run **in parallel
  with the drag** — zero added serial time (~1.2 s typical, ~2.5 s worst
  case). Lamps flip when real data returns; there is no choreographed
  stagger. Timeout renders `UNKNOWN`, never pass. A poll detent at 35%
  of travel; a hard interlock at the 65% commit detent if blocking is
  dead: `BLOCKING IS OFF — NOTHING WILL BE HELD`. App-count and
  routine-crossing faults are advisory only — they never block the arm.
  Clock starts at the crank-release frame.
- **Rendering — Cato's Interlock Tape.** The track reads as a zip's
  tape: teeth mesh is a pure function of pull position; tooth count
  follows track width alone and **encodes nothing**. The arm callback
  fires on the garage *seat frame*, never at a threshold crossing, via a
  110 ms assist spring over the final 8%. Per-tooth haptics 5 ms @
  0.35/0.28 alternating, coalesced at 12 ms; seat knock 18 ms @ 1.0;
  velocity-tracked zip sound (engine layer, Orson). Pull in matte COMMIT
  orange; meshed teeth satin (+1 tone); matte tape. The wave-1 swinging
  pull-tab is deleted — motion without input is a lie — replaced by grip
  ribs.
- **Mechanism — Opal's Drawbar & Proof Pawl.** Under the rendering, the
  slide is a drawbar: gate, latch, and a proof pawl that seats only when
  the blocking cascade (§2.4) proves its links. The pawl is what Rosa's
  interlock pulls when proof fails.

**Runner-up: Opal's Throw Lever** — one honest mechanism, lost because a
lever is a single ballistic throw: no room for the parallel bulb-check to
report during travel, no damped zone, and it spends the Deck's one big
gesture on speed the law doesn't reward.

**Sound — Orson's Weighted Throw.** 120 ms of scheduled pre-seal silence
(blocking engages at threshold, so arm cost is zero); the harmonic seal
at 0 dB; a bolt layer at +30 ms whose gain renders the committed
duration (−8 dB @ 25 min → −5 dB @ 180 min, capped). The seal is sized
by what it seals.

**Standards:** total cold-open-to-sealed ≈ 2.5 s worst case (ARM);
nothing on the slide asks a question (HOLD); the check strip can say
`UNKNOWN` and the interlock can refuse — the slide never claims a seal
the cascade didn't prove (TRUTH).

## 2. RUNNING SESSION + AOD CLOCK

### 2.1 Running display — Pia (watches)

**Chosen: Deadbeat Jumping-Minute, Still Between Beats.** The display is
motionless for 59 s of every minute. On the minute: the remaining
numeral jumps (140 ms, 2 dp overshoot/settle), the stepped outer arc
advances, and the escape marker moves one tooth — all on one frame.
Remaining is the sole hero: minutes-only while ≥10 min remain; per-second
`MM:SS` hard-cut only in the final 60 s, where seconds are the news.
Elapsed, ends-at, task, and block are each rendered once, demoted.
Session Cloister: nav and editing are sealed; the running Deck has no
doors. Zero orange. Recessed readout well on the locked night staircase.

**Runner-up: Continuous Tourbillon Sweep** — lost because continuous
rotation reads as a loading spinner in peripheral vision, spends
attention and battery every second, is the worst possible AOD burn-in
shape, and its crossfading digits break finale readability.

**Sound — Orson's Escapement in a Held Room.** A −46 dB seeded bed ramps
in over 2.5 s post-seal; minute ticks alternate 620/576 Hz tick-tock at
−24 dB; block tocks at −14 dB; the final 60 s subdivide; a proximity
fade-out stills the room as he nears the phone; on AOD the tick is a
ghost — visual only. Runner-up block-only ticks is kept as the
degradation mode.

**Language — Suki's Plain Count, Banked Abort.** One clock, human
sentences; seconds appear only when seconds decide something. Abort
language names the banked state, never a bare "quit."

### 2.2 Completion — Fen (beat) + Orson (sound) + Wren (exit)

**Chosen composite.** The beat is keyed to the persisted
`RUNNING → COMPLETED` transition by `sessionId`, written **before** any
animation, so process death or reopen cannot re-fire it — the completion
happens once, ever (Fen). Sequence: **400 ms of absolute stillness** at
zero (Wren's Held Frame envelope extends the held moment to ≈1.2 s
total), during which Orson's **Room Stops**: the bed fades 300 ms, 400 ms
of true silence, then the completion pulse alone at 0 dB — perceived
+6 dB by contrast, and reserved strictly for verified fully-elapsed
sessions. One low thock (knock 20 ms @ 1.0, second @ +120 ms), then the
receipt (§2.3) as the reverse-vertigo resolves to the idle Deck.
Aborted sessions get no run-down, no pulse, no receipt dressed as
completion. Pia's finish mechanics agree: 1000 ms held `00:00`, then the
run-down steps (≈2.4 s, tap-skippable) — the stillness and the run-down
are the same held moment, specced once here.

**Runner-up: Fen's Held Close** (hold-to-file the receipt) — lost: it
demands labour at peak depletion and resurrects the Ledger as a gesture.

**Too good to lose within this element:** Orson's **Un-Completion** — a
tape-stop spin-down voice for sessions ended early, so *ended* ≠
*finished* in the ear; and Luz's last-10-seconds escalating haptic ticks,
handed here from Tasks — the finale gets a rising physical pulse that
the stillness then cuts.

### 2.3 The session-end receipt — Emil (print) + Uma (museums)

**Chosen composite: the Numbered Single Strike.** One flat slip, inline
on the Deck's end state — no overlay, no archive. Emil's strike (220 ms
empty beat → sheet rises 24 dp over 380 ms `CubicBezier(0.2,0.9,0.25,1)`
→ all type arrives in one 110 ms blow, thump 28 ms @ 1.0 + tick 6 ms
@ 0.3 at +90 ms, one press thock) carries Uma's provenance:

- **Arm-time frozen snapshot:** task title as it stood at sealing,
  loadout counts as actually sealed, arm/seal timestamps — later edits
  can never rewrite a finished session.
- **Truth guards:** the routine line says *within* his routine, never
  *by* it (timestamp-in-window check only); banked-break seconds print
  separately, never folded into sealed time; deflections itemised per
  app with timestamps (break-window openings excluded, 30 s dedupe),
  headed `AS LOGGED BY THE SHIELD`; **no deflection total is printed
  anywhere in the app.**
- **Permanence, honestly:** a monotonic sequence counter, incremented
  only at genuine completion, is the only permanent artefact — `№ 048`
  proves `№ 047` existed; nothing of 047 is stored. Single `lastReceipt`
  slot, overwritten by the next session. The slip states its own terms
  in the footer (Emil's colophon, Ledger-dependent wording — ⚑ see
  §6.2): under kill, `MONOLITH KEEPS NO COPY · YOUR STUDY RECORD LIVES
  IN YPT`; under demote/keep, `THE MACHINE KEEPS ITS OWN LOG · YOUR
  STUDY RECORD LIVES IN YPT`.
- No day total (YPT owns the day; Monolith would undercount), no streak,
  no orange (the committing is over). Ink-squeeze type, seeded
  −1.2°…+1.2° off-register, night staircase tiers.

**Runner-up: the Accession Stamp / Torn Stub** (both seats' wave-1 and
print alternatives) — lost on the same ground from two directions: the
stamp's registry theatre implies retrievability that doesn't exist
(false permanence), and the stub's tear-off implies a kept other half.
The strike's metaphor — a mark made once, in the open — stays true.

### 2.4 The blocking cascade — Opal (machinery)

**Chosen: the Series Interlock.** Blocking engages as a series chain
with per-link proof and a visible halt: if a link fails, the chain stops
at that link and says so (this is what Rosa's `BLOCK` lamp and Suki's
Truth Line report). No parallel barrage, no "mostly blocked."

**Runner-up: the Parallel Barrage** — lost because simultaneous
engagement can't prove order or completeness; a barrage that half-fires
is a seal that lies.

### 2.5 AOD desk clock — Pia (watches) + Mara (light)

**Chosen: the Rigid Minute Plate That Tours True Black.** True `#000000`,
no container, no chrome. Remaining is the hero (Anonymous Pro Bold 132 sp
at ≤4 glyphs / 112 sp in the finale, 88% cream, colon at 45%); elapsed +
ends-at micro-block beside it; an elapsed-fraction seam hairline (2 dp,
block ticks cut in black, 100% head marker, no `%` text — the fraction is
drawn, never numbered); footnote with the block word + time of day. One
wakeup per minute performs the numeral jump and the burn-in move
together: the whole rigid plate tours a golden-angle Lissajous figure-8
(52×34 dp field, 520 ms heavy settle). Staged quieting after 20 min.
Keep-screen-on, 0.35 brightness cap, restore-on-dispose. **Total haptic
and sound silence.** Finish dismisses automatically — no brightness or
colour breach at the moment he may be asleep.

Mara's layer: **Frozen Specular** — the travelling functions are never
called on AOD; only the panel's static top/left catch edge at 6% alpha,
shifting exactly with the anti-burn-in shift; digits untouched.

**Runner-up: Rising Light / Moon Aperture** — lost three ways: it
double-renders elapsed progress against the seam (one fact, two
renderings), its moon mapping is invented and needs a teaching label,
and a rising lit field is maximum OLED area, battery, and burn-in risk
for the least precise rendering on the surface.

## 3. AIRLOCK + EMERGENCY OVERLAYS

### 3.1 Readiness — Suki (space), in Emil's chrome

**Chosen: the Truth Line.** Readiness is **measured before he arrives**
and rendered once, in chrome: the app's real answer to *will blocking
work right now* — permissions, accessibility, usage access, battery —
as one human sentence when something is wrong, and silence when all is
true. Placement ruling (coordinator merge with §5.1): the Truth Line
occupies the imprint's state slot conditionally — when a check fails,
the slot carries the failure in words (`BLOCKING IS OFF — NOTHING WILL
BE HELD` class); when all is true, the slot carries the session state
word. One slot, two tenses of the same truth; the Deck itself stays
silent when true (Suki). A permission revoked in Settings mid-week is
visible before the next arm without opening anything — that liveness
under external change is the only ground on which any fact earns chrome.

**Runner-up: the Poll Rail** (wave-1 GO Poll, cut down) — lost because a
rail of polls invites watching the machine check itself; readiness is a
sentence, not a show.

### 3.2 The airlock interception overlay — Suki (space) + Wren (cinema)

**Chosen: the Wall Card.** When the shield intercepts a launch mid-
session, the overlay is full-screen, **zero choices**, one fact per
line: what tried, when, that it was turned away. No buttons to negotiate
with at the tired moment — HOLD absolute means the overlay cannot become
a door. Wren's Aperture Sheet governs its mechanics: full-width plate
flush to the aperture, background racks shallow (7 dp), one animatable
for plate + room, every dismiss route runs the same 190 ms exit, sheets
never stack. Suki's Settle Second is the micro-behaviour: the card holds
one settle second before any dismiss is live, so a reflexive tap can't
punch through the one fact he should register.

**Runner-up: the Five-Second Veil** — lost because a timed veil is a
countdown he waits out like an ad; the Wall Card ends when he leaves,
not when a timer forgives him.

**⚑ Ledger-dependent:** the coverage-gap line — if the shield was off
for part of a session, the receipt carries Suki's Gap Line
(`THE SHIELD WAS OFF FOR PART OF THAT SESSION`). The line is *issued*
under every Ledger outcome; whether the app *keeps* the admission is the
Ledger question (§6.2).

### 3.3 The emergency valve — Rosa (control) + Cato (rendering) + Orson (sound)

The banked 60-second hold (settled law: banks at 15/30/45 s, slip drains
the unbanked tail only, 1/week), specced as one control:

- **Control — Rosa's Full-Width Hold Bar.** A 56 dp quiet entry strip
  expands to a full-width × 112 dp hold surface. Hold time accrues from
  first contact on a monotonic clock; **any contact holds** (centroid
  ignored) — a dying grip at 03:30 is not an aim test. Slip behaviour,
  exact: an 800 ms no-drain grace (debounce), then only the unbanked
  tail drains at 2 banked-seconds per real second, with a descending
  250 ms drain tick; the banked floor never drains. Checkpoints bank
  with segment-lock + double-knock haptics. Banking alone never consumes
  the weekly break; the use is written only at the completion frame
  (60.0 s), with a reverse-thunk unlatch. Eyes-off throughout: 5 s
  metronome ticks, rising ticks in the final 10 s.
- **Rendering — Cato's Tension Strap.** Progress renders as webbing
  feeding through a ladder-lock at a linear 2.8 dp/s (168 dp = 60 s):
  one woven rib per second is the **only** progress rendering — no
  numeral, no percentage, no ring. Bartack stitches at 15/30/45 stay
  visible as stitched length; a slip drains at 3× feed rate and catches
  hard at the last bartack. Interruption freezes with neither gain nor
  drain. Zero orange — orange is COMMIT; this is the sanctioned
  un-commit. Wren's two-tone fill (solid to the banked checkpoint,
  lighter beyond) is the strap's tonal equivalent and is adopted as its
  night rendering.
- **Sound — Orson's Bank You Can Hear.** A 2 s pulse train falling
  700→440 Hz; each bank latch drops lower and heavier, slaved to the
  *banked* state in the store; slips render as a proportional drain
  (rising pitch, latch lifts if a bank is crossed downward) — never a
  buzzer. Disarm is the seal's inverted-partial sibling at −4 dB.

**Runner-up: Rosa's hazard pad** (96 dp) — lost on aim cost, thumb
occlusion, and roll-off slips; and **Cato's Reverse Zip** — rejected
with reasons: a 60 s unzip is a scale lie (garment zips close in one
throw), zips have no intermediate stops so the banks would be invented
theatre, and reusing the arm track to undo blurs the 03:30 gesture.

**Exit truth — Wren:** an emergency exit is a 0 ms splice cut, never the
completion's reverse-vertigo (a symmetric exit renders a broken session
as a finished one — a lie by form). The Deck then states it plainly:
`EMERGENCY BREAK USED — NEXT AVAILABLE <weekday>`, and the receipt, if
one is issued, is labelled `ENDED BY EMERGENCY BREAK`.

**Guard fork:** the valve's spec is identical under both Guard outcomes;
only the hold record's long-term home varies (⚑ §6.2).

## 4. SHIELD

### 4.1 The routine structure — Kato (architecture)

**Chosen: the Registered Section.** Shield's routine section is a
24-hour elevation drawn like a building section: 00:00→24:00 at a
uniform 22 dp/h (528 dp) — **no compression anywhere; scale integrity is
the truth guarantee**. A 64 dp left column (28 dp hour-label margin +
36 dp block lane); 3-hour hairlines extend across the row field. Rows
anchor at their routine's start (FASTED 05:00 → 110 dp, DAY BLOCKS 09:00
→ 198 dp, SLEEP 20:00 → 440 dp, at 76 dp minimum pitch).

- **Registration:** collisions and editor-open displacement are *drawn*,
  not absorbed — elbow leaders (90° bends only) plus a 4 dp open circle
  at the true start. Blocks never move; leaders lengthen.
- **Overnight wrap:** SLEEP 20:00–03:30 is two segments (20:00→lane
  bottom, 00:00→03:30) with continuation serifs, **one row only**,
  `7H 30M` printed, `+1D` in the editor — never two routines, never a
  second name.
- **Gaps:** courtyards are measured and labelled in the row-field void
  (`1H 30M OPEN`; ≥60 min only; derived from enabled routines; never
  interpreted; hidden during edit). Overlaps split the lane into 17 dp
  sub-lanes — shown as collisions, no warnings.
- **Loadout coupling, corrected:** Kato's wave-1 global re-tone sweep is
  killed as false theatre. Fills encode each routine's **own** loadout
  (STUDY −1 step / DEEP −2 / SLEEP −0.5; night capped at `#4A423C` on
  `#171412`), one legend at the strip's foot; the cap never re-tones the
  schedule. An editor loadout change re-tones only that block, 180 ms.
- **Wording ceiling (binding):** a routine window may say `WINDOW OPEN —
  ENDS 03:30`; it may never read as a running session. A routine never
  records a session he wasn't in.
- Motion: build wipe up 260 ms `CubicBezier(0.3,0,0.2,1)` with a
  footing/course double-knock (14 ms @ 0.8, +50 ms, 8 ms @ 0.5);
  demolish 220 ms down with 3× 4 ms @ 0.35 crumble ticks; editor
  420/260 ms. The elevation itself is silent — only hosted controls fire
  engine patterns.

Aldo's timetable law layers on: **one interval, one object** — a
routine's span is a single drawn object with a single time column, never
a pair of from/to fragments competing with the drawing.

**Runner-up: Teo's Bedded Day** — a horizontal stratigraphic stack on his
own axis (03:30→03:30, sleep contiguous, beds stacked by deposition,
thickness ∝ duration at a scale printed on the band). It lost narrowly:
the Section's uniform vertical scale is the stronger truth guarantee,
its row field hosts the editor directly, and the rotated/wrapped axis
merely relocates the seam onto his wake. Two of its organs are adopted
as fragments (§7): the **ERODED state** (a missed routine visibly
shortens the day) is the candidate rendering for routine attendance if
the Ledger verdict keeps it (⚑ §6.2), and the axis origin question —
his day starts at 03:30 — is recorded as an open preference inside the
chosen form: the Section's scale is origin-agnostic and can be re-based
to 03:30 without redrawing.

**Guard fork:** the elevation holds no Guard fact under either outcome;
if Guard dissolves, its modules land below the app list and the section
is untouched. **His call.**

### 4.2 Routine editing — Joss (kitchens)

**Chosen: the Prep Bench.** One editor sheet, stations in firing order
(WHEN → WHAT'S BLOCKED → WHAT RUNS FIRST). The sticky footer renders the
routine exactly as its future list line, generated from the **same draft
fields Save commits** — the preview cannot drift from the result.
Overlap is checked live and stated in plain words; overnight windows
print `+1D`. Full create / rename / duplicate / delete / pause: pause is
a one-gesture switch on the list row; duplicate is two taps; a deleted
first-task pick announces its fallback instead of failing silently.
Reseeds ship his real day (fasted 05:00–08:00 daily STUDY, day blocks,
sleep 20:00–03:30 SLEEP); rows matching the old seeds exactly migrate,
user-edited rows stay untouched.

**Runner-up: the four-step wizard** — lost because editing one field
shouldn't require walking past unrelated stations, and its progress dots
are decorative readiness.

**⚑ Ledger-dependent:** under the demote/keep outcomes, each routine row
carries its attendance truth in small marks (fired / joined / missed
over its last firings) — the Prep Bench's feedback loop. Under kill, the
rows carry no past (Kato's law, unamended).

## 5. TASKS

*Teo's non-duplication verdict is adopted as cohort law for this surface:
no accumulation/horizon rendering on Tasks — the queue's own numeral
column owns order, and study accumulation is YPT's. The wave-1 horizon
object stays dead.*

### 5.1 The queue — Nils (paper) + Aldo (numerals)

**Chosen: the Stack of Slips.** The whole Tasks surface is one sheet,
edge to edge — no cards, margins, borders, or shadows. Rows are defined
only by edge-to-edge perforations (solid rule = write here; dashed
perforation = this can come away). Each slip's fibre grain is seeded in
slip-local coordinates and travels 1:1 with the slip — grain fixed to
the glass reads as a screen effect; grain moving with the slip reads as
a thing. The head slip alone sits +1 tone step with the sole catch-light
(physical top-of-stack, not a progress encoding) and carries the
screen's single orange START fill. Aldo's numeral law: **one numeral
column, two tiers, nothing else numeric** — Anonymous Pro order numerals
are the rendering of order, and no other number competes with them.

- **The settle (the named behaviour):** displaced slips travel 200–280 ms
  `EaseOutCubic` with adjacency stagger, 1 dp overshoot, re-inking their
  perforation on landing; numerals re-strike only at rest; one knock for
  the whole event.
- **Reorder** = lift a slip (neighbours part, showing a physical hole).
  **Delete** = the same tear as completion — paper doesn't distinguish
  done from discarded.
- While sealed, the queue is a read-only printed order. All editing is
  unsealed-only.

**Runner-up: Rhea's Puck Rows** — solid tiles in a recessed track,
carried 1:1 with velocity tilt and discharged into a chute. Wilder to
the hand, lost on the house law: tiles are containers, and the Tasks
list already proved itself as text + hairlines; a tile's thickness also
invites reading size as importance. Her tray clack is salvaged into the
settle's knock. (Second runner-up, Nils's own Folded Sheet, lost because
it hides facts behind gestures and invents panel-classification
decisions.)

### 5.2 The composer — Nils (form) + Joss (behaviour)

**Chosen: the Blank Slip, docked.** No input box, no "+" button, no
modal: the next blank line at the foot of the queue **is** the composer
— a solid rule, a pencil-tier placeholder, a deliberately incomplete
perforation. Joss's Station Dock behaviours run it: the composer keeps
duration/loadout between adds (keyboard `Done` = ADD, field refocuses),
so batch entry is type → Done → type. Commit inks the perforation
complete (240 ms stagger), then a fresh blank slip feeds in from the
bottom edge (260 ms `EaseOutCubic`) — the composer never changes
address. Typing gets a 90 ms ink-soak glyph settle; haptics and sound
are explicit silence except one feed-in knock. ADD is deliberately **not
orange** — START keeps Tasks' one commit fill.

**Runner-up: the Pulled Tab / full-screen compose sheet** — lost because
it adds a gesture per entry, covers the queue while writing, and hides
the rail where duplicates are born.

### 5.3 Sub-steps and completion feel — Luz (games) + Ivo (sound)

One escalating ladder; freeze is the currency. **Shared input law:**
acknowledgement on pointer-DOWN ≤16 ms everywhere; input during any
freeze is queued, never dropped (a 2nd queued strike fires at half
freeze; a 3rd coalesces to a plain 90 ms strike).

- **Sub-step — Luz's Buffered Strike (chosen).** Wave-1's hit-stop
  retuned 90→60 ms (justified by ~30 taps/day), a 120 ms drawn nib
  strike across the step's text, baseline-anchored squash, thock+settle
  haptics. Un-strike is a cheaper reverse wipe — corrections never pay
  the celebration price. Ivo's voice: each step is its note (STEP SET:
  01=E4 … 05=E5, fixed pitches, zero formulas; beyond 05 the damped
  Step-05 voice is reused — no sixth pitch is ever invented), kept as a
  drawn waveform fossil in the margin; un-complete plays the same pitch
  damped and erases the fossil, so record and state can't diverge.
  Runner-up: Luz's freeze-less Letterpress Press — kept as the
  reduced-motion fallback.
- **Final step — Luz's Closing Strike (chosen).** 110 ms freeze, heavier
  wipe, a third 20 ms @ 1.0 pulse; the title's hairline closes like a
  bracket and the block recesses one tone step. **The parent title is
  NOT struck** — steps-done ≠ task-done, and striking it would lie.
  Luz's own wave-1 Parent Strike is the runner-up, rejected by its
  author on truth grounds. Ivo's voice: the task strums its own step
  pitches (12 ms stagger, −1 dB, shared room tail at −26 dB wet), then a
  Tasks-local paper stamp. Binding guards: no second chord on a later
  DONE; no chord or stamp for a task closed incomplete.
- **Whole-task completion — Nils's tear-off, with Luz's timing law.**
  The slip tears along its perforation (dash-crossing ticks 6 ms @ 0.35,
  stepped 240 ms rip, fibre strands) and leaves **nothing** behind — no
  stub archive. Where a done count exists (⚑ Ledger-dependent, §6.2),
  it increments at the tear's completion frame, so count and list never
  disagree (Luz's landing rule, transposed). Runner-up: Luz's Ballast
  Drop (gravity fall to a done zone under a foundation line) — the drop
  is the better *animation*, but a done zone is a kept pile, and a kept
  pile is the Ledger regrowing on the Tasks surface; the tear is the
  honest end for a paper system. Its landing-scaled haptic (heavier for
  longer tasks) is adopted for the tear's final tick.
- **Duration caps — Ivo.** Four struck wooden blocks (CAP SET: 90m=D3,
  45m=F3, 25m=A3, 15m=C4). Re-tapping the selected cap plays the same
  pitch felt-damped — "already down" never sounds like "going down."
- **Delete / un-add — Ivo's un-punch.** ADD is a two-stage card punch +
  seating clack at the new task's own cap pitch, landing on the queue
  row; an empty-field ADD refuses to punch. Delete plays the punch with
  a reversed envelope; restore via empty-field ADD within a stated
  window, forward punch + seating clack.

## 11. PHASE-2 ADDENDUM — cross-audit amendments and registered positions

*Filed by Marlowe after reading CRITICS-PLAN (Hale) and IMPROVERS-PLAN
(Ilya) in full, and all five Ledger verdicts. Concessions below amend the
sections named; everything not mentioned stands as written. The merged
Designers and MaxImpl plans and the HUMANS plan had not landed when this
was written; the audit continues when they do.*

### 11.1 Concessions adopted (Critics' measurements beat our paper)

1. **The total includes the breaks (amends §1.3).** Critics T-11: the
   honest span of a multi-block session includes the 10-minute safe
   breaks — `100 MIN TOTAL` over two blocks is a 110-minute span.
   Vega's Still Witness total is therefore **Σ blocks + the breaks
   between them**, and `FINISHES` prints the true wall-clock end
   including breaks. Block length lives on the wheel's printed scale
   and nowhere else (Critics' ruling, adopted verbatim).
2. **The running Deck declares the break (amends §2.1).** Critics T-13:
   during a safe break the Deck currently shows `00:00` and never says
   "break." Pia's display gains the break state in its demoted line —
   `BREAK · 9:41` (the Shield notification already computes this string;
   the big surface uses it too). The hero remains the session's
   remaining time; the break is a state word, not a second clock.
3. **SHUT wording gets tenses (amends §6.1, slot 3).** Critics T-14:
   `SHUT n APPS` in the present tense while nothing is shut is a small
   lie, and the count must cover everything the seal actually stops.
   Amended: idle renders `7 APPS WILL SHUT`; sealed renders the count of
   everything the seal stops, PEEK intercepts included.
4. **The hub sets directly (amends §1.2).** Critics A-6: a one-way wrap
   hub makes 2→1 cost three taps. Vega's pips are individually tappable
   targets — direct-set by tapping the pip — with press-cycle retained
   as the quick path. Pips as targets is what the rendering always
   invited; the spec now says it. Touch target ≥ drawn size, per A-6.
5. **The airlock carries its lawful binary (amends §3.2).** Critics'
   audit of the shipped render: the airlock is a RETURN/PEEK binary,
   the reason picker does not exist, and every intercept is logged with
   a fabricated cause ("Habit"). Suki's Wall Card is amended to carry
   exactly the two lawful exits — `RETURN` and `PEEK · 2M`, with the
   120-second lease countdown rendered (a blind lease is a pretence) —
   and nothing else: no picker, no third option, no negotiation. Cause
   is never fabricated: the card and the log state app, time, outcome —
   a cause is recorded only when a cause exists in the data. Unknown
   packages default to no-peek.
6. **The Blank Slip pins (amends §5.2).** Improvers (Fach Rung 2): a
   composer that scrolls away taxes batch entry. Nils's Blank Slip is
   pinned at the queue's foot (sticky), so the composer's address is
   fixed in the viewport as well as in the list. Fach's ADD-as-text
   arbitration is noted; WILD's form keeps Ivo's punch as the submit
   *sound* on either ruling — the sound was never the dispute.
7. **The sealed band gets its mechanism (amends §6.2).** Improvers
   (Band Rung 3): the Cloister now has a chrome mechanism — a locking
   pin through the nav cap, unselected stops dimmed, all touch
   swallowed to one dull latch per press, no exit gesture in the
   chrome. Wren's "transitions don't exist while sealed" is the law;
   Band's pin is how the rail shows it.

### 11.2 Clarifications (our paper already answered; saying it plainly)

8. **FLOW has one author: the wheel (clarifies §1.1).** Critics A-3 /
   T-12 and Improvers' Latch ladder dissect the shipped trap — a footer
   key that is `selectStudyPreset`'s only caller, with three divergent
   definitions of the state. Under WILD there is **no footer FLOW key**:
   Vega's stop 0, printed as the word `FLOW` behind a marked axis-break,
   is the only FLOW control and the only author of the state; the
   repository reads the wheel's stop, so screen and engine cannot
   diverge. The key dies; the trap's exit is the wheel itself.
9. **Attendance presence, defined (answers Critics C4).** "Attendance
   logged" in LEDGER-wild and §4.2 means exactly this: a join of two
   recorded facts — the routine's fire timestamp and the seal intervals
   in the event store. A routine is *attended* if a sealed session
   overlaps its window; *missed* if none does (Opal's roll writes it
   only at window close, from real overlap). It claims nothing about
   studying, effort, or presence of body — only that a seal overlapped
   the window. That is the same evidence standard as Critics T-7's own
   replacement ("fired, unattended"), and it is why the marks can be
   printed without lying. Improvers' presence-grace variant (a seal
   begun within 10 minutes of the window mints attendance) is recorded
   as the alternative definition for the master plan to settle; WILD's
   is overlap, no grace, because grace is a judgement and overlap is a
   fact.
10. **Slide thresholds are three different events (answers Improvers
    Arbitration 2).** The numbers name different things: Rosa's
    interlock sits at 0.65 of travel (the last point the arm can be
    refused), Fen's resistance gate at 0.68 (where the damped zone
    begins), the shipped gate-crossing event at 0.82 (where the firm
    click fires), Fen's latch at 0.97, Cato's seat frame at the end of
    the 110 ms assist. WILD does not contest 0.82 as the gate *event*;
    it contests only any reading in which one number must do all three
    jobs. The master plan should adopt one vocabulary — interlock /
    resistance / latch / seat — and pin each number to its event.

### 11.3 Registered disagreements (defended, not conceded)

11. **No auto-pause. Ever. (against Designers §1.3's framing and
    Improvers Reihe Rung 5.)** Both cohorts let three misses pause a
    routine by the machine's own act. WILD sides with Critics C3: that
    is the machine editing his routine — a decision taken on his behalf,
    discovered after the fact, at exactly the surface (his mornings)
    where a silent change does the most damage. The lawful form is the
    one all three cohorts' data models already support: the printed
    fact (`MISSED ×3`, attendance marks on the row) and **his**
    one-gesture pause on that row (Joss). Data yes; agency no.
12. **The valve renders length, not numerals (open argument with
    Improvers Zug-II B1).** Zug-II would print `BANKED 30 · HOLDING 7`.
