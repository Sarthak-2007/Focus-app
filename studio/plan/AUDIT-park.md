to fake a measurement. The testimony is not: a board that
cannot re-prove, cannot say LIVE, prints its count
everywhere but on itself, and lets its one repair verb

than anything." With §4 landed, PARK becomes what its
first half always claimed: the drawer where the app
tells the truth about its own state — and can prove it
