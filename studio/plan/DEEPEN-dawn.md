## 1. THE GENERATING LOGIC — the sky keeps his hours; the screens are its depths

Phase 2's grammar said: the sky is the ground, glass is the
only material, light pools at the top. True — and the
composition ignored it, stacking the trunk's furniture on the
gradient and calling the gradient a world. The deepening takes
the grammar literally, as a layout engine:

**The one event is light arriving on schedule.** DAWN's sky is
keyed to his routine windows (A5, as amended by Zunder and
ratified in phase 2 §11 — a feature riding the routine state,
not a clock tint). Five phases, one sky:

| Phase | When | The sky |
|---|---|---|
| HELD BREATH | SLEEP armed (20:00–03:30), and the unarmed deep night before FASTED | near-black violet — the held breath before dawn (§10) |
| FIRST LIGHT | FASTED armed (05:00–08:00) | the sunrise stop-sets, crossing slowly toward day across the window |
| FULL DAY | DAY armed (09:00–19:30) | the day gradient at full saturation (the NOON set, §10) |
| EMBER HOUR | unarmed, 19:30–20:00 | gold sinking to ember — the day letting go |
| (the held night) | any session sealed, any phase | the light-step drops the sky to the session's night, exactly as shipped |

A session's seal overrides the phase (the shipped light-step,
grammar §1.4 of phase 2); a finish returns the sky to whatever
phase is actually true (§10d). The phases change when his
windows arm — at 20:00 the glass goes held-breath because
SLEEP arms, even in June when the real sky disagrees. A hundred
apps tint by time of day; only this one knows his blocks.

From that event, three compositional laws generate every
screen:

1. **THE HORIZON IS THE SPINE.** Every screen is organised
   around its horizon — the line where the light sits in this
   phase. Above the horizon: light, and the one figure the
   screen exists for, standing in it. Below: glass at graded
   depths, holding everything else. A screen is read the way a
   morning is read: light first, then the room.
2. **SURFACES ARE DEPTHS OF FROST, NEVER CARDS.** Wetter's
   frost-depth transplant (phase 2 §11), generalised from a
   finish into the layout system: **clear glass = what is
   present and primary; frost = what is recorded or secondary;
   deep frost = the archive and the sworn record.** Depth IS
   the hierarchy. Nothing is boxed; things are nearer or
   farther in the same light.
3. **THE FIGURE STANDS IN THE LIGHT.** Each screen's one
   number — span, posture, count — is set directly on the sky
   at the horizon, in plum or violet ink, with no pane, plate
   or halo under it. If a figure needs a card to be read, the
   sky has failed, not the figure.

**What this kills, globally:** the header BAND as a bar (its
facts become etched margin print, §2); the bottom RAIL as
furniture (destinations become etched words at the sky's
foot); the hero PANE (already dead in code — this buries its
composition); Shield's section CARDS (frost fields divided by
etched hairlines); the proof strip AS A BAR (it sinks to the
composition's floor, the deep-frost record stratum — its facts
and words untouched, per the Held Door baseline's law that
acquitted sentences keep their words).

**The soul test, self-applied in advance:** strip the colour
and the other filings collapse to a header, a hero card, a
list. DAWN collapses to a gradient field with a horizon, a
figure standing in light, an instrument of clear glass, and
strata of frost below. That is a landscape, not furniture —
and it is a claim about pixels this file cannot yet show
(§14). Fremd judges the renders, not the sentence.
