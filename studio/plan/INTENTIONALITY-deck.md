## §3 · THE NEW CHARGES (not in the brutal audit, or born since)

**3.1 The recipe lies for the most common session (eyebrow, F4's
own landing).** Idle prints `$blocks × $dial + BREAKS`
unconditionally. The span it explains is n×block + (n−1)×10: at
n=1 there are NO breaks, yet the face charges for them —
`1 × 50 + BREAKS` over a 50-minute session. And at dial <10 the
hero says FLOW and TIME says NO END while the eyebrow recites a
timed recipe for a session that does not exist. F4 was the right
cure, landed unconditioned. Form to beat: n=1 → `1 × 50`;
n>1 → `3 × 50 + BREAKS`; FLOW → the mode's own line (e.g.
`FLOW — NO END`), so the face speaks one dialect per state.

**3.2 The FLOW cap is a second door to a ghost (again).** F2
ruled the cap KILLED as a control; wave-2 deferred the kill to
the F16/F18 merge and left it rendering. What it does today:
`onSelectStudyPreset(STOPWATCH_FLOW)` — writes a preset the
engine never reads (ESR-08, the file still confesses it) — plus
`onDialMinutesChanged(5)`, which the wheel already does by hand.
A control whose named act is theatre and whose real act is
duplicated has no reason. REMOVE now, not at merge: the cap,
and with it the `onSelectStudyPreset` wire it alone keeps in
the 41 — the contract's next dead wire is being kept alive by
the element that should already be dead.

**3.3 FLOW's empty track.** Running FLOW composes the 6dp track
at fraction 0f — permanently. F3's ruling was "FLOW carries no
bar"; the code kept the bar and emptied it. An instrument that
reads zero in every instance of a state is not an instrument,
it's a groove. REMOVE the track in FLOW (the hero's count-up is
FLOW's whole readout); timed keeps it — there it finally tells
the truth.

**3.4 The seam row: 12dp of nothing.** F8's Deck half nulled
`stampLeft/stampRight`; ProofStripBand kept its 12dp seam box —
a hairline over a blank strip, inside a permanent band, on every
frame, in every state. This is the audit's "reserved emptiness"
disease in its purest form, created by a fix. REMOVE the row
from the Deck's band (53→41dp) until F16 rehouses the strip;
PARK's receiving seam is that cell's business, not a reason to
keep a blank on the Deck.

**3.5 The seal's record prints at 88%.** Brutal audit §3:
"Tenant ink to full alpha when struck (Schein)." The code's
ink Animatable settles at 0.88 — the imprint's tenant, the one
print on the page that is a permanent record, is permanently
dimmed by design accident (no ruling I can find chose 0.88 as
the settled value; the Strike's dip to 0.82 settles back to it).
REDESIGN: settle at 1.0. The record of what the machine held
should be the most legible small print on the Deck, not the
least.

**3.6 The Guard line's frozen tense is unlabelled.** The imprint
learned `· AS SEALED` (UNA2-04) when its frozen words diverge
from the live wire. The header's Guard line freezes at the seal
for the session's whole life with no mark at all (brutal audit
truth §5.7 — still standing). Same lie family, one surface
cured, one not. REDESIGN: parity — the frozen Guard print
carries its tense mark, or stops printing a value it is no
longer measuring.

**3.7 The hub is the last secret control.** Centre press cycles
block count; eight wells set it directly; the hub prints no
numeral and no word (the view's own comment: "as hub geometry —
no numeral, no word"). Muede's answer in the audit — "the count
is on the face, the hub gesture stays" — put the count in the
recipe (F4), but nothing on or near the hub says the hub is
where the count changes. A first-time hand finds it by accident
or never. REDESIGN, priced inside F18: the hub prints the count
it sets (`×3`), or under the Fusion, the span it composes. A
control that cannot be discovered cannot be intentional.

**3.8 Slide words put TAP level with SLIDE.** `SLIDE OR TAP TO
START` — the label offers the cheap gesture at equal billing
with the committing one, on the surface whose whole physics
argue that sealing should cost a deliberate act. (C8 corrected
the travel-fade charge; this is a different, smaller charge:
the words' hierarchy, not their visibility.) REDESIGN, minor:
`SLIDE TO START`; the tap remains as the unadvertised path
(F22's semantics work will need a non-gesture route anyway) —
advertising is hierarchy, and the hierarchy should preach the
slide.

**3.9 The vertigo still strikes the instrument.** F15 ruled:
the page recedes, the instrument seals in focus; wave-2 deleted
the rise and declared the item. The transform that remains —
scale 0.94 and blur peaking 6dp — is applied to the hero CARD
itself. Blurring the readout at the exact moment it becomes a
clock is the gimmick aim Schein charged ("it blurs the
instrument being sealed"), half-cured. REDESIGN: move scale +
blur to the ground behind the card, curves and 1050ms kept
verbatim, as ruled.
