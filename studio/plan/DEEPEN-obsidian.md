## 1. THE GENERATING LOGIC — CUT STONE

Phase 1 gave this world a palette. Phase 2 gave it grammars. Neither
gave it a *composition* — the screens were still the skeleton's rooms
with black furniture in them. What follows is the logic that
generates the arrangement itself. Five generators; every element in
§§3–11 is one of them applied, and anything in my composition that
cannot be derived from one of them is named in §13 and cut or
naturalised.

**G1 — ONE MASS.** Obsidian is a single flow of glass cooled as one
body. Nothing in this world is *placed on* the stone; everything is
*cut into* it or *raised from* it. Consequence: no cards, no floating
panels, no capsules, no shadows-as-elevation. A surface's rank is
carved, not stacked. The screen is one face, edge to edge.

**G2 — STRATA.** A cut face is read in layers. The composition of
every screen is a vertical section of strata — full-width courses
separated by incised kerf lines, ordered by rank: the highest
stratum is what the moment is about; courses below it support, in
descending rank, down to the plinth the face stands on. There are no
margins in a section: courses run to the face's edges, because the
edge of the screen is the edge of the stone.

**G3 — RELIEF AND INTAGLIO.** Importance is relief depth. What
decides is raised (the monument). What is operated is cut in:
tracks, grooves, sockets, slots. A control you drag travels in a
channel; a control you seat drops into a socket; a verb you press is
a key-stone seated in its socket, flush when rested. Nothing in this
world is a rounded pill hovering over a surface — that is a plastic
world's object, and it is unrepresented here.

**G4 — THE EMBER IS THE HOT CUT.** One ember per screen, spent on
the commit (phase 2's law stands). In composition terms: the commit
is a channel cut across the face, and the stone that travels in it
is the one hot thing. Warmth is never ambient, never keyed to the
clock, never decorative. Fire appears where an irreversible act is,
and nowhere else — including, by §10, the one lawful door out of a
held app, whose key is a commit-class act.

**G5 — THE TAG LEAVES THE BUILDING.** A quarried block is tagged:
a numbered bone tag wired to the stone, torn off when the block is
delivered. The receipt is this world's tag (§8) — the one object
not made of the stone, because it is the record that leaves with
the man. Its paleness against the black face is quarry practice,
not nostalgia.

**Day and night — one stone, two lights (Zunder's ASH, adopted).**
Obsidian in daylight is not a black screen with the brightness up:
it is the volcano's field — cold ash around black rock. The day
face is generated by the same section with the *unworked* strata
lit as ash and the *worked* faces — monument, channels, sockets —
staying black rock in both lights. Night is the whole face gone to
rock (my verified night staircase stands: GAP N1 1.02 · N2 1.01 ·
N3 1.12 · N4 4.08 · N5 3.42 · N6 1.09/1.12, recomputed 6/6 by Salz
from my lane's tokens). The day re-key is specified as targets, not
invented numbers: ash strata in the 0.30–0.40 relative-luminance
band, cold — in every ash token, green must not exceed both red
and blue (the studio's zero-green test), and warmth is zero: no
ash token leans red either. Print on ash is the dark ink my lane
already owns (#17110C family — the value's honest home is as ink
on pale ground). Exact ash hexes are chosen against that ink and
GAP-measured at the first render round; no ratio for them is
claimed in this document. This answers Fremd's day/night charge in
structure: by day he sees a pale ash field with one black monument
standing in it; by night, only the monument and the ember. Same
stone, visibly two faces — because the material has two faces.

**The soul test, answered before it is sat.** Strip the colour,
cover the names. The skeleton reads: a rounded card floating on a
ground, chips and pills on it, a pinned bar, a floating capsule
rail. This composition reads: one full-bleed section of courses; a
raised relief field with a circle incised in it; a groove across
the foot with a stone in it; a plinth. No rounded rectangle floats
anywhere in the app. If a stranger still reads "the same app," it
will not be because the furniture matches.
