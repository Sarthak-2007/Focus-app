## Where the claims actually conflict

Not on the file's existence or content — on its disposition:
B says *no ruling on this file is verifiable in the record*;
C says *the file was superseded along with its seat's other early
filings*. Both can be partly true: a design can be overtaken by
events (the seal shipped in batches 4/6/7 on other terms) without
any record disposing the design itself — which is precisely the
gap the registry's evidence discipline exists to catch. The
neighbouring record cuts both ways and settles neither: Ivo's

fork F13 WAS answered (Sweep-C §4) — but §4 does not state what
F13 asked or answered, so it cannot be credited to the Road
without reading it.
