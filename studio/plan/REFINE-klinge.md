*The refinement ring, first pass. Oct 29, 2026, ~20:33 IST.
Evidence: the four landed directions' finalist renders (Deck day,
Deck night, Shield day), the three case letters (mail/ren/2016,

as a risk, not a charge.*

## DAWN — the sky is real; the furniture is the corpse's

**Charges**

- **D-1 · CORPSE RHYME — the bright hero (phase 1).** Hero panel
  sampled **#FFF9F0**; the corpse's porcelain was **#FAF6F0** —
  five units apart on R, three on G. Dial plate #FFFCF6. The
  composition is the corpse's silhouette in lighter values: one
  big warm pale panel, a dial inside it, beige below. Ren flagged
  this as the test case; judged on the render, the flag stands.
  **Answer on file:** the dissolve — hero alphas moved to
  0xD8/0x8C, **verified in code** (DawnGlass.kt:63–64). But §0's
  claimed proof — "lane renders 01_deck.png (this build)" —
  points at a file whose mtime predates the phase-2 commission
  and whose pixels match the phase-1 viewing copy within lossy
  noise (#FFF9F0 vs #FFF9F1; dial #FDF2E7 vs #FDF3E9). The window
  may be in the code; it is **not yet in any render on disk**.
  Status: *answered in code, unproven in render.*
- **D-2 · CORPSE RHYME / THE ACCIDENT — the tan furniture.**
  The proof strip and the FLOW chip sample **#F2D2B4** — a true
  beige, deeper than the corpse's own #FFE4CF accent tint. The
  same family dresses Shield's sled tracks and search field.
  The world's named sky runs apricot → rose → lavender → pale
  sky; the strip sits on the lavender reach of the screen
  wearing apricot-tan, and **neither filing gives the tone a
  reason**. The dissolve does not touch it. Status: *unanswered.*
- **D-3 · FEEL FRAUD — the phase-1 letter against its own
  render.** The letter claims the warmth "drains out of every
  surface, so nothing reads cream, porcelain, or office."
  D-1 and D-2 are the measurements of that sentence: the hero
  is porcelain's neighbour and the strip is beige outright.
  Phase 2 concedes the panel ("It was fair") — the concession is
  recorded and credited — but the sentence itself is never
  retracted, and it described a render that did not exist.
  A letter may be optimistic; it may not be false.
- **D-4 · THE ACCIDENT (minor) — the ember that brightens.**
  Night cap sampled **#F2728C**; the day flame is #D9385E.
  Phase 1 named the night cap "gone ember — the last coal."
  A banked coal is dimmer than the flame; this one is lighter.
  Phase 2 spends a full §7 on the sky's hours and never
  re-ratifies the metaphor. Small, and exactly the class of
  thing the charter exists to catch: a story a value contradicts.

## NOIR — the boldest face, breaking its own law

**Charges**

- **N-1 · THE ACCIDENT — the lamp that doesn't light.** The
  world's law, stated three times (phase-1 letter: amber is
  danger's colour; spec §0.2/D16: the sentence slot "is the
  amber lamp's home"; §5: errors are "amber light, one
  sentence, no noise"). The phase-1 night Deck prints
  **BLOCKING IS OFF — TAP TO FIX in neon white**, and amber
  burns nowhere on the Deck. The designer convicts himself in
  phase 2 (S1: the banner "should wear the lamp, not the
  primary container it wears today") — the charge stands on
  the render regardless: the one danger sentence in the app
  does not wear the danger colour in the only pictures the
  eye will judge. Status: *cure is his own sentence; hold him
  to it at merge.*
- **N-2 · FEEL — the dry city.** The letter promises wet black
  and light on wet pavement. The hero plate samples a
  **uniform matte #150A26** — no falloff, no reflection, no
  specular event on the largest surface in the world. Phase 2
  relocates the wetness to the grounds ("light pooled at the
  foot of the screen like a wet street"); in the night render
  the foot is near-black around a violet pill — the pool is
  not in the picture. A flat violet card could front any dark
  theme in any app; the city's signature material is claimed
  for every surface except the one that fills the screen.
  Bring the reflection onto the plate, or strike "wet" from
  the claim. (Not BLAND against the direction — the day face
  forbids that verdict — but the night hero, alone, is the
  least specific surface NOIR files.)

**Acquittals, by name**

- **Day Deck + Shield** — acquitted on all four blades. Ren's

  violet-black ink, magenta spent as a hairline on Shield
  (the STUDY underline) and once as the 2m violet. The poster
  system is delivered whole, not gestured at.
- **The §7-4 withdrawal** — Noir charged his own phase-1 hint,
  then withdrew the charge on native-resolution evidence and

  auditor: acquitted, and adopted as this report's method law.
- **The trunk container catch** — the unmapped
  surfaceContainer* leak, found by his first render, fixed in

- *Observation, not a charge (his method law binds me):* the
