## WHO OPENS THIS DRAWER, AND WHEN IT MATTERS

PARK is not opened on good days. It is opened on the morning the
phone feels wrong, or the night something silently died — by a
tired man deciding whether he can trust the machine for the next
twelve hours. The cost unit on this surface is not confusion.
It is a study day: twelve hours he believes are guarded and are
not, discovered at night, unrecoverable. Every charge below is
priced in that unit.

## THE CHARGES, BY COST

**1. P-1 (PARK never re-proves) is the worst-day charge of this
audit, and wave-2's cure is aimed at the right reader.** The
real-world sequence: an OEM battery killer or an OS update
revokes a grant overnight. He opens PARK before studying — the
one surface whose job is the machine's state — and watches rows
rot GRANTED → STALE in front of him with no verb to change it.
Best case he distrusts the board and wastes the morning in
Settings. Worst case he reads a stale GRANTED, studies twelve
hours unblocked, and learns it at 20:00. The CHECK key + re-sweep
on open (§4.1, landed wave-1) converts that morning from a
discovery-at-night into a thirty-second verification. Umsicht:
**this is the highest-care item on the issue and it is done —
guard it in review.**

**2. P-6 (the blank Guard line) — silence is a print, and it
lied.** "While any counted item is UNMEASURED the header prints
nothing; a tired man reads blank as fine." Care law, stated for
every surface, not just this one: **on a surface a man consults
for assurance, an absent print is read as a good print.** The
`GUARD — NOT CHECKED` cure (wave-1, then made unblankable in
type in 7c8c6c6) is small in lines and large in consequence.
Endorsed without reservation.

**3. P-5 (FIX abandons him at the border) — the cost is paid by
the least confident version of him.** FIX landed him on the
general Accessibility list with "find MONOLITH" said nowhere,
Android's warning paragraph unwarned, and a return verdict that
was sound-only: silence reads identical for fixed and
still-broken, so he takes the trip twice — or, on the worst
morning, reads the OS warning, backs out frightened, and the
guard stays off all day. Wave-2's print half answers each leg:
the escort line ("FIX OPENS SETTINGS — FIND MONOLITH IN THE
LIST, TURN IT ON, THEN COME BACK HERE"), the staircase label
("FIX 1 OF 3 — X"), and the PRINTED verdict FIXED / STILL OPEN
persisting under the key. Umsicht adds the consequence that
keeps the remainder honest: **the in-trip half (§4.6) is not a
convenience remainder.** Three separate Settings trips on a
morning already going wrong is three chances to give up; each
trip is attrition aimed at exactly the man least able to pay
it. Vega's Staircase (~80 lines, host-side chaining) should not
drift to the bottom of batch-10 — it is the difference between
a repair a tired man finishes and one he abandons halfway,
believing the app "too hard to fix."

**4. P-4's age column and STALE's half-ink protect him from a
false accusation — including one against himself.** Before the
cure, STALE and PAUSED wore full lack-ink: gone, unproven, and
his own deliberate Safe Break pause printed in one face. A man
who sees his intentional pause dressed as a failure learns the
board cries wolf, and stops believing it on the morning it is
telling the truth. The age column (NOW / 45S AGO / 12M AGO,
derived from stamps the store already buried) lets him tell
"the machine died at 02:47" from "nobody has checked since
Tuesday" — different problems, different responses, and only
one of them is an emergency. Wave-2 landed both halves.
Endorsed.

**5. P-8's remainder (seal-time warning) — name its worst day
so it is not forgotten in batch-10.** A door dying after the
20:00 SLEEP seal cannot even be viewed until morning. The
cheap half — print any ungranted door AT seal time, while he
is awake and can still act — is the whole care content of the
charge: a warning at 20:00 is a two-minute fix; the same fact
at 06:00 is a compromised day. It sits in the VM, not this
drawer, and wave-2 correctly left it — but Umsicht records it
as the highest-consequence remainder on this issue, above the
OFF vs NO DOOR split, because its cost is measured in nights,
not in print precision.

**6. What remains knowingly open, and must stay honestly

lets the board say LIVE (§4.2) — but LIVE there is derived,
not witnessed; the rehearsal that would prove the pipeline

board may ever claim more than derivation supports. The
warrant row derives ONLY through WatchStore.printState on the
same warrant the Deck prints — one derivation, no fork —

**7. Acquittal, in care terms:** nothing destructive lives
inside PARK (Muede) — the drawer's dangers are exported to

return. A surface a frightened man opens at the worst moment
should contain no irreversible act. It doesn't. Keep it that
way when the LOOK picker (§4.10's held slot) eventually lands
here: a theme row is furniture; it must never arrive carrying
a destructive neighbour.
