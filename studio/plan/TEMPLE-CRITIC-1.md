## 1. THE FREMD TEST — every word Temple's surfaces will print

Fremd's test (REFINE-fremd.md): a word a tired man cannot decode in
five seconds fails. Temple answered Fremd well in places. Where it
kept a cipher and called the keeping a design, I charge.

### Charges

**T-F1 — The warrant pair: Temple decoded ONE of SIX prints, and
its witness course compresses the rest into a lie.**
Trunk prints six BIND/PROOF pairs (look-temple
ui/components/MonolithCustomComponents.kt:382–399, `seamMarksFor`):
`BIND HELD · THIS VISIT` / `PROOF: LIVE BIND`; `BIND UNCONFIRMED` /
`PROOF: NONE`; `BIND HELD · THIS VISIT` / `PROOF: BIND ONLY`;
`BIND HELD · UNSEEN` / `PROOF: BIND ONLY`; `BIND NOT HELD` /
`PROOF: POSITIVE`; `BIND HELD · UNSEEN` / `PROOF: POSITIVE`.
Fremd, on the one pair he was shown: "I have read these six times.
I believe they are English. I have no idea what they mean"
(REFINE-fremd §2). Temple's answer (PRESENTATION §1.1) discusses
only `BIND NOT HELD / PROOF: POSITIVE`, respells neither word, and
adds a seal beside POSITIVE — a mark next to a cipher is still a
cipher. Worse, DEEPEN §2's witness course prints the bind state as
"HELD / NOT HELD" — a two-word compression of a six-state truth:
`UNSEEN` (held, not witnessed this visit) printed as plain HELD is
the witness course lying at the foundation it claims to witness.
Replacements, spelled out, one per shipped pair:
- `BIND NOT HELD` → **BLOCKING NOT ON**
- `BIND UNCONFIRMED` → **BLOCKING NOT CHECKED**
- `BIND HELD · THIS VISIT` → **BLOCKING SEEN WORKING NOW**
- `BIND HELD · UNSEEN` → **BLOCKING ON — NOT SEEN THIS TIME**
- `PROOF: POSITIVE` → **LAST CHECK: WORKING**
- `PROOF: LIVE BIND` → **CHECKED LIVE: WORKING**
- `PROOF: BIND ONLY` → **CHECKED: SWITCH ON ONLY**
- `PROOF: NONE` → **NO CHECK YET**
If Temple keeps the word BIND anywhere on glass, it owes the
five-second decode. It has not paid it.

**T-F2 — `TIME ENDS 16:27` on an idle Deck is a fiction, and the
courses keep it.** Fremd (§2): "The block is OFF, but the time
*ends* at 15:19? Ends what, if nothing is running?" Trunk derives
the idle end from nowFrame + the composed span
(TactileDeckScreen.kt:368) — a would-be end, printed as a fact.
DEEPEN §2 promotes the end time into a course of the stele, one
fact per course, with no idle condition named. A course that prints
a hypothetical in the same grammar as witnessed facts breaks the
stele's own law ("Facts are inscriptions", DEEPEN §1). Replacement
on idle: **IF STARTED NOW — ENDS 16:27**; running, `ENDS 16:27`
stands.

**T-F3 — `FLOW` and `TOTAL` kept, unexplained, again.** Fremd on
FLOW: "A button? A mode? It just sits there like a word the app
knows and I don't." On TOTAL: "Total of what?" PRESENTATION §1.1
keeps both, calling them "the two modes, printed where the number
lives" — a rationale for the designer, not a decode for the man.
Replacements: `TOTAL` → **TOTAL TIME**; `FLOW` → **NO SET END**.

**T-F4 — `QUEUE 2` kept whole.** PRESENTATION §1.1: "NOW line +
DONE + QUEUE 2… Kept whole." Fremd had to guess ("Two more tasks
waiting? Two chapters?"). Replacement: **2 TASKS WAITING**, or the
count rides the dedication line as **NEXT: 2 TASKS**.

**T-F5 — `LOADOUT` survives into the wall.** Fremd: "a gamer word
in an app that otherwise talks like a laboratory. Pick a
personality." Temple's Shield keeps LOADOUT as a course head
(DEEPEN §4) and builds a keystone over it. Replacement:
**WHAT GETS BLOCKED** as the course head; the three stones keep
their names (STUDY / SLEEP are plain words already).

**T-F6 — GUARD vs SHIELD: Fremd's split, never answered.** "The
tab is called Shield, the top of the screen says Guard. Two
different words for protection in the same app. Are they different
things?" Temple respells the Guard line's tail (acquitted below)
but keeps the head word GUARD on the lintel (DEEPEN §2) one screen
away from the SHIELD doorway in its own colonnade. One protection,
two names, no sentence in either doc reconciles them. Replacement:
the lintel line drops the noun — **1 OF 4 SET · ACCESSIBILITY
MISSING** — since the counted four are the Shield's own grants.

**T-F7 — The receipt's `WITHIN` line.** PRESENTATION §5 files
"the one quiet line (the WITHIN window)". Within *what* is the
question a tired man asks a slip at midnight; the word is trunk's
(RC-03's derivation), not Temple's invention, but Temple materials
the slip and inherits the word without decoding it. Replacement:
**DURING — 09:00 TO 13:00** (the window itself, no preposition
doing unpaid work).

**T-F8 — `THE LAST PEEK IS STILL COOLING`** (DEEPEN §6, minor).
The fact under it is plain — he peeked, the cooldown runs — and
the paired line `PEEK RETURNS IN 14 MIN` decodes in five seconds.
"Still cooling" is a metaphor doing the explaining. Replacement:
**YOU PEEKED JUST NOW** over `PEEK RETURNS IN 14 MIN`.

**T-F9 — Temple's architecture vocabulary is unfenced.** Lintel,
stele, courses, frieze, crepidoma, entablature, stylobate,
colonnade, niche wall, repose, dedication line, hanging tablet:
DEEPEN uses ~12 architecture nouns as design language and nowhere
— not one sentence in either doc — states that none of them ever
prints. Builders port words; that is how "cabinet" (PRESENTATION
§4's own confession) reached glass in the old world. The break's
room is named *the repose* (DEEPEN §7); the day a builder carves
REPOSE over `BREAK · N MIN`, the tired man fails. Charge is the
missing fence, priced at one sentence in DEEPEN §13's bindings:
*no architecture noun prints; the glass vocabulary is the acquitted
list only.*

**T-F10 — `BLOCKING IS OFF — TAP TO FIX`: the destination is
never named.** Fremd §3, the fumble that lives in all worlds: the
loudest line on the screen is the one the groggy thumb taps, and
"if tapping it doesn't start the block, the app has just punished
the most natural tap a groggy person can make." DEEPEN §2 houses
the sentence in the threshold course and rules "the course itself
is the tap" — and still never says where the tap lands (the Guard
fix flow? the missing permission's settings page?). Temple adopted
the sentence at full voice twice (PRESENTATION §1.1, DEEPEN §2)
and answered Fremd's question zero times. Name the landing, in the
spec, or the charge stands at every merge.

### Charges — drifted since Una, or under-read by her

**T-T1 — ST-04's writer is a trap, and the receipt cannot print
the extension. (DEEPEN §10 — the round's real Temple drift.)**
Temple: "the writer such a verb needs already exists in the tree
wearing the tamper penalty's clothes… the receipt prints the
extension as a fact row — EXTENDED +7 MIN — whenever the session
row carries one." Three facts against the sentence, all batch-8:
1. **The field is write-once guarded against exactly this reuse.**
   Repository:1759: `penaltyAdd = if (tamper &&
   session.penaltySecondsAdded == 0) HMAC_TAMPER_PENALTY_SECONDS
   else session.penaltySecondsAdded` — `HMAC_TAMPER_PENALTY_SECONDS
   = 15 * 60` (:98). If his hand writes +420 into that field first,
   a later tamper adds *nothing*: the extension silently disarms
   the penalty. If the penalty lands first, the extension must add
   to a field whose name and meaning are *penalty* — the row cannot
   tell the two apart, ever.
2. **The field prices EVERY block, not the running one.**
   `effectiveTargetSeconds = targetDurationSeconds +
   penaltySecondsAdded` (data/MonolithModels.kt:370), and the span
   derivation prices every block at the effective target, with the
   tree's own comment stating the law (Models:385–398: the penalty
   "is never cleared at a block boundary, so EVERY block's enforced
   target includes it"). A +7 written there extends blocks 2 and 3
   by 7 minutes each as well. The receipt row `EXTENDED +7 MIN`
   would report a third of what the machine actually did.
3. **No extension exists for the receipt to print.** Grep for
   extension/extended over `data/` + ReceiptSheet.kt: zero hits.
   The receipt assembly never reads `penaltySecondsAdded`. "Whenever
   the session row carries one" — the row carries only the penalty
   (Models:285); if the slip ever printed that field as EXTENDED, a
   tamper's +15 would read as the man's own voluntary extension:
   the register's first quiet lie, printed by the honesty clause
   itself.
Una verified the writer *exists* (her Temple §10 verdict). She did
not price its guard, its per-block reach, or the receipt's missing
reader. **Honest price, restated: ST-04 needs its own field (or a
signed delta the updater applies once, to the running block only)
plus a receipt reader — not "the hand that turns" an existing
writer. The machinery is still small; it is not the machinery
Temple named.** (Relief, for the record: the penalty field sits
outside the HMAC payload — CryptoIntegrityManager.kt:53–60 signs
start/target/stopwatch only — so a lawful extension write would not
itself trip the tamper detector. The trap is semantic, not cryptographic.)

**T-T2 — The frieze's data claim is half-true, and the frieze
cannot draw the day's most important span.**
PRESENTATION §2: "Data already exists (routine rows carry
start/end)". The rows do (data/MonolithModels.kt:526–533:
startMinutes / endMinutes / daysMask / enabled). But the scale
Temple draws is "a brass rule ticked 03–21" (PRESENTATION §2;
DEEPEN §2 same range), and the shipped seeds are (data/
MonolithRepository.kt:777–787): FASTED BLOCK 300–480 (05:00–08:00),
DAY BLOCKS 540–1170 (09:00–19:30), **SLEEP 1200–210 (20:00–03:30 —
end before start, overnight)**. On a 03–21 rule, SLEEP's notch runs
off the right end at 21:00 and the remaining 6.5 of its 7.5 hours
exist nowhere on the band. DEEPEN §2 prints the three spans "FASTED
/ DAY / SLEEP left to right" as the frieze's content — as specified,
the third span is un-drawable. Neither doc mentions the overnight
wrap, nor conditions a notch on `enabled` or the day's `daysMask`
(a Mon–Fri routine would be carved on Saturday's wall). The frieze
is the organ Temple promotes to the entablature (DEEPEN §4) and
miniaturises onto the Held Door (§6) — the flaw propagates to three
surfaces. **Cure, spelled: the rule spans the guarded day
03:30–03:30 (his day boundary, the one DrawerDay already files
by), overnight spans wrap, disabled/other-day routines do not
carve.** Until then the claim "the data already exists" is true of
the rows and false of the drawing.

## 4. THE STELE COMPOSITION, ATTACKED — 03:30, one hand, glare

Proof or silence: each charge cites the spec's own text, a trunk
measurement, or Fremd's filed testimony. Where I have no proof,
I have written nothing.

**T-S1 — The bolt is last in a scrolling elevation, and no doc
draws the fold.** The trunk Deck scrolls: `verticalScroll` at
TactileDeckScreen.kt:503, around a dial already 288dp tall (:646).
DEEPEN §2 stacks, top to bottom: lintel → stele (hero + incised
ring) → set-stones course → three-to-four fact courses → dedication
line → frieze course → three threshold steps → the bolt → the
colonnade. That is nine bands where trunk has seven elements, the
frieze course and the steps are *new* height, and the one action
03:30 exists for — drawing the bolt — sits in the last band before
the rail. Neither doc states a viewport budget, a fold line, or
what may scroll away. If the elevation exceeds the screen, the gate
bolt is the first thing pushed off it, and the tired man's first
interaction with Temple is a scroll hunt. The composition's own
logic (the eye *climbs* to the threshold) is the failure: climbing
is a daytime pleasure and a 03:30 tax.

**T-S2 — DEEPEN specifies no size for anything it prints.** Grep
over DEEPEN-temple.md: the only `sp` figure in the file is 88sp —
inside the *refusal* of Obsidian's Monument. Its `dp` figures are
1, 2, and 7 — all shadow depths. Nine new printed things (lintel

OF 3`, `EXTENDED +7 MIN`, the two duration inscriptions, the
`PEEK — 2 MIN` course head) and two new tap tablets (END BREAK,
PEEK {n} MIN) carry no type size and no target height anywhere.
Zug cannot acquit what is not dimensioned; a builder under time
pressure will guess, and the guess will be small, because small is
what specs that say "glyph tier" drift to. PRESENTATION §10 fences
*contrast* for every tier and *size* for none.

**T-S3 — Three keyboards at 03:30.** DEEPEN introduces typed entry
at three places: peek length (§9.1, "typed minutes"), break length
(§9.1, typed), add-time (§10, "typed entry (minutes, exact-value
law)"). Fremd's 03:30 verdict on precision work stands in the
record (REFINE-fremd §3: a precision task in the dark, half asleep,
is the fumble). The exact-value law was written for routine plaques
edited at a desk in daylight; DEEPEN extends it, unargued, to a man
setting his break length from the Deck's base course. No coarse
alternative (the wheel he already trusts, a keypad sheet of the
kind Signal's A2 designs) is offered anywhere in the doc.

**T-S4 — The desk clock's loudest number is not the time.**
Trunk's clock prints one number: the span remaining, formatted
`%02d:%02d` (AirlockAndDisarmOverlays.kt:100–111) at 96sp. DEEPEN
§9.3's cure for the idle lie adds the real hour "glyph-sized" at
the stand's right end (§5/§9.3). So on the face pointed at a
meditating man at 03:30, the monumental figure is a countdown
wearing a clock's format and the actual time is the smallest print
on the surface. Temple diagnosed the idle clock *lying* (§9.3);
the running clock *misleading by hierarchy* is the same sin in a
quieter voice, and Temple designed it. Either the hour takes the
stand at readable size or the countdown stops formatting itself
as HH:MM's sibling — Temple must pick; at present it has both.

**T-S5 — The chisel pair is halved by glare, by Temple's own
numbers.** DEEPEN §3 makes incision "the daylight legibility
system": a 1dp dark edge + a 1dp light edge, and claims "shape
survives any palette lightness". The light edge is the sheet tone
on the ground: by PRESENTATION §0's own luminances (sheet 0.79,
ground 0.72) that edge measures (0.79+.05)/(0.72+.05) ≈ **1.09:1**
— the first casualty of glare, before any other line on the
screen. What survives is the dark edge alone: a single 1dp line,
which may well be enough — but the system as filed is a *pair*,
its proof render does not exist (§13 admits the day-cure render is
still owed), and "survives any palette lightness" is a claim its
own arithmetic contradicts for half its mechanism. Charge the
sentence, not the groove: file the dark edge as the system and the
light edge as its night rake, or render the pair in glare and prove it.

**T-S6 — The Held Door crosses its own inscription, and puts the
temptation under the thumb.** PRESENTATION §7: the vermilion
outline seal is "centred over the name" — a stroked square "roughly
