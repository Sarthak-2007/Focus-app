## §3 · THE RETURN MOMENT — worst felt moment of the walk

As built in this tree, the return verdict is the standing
one Funke's F-1 condemns: on return the sheet sits exactly
where he left it (scroll preserved — genuine praise, the
sheet does not lose his place), and then **nothing
happens**. The FIX key is still dipped 1dp. The word in
the card's corner has changed to SET — printed *dimmer*
than the NOT SET he left (`onSurfaceVariant` against
`onBackground`): success is the quiet word on the card.
The firm click, if the door repaired, arrives ≥800ms
after his eyes land, plus sweep time — too late to bind
to the door he just fixed; it floats, unattributed. If
the door did *not* repair, the dip silently releases and
the only evidence is re-reading a card he believes he
finished. For a full second or more, the half-awake hand
cannot tell success from failure, and must spend scarce
04:00 attention to find out. Every other flaw in this
walk is friction; this one is *doubt*, four times over.

**The Return Chit, judged as a felt event (Funke §1):**
it cures the moment, and the cure is physical, not
verbal. Three of its properties reach the hand before
the eye finishes reading:

1. **Auto-scroll (`bringIntoView`)** — the sheet comes to
   the thumb. He does not travel to the verdict; the
   card he left is placed under his hand. This is the
   only moment in the whole setup where the document
   moves *for* him.
2. **The click at resolution, not at a fixed 800ms** —
   the host judges on the first post-departure
   measurement, so the sound lands while his eyes are
   still arriving. Sound and sight bind. Hand learns
   first, eye confirms — the correct order at 04:00.
3. **The printed line is past tense** — `WENT OUT NOT
   SET · CAME BACK SET.` He is not asked to compare a
   remembered before with a seen after (he has no
   reliable before at 04:00); the chit *is* the memory.
   The `· NEXT — <door>` suffix then names the thumb's
   next target: the return becomes a runway, not a stop.

Verdict: **the hand learns it succeeded before the eye
does — yes, on the REPAIRED path, by design of properties
1+2.** The chit is the merge's felt priority #1. Two
nicks filed at F-5/F-6 below (its type size; its suffix
on the failure path).

## §5 · THE SKIP PATH — is the debt felt fairly?

names the door.

**Verdict: the debt is felt fairly** — priced before,
named at, and remembered after, with no interest
charged. The one half-debt is not this sheet's: the
warrant's fog branches (UNPROVED / usage-UNSET) still
refuse without naming their door (audit §5/§6 deferred

moment the skipper finally *wants* the information, a

seat seconds the referral; it does not re-file it here.

One asymmetry worth naming, not filing: the 0/4 skipper
and the 3/4 skipper pay DONE identically — one tap —

   row. Zero lateral aim, zero re-grips (§1).

   returns to the exact card he left. Losing his place

   REAL…` — it prices the skip before he makes it, in

   re-acquits it.
7. **DONE live at any door state + the total over the
   tear.** Permission and price on the same screen, in
   that order of encounter. The skip is a decision the
   sheet respects, not a failure it punishes.
8. **The identity + boundary in Movement 1.** One read,
   no score fear, nothing promised that Movement 3
