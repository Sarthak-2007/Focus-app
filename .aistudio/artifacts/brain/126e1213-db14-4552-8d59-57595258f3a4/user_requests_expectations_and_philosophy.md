## PART II — Deep Analysis of Your Mindset, Product Philosophy & Expectations

### 1. Why You Reject Standard UI Templates & Demand Custom-Drawn Hardware (`android.view.View` + `Canvas`)
- **Your Observation**: Most Android apps assemble stock Material 3 components (`Button`, `Card`, `Switch`, `Slider`, `NavigationBar`) with rounded pastel cards floating on a 16dp margin. That produces a generic, forgettable aesthetic that feels like a form rather than a physical instrument.
- **Your Expectation**:
  - You demanded: *"Make more than 100 custom views custom components etc. nothing android ui. Handdraw everything. Apply more learnings from pdf"* and *"Take inspiration from other designs and replicate their designs fully"*.
  - Every interactive surface must be drawn directly on `android.graphics.Canvas` with pre-allocated `final Paint`, `final RectF`, and `final Path` objects (zero allocation in `onDraw`), custom touch/velocity physics, and custom-drawn vector glyphs.
  - Controls must behave like machined hardware: dials with radial detents (`RotaryWheelView`), physical slide-to-bolt levers that spring back if released early (`SlideToStartView`), 3-stop mechanical sled toggles (`AppRowSledToggleView`), 45° diamond rotary switches (`RombiKnobView`), `48dp` suitcase grids with custom `28dp` glyphs (`BagTilesSuitcaseView`), `5×7` dot-matrix counters (`TileNumberCounterView`), 7-brick-per-floor architectural towers (`TowerStreakView`), and 7-day vertical mixing-console faders (`DayFadersView`).

### 2. Why You Reject "Optional / Gentle" Locks & Demand Ruthless Enforcement
- **Your Observation**:
  - *"Whats the point of blocking when u can just stop blocking any time, it should be forceful etc too... THere should not be an option to change lock, it should be just one, if even available, now this just gives you unlimited stopping."*
  - If a focus app gives the user a "Stop Session" button or lets them pick an "Easy Lock" mode, the user will simply quit the moment boredom, friction, or a dopamine craving hits.
- **Your Expectation**:
  - **One Ruthless Lock Standard**: Once the 10-second accidental-start grace window expires, the session is locked.
  - **Emergency Exit Only via 60-Second Continuous Hold + Weekly Token Cap**: If a genuine real-world emergency occurs, the user must hold down the **60-Second Continuous Pressure Valve (`Emergency60sValveView`)** for 60,000 continuous milliseconds without lifting their finger for a single frame (lifting resets progress to `0%`), and doing so consumes a limited weekly emergency token.
  - **Dedicated Active Blocking Experience**: Starting a block must change the screen state into an armed lockdown console—locking the dial, activating the live task execution console, arming the Airlock interceptor, and locking the Shield Matrix.
  - **One-Way Ratchet on App Rules**: While a session is active, the user can tighten app rules (`OPEN -> 2m -> SHUT`), but cannot weaken or unlock any `SHUT` app until the timer reaches `00:00`.

### 3. Why You Critiqued the Sound Engine & How You Expect Hardware Audio to Work
- **Your Observation**:
  - *"And is the sounds even real, they are very bad"* -> *"Remove the sound mixer all together, no need for that."*
  - Naive procedural sine-wave loops sound harsh and synthetic, whereas real tactile satisfaction comes from **microsecond-accurate mechanical click transients** fired at the exact instant a detent is crossed.
- **Your Expectation**:
  - Use the exact `DEVELOPER-GUIDE.pdf` §7 Float-PCM synthesizer recipe: zero `.mp3`/`.wav` files, partial weights `{ 0.6f, 0.28f, 0.37f, 2.2f }`, body decay `exp(-0.0114)` per sample step, and tail decay `exp(-0.0313)` per sample step, triggered from the exact same call site as `VibrationEffect.createOneShot(1/4/8/10ms)`.

### 4. Why You Demanded Creative Research (Dribbble, Reddit, Hardware Studios)
- **Your Observation**:
  - *"Dont just look at normal searches do creative searches and dont just look at mediocre search results do better"*
  - Searching "focus app UI" yields generic Pomodoro clones. Looking at **industrial hardware interfaces** (Teenage Engineering OP-1/TP-7, Braun ET66 by Dieter Rams, Leica M11 rangefinders, Nothing OS dot-matrix displays), **hardcore anti-distraction discussions on Reddit** (`r/nosurf`, `r/getdisciplined`, `r/UI_Design`), and **decompiled production craft** (`DEVELOPER-GUIDE.pdf` / Blockit 2.2.7) produces distinctive, memorable controls.
- **Your Expectation**:
  - Every reference must be analyzed structurally: what exact color contrast, corner radius, tick geometry, spring physics, and typographic proportion makes it work, and how our custom `View` code replicates and improves that mechanic.

### 5. Why You Insisted on Zero Feature Loss Across Redesigns
- **Your Observation**:
  - *"When we started there were many features make sure u didn't remove any"*
  - Often when developers redesign a UI, they simplify away power-user features to make the layout easier to build.
